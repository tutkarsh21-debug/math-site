# Know the relations
| Person | Relation to you |
| Father's or mother's father | Grandfather |
| Father's or mother's mother | Grandmother |
| Father's or mother's brother | Uncle |
| Father's or mother's sister | Aunt |
| Uncle's or aunt's son or daughter | Cousin |
| Brother's or sister's son | Nephew |
| Brother's or sister's daughter | Niece |
| Spouse's father | Father-in-law |
| Spouse's mother | Mother-in-law |
| Spouse's brother or sister's husband | Brother-in-law |
| Same parents as you, male | Brother |
| Same parents as you, female | Sister |
- A **generation** is one step up or down the family: grandparents, parents, you, your children.
- **Siblings** are brothers and sisters. They are in the same generation.
- If a name does not say whether the person is male or female, do not assume. Many puzzles can be solved without it.

# Solving a relation puzzle
- Work **backwards** through the sentence, one small piece at a time, and replace each piece by a simple relation.
- "My father's only son" is **me** (if I am a boy) or **my brother**. "My mother's only daughter" is me (if I am a girl) or my sister.
- Draw dots for the people. Join husband and wife with a horizontal line and draw children below them.
?eg Pointing to a boy, Anil says, "He is the son of my father's only son." How is the boy related to Anil?
= "My father's only son" is Anil himself (Anil is a boy because he is the only son).
= The boy is the son of Anil.
=> Son
?eg Pointing to a woman, Ravi says, "She is the daughter of my father's mother's only son." How is the woman related to Ravi?
= My father's mother's only son is Ravi's father.
= So the woman is the daughter of Ravi's father.
=> Sister
?eg A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. E is D's mother. How is A related to D?
= C is B's mother, and A and B are brother and sister, so C is the mother of A too.
= D is C's father, so D is the grandfather of A.
=> A is the granddaughter of D.

# Counting members
- Add up each distinct person once. Husband and wife are two persons; each child is one person.
?eg A family has a grandfather, a grandmother, a father, a mother, two sons and one daughter. How many members are there?
= $2 + 2 + 3 = 7$.
=> 7 members
!tip After each step, write the relation you have found on the page. Never carry two unfinished relations in your head at the same time.

@@dpp
Q: What is your father's brother called?
A: Uncle.

Q: What is your mother's father called?
A: Grandfather.

Q: Pointing to a lady, Seema says, "She is the mother of my mother's only son." How is the lady related to Seema?
A: Mother (Seema's mother's only son is Seema's brother, and his mother is Seema's mother).

Q: A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?
A: Uncle (A is the brother of C, who is D's father).

Q: Pointing to a man, Kiran, a boy, says, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the man related to Kiran?
A: Nephew (the man's mother is Kiran's sister, so the man is Kiran's sister's son).

Q: A family has a grandfather, a grandmother, a father, a mother, two sons and one daughter. How many members does the family have?
A: 7.

Q: P is the mother of Q. Q is the brother of R. How is P related to R?
A: Mother.

Q: Rohit's father's only brother has a daughter named Neha. How is Neha related to Rohit?
A: Cousin.

Q: My mother's mother's only daughter is my ...?
A: Mother.

Q: Ram is Shyam's brother. Shyam's father is Mohan. Mohan's mother is Leela. How is Leela related to Ram?
A: Grandmother (Leela is Ram's father's mother).
