# Solved HOTS questions
- For a quadratic $ax^2 + bx + c$ with zeroes $\alpha$ and $\beta$: $\alpha + \beta = -\dfrac{b}{a}$ and $\alpha\beta = \dfrac{c}{a}$. Most HOTS questions ask for an expression that can be rewritten using these two numbers.
?eg If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$, find $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta}$.
= $\alpha + \beta = 5$ and $\alpha\beta = 6$.
= $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = 25 - 12 = 13$.
= $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta} = \dfrac{\alpha + \beta}{\alpha\beta} = \dfrac{5}{6}$.
=> 13 and $\dfrac{5}{6}$
?eg If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $2x^2 - 7x + 3$, find $\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta} + \dfrac{\beta}{\alpha}$.
= $\alpha + \beta = \dfrac{7}{2}$ and $\alpha\beta = \dfrac{3}{2}$.
= $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = \dfrac{49}{4} - 3 = \dfrac{37}{4}$.
= $\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta} + \dfrac{\beta}{\alpha} = \dfrac{\alpha^2 + \beta^2}{\alpha\beta} = \dfrac{37/4}{3/2}$.
=> $\dfrac{37}{6}$
?eg Find $k$ if one zero of $3x^2 + 8x + k$ is the reciprocal of the other.
= If the zeroes are $\alpha$ and $\dfrac{1}{\alpha}$, their product is 1.
= The product is $\dfrac{k}{3}$, so $\dfrac{k}{3} = 1$.
=> $k = 3$
?eg The sum of the zeroes of $x^2 - (k + 6)x + 2(2k - 1)$ is half of their product. Find $k$.
= Sum $= k + 6$ and product $= 2(2k - 1)$.
= $k + 6 = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2(2k - 1) = 2k - 1$, so $k = 7$.
=> 7
?eg Divide $x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 3$ by $x^2 - 2$ and find the quotient and the remainder.
= $x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 3 \div (x^2 - 2)$: the first term of the quotient is $x$. Subtract $x(x^2 - 2) = x^3 - 2x$ to get $-3x^2 + 7x - 3$.
= The next term is $-3$. Subtract $-3(x^2 - 2) = -3x^2 + 6$ to get $7x - 9$.
= The degree of $7x - 9$ is less than the degree of the divisor, so we stop.
=> Quotient $x - 3$, remainder $7x - 9$

@@dpp
Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 7x + 10$, find $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$.
A: 29.

Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $3x^2 - 5x - 2$, find $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta}$.
A: $-\dfrac{5}{2}$.

Q: Find $k$ if one zero of $5x^2 - 12x + k$ is the reciprocal of the other.
A: $k = 5$.

Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 6x + 8$, form a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are $2\alpha$ and $2\beta$.
A: $x^2 - 12x + 32$.

Q: Find $k$ if the sum of the zeroes of $kx^2 + 2x + 3k$ equals their product.
A: $k = -\dfrac{2}{3}$.

Q: Find the zeroes of $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$.
A: 1, 2 and 3.

Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 4x + 1$, find $\alpha^3 + \beta^3$.
A: 52.

Q: Find the remainder when $x^3 + 2x^2 - x - 2$ is divided by $x + 2$.
A: 0 (so $x + 2$ is a factor).
