# Speed, distance and time
- $\text{speed} = \dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$, so $\text{distance} = \text{speed} \times \text{time}$ and $\text{time} = \dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$.
- Keep the units matching: km with hours gives km/h, and metres with seconds gives m/s.
- To change km/h to m/s, multiply by $\dfrac{5}{18}$. To change m/s to km/h, multiply by $\dfrac{18}{5}$.
?eg Change 72 km/h to m/s.
= $72 \times \dfrac{5}{18} = 20$.
=> 20 m/s
?eg How long does a car take to cover 150 km at 50 km/h?
= $\text{time} = \dfrac{150}{50}$.
=> 3 hours

# Average speed
- **Average speed** $= \dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$. It is **not** the average of the two speeds unless the times are equal.
- If the **same distance** is covered at speeds $x$ and $y$, the average speed is $\dfrac{2xy}{x + y}$.
?eg A car travels at 60 km/h for 2 hours and at 40 km/h for 3 hours. Find the average speed.
= Distance $= 120 + 120 = 240$ km. Time $= 5$ hours. Speed $= \dfrac{240}{5}$.
=> 48 km/h
?eg A person goes to a place at 40 km/h and returns by the same road at 60 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole journey.
= The distances are equal, so the average speed is $\dfrac{2 \times 40 \times 60}{40 + 60} = \dfrac{4800}{100}$.
=> 48 km/h

# Relative speed
- Two objects moving **towards each other** (or in opposite directions) have relative speed $= $ the **sum** of the speeds.
- Two objects moving in the **same direction** have relative speed $= $ the **difference** of the speeds.
- The time to meet or to catch up is $\dfrac{\text{gap}}{\text{relative speed}}$.
?eg Two cars start 300 km apart and move towards each other at 50 km/h and 70 km/h. When do they meet?
= Relative speed $= 50 + 70 = 120$ km/h. Time $= \dfrac{300}{120}$.
=> 2.5 hours

# Trains and boats
- A train of length $L$ crossing a **pole** or a person must cover its own length $L$. Crossing a **platform** or bridge of length $P$ it must cover $L + P$.
- A boat with speed $u$ in still water in a stream of speed $v$ goes **downstream** at $u + v$ and **upstream** at $u - v$.
?eg A train 150 m long runs at 54 km/h. How long does it take to cross a pole, and to cross a platform 350 m long?
= Speed $= 54 \times \dfrac{5}{18} = 15$ m/s.
= Pole: $\dfrac{150}{15} = 10$ s. Platform: $\dfrac{150 + 350}{15} = 33\dfrac{1}{3}$ s.
=> 10 s and $33\dfrac{1}{3}$ s
?eg A boat has a speed of 10 km/h in still water and the stream flows at 2 km/h. How long does it take to go 24 km downstream?
= Downstream speed $= 10 + 2 = 12$ km/h. Time $= \dfrac{24}{12}$.
=> 2 hours
!tip Change every speed to one unit first, either km/h or m/s, before you divide.
