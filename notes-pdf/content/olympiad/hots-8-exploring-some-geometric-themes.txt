# Solved HOTS questions
- For a solid with $V$ vertices, $E$ edges and $F$ faces, Euler's relation says $V - E + F = 2$. A **net** is a flat pattern that folds into a solid. A **fractal** repeats the same pattern at smaller and smaller scales.
?eg Count the faces, edges and vertices of a cube and check Euler's relation.
= A cube has 6 faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices.
= $V - E + F = 8 - 12 + 6 = 2$.
=> 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices; the relation holds
?eg A solid has 6 vertices and 9 edges. Find the number of faces and name a solid like this.
= $6 - 9 + F = 2$, so $F = 5$.
= A triangular prism has 2 triangular faces and 3 rectangular faces, 6 vertices and 9 edges.
=> 5 faces; a triangular prism
?eg A Sierpinski triangle is made by dividing an equilateral triangle into 4 smaller ones and removing the middle one, then repeating on each remaining triangle. How many small triangles are left after 1, 2 and 3 steps?
= After step 1: 3 triangles. Each step multiplies the count by 3.
= After step 2: $3 \times 3 = 9$. After step 3: $9 \times 3 = 27$.
=> 3, 9 and 27
?eg A line segment of length 1 is changed by replacing its middle third by two sides of an equilateral triangle (a Koch step). Find the length after 1 step and after 2 steps.
= In one step the segment is replaced by 4 pieces, each $\dfrac{1}{3}$ as long, so the length is multiplied by $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
= After 1 step: $\dfrac{4}{3}$. After 2 steps: $\dfrac{16}{9}$.
=> $\dfrac{4}{3}$ and $\dfrac{16}{9}$
?eg What are the front view and the top view of a cylinder standing on its circular base?
= From the front, a cylinder looks like a rectangle.
= From above it looks like a circle.
=> Front view a rectangle, top view a circle

Q: Find the number of faces, edges and vertices of a triangular pyramid (tetrahedron).
A: 4 faces, 6 edges and 4 vertices.

Q: A solid has 8 vertices and 6 faces. How many edges does it have?
A: 12.

Q: How many shaded triangles are left in a Sierpinski triangle after 4 steps?
A: 81.

Q: A Koch step multiplies the length by $\dfrac{4}{3}$. Find the length of a segment of length 1 after 3 steps.
A: $\dfrac{64}{27}$.

Q: How many rectangles are there in the net of a cuboid?
A: 6.

Q: A solid has 12 edges and 6 faces. Find the number of vertices.
A: 8.

Q: How many faces does a prism with a hexagonal base have?
A: 8 (two hexagons and six rectangles).

Q: What is the top view of a cone standing on its base?
A: A circle (with the tip at its centre).
