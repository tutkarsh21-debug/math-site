# Solved HOTS questions
- The graph of $ax + by = c$ is a straight line, and every point on it is a solution. The slope of the line through $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is $\dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$. A pair of lines meets at one point (one solution), is parallel (no solution), or is the same line (infinitely many solutions).
?eg Solve $x + y = 10$ and $x - y = 2$.
= Adding the two equations: $2x = 12$, so $x = 6$.
= Then $y = 10 - 6 = 4$.
=> $x = 6$ and $y = 4$
?eg Find the slope of the line through $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 8)$.
= Slope $= \dfrac{8 - 2}{4 - 1} = \dfrac{6}{3}$.
=> 2
?eg Solve $2x + 3y = 12$ and $4x - y = 10$ by substitution.
= From the second equation $y = 4x - 10$.
= $2x + 3(4x - 10) = 12$, so $14x = 42$ and $x = 3$.
= $y = 4 \times 3 - 10 = 2$.
=> $x = 3$ and $y = 2$
?eg Find $k$ if the point $(2, 3)$ lies on the line $kx + 2y = 12$.
= Substituting: $2k + 6 = 12$, so $k = 3$.
=> $k = 3$
?eg The sum of two numbers is 45 and their difference is 9. Find the numbers.
= $x + y = 45$ and $x - y = 9$. Adding: $2x = 54$, so $x = 27$.
= $y = 45 - 27 = 18$.
=> 27 and 18

Q: Solve $x + y = 14$ and $x - y = 4$.
A: $x = 9$ and $y = 5$.

Q: Find the slope of the line through $(-1, 3)$ and $(2, -3)$.
A: $-2$.

Q: Solve $3x + 2y = 16$ and $x - y = 2$.
A: $x = 4$ and $y = 2$.

Q: Find $k$ if the point $(1, -2)$ lies on the line $3x + ky = 7$.
A: $k = -2$.

Q: The sum of two numbers is 60 and their difference is 14. Find the numbers.
A: 37 and 23.

Q: 5 pens and 3 pencils cost Rs 31, and 2 pens and 4 pencils cost Rs 18. Find the cost of a pen and of a pencil.
A: A pen costs Rs 5 and a pencil costs Rs 2.

Q: Find the point where the lines $2x + y = 5$ and $x - y = 1$ meet.
A: $(2, 1)$.

Q: Do the lines $2x + 3y = 6$ and $4x + 6y = 7$ meet? Why?
A: No. They have the same slope but are different lines, so they are parallel.
