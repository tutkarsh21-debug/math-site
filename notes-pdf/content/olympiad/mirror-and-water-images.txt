# Mirror image and water image
- A **mirror image** is what you see when a mirror is placed **beside** an object, on its left or right side. It turns the object **left to right**: the left side becomes the right side.
- A **water image** is what you see in water **below** an object, or when the mirror lies **under** it. It turns the object **upside down**: the top becomes the bottom, and the left stays on the left.
- In a mirror at the **right** side of a word, the **order of the letters is reversed** and each letter is turned around. The last letter of the word is nearest to the mirror, so it appears at the **left** of the image.
?eg A mirror is placed on the right of the word STAR. Which letter appears at the extreme left of the image?
= The letter nearest to the mirror is R, the last letter of STAR. It appears first in the image.
=> R
- A person raising the right hand sees the image raising the **left** hand.

# Letters and words that do not change
- A letter looks the **same** in a mirror (placed at the side) if it is symmetrical about a **vertical** line: **A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y**.
- A letter looks the **same** in a water image if it is symmetrical about a **horizontal** line: **B, C, D, E, H, I, K, O, X**.
- A word looks the same in a mirror if all its letters are in the vertical list **and** the word reads the same forwards and backwards. Examples: MOM, WOW, TOT, MUM, AHA, OTTO.
- A word looks the same in a water image if all its letters are in the horizontal list. Examples: BOX, DICE, ECHO, CHECK, HIDE, BIKE.
?eg Which of these words looks the same in a water image: DICE, MAN, TAP, WIN?
= D, I, C and E are all symmetrical about a horizontal line. M, A, N, T, P, W are not all so.
=> DICE
?eg Which of these letters looks the same in a mirror: B, D, M, R?
= Only M is symmetrical about a vertical line.
=> M

# Mirror image of a clock
- In the mirror image of a clock, the hour hand and the minute hand both swap sides. The reading is found by subtracting the time from 12:00.
$$\text{mirror image} = 12{:}00 - \text{time}$$
- On a clock face 12:20 is the same position as 0:20, so a time between 12:00 and 1:00 is treated as 0 hours and some minutes.
- You can also subtract the hours from 11 and the minutes from 60: the mirror image of 3:40 is $11 - 3 = 8$ hours and $60 - 40 = 20$ minutes.
?eg Find the mirror image of the time 3:40.
= $12{:}00 - 3{:}40 = 8{:}20$.
=> 8:20
?eg Find the mirror image of the time 12:20.
= Treat 12:20 as 0:20 on the dial.
= $12{:}00 - 0{:}20 = 11{:}40$.
=> 11:40
!tip A mirror image of 3:00 is 9:00, and of 6:00 is 6:00. If your answer to a clock question is far from these, check your subtraction.

@@formulas
Mirror at the side | left and right swap, and the order of letters reverses
Water image | top and bottom swap, and the order of letters stays
Letters same in a mirror | A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y
Letters same in a water image | B, C, D, E, H, I, K, O, X
Mirror image of a clock | $12{:}00 -$ the time

@@dpp
Q: Find the mirror image of the time 3:40.
A: 8:20 ($12{:}00 - 3{:}40$).

Q: Find the mirror image of the time 7:15.
A: 4:45.

Q: Find the mirror image of the time 10:50.
A: 1:10.

Q: A clock shows 6:30. What time does its mirror image show?
A: 5:30.

Q: Which of these letters looks the same in a mirror: B, D, M, R?
A: M.

Q: Which of these letters looks the same in a water image: A, B, M, T?
A: B.

Q: Which of these words looks the same in a mirror: MOM, DAD, BOB, CAT?
A: MOM (all its letters are symmetrical about a vertical line and the word is the same backwards).

Q: A mirror is placed on the right of the word MATH. In what order are the letters seen in the image, from the left?
A: H, T, A, M.

Q: Which of these words looks the same in a water image: DICE, MOM, TOP, WAY?
A: DICE.

Q: Which of these numbers looks the same in a mirror: 808, 828, 345, 267?
A: 808 (the digits 8 and 0 are symmetrical, and the number is the same backwards).
