# Solved HOTS questions
- Area formulas: rectangle $l \times b$; parallelogram base $\times$ height; triangle $\dfrac{1}{2} \times$ base $\times$ height; trapezium $\dfrac{1}{2}(a + b) \times h$; rhombus $\dfrac{1}{2} d_1 d_2$. For a path of width $w$ around a rectangle, find the area of the bigger rectangle and subtract the area of the smaller one. $1$ hectare $= 10\,000$ square metres.
?eg Find the area of a parallelogram with base 15 cm and height 8 cm.
= Area $= 15 \times 8$.
=> 120 square cm
?eg Find the area of a trapezium whose parallel sides are 12 cm and 18 cm and whose height is 10 cm.
= Area $= \dfrac{1}{2}(12 + 18) \times 10 = 15 \times 10$.
=> 150 square cm
?eg The area of a triangle is 84 square cm and its base is 14 cm. Find its height.
= $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 14 \times h = 84$, so $7h = 84$.
=> 12 cm
?eg A path 2 m wide runs around the outside of a rectangular garden 30 m by 20 m. Find the area of the path.
= The outer rectangle is $(30 + 4) \times (20 + 4) = 34 \times 24 = 816$ square m.
= The garden has area $30 \times 20 = 600$ square m.
= The path is $816 - 600$.
=> 216 square m
?eg A rhombus has a side of 10 cm and one diagonal of 12 cm. Find its area.
= Half of the given diagonal is 6 cm. Half of the other diagonal $= \sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = 8$ cm, so the other diagonal is 16 cm.
= Area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 16$.
=> 96 square cm

Q: Find the area of a parallelogram with base 12 cm and height 7 cm.
A: 84 square cm.

Q: Find the area of a trapezium with parallel sides 8 cm and 14 cm and height 6 cm.
A: 66 square cm.

Q: The area of a triangle is 60 square cm and its base is 15 cm. Find its height.
A: 8 cm.

Q: A path 1.5 m wide runs around the inside of a rectangular garden 20 m by 14 m. Find the area of the path.
A: 93 square m (the inner rectangle is $17 \times 11 = 187$ and the garden is 280).

Q: Find the area of a rhombus with diagonals 10 cm and 14 cm.
A: 70 square cm.

Q: A floor 6 m by 4 m is covered with square tiles of side 50 cm. How many tiles are needed?
A: 96.

Q: Find the area, in square metres, of a field of 2.5 hectares.
A: 25 000 square m.

Q: The area of a square is 144 square cm. Find its perimeter.
A: 48 cm.
