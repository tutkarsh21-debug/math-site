# Solved HOTS questions
- In a right-angled triangle the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$. If $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ for the three sides of a triangle, the triangle is right-angled (the converse). The square on the diagonal of a square has twice the area of the square.
?eg The legs of a right triangle are 9 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
= $\sqrt{81 + 144} = \sqrt{225}$.
=> 15 cm
?eg Is $(7, 24, 25)$ a Pythagorean triple?
= $7^2 + 24^2 = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25^2$.
=> Yes
?eg A ladder 13 m long has its foot 5 m from a wall. How high up the wall does it reach?
= Height $= \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{144}$.
=> 12 m
?eg A rectangle has a diagonal of 25 cm and a length of 24 cm. Find its breadth and its area.
= Breadth $= \sqrt{25^2 - 24^2} = \sqrt{49} = 7$ cm.
= Area $= 24 \times 7$.
=> 7 cm and 168 square cm
?eg A square has side 5 cm. Find the side of a square whose area is double that of the first.
= The area of the first square is 25 square cm, so the new area is 50 square cm.
= The side of the new square is $\sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$ cm, which is the diagonal of the first square.
=> $5\sqrt{2}$ cm, about 7.07 cm

Q: The legs of a right triangle are 8 cm and 15 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
A: 17 cm.

Q: Is $(9, 40, 41)$ a Pythagorean triple?
A: Yes, since $81 + 1600 = 1681 = 41^2$.

Q: A ladder 25 m long has its foot 7 m from a wall. How high does it reach?
A: 24 m.

Q: A rectangle has a diagonal of 13 cm and a length of 12 cm. Find its breadth.
A: 5 cm.

Q: Find the diagonal of a square of side 7 cm.
A: $7\sqrt{2}$ cm.

Q: Find the height of an equilateral triangle of side 10 cm.
A: $5\sqrt{3}$ cm, about 8.66 cm.

Q: Write a Pythagorean triple whose members are all multiples of 10.
A: 30, 40, 50 (ten times 3, 4, 5).

Q: Two poles 6 m and 11 m tall stand 12 m apart. Find the distance between their tops.
A: 13 m.
