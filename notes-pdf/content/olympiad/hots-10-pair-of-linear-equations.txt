# Solved HOTS questions
- Two good habits: name the unknowns in the first line, and look for a **substitution** such as $u = \dfrac{1}{x}$ that turns a hard-looking pair into a linear pair.
?eg Solve $\dfrac{2}{x} + \dfrac{3}{y} = 13$ and $\dfrac{5}{x} - \dfrac{4}{y} = -2$.
= Put $u = \dfrac{1}{x}$ and $v = \dfrac{1}{y}$. Then $2u + 3v = 13$ and $5u - 4v = -2$.
= Multiply the first by 4 and the second by 3: $8u + 12v = 52$ and $15u - 12v = -6$. Adding gives $23u = 46$, so $u = 2$.
= Then $3v = 13 - 4 = 9$, so $v = 3$.
=> $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $y = \dfrac{1}{3}$
?eg The digits of a two-digit number add up to 9. The number obtained by reversing the digits is 27 more than the original number. Find the number.
= Let the tens digit be $x$ and the units digit $y$. Then $x + y = 9$.
= $10y + x = 10x + y + 27$ gives $9y - 9x = 27$, so $y - x = 3$.
= Adding $x + y = 9$ and $y - x = 3$ gives $y = 6$ and $x = 3$.
=> 36
?eg Five years ago A was three times as old as B. Ten years from now A will be twice as old as B. Find their present ages.
= Let the present ages be $a$ and $b$. Then $a - 5 = 3(b - 5)$, so $a = 3b - 10$.
= And $a + 10 = 2(b + 10)$, so $a = 2b + 10$.
= $3b - 10 = 2b + 10$ gives $b = 20$, and then $a = 50$.
=> A is 50 years old and B is 20 years old
?eg For what value of $k$ does the pair $kx + 3y = k - 3$ and $12x + ky = k$ have infinitely many solutions?
= For infinitely many solutions $\dfrac{k}{12} = \dfrac{3}{k} = \dfrac{k - 3}{k}$.
= From $\dfrac{k}{12} = \dfrac{3}{k}$ we get $k^2 = 36$, so $k = 6$ or $k = -6$.
= From $\dfrac{3}{k} = \dfrac{k - 3}{k}$ we get $k - 3 = 3$, so $k = 6$.
=> $k = 6$
?eg A boat goes 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours. It goes 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream in 13 hours. Find the speed of the stream and the speed of the boat in still water.
= Let the boat's speed be $b$ and the stream's speed $s$ (km/h). Put $p = \dfrac{1}{b - s}$ and $q = \dfrac{1}{b + s}$.
= $30p + 44q = 10$ and $40p + 55q = 13$.
= Multiply the first by 4 and the second by 3: $120p + 176q = 40$ and $120p + 165q = 39$. Subtracting gives $11q = 1$, so $q = \dfrac{1}{11}$.
= Then $30p = 10 - 4 = 6$, so $p = \dfrac{1}{5}$. So $b - s = 5$ and $b + s = 11$.
=> Boat 8 km/h and stream 3 km/h

Q: Solve $\dfrac{2}{x} + \dfrac{1}{y} = 4$ and $\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{3}{y} = 7$.
A: $x = 1$ and $y = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: The digits of a two-digit number add up to 11. If 45 is subtracted from the number, the digits are reversed. Find the number.
A: 83.

Q: A father is three times as old as his son. Five years later he will be two and a half times as old as his son. Find their present ages.
A: Father 45 years, son 15 years.

Q: For what value of $k$ does $3x + 2y = 5$ and $9x + ky = 10$ have no solution?
A: $k = 6$.

Q: A boat goes 24 km upstream and 28 km downstream in 6 hours. It goes 30 km upstream and 21 km downstream in 6.5 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water and of the stream.
A: Boat 10 km/h and stream 4 km/h.

Q: A fraction becomes 1 if 1 is added to the numerator and 1 is subtracted from the denominator. It becomes $\dfrac{1}{2}$ if only 1 is added to the denominator. Find the fraction.
A: $\dfrac{3}{5}$.

Q: If the length of a rectangle is decreased by 5 and its breadth is increased by 3, its area decreases by 9. If the length is increased by 3 and the breadth by 2, the area increases by 67. Find the length and the breadth.
A: Length 17 and breadth 9.

Q: Find the point where the lines $x + y = 6$ and $x - y = 2$ meet.
A: $(4, 2)$.
