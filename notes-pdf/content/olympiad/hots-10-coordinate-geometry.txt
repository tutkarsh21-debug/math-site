# Solved HOTS questions
- Tools: distance $= \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$; section formula for the ratio $m : n$: $\left(\dfrac{mx_2 + nx_1}{m + n},\ \dfrac{my_2 + ny_1}{m + n}\right)$; midpoint is the case $1 : 1$. A point on the x-axis has $y = 0$, and a point on the y-axis has $x = 0$.
?eg Find the point on the x-axis which is equidistant from $(2, -5)$ and $(-2, 9)$.
= Let the point be $(x, 0)$. Its distances: $(x - 2)^2 + 25 = (x + 2)^2 + 81$.
= $x^2 - 4x + 29 = x^2 + 4x + 85$, so $-8x = 56$ and $x = -7$.
=> $(-7, 0)$
?eg Find the ratio in which the line segment joining $(-3, 10)$ and $(6, -8)$ is divided by the point $(-1, 6)$.
= Let the ratio be $k : 1$. The x-coordinate is $\dfrac{6k - 3}{k + 1} = -1$, so $6k - 3 = -k - 1$ and $k = \dfrac{2}{7}$.
= Check with y: $\dfrac{-8k + 10}{k + 1} = \dfrac{-16/7 + 10}{9/7} = \dfrac{54/7}{9/7} = 6$. It matches.
=> $2 : 7$
?eg Find the value of $k$ so that the points $(1, 5)$, $(2, 3)$ and $(-2, k)$ are collinear.
= For three points to be collinear the area of the triangle they form is zero.
= $\dfrac{1}{2}\,|1(3 - k) + 2(k - 5) + (-2)(5 - 3)| = 0$.
= $3 - k + 2k - 10 - 4 = 0$, so $k - 11 = 0$.
=> $k = 11$
?eg Show that the points $A(1, 7)$, $B(4, 2)$, $C(-1, -1)$ and $D(-4, 4)$ are the vertices of a square.
= $AB = \sqrt{9 + 25} = \sqrt{34}$, $BC = \sqrt{25 + 9} = \sqrt{34}$, $CD = \sqrt{9 + 25} = \sqrt{34}$, $DA = \sqrt{25 + 9} = \sqrt{34}$. All sides are equal.
= The diagonals are $AC = \sqrt{4 + 64} = \sqrt{68}$ and $BD = \sqrt{64 + 4} = \sqrt{68}$. They are equal.
= A quadrilateral with four equal sides and equal diagonals is a square.
=> ABCD is a square
?eg The centre of a circle is $(2a, a - 7)$. The circle passes through $(11, -9)$ and has radius $5\sqrt{2}$. Find the values of $a$.
= The distance from the centre to $(11, -9)$ is the radius: $(2a - 11)^2 + (a - 7 + 9)^2 = 50$.
= $4a^2 - 44a + 121 + a^2 + 4a + 4 = 50$, so $5a^2 - 40a + 75 = 0$, that is $a^2 - 8a + 15 = 0$.
= $(a - 3)(a - 5) = 0$.
=> $a = 3$ or $a = 5$

@@dpp
Q: Find the point on the y-axis which is equidistant from $(-5, 2)$ and $(9, -2)$.
A: $(0, -7)$.

Q: Find the ratio in which the point $(2, y)$ divides the segment joining $(-4, 3)$ and $(6, 8)$, and find $y$.
A: Ratio $3 : 2$ and $y = 6$.

Q: Find $k$ so that $(k, 2)$, $(4, 6)$ and $(5, 8)$ are collinear.
A: $k = 2$.

Q: Show that $(0, -1)$, $(-2, 3)$, $(6, 7)$ and $(8, 3)$ are the vertices of a rectangle.
A: Opposite sides $\sqrt{20}$ and $\sqrt{80}$ are equal in pairs, and the diagonals are both $\sqrt{100}$.

Q: Find the area of the triangle with vertices $(1, 2)$, $(4, 6)$ and $(7, 2)$.
A: 12 square units.

Q: Find the coordinates of the points which divide the segment joining $(4, -1)$ and $(-2, -3)$ into three equal parts.
A: $(2, -\dfrac{5}{3})$ and $(0, -\dfrac{7}{3})$.

Q: The midpoint of the segment joining $(a, 5)$ and $(7, b)$ is $(3, 4)$. Find $a$ and $b$.
A: $a = -1$ and $b = 3$.

Q: The point $(x, y)$ is equidistant from $(3, 6)$ and $(-3, 4)$. Find the relation between $x$ and $y$.
A: $3x + y - 5 = 0$.
