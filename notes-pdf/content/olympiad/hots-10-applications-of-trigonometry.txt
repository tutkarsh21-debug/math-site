# Solved HOTS questions
- Draw a clear figure first and mark the horizontal line. The angle of elevation or depression is measured from the horizontal. Useful values: $\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$, $\tan 45^\circ = 1$, $\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}$.
?eg From the top of a building 60 m high, the angles of depression of the top and the bottom of a tower are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
= Horizontal distance $d = \dfrac{60}{\tan 60^\circ} = \dfrac{60}{\sqrt{3}} = 20\sqrt{3}$ m.
= The top of the tower is $d \tan 30^\circ = 20\sqrt{3} \times \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = 20$ m below the observer.
= Height of the tower $= 60 - 20$.
=> 40 m
?eg From a point on the ground the angles of elevation of the bottom and the top of a tower fixed at the top of a 30 m high building are $45^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
= The distance from the point to the building is $\dfrac{30}{\tan 45^\circ} = 30$ m.
= The top of the tower is at height $30 \tan 60^\circ = 30\sqrt{3}$ m.
= Height of the tower $= 30\sqrt{3} - 30 = 30(\sqrt{3} - 1)$.
=> $30(\sqrt{3} - 1)$ m, about 22 m
?eg The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point on the ground is $30^\circ$. After walking 20 m towards the tower the angle of elevation becomes $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
= Let the height be $h$ and the new distance from the foot be $x$. Then $\dfrac{h}{x} = \tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}$ and $\dfrac{h}{x + 20} = \tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.
= $h = \sqrt{3}\,x$ and $\sqrt{3}\,h = x + 20$. Substituting: $3x = x + 20$, so $x = 10$.
= $h = 10\sqrt{3}$.
=> $10\sqrt{3}$ m, about 17.3 m
?eg Two poles of equal height stand on either side of a road 80 m wide. At a point on the road between them the angles of elevation of the tops are $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$. Find the height of the poles and the distances of the point from the poles.
= Let the distances be $x$ and $80 - x$ and the common height $h$. $h = x\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}\,x$ and $h = (80 - x)\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{80 - x}{\sqrt{3}}$.
= $3x = 80 - x$, so $x = 20$ and $h = 20\sqrt{3}$.
=> Height $20\sqrt{3}$ m (about 34.6 m); distances 20 m and 60 m
?eg A man on the top of a 75 m high lighthouse sees two ships in the same direction due west, with angles of depression $30^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. Find the distance between the ships.
= The nearer ship is $\dfrac{75}{\tan 45^\circ} = 75$ m from the foot. The farther ship is $\dfrac{75}{\tan 30^\circ} = 75\sqrt{3}$ m from the foot.
= The distance between them is $75\sqrt{3} - 75 = 75(\sqrt{3} - 1)$.
=> $75(\sqrt{3} - 1)$ m, about 55 m

Q: The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point 30 m from its foot on level ground is $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
A: $30\sqrt{3}$ m, about 52 m.

Q: From the top of a 50 m high building the angle of depression of the foot of a tower is $45^\circ$ and the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is $30^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
A: $50 + \dfrac{50}{\sqrt{3}}$ m, about 78.9 m.

Q: The angle of elevation of the top of a tower is $30^\circ$. After walking 30 m towards it the angle becomes $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
A: $15\sqrt{3}$ m, about 26 m.

Q: Two poles of equal height stand on either side of a road 60 m wide. At a point on the road the angles of elevation of their tops are $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$. Find the height and the distances of the point from the poles.
A: Height $15\sqrt{3}$ m, distances 15 m and 45 m.

Q: From the top of a 100 m high hill the angles of depression of two boats in the same direction are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the distance between the boats.
A: $\dfrac{200}{\sqrt{3}}$ m, about 115.5 m.

Q: A kite is flying at a height of 60 m and the string makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the ground. Find the length of the string, assuming it is straight.
A: $40\sqrt{3}$ m, about 69.3 m.

Q: The shadow of a tower on level ground is $\sqrt{3}$ times its height. Find the angle of elevation of the sun.
A: $30^\circ$.

Q: A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the ground. How high up the wall does it reach?
A: $5\sqrt{3}$ m, about 8.66 m.
