# Ratio
- The **ratio** $a : b$ compares two quantities of the same kind. It is the fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$ and has no units.
- A ratio is simplified by dividing both terms by their common factor: $45 : 60 = 3 : 4$.
- To **share** a quantity in the ratio $a : b : c$, add the parts $a + b + c$. One part is the quantity divided by this sum; then multiply by $a$, $b$ and $c$.
- If $a : b$ and $b : c$ are given, make the value of $b$ the same in both, then join them to get $a : b : c$.
?eg Divide Rs 360 in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$.
= Total parts $= 2 + 3 + 4 = 9$. One part $= \dfrac{360}{9} = 40$.
= Shares: $2 \times 40,\ 3 \times 40,\ 4 \times 40$.
=> Rs 80, Rs 120 and Rs 160
?eg If $a : b = 2 : 3$ and $b : c = 4 : 5$, find $a : b : c$.
= Make $b$ equal in both: $a : b = 8 : 12$ and $b : c = 12 : 15$.
=> $8 : 12 : 15$
?eg The ages of two people are in the ratio $3 : 5$ and their sum is 40 years. Find the ages.
= One part $= \dfrac{40}{8} = 5$.
=> 15 years and 25 years

# Proportion
- Four numbers $a, b, c, d$ are in **proportion** if $a : b = c : d$. Then $a \times d = b \times c$ (product of extremes = product of means).
- The **fourth proportional** to $a, b, c$ is $d = \dfrac{bc}{a}$. The **mean proportional** between $a$ and $b$ is $\sqrt{ab}$.
?eg Find $x$ if $4 : 7 = 12 : x$.
= $4x = 7 \times 12 = 84$.
=> $x = 21$
?eg Find the mean proportional between 4 and 9.
= $\sqrt{4 \times 9} = \sqrt{36}$.
=> 6

# Direct and inverse proportion
- If two quantities increase together at the same rate (cost and number of articles), they are in **direct proportion**: their ratio stays the same.
- If one quantity increases when the other decreases so that the **product** stays the same (workers and days), they are in **inverse proportion**.
?eg The cost of 5 pens is Rs 40. Find the cost of 12 pens.
= Cost of one pen $= 8$, so 12 pens cost $12 \times 8$.
=> Rs 96
?eg 6 workers can finish a job in 12 days. How many days will 9 workers take?
= The work is $6 \times 12 = 72$ worker-days. Days $= \dfrac{72}{9}$.
=> 8 days
!tip Before you calculate, decide in words: "more workers, fewer days" means inverse. "More pens, more cost" means direct.

@@formulas
Sharing in $a : b : c$ | one part $= \dfrac{\text{total}}{a + b + c}$
Proportion | $a : b = c : d \iff ad = bc$
Fourth proportional to $a, b, c$ | $\dfrac{bc}{a}$
Mean proportional between $a$ and $b$ | $\sqrt{ab}$
Direct proportion | $\dfrac{x_1}{y_1} = \dfrac{x_2}{y_2}$
Inverse proportion | $x_1 y_1 = x_2 y_2$
