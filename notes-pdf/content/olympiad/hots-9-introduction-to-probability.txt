# Solved HOTS questions
- $P(E) = \dfrac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{all possible outcomes}}$, always between 0 and 1. List the sample space in an organised way (a table or a tree diagram) so that no outcome is missed. Experimental probability uses the results of real trials: $\dfrac{\text{number of times the event happened}}{\text{number of trials}}$.
?eg A coin is tossed three times. Write the sample space and find the probability of getting exactly two heads.
= The outcomes are HHH, HHT, HTH, HTT, THH, THT, TTH and TTT: 8 in all.
= Exactly two heads: HHT, HTH and THH, which is 3 outcomes.
=> $\dfrac{3}{8}$
?eg Two dice are thrown together. Find the probability that the sum of the numbers is less than 5.
= The sums less than 5 are: 2 from (1,1); 3 from (1,2) and (2,1); 4 from (1,3), (3,1) and (2,2). That is 6 outcomes.
= There are 36 outcomes in all.
=> $\dfrac{6}{36} = \dfrac{1}{6}$
?eg A bag has 4 red, 5 green and 6 blue balls. A ball is drawn at random. Find the probability that it is not green.
= Total balls $= 15$. Balls that are not green $= 4 + 6 = 10$.
= (Or $1 - P(\text{green}) = 1 - \dfrac{5}{15}$.)
=> $\dfrac{2}{3}$
?eg A die is thrown 50 times and the number 6 comes up 9 times. Find the experimental probability of getting a 6 and compare it with the theoretical probability.
= Experimental probability $= \dfrac{9}{50} = 0.18$.
= Theoretical probability $= \dfrac{1}{6} \approx 0.167$.
= The two are close. With more throws the experimental value usually comes closer to the theoretical one.
=> $0.18$, which is close to $\dfrac{1}{6}$
?eg A letter is chosen at random from the word MATHEMATICS. Find the probability that it is a vowel, and that it is the letter M.
= The word has 11 letters: M, A, T, H, E, M, A, T, I, C, S.
= The vowels are A, E, A, I, which is 4 letters. The letter M appears 2 times.
=> $\dfrac{4}{11}$ for a vowel and $\dfrac{2}{11}$ for M

Q: A coin is tossed three times. Find the probability of getting at least two heads.
A: $\dfrac{4}{8} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: Two dice are thrown together. Find the probability that the sum is 8.
A: $\dfrac{5}{36}$.

Q: A bag has 3 red, 5 green and 2 white balls. Find the probability of drawing a red or a white ball.
A: $\dfrac{5}{10} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: A letter is chosen at random from the word PROBABILITY. Find the probability that it is the letter B, and the probability that it is a vowel.
A: $\dfrac{2}{11}$ and $\dfrac{4}{11}$.

Q: What is the probability of an impossible event? Of a certain event?
A: 0 and 1.

Q: A card is drawn from ten cards numbered 1 to 10. Find the probability that it is even or greater than 7.
A: $\dfrac{6}{10} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ (the cards are 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 9).

Q: Out of 40 seeds, 34 germinate. Find the experimental probability that a seed germinates.
A: $\dfrac{17}{20}$.

Q: A die is thrown once. Find the probability of getting a number that is a factor of 6.
A: $\dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
