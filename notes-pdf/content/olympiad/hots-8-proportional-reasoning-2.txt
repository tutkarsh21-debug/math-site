# Solved HOTS questions
- A map scale of $1 : 50\,000$ means 1 cm on the map is 50 000 cm = 0.5 km on the ground. Two quantities in **inverse proportion** have a fixed product (workers $\times$ days). When people work together, add their one-day work.
?eg On a map with the scale $1 : 50\,000$, two towns are 4 cm apart. Find the real distance.
= Real distance $= 4 \times 50\,000 = 200\,000$ cm.
= $200\,000$ cm $= 2000$ m $= 2$ km.
=> 2 km
?eg Divide Rs 1200 in the ratio $2 : 3 : 5$.
= Total parts $= 10$, so one part is Rs 120.
= The shares are $2 \times 120$, $3 \times 120$ and $5 \times 120$.
=> Rs 240, Rs 360 and Rs 600
?eg In a pie chart of 500 students, the sector for cricket has a central angle of $72^\circ$. How many students chose cricket?
= The fraction is $\dfrac{72}{360} = \dfrac{1}{5}$.
= $\dfrac{1}{5} \times 500$.
=> 100 students
?eg 8 workers can finish a job in 15 days. How many days will 12 workers take?
= The total work is $8 \times 15 = 120$ worker-days.
= Days $= \dfrac{120}{12}$.
=> 10 days
?eg A can finish a job in 12 days and B in 6 days. How long will they take working together?
= In one day A does $\dfrac{1}{12}$ and B does $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the job. Together $\dfrac{1}{12} + \dfrac{2}{12} = \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
= So the job takes 4 days.
=> 4 days

Q: On a map with the scale $1 : 200\,000$, two places are 3 cm apart. Find the real distance.
A: 6 km.

Q: Divide Rs 1500 in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$.
A: Rs 250, Rs 500 and Rs 750.

Q: In a pie chart of 800 people, a sector has a central angle of $90^\circ$. How many people does it represent?
A: 200.

Q: 6 men can finish a job in 20 days. How many days will 10 men take?
A: 12 days.

Q: A can finish a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. How long will they take together?
A: 6 days.

Q: One pipe fills a tank in 6 hours and another in 3 hours. How long will both take together?
A: 2 hours.

Q: Two places are 7.5 km apart. How far apart are they on a map with the scale $1 : 150\,000$?
A: 5 cm.

Q: 15 men can dig a trench in 12 days. How many men are needed to dig it in 9 days?
A: 20 men.
