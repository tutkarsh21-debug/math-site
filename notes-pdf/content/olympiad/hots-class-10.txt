# How to use these questions
- A HOTS question joins two ideas or hides its first step. Read it twice and ask: "What is really being asked?"
- Try each question for five minutes before reading the solution. Then compare your method, not only your answer.
- If you get stuck, write what is **given**, what is **needed**, and one fact that connects them.

# Real numbers
?eg The HCF of two numbers is 9 and their LCM is 360. If one number is 72, find the other number.
= For two numbers, $\text{HCF} \times \text{LCM} = \text{product of the numbers}$.
= $9 \times 360 = 72 \times n$, so $n = \dfrac{3240}{72}$.
=> 45
?eg Find the largest number that divides 245 and 1029 leaving remainder 5 in each case.
= The number divides $245 - 5 = 240$ and $1029 - 5 = 1024$ exactly, so it is their HCF.
= $240 = 2^4 \times 3 \times 5$ and $1024 = 2^{10}$, so $\text{HCF} = 2^4 = 16$.
= It is larger than the remainder 5, as it must be.
=> 16

# Quadratic equations
?eg Find the value of $k$ for which $x^2 + 2(k + 1)x + k^2 = 0$ has equal roots.
= Equal roots need $D = 0$: $4(k + 1)^2 - 4k^2 = 0$.
= $4(2k + 1) = 0$, so $k = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
=> $k = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
?eg The sum of the squares of two consecutive positive odd numbers is 290. Find the numbers.
= Let the numbers be $n$ and $n + 2$. Then $n^2 + (n + 2)^2 = 290$.
= $2n^2 + 4n + 4 = 290$, so $n^2 + 2n - 143 = 0$ and $(n + 13)(n - 11) = 0$.
= $n = 11$ since $n$ is positive. The numbers are 11 and 13.
= Check: $121 + 169 = 290$.
=> 11 and 13

# Arithmetic progressions
?eg The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $3n^2 + 5n$. Find its 20th term and common difference.
= The $n$th term is $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$.
= $S_n - S_{n-1} = 3n^2 + 5n - [3(n - 1)^2 + 5(n - 1)] = 3(2n - 1) + 5 = 6n + 2$.
= $a_{20} = 6 \times 20 + 2 = 122$, and the common difference is 6.
=> $a_{20} = 122$ and $d = 6$

# Similar triangles and coordinates
?eg In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC with D on AB and E on AC. AD = 3 cm, DB = 5 cm and the area of triangle ADE is 18 square cm. Find the area of triangle ABC.
= Triangle ADE is similar to triangle ABC, and $AB = 3 + 5 = 8$ cm.
= The ratio of areas is the square of the ratio of sides: $\dfrac{\text{area ADE}}{\text{area ABC}} = \left(\dfrac{3}{8}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{64}$.
= Area ABC $= 18 \times \dfrac{64}{9}$.
=> 128 square cm
?eg In what ratio does the x-axis divide the line segment joining $(2, -3)$ and $(5, 6)$? Find the point of division.
= Let the ratio be $k : 1$. The y-coordinate of the dividing point is $\dfrac{6k - 3}{k + 1}$, and it is 0 on the x-axis.
= $6k = 3$, so $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$, that is, the ratio is $1 : 2$.
= The x-coordinate is $\dfrac{5k + 2}{k + 1} = \dfrac{2.5 + 2}{1.5} = 3$.
=> $1 : 2$, at the point $(3, 0)$

# Trigonometry
?eg If $\tan\theta = \dfrac{3}{4}$, find the value of $\sin\theta \cos\theta$.
= Take a right triangle with opposite side 3, adjacent side 4 and hypotenuse 5.
= $\sin\theta = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\cos\theta = \dfrac{4}{5}$.
=> $\dfrac{12}{25}$
?eg If $\sin\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1$, find the value of $\cos^2\theta + \cos^4\theta$.
= From $\sin\theta = 1 - \sin^2\theta = \cos^2\theta$ we get $\cos^2\theta = \sin\theta$.
= Then $\cos^4\theta = \sin^2\theta$, so $\cos^2\theta + \cos^4\theta = \sin\theta + \sin^2\theta$.
=> 1
?eg From the top of a building 60 m high, the angles of depression of the top and the bottom of a tower are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
= Horizontal distance to the tower: $d = \dfrac{60}{\tan 60^\circ} = \dfrac{60}{\sqrt{3}} = 20\sqrt{3}$ m.
= Drop to the top of the tower: $d \tan 30^\circ = 20\sqrt{3} \times \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = 20$ m.
= Height of the tower $= 60 - 20$.
=> 40 m

# Circles and mensuration
?eg In triangle ABC, AB = 12 cm, BC = 8 cm and CA = 10 cm. The incircle touches the sides. Find the length of the tangent from A to the circle.
= The semi-perimeter $s = \dfrac{12 + 8 + 10}{2} = 15$ cm.
= The tangent from A has length $s - BC = 15 - 8$.
=> 7 cm
?eg A circle is drawn inside a square of side 14 cm so that it touches all four sides. Find the area of the part of the square outside the circle (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$).
= The radius is 7 cm. Area of the circle $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 7 = 154$.
= Area of the square $= 196$.
=> 42 square cm
?eg A metal sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into small cones of radius 2 cm and height 3 cm. How many cones are made?
= Volume of the sphere $= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi \times 6^3 = 288\pi$.
= Volume of one cone $= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi \times 2^2 \times 3 = 4\pi$.
= Number of cones $= \dfrac{288\pi}{4\pi}$.
=> 72

# Probability
?eg Two dice are thrown together. Find the probability that the sum of the numbers is a prime number.
= Prime sums are 2, 3, 5, 7 and 11. The numbers of ways: 1, 2, 4, 6 and 2, which add to 15.
= There are 36 equally likely outcomes.
=> $\dfrac{15}{36} = \dfrac{5}{12}$
!tip In a HOTS question, a quantity you did not know you needed (an area, a tangent length, a total) is often the first step. Ask what the unknown depends on.
