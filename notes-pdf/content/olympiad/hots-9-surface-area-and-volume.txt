# Solved HOTS questions
- Formulas (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$): cuboid volume $lbh$, total surface area $2(lb + bh + hl)$; cylinder $\pi r^2 h$ and curved area $2\pi rh$; cone $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$ and curved area $\pi rl$ with $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$; sphere $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$ and area $4\pi r^2$; hemisphere curved area $2\pi r^2$ and total area $3\pi r^2$. When a solid is melted and recast, the volume stays the same.
?eg A cone has base radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find its slant height, curved surface area and volume.
= $l = \sqrt{7^2 + 24^2} = \sqrt{625} = 25$ cm.
= Curved surface area $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25 = 550$ square cm.
= Volume $= \dfrac{1}{3} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 24 = 1232$ cubic cm.
=> 25 cm, 550 square cm and 1232 cubic cm
?eg A cuboid of dimensions 44 cm by 21 cm by 12 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 14 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.
= Volume of the cuboid $= 44 \times 21 \times 12 = 11\,088$ cubic cm.
= Base area of the cylinder $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 14 \times 14 = 616$.
= Height $= \dfrac{11\,088}{616}$.
=> 18 cm
?eg The total surface area of a solid hemisphere is 462 square cm. Find its radius and volume.
= Total area $= 3\pi r^2 = 462$, so $r^2 = \dfrac{462 \times 7}{3 \times 22} = 49$ and $r = 7$ cm.
= Volume $= \dfrac{2}{3}\pi r^3 = \dfrac{2}{3} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 343$.
=> Radius 7 cm and volume $718\dfrac{2}{3}$ cubic cm
?eg The radius of a sphere is doubled. By what factor do its surface area and volume change?
= Surface area $\propto r^2$, so it becomes $2^2 = 4$ times as large.
= Volume $\propto r^3$, so it becomes $2^3 = 8$ times as large.
=> 4 times and 8 times
?eg How many metres of cloth 2 m wide are needed to make a conical tent with base radius 7 m and height 24 m? Take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$.
= Slant height $= 25$ m. Curved area $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25 = 550$ square m.
= Length of cloth $= \dfrac{550}{2}$.
=> 275 m

Q: A cone has base radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. Find its slant height, curved surface area and volume in terms of $\pi$.
A: 10 cm, $60\pi$ square cm and $96\pi$ cubic cm.

Q: A cuboid 11 cm by 7 cm by 8 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 7 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.
A: 4 cm.

Q: The total surface area of a solid hemisphere is 154 square cm. Find its radius (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$).
A: $\sqrt{\dfrac{154 \times 7}{66}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{49}{3}} \approx 4.04$ cm.

Q: The radius of a sphere is tripled. By what factor do its surface area and volume change?
A: 9 times and 27 times.

Q: How many metres of cloth 5 m wide are needed for a conical tent of base radius 7 m and slant height 12.5 m? (Take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$.)
A: 55 m.

Q: A cube of side 6 cm is melted and cast into 27 equal small cubes. Find the side of each small cube.
A: 2 cm.

Q: Find the volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm, and its total surface area.
A: 1540 cubic cm and 748 square cm.

Q: A solid sphere of radius 3 cm is melted and made into a cone of base radius 3 cm. Find the height of the cone.
A: 12 cm.
