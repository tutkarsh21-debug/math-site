@summary The angle between the hands of a clock, when the hands meet, and how to find the day of the week from odd days.

# The hands of a clock
- A clock face is a full circle of $360^\circ$ with 12 hours, so each hour mark is $30^\circ$ apart and each minute mark is $6^\circ$ apart.
- The **minute hand** turns $6^\circ$ every minute. The **hour hand** turns $0.5^\circ$ every minute ($30^\circ$ in one hour).
- So the minute hand turns $5.5^\circ$ more than the hour hand every minute.
- The angle between the hands at $h$ hours and $m$ minutes is
$$\theta = \lvert 30h - 5.5m \rvert$$
- If the answer is more than $180^\circ$, subtract it from $360^\circ$ to get the smaller angle.
?eg Find the angle between the hands at 4:30.
= $\theta = \lvert 30 \times 4 - 5.5 \times 30 \rvert = \lvert 120 - 165 \rvert = 45$.
=> $45^\circ$
?eg Find the angle between the hands at 7:20.
= $\theta = \lvert 30 \times 7 - 5.5 \times 20 \rvert = \lvert 210 - 110 \rvert = 100$.
=> $100^\circ$

# When the hands meet
- In every 12 hours the two hands **coincide 11 times** (not 12), and are **opposite** 11 times.
- In 24 hours the hands coincide 22 times and are at right angles 44 times.
- The hands coincide between $h$ and $h + 1$ at $\dfrac{60h}{11}$ minutes past $h$.
?eg At what time between 4 and 5 do the hands coincide?
= $\dfrac{60 \times 4}{11} = 21\dfrac{9}{11}$ minutes past 4.
=> 4:21 and $\dfrac{9}{11}$ minutes
?eg How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in one full day (24 hours)?
= $11$ times in every 12 hours.
=> 22 times

# Odd days and the calendar
- A week has 7 days, so any number of days leaves a remainder when divided by 7. That remainder is the number of **odd days**.
- An **ordinary year** has 365 days = 52 weeks + **1 odd day**. A **leap year** has 366 days = 52 weeks + **2 odd days**.
- A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4. A century year (1900, 2000, 2100) is a leap year only if it is divisible by **400**. So 2000 is a leap year, but 1900 and 2100 are not.
| Month | Days | Month | Days |
| January | 31 | July | 31 |
| February | 28 or 29 | August | 31 |
| March | 31 | September | 30 |
| April | 30 | October | 31 |
| May | 31 | November | 30 |
| June | 30 | December | 31 |
- To find a day, count the days between the two dates, divide by 7, and move forward by the remainder.
?eg 5 March of a year is a Wednesday. What day of the week is 26 March of the same year?
= From 5 March to 26 March is 21 days. $21 = 7 \times 3$, so the remainder is 0.
=> Wednesday
?eg Today is Monday. What day will it be after 100 days?
= $100 = 7 \times 14 + 2$, so move 2 days forward.
=> Wednesday
?eg A month of 31 days begins on a Tuesday. How many Tuesdays does it have?
= $31 = 4 \times 7 + 3$. The first three days of the week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) occur 5 times.
=> 5
!tip In a month the extra days (28, 29, 30 or 31 days minus 28) are the first few days of the week of that month. Those weekdays occur five times.

@@dpp
Q: Find the angle between the hands at 6:00.
A: $180^\circ$.

Q: Find the angle between the hands at 2:30.
A: $105^\circ$ ($\lvert 60 - 165 \rvert$).

Q: Find the angle between the hands at 8:20.
A: $130^\circ$ ($\lvert 240 - 110 \rvert$).

Q: How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in 24 hours?
A: 22.

Q: At what time between 5 and 6 do the hands coincide?
A: $27\dfrac{3}{11}$ minutes past 5.

Q: Through what angle does the minute hand turn in 15 minutes? Through what angle does the hour hand turn in 1 hour?
A: $90^\circ$ and $30^\circ$.

Q: Is 1900 a leap year? Is 2000 a leap year?
A: 1900 is not a leap year. 2000 is a leap year.

Q: Today is Monday. What day will it be after 100 days?
A: Wednesday.

Q: 1 January 2022 was a Saturday. What day was 1 January 2023?
A: Sunday (2022 was an ordinary year, so one day forward).

Q: A month of 31 days begins on a Tuesday. How many Tuesdays does it have?
A: 5.
