T: Forms of the equation
Q: [ICSE 2023, 3 marks] Triangle $ABC$ has vertices $A(1, -1)$, $B(0, 4)$ and $C(-6, 4)$, and $D$ is the mid-point of $BC$. Find (a) the coordinates of $D$ (b) the equation of the median $AD$.
S: (a) $D = \left(\dfrac{0 - 6}{2}, \dfrac{4 + 4}{2}\right) = (-3, 4)$
S: (b) Slope of $AD = \dfrac{4 - (-1)}{-3 - 1} = -\dfrac{5}{4}$. Through $A$: $y + 1 = -\dfrac{5}{4}(x - 1)$, so $4y + 4 = -5x + 5$.
S: **Answer: (a) $D(-3, 4)$ (b) $5x + 4y - 1 = 0$**
