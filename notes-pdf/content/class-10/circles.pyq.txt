T: Theorem 1: tangent and radius
Q: [CBSE 2024, 1 mark] Assertion (A): The tangents drawn at the end points of a diameter of a circle are parallel. Reason (R): Diameter of a circle is the longest chord. Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A. (c) A is true but R is false. (d) A is false but R is true.
S: A is true: each tangent is perpendicular to the diameter at its end point, and two lines perpendicular to the same line are parallel.
S: R is true, but it is not the reason why the tangents are parallel.
S: **Answer: (b)**

T: Theorem 2: tangents from an external point
Q: [CBSE 2024, 3 marks] $AB$ is a diameter of a circle with centre $O$. $AQ$, $BP$ and $PQ$ are tangents to the circle, where $Q$ lies on the tangent at $A$ and $P$ lies on the tangent at $B$. Prove that $\angle POQ = 90°$.
S: Let $PQ$ touch the circle at $C$. Join $OC$.
S: In $\triangle OAQ$ and $\triangle OCQ$: $OA = OC$ (radii), $OQ$ is common and $QA = QC$ (tangents from $Q$). So $\triangle OAQ \cong \triangle OCQ$ (SSS), and $\angle AOQ = \angle COQ$.
S: Similarly $\triangle OBP \cong \triangle OCP$, so $\angle BOP = \angle COP$.
S: $AOB$ is a straight line: $\angle AOQ + \angle COQ + \angle COP + \angle BOP = 180°$, so $2\angle COQ + 2\angle COP = 180°$.
S: **Hence $\angle POQ = \angle COQ + \angle COP = 90°$.**
Q: [CBSE 2024, 3 marks] A circle with centre $O$ and radius 8 cm is inscribed in a quadrilateral $ABCD$ in which $P$, $Q$, $R$, $S$ are the points of contact on $AD$, $DC$, $CB$ and $BA$ respectively. If $AD$ is perpendicular to $DC$, $BC = 30$ cm and $BS = 24$ cm, then find the length $DC$.
S: Tangents from $B$ are equal: $BR = BS = 24$ cm, so $CR = BC - BR = 30 - 24 = 6$ cm.
S: Tangents from $C$ are equal: $CQ = CR = 6$ cm.
S: In quadrilateral $OPDQ$: $\angle D = 90°$ (given) and $\angle OPD = \angle OQD = 90°$ (radius is perpendicular to tangent), and $OP = OQ = 8$ cm. So $OPDQ$ is a square and $DQ = 8$ cm.
S: $DC = DQ + QC = 8 + 6$
S: **Answer: $DC = 14$ cm**
Q: [CBSE 2024, 4 marks, case study] A backyard is in the shape of a triangle $ABC$ with right angle at $B$. $AB = 7$ m and $BC = 15$ m. A circular pit was dug inside it such that it touches the walls $AC$, $BC$ and $AB$ at $P$, $Q$ and $R$ respectively such that $AP = x$ m. (i) Find the length of $AR$ in terms of $x$. (ii) Write the type of quadrilateral $BQOR$. (iii) Find the length $PC$ in terms of $x$ and hence find the value of $x$, OR find $x$ and hence find the radius $r$ of the circle.

S: (i) Tangents from $A$ are equal: $AR = AP = x$ m.
S: (ii) $\angle B = \angle OQB = \angle ORB = 90°$ and $OQ = OR$ (radii), so $BQOR$ is a square.
S: (iii) $BR = AB - AR = 7 - x$, so $BQ = 7 - x$ and $QC = 15 - (7 - x) = 8 + x$. Tangents from $C$ are equal, so $PC = QC = (8 + x)$ m.
S: $AC = \sqrt{7^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{274}$ m, and $AC = AP + PC = 2x + 8$. So $x = \dfrac{\sqrt{274} - 8}{2} \approx \dfrac{16.55 - 8}{2} \approx 4.28$.
S: OR: the radius equals the side of the square, $r = BR = 7 - x = \dfrac{22 - \sqrt{274}}{2} \approx 2.72$ m.
S: **Answer: (i) $x$ m (ii) a square (iii) $PC = (8 + x)$ m, $x \approx 4.28$; or $r \approx 2.72$ m**
