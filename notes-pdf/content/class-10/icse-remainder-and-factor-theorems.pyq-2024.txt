T: The remainder theorem
Q: [ICSE 2024, 1 mark] What must be subtracted from $x^3 + x^2 - 2x + 1$ so that the result is exactly divisible by $(x - 3)$? (a) $-31$  (b) $-30$  (c) 30  (d) 31
S: The number to subtract is the remainder on division by $(x - 3)$, which is $f(3)$.
S: $f(3) = 27 + 9 - 6 + 1 = 31$
S: **Answer: (d)**

T: Factorising a cubic polynomial
Q: [ICSE 2024, 3 marks] $(x - 1)$ is a factor of $3x^3 + 8x^2 - 15x + k$. Find $k$, and then factorise the polynomial completely.
S: $f(1) = 0$: $3 + 8 - 15 + k = 0$, so $k = 4$.
S: Divide $3x^3 + 8x^2 - 15x + 4$ by $(x - 1)$: the quotient is $3x^2 + 11x - 4$.
S: $3x^2 + 11x - 4 = 3x^2 + 12x - x - 4 = (3x - 1)(x + 4)$
S: **Answer: $k = 4$; $(x - 1)(3x - 1)(x + 4)$**
