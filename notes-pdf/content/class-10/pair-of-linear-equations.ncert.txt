Q: [Q2] By comparing $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}$, $\dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$ and $\dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$, say whether the lines intersect at a point, are parallel or coincide: (i) $5x - 4y + 8 = 0$, $7x + 6y - 9 = 0$ (ii) $9x + 3y + 12 = 0$, $18x + 6y + 24 = 0$ (iii) $6x - 3y + 10 = 0$, $2x - y + 9 = 0$.
S: (i) $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{5}{7}$ and $\dfrac{b_1}{b_2} = \dfrac{-4}{6} = -\dfrac{2}{3}$. These are not equal, so the lines intersect at a point.
S: (ii) $\dfrac{9}{18} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{12}{24} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. All three ratios are equal, so the lines coincide.
S: (iii) $\dfrac{6}{2} = 3$, $\dfrac{-3}{-1} = 3$ and $\dfrac{10}{9}$. The first two ratios are equal but the third is different, so the lines are parallel.
S: **Answer: (i) intersecting (ii) coincident (iii) parallel**
Q: [Q3] By comparing the ratios, say whether each pair is consistent or inconsistent: (i) $3x + 2y = 5$, $2x - 3y = 7$ (ii) $2x - 3y = 8$, $4x - 6y = 9$ (iii) $\dfrac{3}{2}x + \dfrac{5}{3}y = 7$, $9x - 10y = 14$ (iv) $5x - 3y = 11$, $-10x + 6y = -22$ (v) $\dfrac{4}{3}x + 2y = 8$, $2x + 3y = 12$.
S: A pair is consistent if it has at least one solution (intersecting or coincident lines) and inconsistent if it has none (parallel lines).
S: (i) $\dfrac{3}{2} \neq \dfrac{2}{-3}$, so the lines intersect. Consistent.
S: (ii) $\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{-3}{-6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ but $\dfrac{8}{9} \neq \dfrac{1}{2}$, so the lines are parallel. Inconsistent.
S: (iii) $\dfrac{3/2}{9} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ and $\dfrac{5/3}{-10} = -\dfrac{1}{6}$. These are not equal, so the lines intersect. Consistent.
S: (iv) $\dfrac{5}{-10} = \dfrac{-3}{6} = \dfrac{11}{-22} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the lines coincide. Consistent, with infinitely many solutions.
S: (v) $\dfrac{4/3}{2} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{8}{12} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so the lines coincide. Consistent, with infinitely many solutions.
S: **Answer: (i) consistent (ii) inconsistent (iii) consistent (iv) consistent (v) consistent**

Q: [Q5] Half the perimeter of a rectangular garden is 36 m, and its length is 4 m more than its width. Find its dimensions.
S: Let the width be $x$ m and the length be $y$ m. Then $y = x + 4$ and $x + y = 36$.
S: Substituting, $x + x + 4 = 36$, so $x = 16$ and $y = 20$.
S: **Answer: length 20 m and width 16 m**

Q: [Q1] Solve by the substitution method: (i) $x + y = 14$, $x - y = 4$ (ii) $s - t = 3$, $\dfrac{s}{3} + \dfrac{t}{2} = 6$ (iii) $3x - y = 3$, $9x - 3y = 9$ (iv) $0.2x + 0.3y = 1.3$, $0.4x + 0.5y = 2.3$ (v) $\sqrt{2}\,x + \sqrt{3}\,y = 0$, $\sqrt{3}\,x - \sqrt{8}\,y = 0$ (vi) $\dfrac{3x}{2} - \dfrac{5y}{3} = -2$, $\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{y}{2} = \dfrac{13}{6}$.
S: (i) From the second equation, $x = y + 4$. Then $y + 4 + y = 14$, so $y = 5$ and $x = 9$.
S: (ii) $s = t + 3$. Then $\dfrac{t + 3}{3} + \dfrac{t}{2} = 6$ gives $2t + 6 + 3t = 36$, so $t = 6$ and $s = 9$.
S: (iii) $y = 3x - 3$. Then $9x - 3(3x - 3) = 9$ gives $9 = 9$, which is always true. The two equations are the same line, so there are infinitely many solutions.
S: (iv) Multiply both by 10: $2x + 3y = 13$ and $4x + 5y = 23$. From the first, $x = \dfrac{13 - 3y}{2}$. Then $2(13 - 3y) + 5y = 23$ gives $y = 3$ and $x = 2$.
S: (v) From the first, $x = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{2}}\,y$. Then $-\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{2}}\,y - 2\sqrt{2}\,y = 0$, so $y = 0$ and $x = 0$.
S: (vi) Clearing fractions: $9x - 10y = -12$ and $2x + 3y = 13$. From the second, $x = \dfrac{13 - 3y}{2}$. Then $9(13 - 3y) - 20y = -24$ gives $47y = 141$, so $y = 3$ and $x = 2$.
S: **Answer: (i) $x = 9$, $y = 5$ (ii) $s = 9$, $t = 6$ (iii) infinitely many solutions (iv) $x = 2$, $y = 3$ (v) $x = 0$, $y = 0$ (vi) $x = 2$, $y = 3$**
Q: [Q2] Solve $2x + 3y = 11$ and $2x - 4y = -24$, and hence find $m$ for which $y = mx + 3$.
S: From the first equation, $2x = 11 - 3y$. Substituting in the second: $11 - 3y - 4y = -24$, so $7y = 35$ and $y = 5$.
S: Then $2x = 11 - 15 = -4$, so $x = -2$.
S: In $y = mx + 3$: $5 = -2m + 3$, so $m = -1$.
S: **Answer: $x = -2$, $y = 5$ and $m = -1$**
Q: [Q3 (i)] Two numbers differ by 26, and one of them is three times the other. What are the numbers?
S: Let the numbers be $x$ and $y$ with $x > y$. Then $x - y = 26$ and $x = 3y$.
S: Substituting, $3y - y = 26$, so $y = 13$ and $x = 39$.
S: **Answer: 39 and 13**
Q: [Q3 (ii)] Two angles are supplementary, and the bigger one is $18°$ more than the smaller one. Find both angles.
S: Let the angles be $x°$ and $y°$ with $x > y$. Then $x + y = 180$ and $x = y + 18$.
S: Substituting, $2y + 18 = 180$, so $y = 81$ and $x = 99$.
S: **Answer: $99°$ and $81°$**
Q: [Q3 (iii)] 7 bats and 6 balls cost ₹3800, and 3 bats and 5 balls cost ₹1750. Find the cost of each bat and each ball.
S: Let a bat cost ₹$x$ and a ball cost ₹$y$. Then $7x + 6y = 3800$ and $3x + 5y = 1750$.
S: From the second, $x = \dfrac{1750 - 5y}{3}$. Then $7(1750 - 5y) + 18y = 11400$ gives $17y = 850$, so $y = 50$ and $x = \dfrac{1750 - 250}{3} = 500$.
S: **Answer: a bat costs ₹500 and a ball costs ₹50**
Q: [Q3 (iv)] A taxi charges a fixed amount plus a charge per km. A 10 km journey costs ₹105 and a 15 km journey costs ₹155. Find the fixed charge and the charge per km. What does a 25 km journey cost?
S: Let the fixed charge be ₹$x$ and the charge per km be ₹$y$. Then $x + 10y = 105$ and $x + 15y = 155$.
S: From the first, $x = 105 - 10y$. Then $105 - 10y + 15y = 155$, so $y = 10$ and $x = 5$.
S: For 25 km: $5 + 25 \times 10 =$ ₹255.
S: **Answer: fixed charge ₹5, ₹10 per km, and ₹255 for 25 km**
Q: [Q3 (v)] A fraction becomes $\dfrac{9}{11}$ if 2 is added to both the numerator and the denominator, and $\dfrac{5}{6}$ if 3 is added to both. Find the fraction.
S: Let the fraction be $\dfrac{x}{y}$. Then $\dfrac{x + 2}{y + 2} = \dfrac{9}{11}$ gives $11x - 9y = -4$, and $\dfrac{x + 3}{y + 3} = \dfrac{5}{6}$ gives $6x - 5y = -3$.
S: From the second, $x = \dfrac{5y - 3}{6}$. Then $11(5y - 3) - 54y = -24$ gives $y = 9$ and $x = 7$.
S: **Answer: $\dfrac{7}{9}$**
Q: [Q3 (vi)] Five years from now a father will be three times as old as his son. Five years ago he was seven times as old as his son. Find their present ages.
S: Let the father be $x$ years and the son $y$ years old. Then $x + 5 = 3(y + 5)$ gives $x - 3y = 10$, and $x - 5 = 7(y - 5)$ gives $x - 7y = -30$.
S: From the first, $x = 3y + 10$. Then $3y + 10 - 7y = -30$, so $y = 10$ and $x = 40$.
S: **Answer: the father is 40 years old and the son is 10 years old**

Q: [Q1] Solve by the elimination method and by the substitution method: (i) $x + y = 5$, $2x - 3y = 4$ (ii) $3x + 4y = 10$, $2x - 2y = 2$ (iii) $3x - 5y - 4 = 0$, $9x = 2y + 7$ (iv) $\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{2y}{3} = -1$, $x - \dfrac{y}{3} = 3$.
S: (i) Elimination: multiply the first by 3 to get $3x + 3y = 15$ and add the second: $5x = 19$, so $x = \dfrac{19}{5}$ and $y = 5 - \dfrac{19}{5} = \dfrac{6}{5}$. Substitution: $x = 5 - y$ gives $10 - 2y - 3y = 4$, so $y = \dfrac{6}{5}$, the same result.
S: (ii) Elimination: multiply the second by 2 to get $4x - 4y = 4$ and add the first: $7x = 14$, so $x = 2$ and $y = 1$. Substitution: $x = y + 1$ gives $3y + 3 + 4y = 10$, so $y = 1$.
S: (iii) The equations are $3x - 5y = 4$ and $9x - 2y = 7$. Elimination: multiply the first by 3 to get $9x - 15y = 12$ and subtract the second: $-13y = 5$, so $y = -\dfrac{5}{13}$ and $x = \dfrac{4 + 5y}{3} = \dfrac{9}{13}$. Substitution: $y = \dfrac{9x - 7}{2}$ gives $6x - 45x + 35 = 8$, so $x = \dfrac{9}{13}$.
S: (iv) Clearing fractions: $3x + 4y = -6$ and $3x - y = 9$. Elimination: subtracting, $5y = -15$, so $y = -3$ and $x = 2$. Substitution: $y = 3x - 9$ gives $3x + 12x - 36 = -6$, so $x = 2$.
S: **Answer: (i) $x = \dfrac{19}{5}$, $y = \dfrac{6}{5}$ (ii) $x = 2$, $y = 1$ (iii) $x = \dfrac{9}{13}$, $y = -\dfrac{5}{13}$ (iv) $x = 2$, $y = -3$**
Q: [Q2 (i)] A fraction becomes 1 if 1 is added to the numerator and 1 is subtracted from the denominator. It becomes $\dfrac{1}{2}$ if 1 is added to the denominator only. Find the fraction.
S: Let the fraction be $\dfrac{x}{y}$. Then $x + 1 = y - 1$ gives $x - y = -2$, and $2x = y + 1$ gives $2x - y = 1$.
S: Subtracting the first from the second, $x = 3$. Then $y = 5$.
S: **Answer: $\dfrac{3}{5}$**
Q: [Q2 (ii)] Five years ago one person was three times as old as another. Ten years from now the first will be twice as old as the second. Find their present ages.
S: Let their ages be $x$ and $y$ years. Then $x - 5 = 3(y - 5)$ gives $x - 3y = -10$, and $x + 10 = 2(y + 10)$ gives $x - 2y = 10$.
S: Subtracting the first from the second, $y = 20$. Then $x = 50$.
S: **Answer: 50 years and 20 years**
Q: [Q2 (iii)] The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Nine times the number is twice the number obtained by reversing its digits. Find the number.
S: Let the tens digit be $x$ and the units digit be $y$. Then $x + y = 9$, and $9(10x + y) = 2(10y + x)$ gives $88x - 11y = 0$, that is $8x - y = 0$.
S: Adding the two equations, $9x = 9$, so $x = 1$ and $y = 8$.
S: **Answer: 18**
Q: [Q2 (iv)] A person withdraws ₹2000 from a bank in ₹50 and ₹100 notes only, and gets 25 notes in all. How many notes of each kind are there?
S: Let there be $x$ notes of ₹50 and $y$ notes of ₹100. Then $x + y = 25$, and $50x + 100y = 2000$ gives $x + 2y = 40$.
S: Subtracting the first from the second, $y = 15$. Then $x = 10$.
S: **Answer: 10 notes of ₹50 and 15 notes of ₹100**
Q: [Q2 (v)] A library has a fixed charge for the first three days and an extra charge for each day after that. One reader paid ₹27 for seven days and another paid ₹21 for five days. Find the fixed charge and the charge for each extra day.
S: Let the fixed charge be ₹$x$ and the charge per extra day be ₹$y$. Seven days means 4 extra days and five days means 2 extra days: $x + 4y = 27$ and $x + 2y = 21$.
S: Subtracting, $2y = 6$, so $y = 3$ and $x = 15$.
S: **Answer: fixed charge ₹15 and ₹3 for each extra day**
