T: Multiplication of matrices
Q: [ICSE 2023, 3 marks] Given $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$, $B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$, $C = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 1 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$ and $I = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$, find $A(B + C) - 14I$.
S: $B + C = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 3 \\ 3 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
S: $A(B + C) = \begin{bmatrix} 5 + 9 & 3 + 27 \\ 10 + 12 & 6 + 36 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 14 & 30 \\ 22 & 42 \end{bmatrix}$
S: $14I = \begin{bmatrix} 14 & 0 \\ 0 & 14 \end{bmatrix}$
S: **Answer: $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 30 \\ 22 & 28 \end{bmatrix}$**

T: Solving matrix equations
Q: [ICSE 2023, 1 mark] If $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 4 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -8 \end{bmatrix}$, find $x$ and $y$. (a) $1, -2$  (b) $-2, 1$  (c) $1, 2$  (d) $-2, -1$
S: Multiply out: $\begin{bmatrix} 2x \\ 4y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -8 \end{bmatrix}$, so $x = 1$ and $y = -2$.
S: **Answer: (a)**
