T: Reflection of figures
Q: [ICSE 2023, 5 marks] Plot $A(0, 4)$, $B(1, 1)$ and $C(4, 0)$. (a) Reflect $A$ and $B$ in the $x$-axis to get $E$ and $D$. (b) Reflect $B$ in the origin to get $F$, and write its coordinates. (c) Reflect $B$ and $C$ in the $y$-axis to get $H$ and $G$. (d) Join $A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, A$ in order and name the closed figure.
S: (a) $E(0, -4)$ and $D(1, -1)$.
S: (b) Reflection in the origin changes both signs: $F(-1, -1)$.
S: (c) $H(-1, 1)$ and $G(-4, 0)$.
