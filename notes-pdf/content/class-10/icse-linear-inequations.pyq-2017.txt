T: Combining inequations
Q: [ICSE 2017, 3 marks] Solve $-8\dfrac{1}{2} < -\dfrac{1}{2} - 4x \leq 7\dfrac{1}{2}$, where $x$ is an integer, and show the solution set on the number line.
S: Add $\dfrac{1}{2}$ throughout: $-8 < -4x \leq 8$
S: Divide by $-4$ and reverse the signs: $2 > x \geq -2$
S: **Solution set: $\{-2, -1, 0, 1\}$**
