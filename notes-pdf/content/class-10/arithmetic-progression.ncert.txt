Q: [Q1] In which of these situations does the list of numbers form an AP, and why? (i) The taxi fare after each km, when the fare is ₹15 for the first km and ₹8 for each additional km. (ii) The amount of air left in a cylinder when a pump removes $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the remaining air each time. (iii) The cost of digging a well after each metre, when it costs ₹150 for the first metre and ₹50 more for each further metre. (iv) The amount in an account each year, when ₹10,000 is deposited at 8% compound interest per annum.
S: A list is an AP when each term is obtained by adding the same fixed number to the term before it.
S: (i) The fares are 15, 23, 31, 39, ... Each is 8 more than the one before, so it is an AP.
S: (ii) If the cylinder holds $V$ units of air, the amounts left are $V$, $\dfrac{3V}{4}$, $\dfrac{9V}{16}$, ... The differences $-\dfrac{V}{4}$ and $-\dfrac{3V}{16}$ are not equal, so it is not an AP.
S: (iii) The costs are 150, 200, 250, 300, ... Each is 50 more than the one before, so it is an AP.
S: (iv) The amounts are $10000$, $10000(1.08)$, $10000(1.08)^2$, ... that is 10000, 10800, 11664, ... The differences 800 and 864 are not equal, so it is not an AP.
S: **Answer: (i) and (iii) form APs. (ii) and (iv) do not.**

Q: [Q18] A spiral is made of 13 consecutive semicircles of radii 0.5 cm, 1.0 cm, 1.5 cm, 2.0 cm, ... Find its total length. (Take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$.)
S: A semicircle of radius $r$ has length $\pi r$, so the total length is $\pi$ times the sum of the 13 radii.
S: Sum of radii $= \dfrac{13}{2}[2 \times 0.5 + 12 \times 0.5] = \dfrac{13}{2} \times 7 = 45.5$ cm.
S: Total length $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 45.5 = 143$ cm.
S: **Answer: 143 cm**
Q: [Q19] 200 logs are stacked with 20 logs in the bottom row, 19 in the next, 18 in the next and so on. In how many rows are they placed, and how many logs are in the top row?
S: $\dfrac{n}{2}[40 - (n - 1)] = 200$ gives $n^2 - 41n + 400 = 0$, so $(n - 16)(n - 25) = 0$.
S: For $n = 25$ the top row would have $20 - 24 = -4$ logs, which is impossible. So $n = 16$, and the top row has $20 - 15 = 5$ logs.
S: **Answer: 16 rows, with 5 logs in the top row**
Q: [Q20] In a potato race, a bucket is 5 m from the first potato and the 10 potatoes are 3 m apart in a straight line. A runner starts at the bucket, fetches one potato at a time and drops it in the bucket. What total distance does the runner cover?
S: For each potato the runner goes there and back. The distances are $2 \times 5$, $2 \times 8$, $2 \times 11$, ..., that is $10, 16, 22, \dots$ for 10 potatoes.
S: $S_{10} = \dfrac{10}{2}[20 + 9 \times 6] = 5 \times 74 = 370$.
S: **Answer: 370 m**

Q: [Q1] Which term of the AP $121, 117, 113, \dots$ is its first negative term?
S: $a_n = 121 - 4(n - 1) < 0$ gives $n - 1 > 30.25$, so $n > 31.25$.
S: The smallest such whole number is 32. Check: $a_{31} = 1$ and $a_{32} = -3$.
S: **Answer: the 32nd term**
Q: [Q2] The sum of the third and seventh terms of an AP is 6 and their product is 8. Find the sum of the first sixteen terms.
S: $(a + 2d) + (a + 6d) = 6$ gives $a + 4d = 3$. So $a_3 = 3 - 2d$ and $a_7 = 3 + 2d$.
S: $(3 - 2d)(3 + 2d) = 8$ gives $9 - 4d^2 = 8$, so $d = \pm\dfrac{1}{2}$.
S: If $d = \dfrac{1}{2}$, then $a = 1$ and $S_{16} = 8\,[2 + 15 \times \tfrac{1}{2}] = 76$. If $d = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, then $a = 5$ and $S_{16} = 8\,[10 - 15 \times \tfrac{1}{2}] = 20$.
S: **Answer: 76 or 20**
Q: [Q3] A ladder has rungs 25 cm apart. The rungs decrease uniformly in length from 45 cm at the bottom to 25 cm at the top, and the top and bottom rungs are $2\dfrac{1}{2}$ m apart. What length of wood is needed for the rungs?
S: The gap of 250 cm is made of spaces of 25 cm, so there are $\dfrac{250}{25} = 10$ spaces and 11 rungs.
S: The lengths form an AP from 45 to 25 with 11 terms: $S = \dfrac{11}{2}(45 + 25) = 385$ cm.
S: **Answer: 385 cm (3.85 m)**
Q: [Q4] Houses in a row are numbered from 1 to 49. Show that there is a house number $x$ such that the sum of the numbers before it equals the sum of the numbers after it, and find $x$.
S: Sum of the numbers before $x$: $S_{x-1} = \dfrac{(x - 1)x}{2}$. Sum after $x$: $S_{49} - S_x = 1225 - \dfrac{x(x + 1)}{2}$.
S: Setting them equal: $\dfrac{x^2 - x}{2} + \dfrac{x^2 + x}{2} = 1225$, so $x^2 = 1225$ and $x = 35$.
S: Since 35 is a whole number between 1 and 49, such a house exists.
S: **Answer: $x = 35$**
Q: [Q5] A terrace has 15 steps, each 50 m long, with a rise of $\dfrac{1}{4}$ m and a tread of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ m. Find the total volume of concrete needed.
S: The $k$th step stands $\dfrac{k}{4}$ m high, so its volume is $\dfrac{k}{4} \times \dfrac{1}{2} \times 50 = \dfrac{25k}{4}$ m$^3$.
S: Total volume $= \dfrac{25}{4}(1 + 2 + \dots + 15) = \dfrac{25}{4} \times 120 = 750$ m$^3$.
S: **Answer: 750 m$^3$**
