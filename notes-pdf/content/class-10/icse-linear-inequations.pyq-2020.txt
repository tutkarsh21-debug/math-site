T: Combining inequations
Q: [ICSE 2020, 3 marks] Solve $\dfrac{3x}{5} + 2 < x + 4 \leq \dfrac{x}{2} + 5$, $x \in R$, and show the solution set on the number line.
S: Left part: $2 - 4 < x - \dfrac{3x}{5}$, so $-2 < \dfrac{2x}{5}$ and $x > -5$.
S: Right part: $x - \dfrac{x}{2} \leq 5 - 4$, so $\dfrac{x}{2} \leq 1$ and $x \leq 2$.
S: **Solution set: $\{x : -5 < x \leq 2,\ x \in R\}$**
