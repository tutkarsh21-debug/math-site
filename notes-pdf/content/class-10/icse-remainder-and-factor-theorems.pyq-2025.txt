T: The factor theorem
Q: [ICSE 2025, 1 mark] Which factor is common to $x^2 - 4$ and $x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4$? (a) $(x + 1)$  (b) $(x - 1)$  (c) $(x - 2)$  (d) $(x - 4)$
S: $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$
S: $x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 = x^2(x - 1) - 4(x - 1) = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 2)$
S: Of the options, $(x - 2)$ divides both.
S: **Answer: (c)**

T: Factorising a cubic polynomial
Q: [ICSE 2025, 3 marks] $(x - 2)$ is a factor of $2x^3 - 7x^2 + kx - 2$. Find $k$, and then factorise the polynomial completely.
S: By the factor theorem $f(2) = 0$: $16 - 28 + 2k - 2 = 0$, so $k = 7$.
S: Divide $2x^3 - 7x^2 + 7x - 2$ by $(x - 2)$: the quotient is $2x^2 - 3x + 1$.
S: $2x^2 - 3x + 1 = (2x - 1)(x - 1)$
S: **Answer: $k = 7$; $2x^3 - 7x^2 + 7x - 2 = (x - 2)(2x - 1)(x - 1)$**
