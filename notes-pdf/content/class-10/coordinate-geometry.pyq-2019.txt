T: The distance formula
Q: [CBSE 2019, 3 marks] Find the point on the $y$-axis that is equidistant from $(5, -2)$ and $(-3, 2)$.
S: Let the point be $P(0, y)$. Equate the squares of the two distances.
S: $(0 - 5)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = (0 + 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2$
S: $25 + y^2 + 4y + 4 = 9 + y^2 - 4y + 4$, so $8y = -16$ and $y = -2$.
S: **Answer: $(0, -2)$**

T: The section formula
Q: [CBSE 2019, 1 mark] $AB$ is a diameter of a circle with centre $(2, -3)$, and $B$ is the point $(1, 4)$. Find the coordinates of $A$.
S: The centre is the mid-point of the diameter. Let $A = (x, y)$.
S: $\dfrac{x + 1}{2} = 2$ gives $x = 3$, and $\dfrac{y + 4}{2} = -3$ gives $y = -10$.
S: **Answer: $A(3, -10)$**
Q: [CBSE 2019, 2 marks] In what ratio does the $x$-axis divide the segment joining $(1, -3)$ and $(4, 5)$? Find the point of division.
S: Let the ratio be $k : 1$. On the $x$-axis the $y$-coordinate is 0: $\dfrac{5k - 3}{k + 1} = 0$, so $k = \dfrac{3}{5}$.
S: The ratio is $3 : 5$. Then $x = \dfrac{3 \times 4 + 5 \times 1}{3 + 5} = \dfrac{17}{8}$.
S: **Answer: ratio $3 : 5$; the point is $\left(\dfrac{17}{8}, 0\right)$**
Q: [CBSE 2019, 3 marks] The segment joining $A(2, 1)$ and $B(5, -8)$ is trisected at $P$ and $Q$, with $P$ nearer to $A$. $P$ also lies on the line $2x - y + k = 0$. Find $k$.
S: $P$ divides $AB$ in the ratio $1 : 2$.
S: $P = \left(\dfrac{1 \times 5 + 2 \times 2}{3}, \dfrac{1 \times (-8) + 2 \times 1}{3}\right) = (3, -2)$
S: $P$ lies on the line: $2(3) - (-2) + k = 0$, so $8 + k = 0$.
S: **Answer: $k = -8$**
