S: (iii) $EQ = 1.28 - 0.18 = 1.10$ and $FR = 2.56 - 0.36 = 2.20$. $\dfrac{0.18}{1.10} = \dfrac{9}{55}$ and $\dfrac{0.36}{2.20} = \dfrac{9}{55}$. Equal, so $EF \parallel QR$.
S: **Answer: (i) no (ii) yes (iii) yes**
Q: [Q3] In the figure, $L$ lies on the diagonal $AC$ of quadrilateral $ABCD$, with $M$ on $AB$ and $N$ on $AD$ such that $LM \parallel CB$ and $LN \parallel CD$. Prove that $\dfrac{AM}{AB} = \dfrac{AN}{AD}$.

Q: [Q4] In the figure, $D$ is on $AB$ and $F$, $E$ are on $BC$ such that $DE \parallel AC$ and $DF \parallel AE$. Prove that $\dfrac{BF}{FE} = \dfrac{BE}{EC}$.

Q: [Q5] In the figure, $O$ is a point inside $\triangle PQR$, $D$ is on $PO$, $E$ is on $PQ$ and $F$ is on $PR$, with $DE \parallel OQ$ and $DF \parallel OR$. Show that $EF \parallel QR$.

S: In $\triangle POR$, $DF \parallel OR$, so $\dfrac{PF}{FR} = \dfrac{PD}{DO}$.
S: So $\dfrac{PE}{EQ} = \dfrac{PF}{FR}$ in $\triangle PQR$.

Q: [Q6] In the figure, $O$ is a point inside $\triangle PQR$, and $A$, $B$, $C$ are points on $OP$, $OQ$, $OR$ such that $AB \parallel PQ$ and $AC \parallel PR$. Show that $BC \parallel QR$.
!fig w=330 h=225; P P 160 20 t; P Q 40 190 bl; P R 290 195 br; P O 165 130 b; P A 162.5 75 r; P B 102.5 160 b; P C 227.5 162.5 b; L P Q; L Q R; L P R; L O P; L O Q; L O R; H A B; H A C; D B C
S: In $\triangle OPQ$, $AB \parallel PQ$, so $\dfrac{OA}{AP} = \dfrac{OB}{BQ}$.
S: In $\triangle OPR$, $AC \parallel PR$, so $\dfrac{OA}{AP} = \dfrac{OC}{CR}$.
S: So $\dfrac{OB}{BQ} = \dfrac{OC}{CR}$ in $\triangle OQR$.
S: **By the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem, $BC \parallel QR$.**
Q: [Q7] Using the Basic Proportionality Theorem, prove that a line through the mid-point of one side of a triangle, parallel to another side, bisects the third side.
S: In $\triangle ABC$, let $D$ be the mid-point of $AB$ and let the line through $D$ parallel to $BC$ meet $AC$ at $E$.
S: Since $DE \parallel BC$, $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$. But $AD = DB$, so $\dfrac{AE}{EC} = 1$.
S: **Hence $AE = EC$, so $E$ is the mid-point of $AC$.**
Q: [Q8] Using the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem, prove that the line joining the mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side.
S: In $\triangle ABC$, let $D$ and $E$ be the mid-points of $AB$ and $AC$. Then $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = 1$ and $\dfrac{AE}{EC} = 1$.
S: So $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$.
S: **By the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem, $DE \parallel BC$.**
Q: [Q9] $ABCD$ is a trapezium with $AB \parallel DC$, and its diagonals meet at $O$. Show that $\dfrac{AO}{BO} = \dfrac{CO}{DO}$.
S: Through $O$ draw $EO \parallel AB$, meeting $AD$ at $E$. Then $EO \parallel DC$ as well.
S: In $\triangle ADC$, $EO \parallel DC$, so $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{AO}{OC}$.
S: In $\triangle DAB$, $EO \parallel AB$, so $\dfrac{DE}{EA} = \dfrac{DO}{OB}$, that is $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{BO}{OD}$.
S: So $\dfrac{AO}{OC} = \dfrac{BO}{OD}$.
S: **Rearranging, $\dfrac{AO}{BO} = \dfrac{CO}{DO}$.**
Q: [Q10] The diagonals of a quadrilateral $ABCD$ meet at $O$ such that $\dfrac{AO}{BO} = \dfrac{CO}{DO}$. Show that $ABCD$ is a trapezium.
S: The given relation can be written as $\dfrac{AO}{OC} = \dfrac{BO}{OD}$.
S: Through $O$ draw $EO \parallel AB$, meeting $AD$ at $E$. In $\triangle DAB$, $\dfrac{DE}{EA} = \dfrac{DO}{OB}$, so $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{BO}{OD} = \dfrac{AO}{OC}$.
S: In $\triangle ADC$, $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{AO}{OC}$, so by the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem $EO \parallel DC$.
S: **$AB \parallel EO$ and $EO \parallel DC$, so $AB \parallel DC$ and $ABCD$ is a trapezium.**
