Q: [ICSE 2024, 3 marks] Solve $2x^2 - 10x + 5 = 0$, giving the answers correct to three significant figures.
S: $a = 2$, $b = -10$, $c = 5$. $D = 100 - 40 = 60$ and $\sqrt{60} \approx 7.746$.

T: Nature of the roots
Q: [ICSE 2024, 1 mark] The roots of $px^2 - qx + r = 0$ are real and equal when: (a) $p^2 = 4qr$  (b) $q^2 = 4pr$  (c) $-q^2 = 4pr$  (d) $p^2 > 4qr$
S: Equal roots need $D = 0$: $(-q)^2 - 4pr = 0$, so $q^2 = 4pr$.
S: **Answer: (b)**
