T: Sphere and hemisphere
Q: [ICSE 2025, 1 mark] The volumes of two spheres are in the ratio $27 : 64$. Find the ratio of their radii. (a) $3 : 4$  (b) $4 : 3$  (c) $9 : 16$  (d) $16 : 9$
S: Volume is proportional to $r^3$: $\dfrac{r_1^3}{r_2^3} = \dfrac{27}{64}$, so $\dfrac{r_1}{r_2} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
S: **Answer: (a)**

T: Melting and recasting
Q: [ICSE 2025, 3 marks] A hollow sphere has an external diameter of 10 cm and an internal diameter of 6 cm. It is melted and made into a solid cone of height 8 cm. Find the radius of the cone.
S: Volume of metal $= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi(R^3 - r^3) = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi(5^3 - 3^3) = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi \times 98$
S: Volume of the cone $= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi x^2 \times 8$, where $x$ is its radius.
S: $\dfrac{8}{3}\pi x^2 = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi \times 98$, so $x^2 = 49$.
S: **Answer: 7 cm**

T: Combination of solids
Q: [ICSE 2025, 4 marks] A solid wooden capsule is a cylinder with a hemisphere on each end. Its radius is 3.5 cm and the cylindrical part is 14 cm long. The capsule is cut into three separate solids: the cylinder and the two hemispheres. Find the sum of the total surface areas of the three pieces. Take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$.
S: Each piece is now a separate solid, so its flat faces count as well.
S: Cylinder: $2\pi r(h + r) = 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times (14 + 3.5) = 22 \times 17.5 = 385$ cm²
S: One solid hemisphere: $3\pi r^2 = 3 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 3.5 = 115.5$ cm². Two hemispheres: 231 cm².
S: Sum $= 385 + 231$
S: **Answer: 616 cm²**
