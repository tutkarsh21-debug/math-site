T: Properties of proportion
Q: [ICSE 2025, 3 marks] Use the properties of proportion to solve $\dfrac{6x^2 + 3x - 5}{3x - 5} = \dfrac{9x^2 + 2x + 5}{2x + 5}$, where $x \neq 0$.
S: Apply dividendo: if $\dfrac{a}{b} = \dfrac{c}{d}$ then $\dfrac{a - b}{b} = \dfrac{c - d}{d}$.
S: $\dfrac{6x^2 + 3x - 5 - (3x - 5)}{3x - 5} = \dfrac{9x^2 + 2x + 5 - (2x + 5)}{2x + 5}$, so $\dfrac{6x^2}{3x - 5} = \dfrac{9x^2}{2x + 5}$
S: Since $x \neq 0$, divide both sides by $3x^2$: $\dfrac{2}{3x - 5} = \dfrac{3}{2x + 5}$
S: $4x + 10 = 9x - 15$, so $5x = 25$.
S: **Answer: $x = 5$**
