T: Reflection of figures
Q: [ICSE 2020, 4 marks] Plot $A(-4, 0)$, $B(-3, 2)$, $C(0, 4)$, $D(4, 1)$ and $E(7, 3)$. Reflect $B$, $C$, $D$ and $E$ in the $x$-axis to get $B'$, $C'$, $D'$ and $E'$. Join $A, B, C, D, E, E', D', C', B', A$ in order and name the closed figure.
S: Reflection in the $x$-axis changes the sign of $y$: $B'(-3, -2)$, $C'(0, -4)$, $D'(4, -1)$ and $E'(7, -3)$.
