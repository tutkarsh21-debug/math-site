T: HCF and LCM by prime factorisation
Q: [CBSE 2024, 1 mark] If two positive integers $p$ and $q$ can be expressed as $p = 18a^2b^4$ and $q = 20a^3b^2$, where $a$ and $b$ are prime numbers, then LCM $(p, q)$ is: (a) $2a^2b^2$  (b) $180a^2b^2$  (c) $12a^2b^2$  (d) $180a^3b^4$
S: $p = 2 \times 3^2 \times a^2 \times b^4$ and $q = 2^2 \times 5 \times a^3 \times b^2$
S: LCM takes the greatest power of every prime: $2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 \times a^3 \times b^4 = 180a^3b^4$
S: **Answer: (d)**
Q: [CBSE 2024, 3 marks] In a teachers' workshop, the number of teachers teaching French, Hindi and English are 48, 80 and 144 respectively. Find the minimum number of rooms required if in each room the same number of teachers are seated and all of them are of the same subject.
S: For the fewest rooms, each room must seat the greatest possible number, which is the HCF of 48, 80 and 144.
S: $48 = 2^4 \times 3$, $80 = 2^4 \times 5$, $144 = 2^4 \times 3^2$, so HCF $= 2^4 = 16$
S: Number of rooms $= \dfrac{48}{16} + \dfrac{80}{16} + \dfrac{144}{16} = 3 + 5 + 9$
S: **Answer: 17 rooms**

T: The Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic
Q: [CBSE 2024, 2 marks] Show that the number $5 \times 11 \times 17 + 3 \times 11$ is a composite number.
S: $5 \times 11 \times 17 + 3 \times 11 = 11\,(5 \times 17 + 3) = 11 \times 88$
S: The number has factors other than 1 and itself (for example 11 and 88).
S: **Hence it is a composite number.**

T: Irrational numbers and their proofs
Q: [CBSE 2024, 2 marks] Prove that $5 - 2\sqrt{3}$ is an irrational number. It is given that $\sqrt{3}$ is an irrational number.
S: Assume, to the contrary, that $5 - 2\sqrt{3}$ is rational. Then $5 - 2\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers and $b \neq 0$.
S: Rearranging: $\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{5b - a}{2b}$
S: Since $a$ and $b$ are integers, the right side is rational, so $\sqrt{3}$ is rational. This contradicts the given fact that $\sqrt{3}$ is irrational.
S: **Hence $5 - 2\sqrt{3}$ is irrational.**
