T: Sum of n terms
Q: [ICSE 2024, 4 marks] $15, 30, 60, 120, \ldots$ is a G.P. (a) Write its $n$th term. (b) How many terms add up to 945?
S: (a) $a = 15$ and $r = 2$, so $T_n = 15 \times 2^{n-1}$.
S: (b) $S_n = \dfrac{a(r^n - 1)}{r - 1} = 15(2^n - 1) = 945$
S: $2^n - 1 = 63$, so $2^n = 64 = 2^6$.
S: **Answer: (a) $T_n = 15 \times 2^{n-1}$ (b) 6 terms**
