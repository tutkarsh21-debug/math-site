T: Surface area
Q: [CBSE 2024, 1 mark] A solid sphere is cut into two hemispheres. The ratio of the surface areas of sphere to that of two hemispheres taken together, is: (a) $1 : 1$  (b) $1 : 4$  (c) $2 : 3$  (d) $3 : 2$
S: Surface area of the sphere $= 4\pi r^2$
S: Each solid hemisphere has total surface area $3\pi r^2$, so the two together have $6\pi r^2$.
S: Ratio $= 4\pi r^2 : 6\pi r^2 = 2 : 3$
S: **Answer: (c)**

T: Volume
Q: [CBSE 2024, 1 mark] The volume of the largest right circular cone that can be carved out from a solid cube of edge 2 cm is: (a) $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ cu cm  (b) $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$ cu cm  (c) $\dfrac{8\pi}{3}$ cu cm  (d) $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ cu cm
S: The largest cone has base diameter 2 cm and height 2 cm, so $r = 1$ cm and $h = 2$ cm.
S: Volume $= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2h = \dfrac{1}{3} \times \pi \times 1 \times 2 = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ cu cm
S: **Answer: (d)**
Q: [CBSE 2024, 3 marks] The difference between the outer and inner radii of a hollow right circular cylinder of length 14 cm is 1 cm. If the volume of the metal used in making the cylinder is 176 cm³, find the outer and inner radii of the cylinder.
S: Let the outer and inner radii be $R$ and $r$. Then $R - r = 1$.
S: Volume of metal $= \pi(R^2 - r^2)h$: $\dfrac{22}{7} \times (R + r)(R - r) \times 14 = 176$
S: $44(R + r) = 176$, so $R + r = 4$.
S: Solving with $R - r = 1$: $R = 2.5$ and $r = 1.5$.
S: **Answer: outer radius 2.5 cm, inner radius 1.5 cm**
