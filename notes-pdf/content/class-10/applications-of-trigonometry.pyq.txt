T: Two right-angled triangles
Q: [CBSE 2024, 5 marks] A pole 6 m high is fixed on the top of a tower. The angle of elevation of the top of the pole observed from a point $P$ on the ground is $60°$ and the angle of depression of the point $P$ from the top of the tower is $45°$. Find the height of the tower and the distance of point $P$ from the foot of the tower. (Use $\sqrt{3} = 1.73$)
!fig w=300 h=235; P P 50 205 bl; P F 190 205 br; P T 190 65 r; P Q 190 20 tr; L P F; H F T; L T Q; L P T; L P Q; R P F T; A F P T 45°; T 215 45 6 m; T 204 140 h; cap FT is the tower and TQ is the pole
S: Let the height of the tower be $h$ m and the distance of $P$ from its foot be $d$ m.
S: The angle of depression of $P$ from the top of the tower is $45°$, so the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from $P$ is $45°$: $\tan 45° = \dfrac{h}{d}$, so $d = h$.
S: For the top of the pole: $\tan 60° = \dfrac{h + 6}{d}$, so $\sqrt{3}\,h = h + 6$.
S: $h = \dfrac{6}{\sqrt{3} - 1} = \dfrac{6(\sqrt{3} + 1)}{2} = 3(\sqrt{3} + 1) = 3 \times 2.73 = 8.19$
S: **Answer: the tower is 8.19 m high and $P$ is 8.19 m from its foot.**
