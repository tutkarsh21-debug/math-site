T: Lines parallel or perpendicular to a given line
Q: [ICSE 2017, 3 marks] $A(-1, 3)$, $B(4, 2)$ and $C(3, -2)$ are the vertices of a triangle. Find (a) the centroid $G$ (b) the equation of the line through $G$ parallel to $AC$.
S: (a) $G = \left(\dfrac{-1 + 4 + 3}{3}, \dfrac{3 + 2 - 2}{3}\right) = (2, 1)$
S: (b) Slope of $AC = \dfrac{-2 - 3}{3 - (-1)} = -\dfrac{5}{4}$. The line is $y - 1 = -\dfrac{5}{4}(x - 2)$, so $4y - 4 = -5x + 10$.
S: **Answer: (a) $G(2, 1)$ (b) $5x + 4y - 14 = 0$**
