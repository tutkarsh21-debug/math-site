T: The distance formula
Q: [CBSE 2026, 2 marks] The centre of a circle is $(x - 7, 2x)$. The circle passes through $(-9, 11)$ and its radius is $5\sqrt{2}$ units. Find the possible values of $x$.
S: The distance from the centre to $(-9, 11)$ equals the radius.
S: $(x - 7 + 9)^2 + (2x - 11)^2 = (5\sqrt{2})^2$
S: $x^2 + 4x + 4 + 4x^2 - 44x + 121 = 50$, so $5x^2 - 40x + 75 = 0$
S: $x^2 - 8x + 15 = 0$, so $(x - 3)(x - 5) = 0$
S: **Answer: $x = 3$ or $x = 5$**

T: The section formula
Q: [CBSE 2026, 1 mark] Where does the mid-point of the segment joining $(5, -4)$ and $(6, 4)$ lie? (a) on the $x$-axis  (b) on the $y$-axis  (c) at the origin  (d) on neither axis
S: Mid-point $= \left(\dfrac{5 + 6}{2}, \dfrac{-4 + 4}{2}\right) = \left(\dfrac{11}{2}, 0\right)$
S: Its $y$-coordinate is 0 and its $x$-coordinate is not 0.
S: **Answer: (a)**
Q: [CBSE 2026, 3 marks] In what ratio does the $x$-axis divide the segment joining $(-6, 5)$ and $(-4, -1)$? Find the point where they meet.
S: Let the ratio be $k : 1$. The point of division is $\left(\dfrac{-4k - 6}{k + 1}, \dfrac{-k + 5}{k + 1}\right)$.
S: On the $x$-axis the $y$-coordinate is 0: $-k + 5 = 0$, so $k = 5$.
S: $x = \dfrac{-4(5) - 6}{5 + 1} = -\dfrac{26}{6} = -\dfrac{13}{3}$
S: **Answer: ratio $5 : 1$; the point is $\left(-\dfrac{13}{3}, 0\right)$**
