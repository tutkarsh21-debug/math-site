Q: [CBSE 2026, 1 mark] The graph of a pair of linear equations in two variables is a pair of coincident lines. How many solutions does the pair have? (a) exactly one  (b) two  (c) none  (d) infinitely many
S: Coincident lines lie on top of each other, so every point of the line is common to both.
S: **Answer: (d)**
Q: [CBSE 2026, 5 marks] The lines $2x - 3y + 6 = 0$, $2x + 3y - 18 = 0$ and $x = 0$ form a triangle. Draw the graph, write the coordinates of its vertices, and find its area.
S: Points on $2x - 3y + 6 = 0$:
| $x$ | 0 | 3 | $-3$ |
| $y$ | 2 | 4 | 0 |
S: Points on $2x + 3y - 18 = 0$:
| $x$ | 0 | 3 | 9 |
| $y$ | 6 | 4 | 0 |
S: The line $x = 0$ is the $y$-axis. Plot the points and join them.

S: The two slanting lines meet at $(3, 4)$. They meet the $y$-axis at $(0, 2)$ and $(0, 6)$.
S: Base along the $y$-axis $= 6 - 2 = 4$ units. Height $=$ distance of $(3, 4)$ from the $y$-axis $= 3$ units.
S: Area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 3$
S: **Answer: vertices $(0, 2)$, $(0, 6)$ and $(3, 4)$; area $= 6$ square units**
