T: Addition, subtraction and multiplication by a number
Q: [ICSE 2019, 3 marks] Simplify $\sin A\begin{bmatrix} \sin A & -\cos A \\ \cos A & \sin A \end{bmatrix} + \cos A\begin{bmatrix} \cos A & \sin A \\ -\sin A & \cos A \end{bmatrix}$.
S: Multiply each matrix by its number, then add the matching entries.
S: $= \begin{bmatrix} \sin^2 A + \cos^2 A & -\sin A\cos A + \sin A\cos A \\ \sin A\cos A - \sin A\cos A & \sin^2 A + \cos^2 A \end{bmatrix}$
S: **Answer: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$**

T: Solving matrix equations
Q: [ICSE 2019, 4 marks] Given $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}M = 6I$, where $I$ is the unit matrix of order $2 \times 2$, (a) state the order of $M$ (b) find $M$.
S: (a) The product is a $2 \times 2$ matrix and the first matrix is $2 \times 2$, so $M$ is $2 \times 2$.
S: (b) Let $M = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$. Then $\begin{bmatrix} 4a + 2c & 4b + 2d \\ -a + c & -b + d \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 6 & 0 \\ 0 & 6 \end{bmatrix}$.
S: $-a + c = 0$ gives $c = a$, and then $6a = 6$, so $a = 1$ and $c = 1$.
S: $-b + d = 6$ gives $d = b + 6$, and then $4b + 2b + 12 = 0$, so $b = -2$ and $d = 4$.
S: **Answer: $M = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$**
