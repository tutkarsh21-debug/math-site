T: Finding unknown constants
Q: [ICSE 2023, 1 mark] $(x - 2)$ is a factor of $x^3 - kx - 12$. Find $k$. (a) 3  (b) 2  (c) $-2$  (d) $-3$
S: $f(2) = 0$: $8 - 2k - 12 = 0$, so $k = -2$.
S: **Answer: (c)**
Q: [ICSE 2023, 4 marks] Find $a$ if $(x - a)$ is a factor of $3x^3 + x^2 - ax - 81$.
S: By the factor theorem $f(a) = 0$: $3a^3 + a^2 - a \times a - 81 = 0$
S: $3a^3 = 81$, so $a^3 = 27$.
S: **Answer: $a = 3$**

T: Factorising a cubic polynomial
Q: [ICSE 2023, 4 marks] Use the factor theorem to factorise $2x^3 - x^2 - 13x - 6$ completely.
S: Try $x = -2$: $f(-2) = -16 - 4 + 26 - 6 = 0$, so $(x + 2)$ is a factor.
S: Dividing by $(x + 2)$ gives the quotient $2x^2 - 5x - 3$.
S: $2x^2 - 5x - 3 = 2x^2 - 6x + x - 3 = (2x + 1)(x - 3)$
S: **Answer: $(x + 2)(2x + 1)(x - 3)$**
