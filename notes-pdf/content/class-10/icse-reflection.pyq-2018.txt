T: Invariant points
Q: [ICSE 2018, 4 marks] $ABCD$ is a quadrilateral with vertices $A(2, 2)$, $B(2, -2)$, $C(0, -1)$ and $D(0, 1)$. (a) Reflect $ABCD$ in the $y$-axis to get $A'B'CD$, and write the coordinates of $A'$ and $B'$. (b) Name two points that are invariant under this reflection. (c) Name the polygon $A'B'CD$.
S: (a) $A'(-2, 2)$ and $B'(-2, -2)$.
S: (b) $C$ and $D$ lie on the $y$-axis, which is the mirror line, so they are invariant.

S: (c) $A'B' \parallel DC$ (both vertical) and $A'D = B'C = \sqrt{5}$.
S: **Answer: $A'(-2, 2)$, $B'(-2, -2)$; invariant points $C$ and $D$; $A'B'CD$ is an isosceles trapezium**
