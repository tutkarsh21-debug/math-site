T: Reflection of figures
Q: [ICSE 2025, 5 marks] Plot $A(1, 3)$, $B(1, 2)$ and $C(3, 0)$ on graph paper. (a) Reflect $A$ and $B$ in the $x$-axis to get $E$ and $D$. (b) Reflect $A$ and $B$ in the origin to get $F$ and $G$. (c) Reflect $A$, $B$ and $C$ in the $y$-axis to get $J$, $I$ and $H$. (d) Join $A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J$ in order and name the closed figure.
S: (a) Reflection in the $x$-axis changes the sign of $y$: $E(1, -3)$ and $D(1, -2)$.
S: (b) Reflection in the origin changes both signs: $F(-1, -3)$ and $G(-1, -2)$.
S: (c) Reflection in the $y$-axis changes the sign of $x$: $J(-1, 3)$, $I(-1, 2)$ and $H(-3, 0)$.

S: (d) The figure has 10 sides and two of its angles point inwards on each side.
