T: Forms of the equation
Q: [ICSE 2025, 5 marks] $ABCD$ is a parallelogram with vertices $A(3, 3)$, $B(0, -2)$, $C(-4, -2)$ and $D(-1, 3)$, read from a graph. (a) Find the point $P$ where the diagonals meet. (b) Find the slopes of $CB$ and $DA$ and check that these sides are parallel. (c) Find the equation of the diagonal $AC$.
S: (a) The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other, so $P$ is the mid-point of $AC$: $P = \left(\dfrac{3 - 4}{2}, \dfrac{3 - 2}{2}\right) = \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.
S: (b) Slope of $CB = \dfrac{-2 - (-2)}{0 - (-4)} = 0$ and slope of $DA = \dfrac{3 - 3}{3 - (-1)} = 0$. The slopes are equal, so $CB \parallel DA$.
S: (c) Slope of $AC = \dfrac{3 - (-2)}{3 - (-4)} = \dfrac{5}{7}$. Using $A(3, 3)$: $y - 3 = \dfrac{5}{7}(x - 3)$, so $7y - 21 = 5x - 15$.
S: **Answer: (a) $P\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$ (b) both slopes are 0 (c) $5x - 7y + 6 = 0$**

T: Lines parallel or perpendicular to a given line
Q: [ICSE 2025, 1 mark] Which line passes through the origin and is parallel to $3x + 4y + 7 = 0$? (a) $3x + 4y + 5 = 0$  (b) $4x - 3y - 5 = 0$  (c) $4x - 3y = 0$  (d) $3x + 4y = 0$
S: A parallel line has the same $x$ and $y$ coefficients: $3x + 4y + k = 0$.
S: It passes through $(0, 0)$, so $k = 0$.
S: **Answer: (d)**
Q: [ICSE 2025, 3 marks] The line $PQ$ meets the positive $x$-axis at $P$, 5 units from the origin, and the negative $y$-axis at $Q$, 3 units from the origin. The line $RS$ passes through the origin and is perpendicular to $PQ$. Find (a) the coordinates of $P$ and $Q$ (b) the equation of $RS$.
S: (a) $P(5, 0)$ and $Q(0, -3)$.
S: (b) Slope of $PQ = \dfrac{0 - (-3)}{5 - 0} = \dfrac{3}{5}$, so the slope of $RS$ is $-\dfrac{5}{3}$.
S: $RS$ passes through the origin: $y = -\dfrac{5}{3}x$.
S: **Answer: (a) $P(5, 0)$, $Q(0, -3)$ (b) $5x + 3y = 0$**
