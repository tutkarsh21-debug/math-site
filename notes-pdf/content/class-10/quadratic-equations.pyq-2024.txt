T: Nature of roots
Q: [CBSE 2024, 1 mark] The equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, $a \neq 0$, has real and equal roots. Which relation holds? (a) $a = \dfrac{b^2}{c}$  (b) $b^2 = ac$  (c) $ac = \dfrac{b^2}{4}$  (d) $c = \dfrac{b^2}{a}$
S: For real and equal roots, $D = b^2 - 4ac = 0$.
S: So $b^2 = 4ac$, that is $ac = \dfrac{b^2}{4}$.
S: **Answer: (c)**

T: Word problems
Q: [CBSE 2024, 4 marks, case study] A rectangular floor is covered exactly by 200 square tiles. With tiles whose side is 1 unit longer, only 128 tiles are needed. Let the side of an original tile be $x$ units. (i) Form a quadratic equation in $x$. (ii) Write it in standard form. (iii) Find $x$ by factorisation, OR solve the equation by the quadratic formula.
S: (i) The floor area is the same in both cases: $200x^2 = 128(x + 1)^2$
S: (ii) $200x^2 = 128x^2 + 256x + 128$, so $72x^2 - 256x - 128 = 0$. Dividing by 8: $9x^2 - 32x - 16 = 0$
S: (iii) By factorisation: $9x^2 - 36x + 4x - 16 = 0$, so $9x(x - 4) + 4(x - 4) = 0$ and $(9x + 4)(x - 4) = 0$
S: $x = 4$ or $x = -\dfrac{4}{9}$. A length cannot be negative, so $x = 4$.
S: OR by the formula: $x = \dfrac{32 \pm \sqrt{1024 + 576}}{18} = \dfrac{32 \pm 40}{18}$, which gives $x = 4$ or $x = -\dfrac{4}{9}$.
S: **Answer: the side of a tile is 4 units.**
