T: Multiplication of matrices
Q: [ICSE 2017, 4 marks] $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$, $B = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $A^2 - 5B^2 = 5C$. Find the matrix $C$.
S: $A^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 + 9 & 3 + 12 \\ 3 + 12 & 9 + 16 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 10 & 15 \\ 15 & 25 \end{bmatrix}$
S: $B^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 4 - 3 & -2 + 2 \\ 6 - 6 & -3 + 4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
S: $5C = A^2 - 5B^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 15 \\ 15 & 20 \end{bmatrix}$
S: **Answer: $C = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$**

T: Solving matrix equations
Q: [ICSE 2017, 4 marks] Given $B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 8 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$, find $X = B^2 - 4B$. Then solve $X\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 50 \end{bmatrix}$ for $a$ and $b$.
S: $B^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 + 8 & 1 + 3 \\ 8 + 24 & 8 + 9 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 9 & 4 \\ 32 & 17 \end{bmatrix}$ and $4B = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 4 \\ 32 & 12 \end{bmatrix}$
S: $X = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 0 \\ 0 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$
S: $X\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 5a \\ 5b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 50 \end{bmatrix}$
S: **Answer: $X = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 0 \\ 0 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$; $a = 1$, $b = 10$**
