T: Elimination method
Q: [CBSE 2025, 1 mark] The pair of equations $2x - 3y + a = 0$ and $2x + 3y - b = 0$ has the solution $x = 1$, $y = 2$. Find $a$ and $b$, and state how they are related.
S: First equation: $2(1) - 3(2) + a = 0$, so $a = 4$.
S: Second equation: $2(1) + 3(2) - b = 0$, so $b = 8$.
S: **Answer: $a = 4$, $b = 8$, so $b = 2a$**

T: Word problems
Q: [CBSE 2025, 5 marks] A person puts money into two schemes, A and B, which pay simple interest at 8% and 9% a year. The interest for one year comes to ₹1,860. If the two amounts had been swapped between the schemes, the interest would have been ₹20 more. How much was put into each scheme?
S: Let ₹$x$ be invested in scheme A and ₹$y$ in scheme B.
S: Interest as invested: $\dfrac{8x}{100} + \dfrac{9y}{100} = 1860$, so $8x + 9y = 186000$ ... (1)
S: Interest after swapping: $\dfrac{9x}{100} + \dfrac{8y}{100} = 1880$, so $9x + 8y = 188000$ ... (2)
S: Adding (1) and (2): $17(x + y) = 374000$, so $x + y = 22000$ ... (3)
S: Subtracting (1) from (2): $x - y = 2000$ ... (4)
S: Adding (3) and (4): $2x = 24000$, so $x = 12000$ and $y = 10000$.
S: Check: $8\%$ of 12000 $+$ $9\%$ of 10000 $= 960 + 900 = 1860$.
S: **Answer: ₹12,000 in scheme A and ₹10,000 in scheme B**
