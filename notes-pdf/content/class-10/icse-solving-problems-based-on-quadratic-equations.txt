@title Solving Problems Based on Quadratic Equations
@summary Many word problems lead to a quadratic equation. This chapter is about turning the words into an equation, solving it, and keeping only the answer that makes sense.

# The method
- **Step 1**: read the problem and decide what is to be found. Let that quantity be $x$.
- **Step 2**: write the other quantities in terms of $x$.
- **Step 3**: use the condition given in the problem to form an equation, and simplify it to $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.
- **Step 4**: solve the equation.
- **Step 5**: check each root against the problem. Reject a root that is negative when the quantity must be positive, or a fraction when it must be a whole number.
- **Step 6**: write the answer in words, with units.

# Problems on numbers
- Two consecutive numbers: $x$ and $x + 1$. Two consecutive even (or odd) numbers: $x$ and $x + 2$.
- A number and its reciprocal: $x$ and $\dfrac{1}{x}$.
- A two-digit number with tens digit $x$ and units digit $y$ is $10x + y$.
?eg The product of two consecutive natural numbers is 132. Find the numbers.
= Let the numbers be $x$ and $x + 1$. Then $x(x + 1) = 132$, so $x^2 + x - 132 = 0$.
= $(x + 12)(x - 11) = 0$, so $x = 11$ (a natural number cannot be $-12$).
=> The numbers are 11 and 12.
?eg The sum of a number and its reciprocal is $\dfrac{10}{3}$. Find the number.
= $x + \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{10}{3}$. Multiply by $3x$: $3x^2 + 3 = 10x$, so $3x^2 - 10x + 3 = 0$.
= $(3x - 1)(x - 3) = 0$
=> The number is 3 or $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

# Problems on ages
- If the present age is $x$ years, the age $n$ years ago was $x - n$ and the age $n$ years from now will be $x + n$.
?eg The product of Ravi's age 3 years ago and his age 5 years from now is 84. Find his present age.
= Let his present age be $x$ years. Then $(x - 3)(x + 5) = 84$, so $x^2 + 2x - 99 = 0$.
= $(x + 11)(x - 9) = 0$, so $x = 9$ (age cannot be negative).
=> Ravi is 9 years old.

# Problems on geometry
- Rectangle: area $=$ length $\times$ breadth; perimeter $= 2(\text{length} + \text{breadth})$.
- Right-angled triangle: $(\text{hypotenuse})^2 = $ sum of the squares of the other two sides.
?eg The hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle is 13 cm and the other two sides differ by 7 cm. Find the two sides.
= Let the shorter side be $x$ cm; the other is $(x + 7)$ cm. Then $x^2 + (x + 7)^2 = 13^2$.
= $2x^2 + 14x + 49 = 169$, so $x^2 + 7x - 60 = 0$ and $(x + 12)(x - 5) = 0$.
= $x = 5$ (a length cannot be negative).
=> The sides are 5 cm and 12 cm.
?eg The perimeter of a rectangular field is 46 m and its area is 120 m². Find its dimensions.
= Length $+$ breadth $= 23$. Let the length be $x$ m; the breadth is $(23 - x)$ m.
= $x(23 - x) = 120$, so $x^2 - 23x + 120 = 0$ and $(x - 15)(x - 8) = 0$.
=> The field is 15 m long and 8 m wide.

# Problems on speed, distance and time
$$\text{time} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$$
- Write the time for each case and use the given difference in time.
- Boat in a stream: speed downstream $= x + y$ and speed upstream $= x - y$, where $x$ is the speed in still water and $y$ the speed of the stream.
- Keep the units consistent: 1 hour 40 minutes $= \dfrac{5}{3}$ hours.
?eg A car covers 400 km at a uniform speed. If its speed had been 12 km/h more, it would have taken 1 hour 40 minutes less. Find the speed of the car.
= Let the speed be $x$ km/h. Then $\dfrac{400}{x} - \dfrac{400}{x + 12} = \dfrac{5}{3}$.
= $\dfrac{400 \times 12}{x(x + 12)} = \dfrac{5}{3}$, so $x(x + 12) = 2880$ and $x^2 + 12x - 2880 = 0$.
= $(x + 60)(x - 48) = 0$, so $x = 48$.
=> The speed of the car is 48 km/h.

# Problems on cost and on work
- Cost of one article $= \dfrac{\text{total cost}}{\text{number of articles}}$.
- If a person finishes a job in $x$ days, the work done in one day is $\dfrac{1}{x}$.
?eg A shopkeeper buys a number of books for ₹600. If each book had cost ₹5 less, he could have bought 4 more books for the same amount. Find the number of books he bought.
= Let the number of books be $x$. Then $\dfrac{600}{x} - \dfrac{600}{x + 4} = 5$.
= $\dfrac{600 \times 4}{x(x + 4)} = 5$, so $x(x + 4) = 480$ and $x^2 + 4x - 480 = 0$.
= $(x + 24)(x - 20) = 0$, so $x = 20$.
=> He bought 20 books.
?eg A takes 6 days less than B to finish a piece of work. Working together they finish it in 4 days. Find the time B takes alone.
= Let B take $x$ days; then A takes $(x - 6)$ days. $\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{x - 6} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
= $4(2x - 6) = x(x - 6)$, so $x^2 - 14x + 24 = 0$ and $(x - 12)(x - 2) = 0$.
= $x = 2$ would make A's time negative, so $x = 12$.
=> B takes 12 days (and A takes 6 days).

# Common mistakes
- Not rejecting the root that does not fit the problem.
- Writing the smaller time first, which gives a negative difference. The slower journey takes the longer time.
- Mixing minutes with hours.
- Stopping at the value of $x$ when the question asks for something else, such as both numbers or both dimensions.

Consecutive numbers | $x$, $x + 1$
Consecutive even or odd numbers | $x$, $x + 2$
Number and its reciprocal | $x$, $\dfrac{1}{x}$
Two-digit number | $10x + y$
Ages | $n$ years ago: $x - n$;  $n$ years hence: $x + n$
Area of a rectangle | Length $\times$ breadth
Perimeter of a rectangle | $2(\text{length} + \text{breadth})$
Pythagoras theorem | $(\text{hypotenuse})^2 = (\text{base})^2 + (\text{perpendicular})^2$
Time | $\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$
Boat and stream | Downstream speed $= x + y$;  upstream speed $= x - y$
Cost per article | $\dfrac{\text{total cost}}{\text{number of articles}}$
One day's work | $\dfrac{1}{\text{number of days to finish the job}}$
