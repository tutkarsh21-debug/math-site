Q: [Q1] Six graphs of $y = p(x)$ are given in the textbook (Fig. 2.10). Find the number of zeroes of $p(x)$ in each case.
S: The zeroes of $p(x)$ are the $x$-coordinates of the points where the graph of $y = p(x)$ meets the $x$-axis. So count the points where each graph cuts or touches the $x$-axis.
| Graph | What the graph does | Number of zeroes |
| (i) | A straight line parallel to the $x$-axis, above it. It never meets the $x$-axis. | 0 |
| (ii) | A curve that stays below the $x$-axis on the left and crosses it once on the right. | 1 |
| (iii) | A curve that crosses the $x$-axis at three points. | 3 |
| (iv) | A U-shaped curve that crosses the $x$-axis at two points. | 2 |
| (v) | A wavy curve that crosses the $x$-axis at four points. | 4 |
| (vi) | A curve that crosses the $x$-axis once and touches it at two more points. | 3 |
S: **Answer: (i) 0 (ii) 1 (iii) 3 (iv) 2 (v) 4 (vi) 3**

Q: [Q1 (i)] Find the zeroes of $x^2 - 2x - 8$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.
S: $x^2 - 2x - 8 = x^2 - 4x + 2x - 8 = x(x - 4) + 2(x - 4) = (x - 4)(x + 2)$. The zeroes are $4$ and $-2$.
S: Sum of zeroes $= 4 + (-2) = 2 = \dfrac{-(-2)}{1} = -\dfrac{\text{coefficient of } x}{\text{coefficient of } x^2}$.
S: Product of zeroes $= 4 \times (-2) = -8 = \dfrac{-8}{1} = \dfrac{\text{constant term}}{\text{coefficient of } x^2}$.
S: **Answer: zeroes $4$ and $-2$. Verified.**
Q: [Q1 (ii)] Find the zeroes of $4s^2 - 4s + 1$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.
S: $4s^2 - 4s + 1 = (2s - 1)^2$. Both zeroes are $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
S: Sum of zeroes $= \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2} = 1 = \dfrac{-(-4)}{4}$.
S: Product of zeroes $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{\text{constant term}}{\text{coefficient of } s^2}$.
S: **Answer: zeroes $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Verified.**
Q: [Q1 (iii)] Find the zeroes of $6x^2 - 3 - 7x$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.
S: In standard form the polynomial is $6x^2 - 7x - 3 = 6x^2 - 9x + 2x - 3 = 3x(2x - 3) + 1(2x - 3) = (3x + 1)(2x - 3)$. The zeroes are $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
S: Sum of zeroes $= -\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{7}{6} = \dfrac{-(-7)}{6}$.
S: Product of zeroes $= -\dfrac{1}{3} \times \dfrac{3}{2} = -\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{-3}{6}$.
S: **Answer: zeroes $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Verified.**
Q: [Q1 (iv)] Find the zeroes of $4u^2 + 8u$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.
S: $4u^2 + 8u = 4u(u + 2)$. The zeroes are $0$ and $-2$.
S: Sum of zeroes $= 0 + (-2) = -2 = \dfrac{-8}{4}$.
S: Product of zeroes $= 0 \times (-2) = 0 = \dfrac{0}{4}$, since the constant term is 0.
S: **Answer: zeroes $0$ and $-2$. Verified.**
Q: [Q1 (v)] Find the zeroes of $t^2 - 15$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.
S: $t^2 - 15 = (t - \sqrt{15})(t + \sqrt{15})$. The zeroes are $\sqrt{15}$ and $-\sqrt{15}$.
S: Sum of zeroes $= \sqrt{15} - \sqrt{15} = 0 = \dfrac{-0}{1}$, since the coefficient of $t$ is 0.
S: Product of zeroes $= \sqrt{15} \times (-\sqrt{15}) = -15 = \dfrac{-15}{1}$.
S: **Answer: zeroes $\sqrt{15}$ and $-\sqrt{15}$. Verified.**
Q: [Q1 (vi)] Find the zeroes of $3x^2 - x - 4$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.
S: $3x^2 - x - 4 = 3x^2 + 3x - 4x - 4 = 3x(x + 1) - 4(x + 1) = (3x - 4)(x + 1)$. The zeroes are $\dfrac{4}{3}$ and $-1$.
S: Sum of zeroes $= \dfrac{4}{3} - 1 = \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{-(-1)}{3}$.
S: Product of zeroes $= \dfrac{4}{3} \times (-1) = -\dfrac{4}{3} = \dfrac{-4}{3}$.
S: **Answer: zeroes $\dfrac{4}{3}$ and $-1$. Verified.**
Q: [Q2] Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes have the given sum and product: (i) $\dfrac{1}{4}$, $-1$ (ii) $\sqrt{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{3}$ (iii) $0$, $\sqrt{5}$ (iv) $1$, $1$ (v) $-\dfrac{1}{4}$, $\dfrac{1}{4}$ (vi) $4$, $1$.
S: A quadratic polynomial with sum of zeroes $S$ and product of zeroes $P$ is $x^2 - Sx + P$. Any non-zero multiple of it has the same zeroes, so fractions can be cleared.
S: (i) $x^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}x - 1$. Multiplying by 4: $4x^2 - x - 4$.
S: (ii) $x^2 - \sqrt{2}\,x + \dfrac{1}{3}$. Multiplying by 3: $3x^2 - 3\sqrt{2}\,x + 1$.
S: (iii) $x^2 - 0x + \sqrt{5} = x^2 + \sqrt{5}$.
S: (iv) $x^2 - x + 1$.
S: (v) $x^2 + \dfrac{1}{4}x + \dfrac{1}{4}$. Multiplying by 4: $4x^2 + x + 1$.
S: (vi) $x^2 - 4x + 1$.
S: **Answer: (i) $4x^2 - x - 4$ (ii) $3x^2 - 3\sqrt{2}\,x + 1$ (iii) $x^2 + \sqrt{5}$ (iv) $x^2 - x + 1$ (v) $4x^2 + x + 1$ (vi) $x^2 - 4x + 1$**
