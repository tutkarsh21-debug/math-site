# Cuboid
- A **cuboid** has 6 rectangular faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices. Its dimensions are length $l$, breadth $b$ and height $h$.
| Quantity | Formula |
| Volume | $l \times b \times h$ |
| Total surface area | $2(lb + bh + hl)$ |
| Lateral surface area (four walls) | $2(l + b)h$ |
| Diagonal | $\sqrt{l^2 + b^2 + h^2}$ |
| Sum of all the edges | $4(l + b + h)$ |
!fig w=320 h=190; Q a 50 160; Q b 220 160; Q c 220 80; Q d 50 80; Q e 100 120; Q f 270 120; Q g 270 40; Q h 100 40; L a b; L b c; L c d; L d a; L b f; L f g; L g c; L g h; L h d; D a e; D e f; D e h; T 135 176 l; T 256 148 b; T 282 84 h; cap Cuboid with length l, breadth b and height h
- The area of the four walls of a room is the lateral surface area.
- An **open box** has no top: surface area $= lb + 2(l + b)h$.
?eg A cuboid is 12 cm long, 9 cm broad and 8 cm high. Find its volume, total surface area and diagonal.
= Volume $= 12 \times 9 \times 8$. Surface area $= 2(108 + 72 + 96)$. Diagonal $= \sqrt{144 + 81 + 64}$.
=> 864 cu cm; 552 sq cm; 17 cm
?eg A room is 8 m long, 6 m wide and 4 m high. Find the cost of painting its four walls and ceiling at ₹50 per sq m.
= Walls: $2(8 + 6) \times 4 = 112$ sq m. Ceiling: 48 sq m. Total 160 sq m.
=> ₹8000

# Cube
- A **cube** is a cuboid with all edges equal to $a$.
| Quantity | Formula |
| Volume | $a^3$ |
| Total surface area | $6a^2$ |
| Lateral surface area | $4a^2$ |
| Diagonal | $a\sqrt{3}$ |
?eg The total surface area of a cube is 294 sq cm. Find its edge, volume and diagonal.
= $6a^2 = 294$, so $a = 7$ cm.
=> 7 cm; 343 cu cm; $7\sqrt{3}$ cm
?eg Three cubes of edges 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm are melted to form one cube. Find its edge.
= Volume $= 27 + 64 + 125 = 216$ cu cm
=> 6 cm
?eg How many cubes of edge 4 cm can be cut from a cuboid 16 cm by 12 cm by 8 cm?
= $\dfrac{16 \times 12 \times 8}{4 \times 4 \times 4}$
=> 24

# Units and capacity
| Conversion | Value |
| 1 cu m | 1000 litres $= 1{,}000{,}000$ cu cm |
| 1 litre | 1000 cu cm |
| 1 cu cm | 1 mL |
| 1 kilolitre | 1 cu m |
- Mass $=$ volume $\times$ density.
?eg A tank is 3 m long, 2 m wide and 1.5 m deep. How many litres of water does it hold?
= Volume $= 9$ cu m
=> 9000 litres

# Hollow boxes
- For a box made of material of thickness $t$ (closed on all sides): internal dimensions $=$ external dimensions $- 2t$.
- For an **open** box, the internal height is the external height $- t$.
- Volume of material $=$ external volume $-$ internal volume.
?eg A closed wooden box has external dimensions 50 cm, 40 cm and 30 cm, and the wood is 2 cm thick. Find the volume of the wood.
= Internal dimensions: 46 cm, 36 cm, 26 cm.
= $60000 - 43056$
=> 16,944 cu cm

# Uniform cross-section
- A solid whose cross-section is the same all along its length (a prism) has
$$\text{Volume} = \text{area of cross-section} \times \text{length}$$
$$\text{Lateral surface area} = \text{perimeter of cross-section} \times \text{length}$$
- Total surface area $=$ lateral surface area $+ 2 \times$ area of cross-section.
?eg A swimming pool is 40 m long and 15 m wide. Its depth increases uniformly from 1 m at one end to 3 m at the other. Find the volume of water it holds.
= The cross-section is a trapezium with parallel sides 1 m and 3 m and width 40 m: area $= \dfrac{1}{2}(1 + 3) \times 40 = 80$ sq m.
= Volume $= 80 \times 15$
=> 1200 cu m
?eg A metal bar 2 m long has a cross-section that is a right triangle with legs 6 cm and 8 cm. Find its volume and lateral surface area.
= Area of cross-section $= 24$ sq cm; hypotenuse 10 cm; perimeter 24 cm; length 200 cm.
=> 4800 cu cm; 4800 sq cm

# Flow of water
- Volume of water flowing in time $t$ $=$ area of cross-section of the pipe or canal $\times$ speed $\times$ time.
- Keep all lengths in the same unit.
?eg Water flows at 5 m/s through a rectangular channel 2 m wide and 1.5 m deep. How many litres flow in one minute?
= $2 \times 1.5 \times 5 \times 60 = 900$ cu m
=> 9,00,000 litres
?eg A river 3 m deep and 40 m wide flows at 2 km/h. How much water falls into the sea in one minute?
= Speed $= \dfrac{2000}{60}$ m per minute. Volume $= 3 \times 40 \times \dfrac{2000}{60}$.
=> 4000 cu m
?eg A field 500 m by 200 m is covered by rain to a depth of 2 cm. Find the volume of water.
= $500 \times 200 \times 0.02$
=> 2000 cu m

# Common mistakes
- Forgetting to subtract twice the thickness for a closed box.
- Using $2(lb + bh + hl)$ when only the walls or an open box are meant.
- Mixing centimetres and metres.
- Writing 1 cu m $=$ 100 litres. It is 1000 litres.
- For flow problems, forgetting to multiply by the time.
- Thinking that doubling the edge of a cube doubles the volume. The volume becomes 8 times and the surface area 4 times.

A: 17 m

A: $300 + 700 = 1000$ sq cm

A: 420 cu cm
