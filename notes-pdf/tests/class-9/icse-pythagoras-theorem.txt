Q: The legs of a right triangle are 8 cm and 15 cm. The hypotenuse is
- 16 cm
* 17 cm
- 19 cm
- 23 cm
W: $\sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17$.

Q: Which of these is a Pythagorean triplet?
- 10, 12, 15
- 7, 9, 11
* 20, 21, 29
- 6, 9, 12
W: $400 + 441 = 841 = 29^2$.

Q: The altitude of an equilateral triangle of side 6 cm is
- 3 cm
* $3\sqrt{3}$ cm
- $6\sqrt{3}$ cm
- $2\sqrt{3}$ cm
W: $\sqrt{6^2 - 3^2} = \sqrt{27} = 3\sqrt{3}$.

Q: The diagonals of a rhombus are 10 cm and 24 cm. Its side is
- 12 cm
* 13 cm
- 17 cm
- 34 cm
W: The half-diagonals 5 cm and 12 cm are the legs of a right triangle.

Q: A man walks 9 m east and then 12 m north. His distance from the starting point is
- 21 m
* 15 m
- 3 m
- 18 m
W: $\sqrt{81 + 144} = 15$.

Q: An isosceles triangle has sides 13 cm, 13 cm and 10 cm. The altitude to the 10 cm side is
- 8 cm
- 10 cm
* 12 cm
- 13 cm
W: The altitude bisects the base: $\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12$.

Q: In triangle ABC, $\angle B = 90°$. Then
- $AB^2 = AC^2 + BC^2$
* $AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2$
- $BC^2 = AB^2 + AC^2$
- $AC = AB + BC$
W: AC is opposite the right angle, so it is the hypotenuse.

Q: A 25 m ladder reaches a window 24 m above the ground. The distance of its foot from the wall is
- 1 m
* 7 m
- 12 m
- 49 m
W: $\sqrt{625 - 576} = 7$.

Q: A triangle has sides 7 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm. It is
- right-angled
* acute-angled
- obtuse-angled
- isosceles
W: $7^2 + 8^2 = 113$ is greater than $10^2 = 100$, so the largest angle is less than 90°.

Q: The diagonal of a square is 10 cm. Its side is
- 5 cm
* $5\sqrt{2}$ cm
- $10\sqrt{2}$ cm
- 20 cm
W: $2s^2 = 100$, so $s = \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$.
