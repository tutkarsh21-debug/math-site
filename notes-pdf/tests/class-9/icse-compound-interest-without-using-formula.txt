Q: The compound interest on Rs 5000 at 10% per year for 2 years is
- Rs 1000
* Rs 1050
- Rs 1100
- Rs 500
W: First year Rs 500; second year 10% of Rs 5500 = Rs 550.

Q: Rs 10,000 is invested at 8% per year compound interest. The amount after 2 years is
- Rs 11,600
* Rs 11,664
- Rs 11,680
- Rs 11,800
W: After one year Rs 10,800; after two years $10800 + 864 = 11664$.

Q: Rs 8000 is invested at 5% per year compound interest. The interest for the second year is
- Rs 400
* Rs 420
- Rs 441
- Rs 820
W: The principal for the second year is Rs 8400, and 5% of it is Rs 420.

Q: The difference between the compound and simple interest on Rs 2000 at 10% per year for 2 years is
- Rs 10
* Rs 20
- Rs 40
- Rs 200
W: It is the interest on the first year's interest: 10% of Rs 200.

Q: The compound interest on Rs 4000 for 1 year at 10% per year, compounded half-yearly, is
- Rs 400
* Rs 410
- Rs 420
- Rs 440
W: Two half-years at 5%: Rs 200, then 5% of Rs 4200 = Rs 210.

Q: For the first year, the compound interest and the simple interest on the same sum at the same rate (compounded yearly) are
* equal
- in the ratio 2 : 1
- different, with C.I. greater
- different, with S.I. greater
W: The principal is the same in the first year. The difference begins in the second year.

Q: A sum amounts to Rs 6050 in 2 years and to Rs 6655 in 3 years at compound interest. The rate per year is
- 5%
- 8%
* 10%
- 12%
W: The third year's interest is Rs 605 on Rs 6050, which is 10%.

Q: The compound interest on a sum for two successive years is Rs 400 and Rs 440. The rate per year is
- 4%
- 8%
* 10%
- 40%
W: The extra Rs 40 is one year's interest on Rs 400.

Q: A man borrows Rs 10,000 at 10% per year compound interest and repays Rs 4000 at the end of the first year. The principal for the second year is
- Rs 6000
- Rs 6600
* Rs 7000
- Rs 11,000
W: The amount after one year is Rs 11,000, and $11000 - 4000 = 7000$.

Q: The difference between the compound and simple interest on Rs 5000 at 4% per year for 2 years is
- Rs 4
* Rs 8
- Rs 16
- Rs 20
W: The first year's interest is Rs 200, and 4% of Rs 200 is Rs 8.
