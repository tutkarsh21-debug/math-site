Q: Class A has 20 students with mean marks 60, and class B has 30 students with mean marks 70. The mean for all 50 students is
- 64
- 65
* 66
- 67
W: $\dfrac{20 \times 60 + 30 \times 70}{50} = \dfrac{3300}{50} = 66$.

Q: The average of two group averages equals the overall average only when
- the groups have the same average
* the groups have the same size
- the groups are large
- always
W: Otherwise the larger group must be given more weight.

Q: 2 kg of rice at Rs 40 per kg is mixed with 3 kg of rice at Rs 50 per kg. The cost of the mixture per kg is
- Rs 44
- Rs 45
* Rs 46
- Rs 48
W: $\dfrac{2 \times 40 + 3 \times 50}{5} = \dfrac{230}{5} = 46$.

Q: A test has weight 40% and an exam has weight 60%. A student scores 70 in the test and 80 in the exam. The final score is
- 74
- 75
* 76
- 78
W: $0.4 \times 70 + 0.6 \times 80 = 28 + 48$.

Q: The mean of 9 values is 20. A tenth value, 30, is added. The new mean is
- 20
* 21
- 23
- 25
W: $\dfrac{180 + 30}{10} = 21$.

Q: The mean of 6 values is 15. One value, 25, is removed. The mean of the remaining values is
- 10
- 12
* 13
- 14
W: $\dfrac{90 - 25}{5} = 13$.

Q: In a 100% stacked bar chart, every bar
- has a different total height
* has the same total height
- shows only one category
- starts at a different level
W: Each bar is the whole (100%), divided into parts by percentage.

Q: A stacked bar chart shows
- only the totals
- only one part of each total
* each total and the parts that make it up
- how one quantity changes continuously over time
W: The segments of a bar are the parts, and the full bar is the total.

Q: A car travels 60 km at 30 km/h and the next 60 km at 60 km/h. Its average speed for the whole journey is
- 45 km/h
* 40 km/h
- 50 km/h
- 35 km/h
W: The total time is $2 + 1 = 3$ hours for 120 km. The two speeds are weighted by the time spent, not averaged directly.

Q: The weighted mean of 10 with weight 1 and 20 with weight 3 is
- 15
- 16
* 17.5
- 18.5
W: $\dfrac{10 \times 1 + 20 \times 3}{4} = \dfrac{70}{4} = 17.5$.
