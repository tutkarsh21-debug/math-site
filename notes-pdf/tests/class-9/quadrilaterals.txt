Q: The segment joining the mid-points of two sides of a triangle is
- equal to the third side
* parallel to the third side and half of it
- perpendicular to the third side
- twice the third side
W: This is the Midpoint Theorem.

Q: In triangle ABC, D and E are the mid-points of AB and AC. If BC = 14 cm, then DE is
- 14 cm
* 7 cm
- 28 cm
- 3.5 cm
W: DE is half of BC.

Q: The centroid of a triangle divides each median in the ratio
- 1 : 1
* 2 : 1
- 3 : 1
- 3 : 2
W: The longer part is towards the vertex.

Q: AD is a median of triangle ABC, G is the centroid and AD = 9 cm. Then AG is
- 3 cm
- 4.5 cm
* 6 cm
- 9 cm
W: $AG = \dfrac{2}{3} \times 9 = 6$.

Q: The figure formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of any quadrilateral, in order, is a
- rectangle
- rhombus
* parallelogram
- trapezium
W: Each pair of opposite sides is parallel to a diagonal and half of it.

Q: A quadrilateral is a parallelogram if
- one pair of opposite sides is equal
- its diagonals are equal
* one pair of opposite sides is equal and parallel
- one pair of adjacent sides is equal
W: This is one of the standard tests for a parallelogram.

Q: In parallelogram ABCD, $\angle A = (2x + 10)°$ and $\angle C = (3x - 20)°$. The value of $x$ is
- 20
* 30
- 38
- 50
W: Opposite angles are equal: $2x + 10 = 3x - 20$.

Q: The diagonals AC = 10 cm and BD = 16 cm of a parallelogram meet at O. Then AO + BO is
- 26 cm
* 13 cm
- 6 cm
- 8 cm
W: The diagonals bisect each other: $5 + 8 = 13$.

Q: The figure formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of a rectangle is a
- rectangle
* rhombus
- square
- kite
W: Its sides are each half of a diagonal, and the diagonals of a rectangle are equal.

Q: Which regular polygon cannot tile the plane by itself?
- Equilateral triangle
- Square
* Regular pentagon
- Regular hexagon
W: The interior angle 108° does not divide 360°.
