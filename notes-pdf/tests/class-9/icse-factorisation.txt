Q: $a^2 - ab + ac - bc$ is equal to
- $(a + b)(a - c)$
* $(a - b)(a + c)$
- $(a - b)(a - c)$
- $(a + b)(a + c)$
W: $a(a - b) + c(a - b)$.

Q: $6x^2 + 5x - 6$ is equal to
- $(2x - 3)(3x + 2)$
* $(2x + 3)(3x - 2)$
- $(6x - 1)(x + 6)$
- $(3x + 3)(2x - 2)$
W: Split $5x$ as $9x - 4x$: $3x(2x + 3) - 2(2x + 3)$.

Q: $x^2 - 5x + 6$ is equal to
- $(x + 2)(x + 3)$
* $(x - 2)(x - 3)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 6)$
- $(x - 6)(x + 1)$
W: Two numbers with sum $-5$ and product 6 are $-2$ and $-3$.

Q: $9a^2 - 49b^2$ is equal to
- $(3a - 7b)^2$
* $(3a - 7b)(3a + 7b)$
- $(9a - 49b)(a + b)$
- $(3a + 7b)^2$
W: $(3a)^2 - (7b)^2$.

Q: $8x^3 + 27$ is equal to
- $(2x + 3)^3$
- $(2x + 3)(4x^2 + 6x + 9)$
* $(2x + 3)(4x^2 - 6x + 9)$
- $(2x - 3)(4x^2 + 6x + 9)$
W: $a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$ with $a = 2x$, $b = 3$.

Q: $a^3 - 64$ is equal to
- $(a - 4)^3$
* $(a - 4)(a^2 + 4a + 16)$
- $(a - 4)(a^2 - 4a + 16)$
- $(a + 4)(a^2 - 4a + 16)$
W: $a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)$ with $b = 4$.

Q: $x^2 - y^2 - 2y - 1$ is equal to
- $(x - y - 1)^2$
* $(x - y - 1)(x + y + 1)$
- $(x + y - 1)(x - y + 1)$
- $(x - y)(x + y - 1)$
W: $x^2 - (y + 1)^2$, a difference of two squares.

Q: $2x^2 - 7x + 3$ is equal to
- $(2x - 3)(x - 1)$
* $(2x - 1)(x - 3)$
- $(2x + 1)(x + 3)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 3)$
W: Split $-7x$ as $-6x - x$.

Q: The complete factorisation of $12x^2 - 27$ is
- $3(4x^2 - 9)$
* $3(2x - 3)(2x + 3)$
- $(6x - 9)(2x + 3)$
- $3(2x - 3)^2$
W: Take out 3, then use the difference of two squares.

Q: $x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x^2} - 2$ is equal to
- $\left(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2$
* $\left(x - \dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2$
- $\left(x - \dfrac{1}{x}\right)\left(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)$
- $\left(x - 1\right)^2$
W: $x^2 - 2 \cdot x \cdot \dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{x^2}$.
