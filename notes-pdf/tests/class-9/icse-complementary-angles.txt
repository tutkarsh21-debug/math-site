Q: $\sin(90° - \theta)$ is equal to
- $\sin\theta$
* $\cos\theta$
- $\tan\theta$
- $\operatorname{cosec}\theta$
W: The sine of an angle is the cosine of its complement.

Q: $\dfrac{\sin 35°}{\cos 55°}$ is equal to
- 0
* 1
- $\dfrac{7}{11}$
- $\tan 35°$
W: $\cos 55° = \sin 35°$, because $55° + 35° = 90°$.

Q: $\tan 20° \tan 70°$ is equal to
- 0
* 1
- $\tan 90°$
- $\tan^2 20°$
W: $\tan 70° = \cot 20°$, and $\tan\theta \cot\theta = 1$.

Q: $\sin^2 40° + \sin^2 50°$ is equal to
- 0
* 1
- 2
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: $\sin 50° = \cos 40°$, and $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$.

Q: $\sec(90° - \theta)$ is equal to
- $\sec\theta$
- $\cos\theta$
* $\operatorname{cosec}\theta$
- $\cot\theta$
W: The secant of an angle is the cosecant of its complement.

Q: $\sin 70°$ expressed as a ratio of an angle less than 45° is
- $\sin 20°$
* $\cos 20°$
- $\tan 20°$
- $\cos 70°$
W: $70° = 90° - 20°$.

Q: If $\sin 3A = \cos 2A$, where $3A$ and $2A$ are acute, then A is
- 15°
* 18°
- 30°
- 45°
W: $3A + 2A = 90°$.

Q: $\cos 48° - \sin 42°$ is equal to
* 0
- 1
- $\cos 6°$
- $-1$
W: $\cos 48° = \sin 42°$.

Q: The value of $\tan 1° \tan 2° \tan 3° \cdots \tan 89°$ is
- 0
* 1
- 89
- not defined
W: Pair $\tan 1°$ with $\tan 89°$, and so on: each pair has product 1, and $\tan 45° = 1$.

Q: If $\tan\theta = \cot(\theta + 30°)$, where both angles are acute, then $\theta$ is
- 15°
* 30°
- 45°
- 60°
W: $\theta + \theta + 30° = 90°$.
