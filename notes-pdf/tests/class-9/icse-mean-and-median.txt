Q: The mean of 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14 is
- 8
* 10
- 12
- 50
W: The sum is 50, and $50 \div 5 = 10$.

Q: The mean of 5 numbers is 18. If each number is increased by 3, the new mean is
- 18
* 21
- 33
- 54
W: Adding the same number to every value adds it to the mean.

Q: The mean of 10 numbers was found to be 15. Later it was noticed that 24 had been read as 42. The correct mean is
- 16.8
* 13.2
- 14.2
- 15
W: The correct total is $150 - 42 + 24 = 132$.

Q: The median of 11, 7, 15, 9, 13, 5, 17 is
- 9
* 11
- 13
- 15
W: In order: 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17. The middle value is 11.

Q: The median of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 is
- 6
* 7
- 8
- 6.5
W: The mean of the two middle values: $\dfrac{6 + 8}{2}$.

Q: The mean of $x$, $x + 2$, $x + 4$, $x + 6$ and $x + 8$ is 11. The value of $x$ is
- 5
* 7
- 9
- 11
W: The mean is the middle term $x + 4$.

Q: The mean mark of 20 boys is 50 and that of 30 girls is 60. The mean mark of all 50 students is
- 55
* 56
- 57
- 58
W: $\dfrac{1000 + 1800}{50} = 56$.

Q: The sum of the deviations of a set of values from their mean is
* 0
- 1
- the mean
- the number of values
W: The positive and negative deviations cancel.

Q: The values 1, 2 and 3 occur with frequencies 2, 3 and 5. The mean is
- 2
* 2.3
- 2.5
- 3
W: $\dfrac{2 + 6 + 15}{10} = 2.3$.

Q: The median of the first 10 natural numbers is
- 5
* 5.5
- 6
- 10
W: The two middle values are 5 and 6.
