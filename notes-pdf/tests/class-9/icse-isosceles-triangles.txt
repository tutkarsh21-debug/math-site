Q: In triangle ABC, AB = AC and $\angle B = 65°$. Then $\angle A$ is
- 65°
* 50°
- 115°
- 57.5°
W: $\angle C = 65°$ too, and $180° - 130° = 50°$.

Q: The vertical angle of an isosceles triangle is 40°. Each base angle is
- 40°
- 60°
* 70°
- 140°
W: $\dfrac{180° - 40°}{2} = 70°$.

Q: Each angle of an equilateral triangle is
- 45°
* 60°
- 90°
- 120°
W: The three equal angles add up to 180°.

Q: In triangle ABC, AB = AC and the exterior angle at C is 110°. Then $\angle A$ is
- 70°
* 40°
- 55°
- 110°
W: $\angle C = 70° = \angle B$, so $\angle A = 180° - 140°$.

Q: In an isosceles right-angled triangle, each acute angle is
- 30°
* 45°
- 60°
- 90°
W: The two equal angles add up to 90°.

Q: In triangle ABC, $\angle A = \angle B$. Then
- AB = AC
* BC = AC
- AB = BC
- all three sides are equal
W: Sides opposite equal angles are equal: BC is opposite A and AC is opposite B.

Q: In an isosceles triangle, the bisector of the vertical angle
- is parallel to the base
* bisects the base at right angles
- is equal to the base
- passes through a base vertex
W: The two triangles formed are congruent by SAS.

Q: In triangle ABC, AB = AC and $\angle A = 2\angle B$. Then $\angle A$ is
- 45°
- 60°
* 90°
- 120°
W: $\angle B = \angle C$, so $4\angle B = 180°$ and $\angle B = 45°$.

Q: In triangle ABC, AB = AC, AD is perpendicular to BC and BC = 10 cm. Then BD is
- 10 cm
* 5 cm
- 2.5 cm
- 20 cm
W: The perpendicular from the vertex of an isosceles triangle bisects the base.

Q: A triangle with angles 50°, 80° and 50° is
- equilateral
* isosceles
- scalene
- right-angled
W: Two equal angles mean two equal sides.
