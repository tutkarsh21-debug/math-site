Q: The value of $\sin 30°$ is
* $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- 1
W: In a 30°-60°-90° triangle the side opposite 30° is half the hypotenuse.

Q: The value of $\tan 60°$ is
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
- 1
* $\sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
W: $\tan 60° = \dfrac{\sin 60°}{\cos 60°} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}/2}{1/2}$.

Q: $\cos 0° + \sin 90°$ is equal to
- 0
- 1
* 2
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: Both values are 1.

Q: $\sin 45° \times \cos 45°$ is equal to
- 1
* $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- 2
W: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \times \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: $\sin 60° \cos 30° + \cos 60° \sin 30°$ is equal to
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
* 1
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
W: $\dfrac{3}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} = 1$.

Q: $2 \sin 30° \cos 30°$ is equal to
- $\sin 30°$
* $\sin 60°$
- $\cos 60°$
- $\tan 60°$
W: $2 \times \dfrac{1}{2} \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

Q: $\tan^2 60° - 2\sin^2 45°$ is equal to
- 1
* 2
- 3
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
W: $3 - 2 \times \dfrac{1}{2} = 2$.

Q: If $\sin\theta = \cos\theta$ and $\theta$ is acute, then $\theta$ is
- 30°
* 45°
- 60°
- 90°
W: Both are $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ at 45°.

Q: If $2\cos\theta = 1$ and $\theta$ is acute, then $\theta$ is
- 30°
- 45°
* 60°
- 0°
W: $\cos\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: If $\tan 3x = 1$ and $3x$ is acute, then $x$ is
- 45°
- 30°
* 15°
- 10°
W: $3x = 45°$.
