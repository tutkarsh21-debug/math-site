Q: D and E are the mid-points of sides AB and AC of triangle ABC. If BC = 18 cm, then DE is
- 18 cm
* 9 cm
- 6 cm
- 36 cm
W: DE is parallel to BC and half of it.

Q: A line through the mid-point of one side of a triangle, parallel to a second side,
- is perpendicular to the third side
* bisects the third side
- is equal to the third side
- passes through the opposite vertex
W: This is the converse of the mid-point theorem.

Q: The sides of a triangle are 8 cm, 10 cm and 12 cm. The perimeter of the triangle formed by joining the mid-points of its sides is
- 30 cm
* 15 cm
- 10 cm
- 7.5 cm
W: Each side of the new triangle is half of a side of the original.

Q: The area of the triangle formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of a triangle is what fraction of the area of the original triangle?
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
* $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
W: The three mid-point segments divide the triangle into four congruent triangles.

Q: The figure formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of a rhombus, in order, is a
- rhombus
* rectangle
- square
- trapezium
W: Its sides are parallel to the diagonals of the rhombus, which are perpendicular.

Q: The parallel sides of a trapezium are 10 cm and 16 cm. The segment joining the mid-points of the non-parallel sides is
- 6 cm
* 13 cm
- 26 cm
- 8 cm
W: It equals half the sum of the parallel sides.

Q: If three parallel lines make equal intercepts on one transversal, then on any other transversal they make
- no intercepts
* equal intercepts
- intercepts in the ratio 1 : 2
- unequal intercepts
W: This is the equal intercept theorem.

Q: In triangle ABC, D is the mid-point of AB and DE, parallel to BC, meets AC at E. If AC = 12 cm, then AE is
- 12 cm
* 6 cm
- 4 cm
- 3 cm
W: By the converse of the mid-point theorem, E is the mid-point of AC.

Q: The diagonals of a quadrilateral are 10 cm and 14 cm. The perimeter of the figure formed by joining the mid-points of its sides is
- 12 cm
* 24 cm
- 48 cm
- 35 cm
W: The figure is a parallelogram with sides 5 cm and 7 cm.

Q: The figure formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of a square is a
- rectangle that is not a square
- rhombus that is not a square
* square
- trapezium
W: The diagonals of a square are equal and perpendicular, so the new figure has equal sides and right angles.
