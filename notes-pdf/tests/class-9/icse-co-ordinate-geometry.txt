Q: The abscissa of the point $(-4, 7)$ is
- 7
* $-4$
- 4
- 3
W: The abscissa is the $x$-co-ordinate.

Q: The point $(0, -5)$ lies
- on the $x$-axis
* on the $y$-axis
- in the third quadrant
- in the fourth quadrant
W: Its $x$-co-ordinate is 0.

Q: The slope of the line $y = 3x - 2$ is
- $-2$
- 2
* 3
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
W: In $y = mx + c$, the slope is $m$.

Q: The $y$-intercept of the line $2y = 4x + 6$ is
- 6
- 4
* 3
- 2
W: Divide by 2 first: $y = 2x + 3$.

Q: A line has inclination 45°. Its slope is
- 0
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
* 1
- $\sqrt{3}$
W: Slope $= \tan 45°$.

Q: A line has slope $\sqrt{3}$. Its inclination is
- 30°
- 45°
* 60°
- 90°
W: $\tan 60° = \sqrt{3}$.

Q: The graph of $x = 4$ is a line
- parallel to the $x$-axis
* parallel to the $y$-axis
- through the origin
- with slope 4
W: Every point on it has $x$-co-ordinate 4.

Q: Which point lies on the line $y = 2x + 1$?
- $(1, 2)$
* $(2, 5)$
- $(3, 6)$
- $(0, 2)$
W: $2 \times 2 + 1 = 5$.

Q: The equation of the line with slope 2 and $y$-intercept $-3$ is
- $y = -3x + 2$
* $y = 2x - 3$
- $y = 2x + 3$
- $x = 2y - 3$
W: Use $y = mx + c$ with $m = 2$ and $c = -3$.

Q: The slope of a line parallel to the $x$-axis is
* 0
- 1
- not defined
- $-1$
W: Its inclination is 0°, and $\tan 0° = 0$.
