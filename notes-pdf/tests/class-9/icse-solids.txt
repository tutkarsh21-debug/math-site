Q: The volume of a cuboid 10 cm by 8 cm by 5 cm is
- 23 cubic cm
- 340 cubic cm
* 400 cubic cm
- 800 cubic cm
W: $10 \times 8 \times 5$.

Q: The total surface area of a cube of edge 4 cm is
- 16 sq cm
- 64 sq cm
* 96 sq cm
- 48 sq cm
W: $6 \times 4^2 = 96$.

Q: The diagonal of a cuboid 3 cm by 4 cm by 12 cm is
- 12 cm
* 13 cm
- 19 cm
- 15 cm
W: $\sqrt{9 + 16 + 144} = \sqrt{169}$.

Q: The diagonal of a cube of edge 5 cm is
- 5 cm
- $5\sqrt{2}$ cm
* $5\sqrt{3}$ cm
- 15 cm
W: The diagonal of a cube of edge $a$ is $a\sqrt{3}$.

Q: One litre is equal to
- 10 cubic cm
- 100 cubic cm
* 1000 cubic cm
- 1 cubic m
W: A cube of edge 10 cm holds one litre.

Q: A closed wooden box measures 20 cm by 16 cm by 12 cm on the outside and the wood is 1 cm thick. Its internal volume is
- 3840 cubic cm
* 2520 cubic cm
- 3135 cubic cm
- 1320 cubic cm
W: Each inner dimension is 2 cm less: $18 \times 14 \times 10$.

Q: A metal bar has a uniform cross-section of area 15 sq cm and is 2 m long. Its volume is
- 30 cubic cm
- 300 cubic cm
* 3000 cubic cm
- 30,000 cubic cm
W: Volume = area of cross-section × length $= 15 \times 200$.

Q: Water flows through a pipe of cross-section 5 sq cm at 40 cm per second. The volume of water that flows out in one minute is
- 200 cubic cm
- 2 litres
* 12 litres
- 120 litres
W: $5 \times 40 = 200$ cubic cm each second, and $200 \times 60 = 12000$ cubic cm.

Q: Three cubes of edge 5 cm are joined end to end. The total surface area of the cuboid formed is
- 450 sq cm
* 350 sq cm
- 375 sq cm
- 300 sq cm
W: The cuboid is 15 cm by 5 cm by 5 cm: $2(75 + 25 + 75) = 350$.

Q: The volume of a cube is 512 cubic cm. Its total surface area is
- 64 sq cm
- 192 sq cm
* 384 sq cm
- 512 sq cm
W: The edge is 8 cm, and $6 \times 64 = 384$.
