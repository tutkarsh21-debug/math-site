Q: The point $(-3, 5)$ lies in which quadrant?
- I
* II
- III
- IV
W: A negative $x$ and a positive $y$ place the point in the second quadrant.

Q: Every point on the $x$-axis has
- $x$-coordinate 0
* $y$-coordinate 0
- equal coordinates
- both coordinates 0
W: Points on the $x$-axis are of the form $(a, 0)$.

Q: The distance of the point $(4, -7)$ from the $x$-axis is
- 4 units
* 7 units
- 11 units
- 3 units
W: The distance from the $x$-axis is the size of the $y$-coordinate.

Q: The distance between $(2, 3)$ and $(5, 7)$ is
- 3 units
- 4 units
* 5 units
- 7 units
W: $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$.

Q: The distance of $(-6, 8)$ from the origin is
- 2 units
- 14 units
* 10 units
- 100 units
W: $\sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$.

Q: The reflection of $(3, -4)$ in the $x$-axis is
- $(-3, -4)$
* $(3, 4)$
- $(-3, 4)$
- $(-4, 3)$
W: Reflection in the $x$-axis changes the sign of the $y$-coordinate only.

Q: The reflection of $(-2, 5)$ in the $y$-axis is
* $(2, 5)$
- $(-2, -5)$
- $(2, -5)$
- $(5, -2)$
W: Reflection in the $y$-axis changes the sign of the $x$-coordinate only.

Q: The distance between $(-3, 2)$ and $(5, 2)$ is
- 2 units
* 8 units
- 4 units
- 10 units
W: The points are on a line parallel to the $x$-axis, so the distance is $5 - (-3) = 8$.

Q: The mid-point of the segment joining $(1, 4)$ and $(7, -2)$ is
- $(3, 3)$
- $(8, 2)$
* $(4, 1)$
- $(6, -6)$
W: $\left(\dfrac{1 + 7}{2}, \dfrac{4 - 2}{2}\right) = (4, 1)$.

Q: For the points A$(1, 2)$, B$(4, 6)$ and C$(7, 10)$, AB = 5, BC = 5 and AC = 10. The points
* are collinear
- form an isosceles triangle
- form a right triangle
- form an equilateral triangle
W: AB + BC = AC, so B lies on the segment AC.
