Q: How many circles can pass through three points that are not on one line?
- None
* Exactly one
- Exactly two
- Infinitely many
W: The perpendicular bisectors of the segments joining the points meet at one point, the centre.

Q: The perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord
- is equal to the chord
* bisects the chord
- is parallel to the chord
- passes through an end of the chord
W: The two right triangles formed are congruent.

Q: A chord of length 16 cm is drawn in a circle of radius 10 cm. Its distance from the centre is
- 4 cm
* 6 cm
- 8 cm
- 12 cm
W: Half the chord is 8 cm, and $\sqrt{10^2 - 8^2} = 6$.

Q: An arc subtends an angle of 100° at the centre. The angle it subtends at a point on the remaining part of the circle is
- 100°
- 200°
* 50°
- 80°
W: The angle at the centre is double the angle at the circumference.

Q: The angle in a semicircle is
- 45°
- 60°
* 90°
- 180°
W: It is half of the angle 180° at the centre.

Q: ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral with $\angle A = 75°$. Then $\angle C$ is
- 75°
- 15°
* 105°
- 285°
W: Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to 180°.

Q: Equal chords of a circle are
- parallel
- perpendicular
* equidistant from the centre
- diameters
W: Chords of equal length lie at the same distance from the centre.

Q: Angles in the same segment of a circle are
- complementary
- supplementary
* equal
- right angles
W: Each is half of the same angle at the centre.

Q: A chord is at a distance of 5 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 13 cm. Its length is
- 12 cm
- 18 cm
* 24 cm
- 26 cm
W: Half the chord is $\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12$ cm.

Q: The longest chord of a circle is its
- radius
* diameter
- arc
- tangent
W: A chord through the centre is the longest.
