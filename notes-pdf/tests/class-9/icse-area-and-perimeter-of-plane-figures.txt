Q: The area of a triangle with sides 5 cm, 12 cm and 13 cm is
- 60 sq cm
* 30 sq cm
- 32.5 sq cm
- 78 sq cm
W: It is right-angled ($25 + 144 = 169$), so the area is $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 12$.

Q: The area of an equilateral triangle of side 6 cm is
- $6\sqrt{3}$ sq cm
* $9\sqrt{3}$ sq cm
- $18\sqrt{3}$ sq cm
- $36\sqrt{3}$ sq cm
W: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 36 = 9\sqrt{3}$.

Q: The perimeter of a rectangle is 46 cm and its length is 15 cm. Its area is
- 345 sq cm
* 120 sq cm
- 240 sq cm
- 465 sq cm
W: The breadth is $23 - 15 = 8$ cm, and $15 \times 8 = 120$.

Q: The area of a rhombus is 120 sq cm and one diagonal is 24 cm. The other diagonal is
- 5 cm
* 10 cm
- 20 cm
- 96 cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 24 \times d = 120$.

Q: The circumference of a circle is 44 cm. Its radius is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 3.5 cm
* 7 cm
- 14 cm
- 22 cm
W: $2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times r = 44$.

Q: The area of a circle of diameter 14 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 44 sq cm
* 154 sq cm
- 308 sq cm
- 616 sq cm
W: The radius is 7 cm, and $\dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 = 154$.

Q: A rectangular field 30 m by 20 m has a path 5 m wide all around it on the outside. The area of the path is
- 250 sq m
- 500 sq m
* 600 sq m
- 1200 sq m
W: The outer rectangle is 40 m by 30 m: $1200 - 600 = 600$.

Q: The parallel sides of a trapezium are 20 cm and 12 cm, and its height is 9 cm. Its area is
- 288 sq cm
* 144 sq cm
- 108 sq cm
- 72 sq cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 32 \times 9 = 144$.

Q: A wheel has radius 35 cm. The distance it covers in 100 revolutions is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 110 m
* 220 m
- 385 m
- 2200 m
W: One revolution covers $2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 35 = 220$ cm, so 100 revolutions cover 22,000 cm.

Q: The area of an isosceles triangle with sides 10 cm, 10 cm and 12 cm is
- 60 sq cm
* 48 sq cm
- 50 sq cm
- 96 sq cm
W: The altitude to the 12 cm side is $\sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$ cm, and $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 8 = 48$.
