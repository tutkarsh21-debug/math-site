Q: The circumference of a circle of radius 14 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 44 cm
* 88 cm
- 154 cm
- 616 cm
W: $2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 14 = 88$.

Q: An arc of a circle of radius 21 cm subtends an angle of 60° at the centre. Its length is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 11 cm
* 22 cm
- 44 cm
- 66 cm
W: $\dfrac{60}{360} \times 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 21 = 22$.

Q: The area of a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm and 15 cm is
- 42 sq cm
* 84 sq cm
- 91 sq cm
- 105 sq cm
W: $s = 21$, and $\sqrt{21 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6} = \sqrt{7056} = 84$.

Q: For a triangle with sides 7 cm, 8 cm and 9 cm, the semi-perimeter $s$ is
- 8 cm
* 12 cm
- 24 cm
- 16 cm
W: $s = \dfrac{7 + 8 + 9}{2} = 12$.

Q: The area of an equilateral triangle of side 4 cm is
- $2\sqrt{3}$ sq cm
* $4\sqrt{3}$ sq cm
- $8\sqrt{3}$ sq cm
- 8 sq cm
W: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 4^2 = 4\sqrt{3}$.

Q: The area of a sector of radius 6 cm and central angle 60° is
- $\pi$ sq cm
- $2\pi$ sq cm
* $6\pi$ sq cm
- $12\pi$ sq cm
W: $\dfrac{60}{360} \times \pi \times 36 = 6\pi$.

Q: Brahmagupta's formula gives the area of
- any quadrilateral
* a cyclic quadrilateral
- a triangle
- a sector
W: For a cyclic quadrilateral with sides $a, b, c, d$ and semi-perimeter $s$, the area is $\sqrt{(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)(s-d)}$.

Q: A chord of a circle of radius 14 cm subtends a right angle at the centre. The area of the minor segment is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 154 sq cm
- 98 sq cm
* 56 sq cm
- 252 sq cm
W: Sector $= \dfrac{1}{4} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 196 = 154$ and triangle $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 14 = 98$. Segment $= 154 - 98$.

Q: A square has the same area as a rectangle 9 cm by 4 cm. The side of the square is
- 5 cm
* 6 cm
- 6.5 cm
- 13 cm
W: The area is 36 sq cm, and $\sqrt{36} = 6$.

Q: The area of a circle of radius 7 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 44 sq cm
- 49 sq cm
* 154 sq cm
- 308 sq cm
