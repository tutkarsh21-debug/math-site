Q: The sum of the interior angles of a pentagon is
- 360°
* 540°
- 720°
- 900°
W: $(2n - 4)$ right angles $= 6 \times 90°$.

Q: Each exterior angle of a regular hexagon is
- 30°
- 45°
* 60°
- 120°
W: $360° \div 6 = 60°$.

Q: Two adjacent angles of a parallelogram are in the ratio 2 : 3. The larger angle is
- 72°
* 108°
- 120°
- 144°
W: $5x = 180°$ gives $x = 36°$, and $3x = 108°$.

Q: In rhombus ABCD, $\angle ABC = 120°$. Then $\angle ABD$ is
- 30°
* 60°
- 90°
- 120°
W: A diagonal of a rhombus bisects the angles at its ends.

Q: The diagonals of rectangle ABCD meet at O and $\angle AOB = 60°$. Then $\angle OAB$ is
- 30°
- 45°
* 60°
- 120°
W: OA = OB, so triangle OAB is isosceles with base angles $\dfrac{180° - 60°}{2}$.

Q: A parallelogram whose diagonals are equal is a
- rhombus
* rectangle
- kite
- trapezium
W: Equal diagonals make each angle a right angle.

Q: A parallelogram whose diagonals are perpendicular is a
* rhombus
- rectangle
- trapezium
- kite only
W: Perpendicular diagonals make all four sides equal.

Q: The sum of the interior angles of a polygon is 1080°. The number of sides is
- 6
- 7
* 8
- 10
W: $(n - 2) \times 180° = 1080°$ gives $n - 2 = 6$.

Q: In a regular polygon, each interior angle is four times the exterior angle. The number of sides is
- 5
- 8
* 10
- 12
W: The exterior angle is $180° \div 5 = 36°$, and $360° \div 36° = 10$.

Q: In a kite,
- both pairs of opposite angles are equal
* one pair of opposite angles is equal
- all four angles are equal
- no two angles are equal
W: The angles between the unequal sides are equal.
