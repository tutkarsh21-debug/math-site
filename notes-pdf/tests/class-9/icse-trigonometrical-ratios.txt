Q: In triangle ABC, $\angle B = 90°$, AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm and AC = 5 cm. Then $\sin A$ is
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
* $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
W: $\sin A = \dfrac{\text{side opposite A}}{\text{hypotenuse}} = \dfrac{BC}{AC}$.

Q: If $\sin\theta = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\theta$ is acute, then $\cos\theta$ is
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
* $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
W: The third side is $\sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$.

Q: If $\tan\theta = \dfrac{5}{12}$ and $\theta$ is acute, then $\sec\theta$ is
- $\dfrac{12}{13}$
- $\dfrac{13}{5}$
* $\dfrac{13}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{13}$
W: The hypotenuse is 13, and $\sec\theta = \dfrac{\text{hypotenuse}}{\text{base}}$.

Q: The reciprocal of $\sin\theta$ is
- $\cos\theta$
- $\sec\theta$
* $\operatorname{cosec}\theta$
- $\cot\theta$
W: $\operatorname{cosec}\theta = \dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}$.

Q: $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ is equal to
- $\cot\theta$
* $\tan\theta$
- $\sec\theta$
- 1
W: Perpendicular over hypotenuse divided by base over hypotenuse gives perpendicular over base.

Q: If $\cos\theta = \dfrac{12}{13}$ and $\theta$ is acute, then $\tan\theta$ is
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
* $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{13}$
- $\dfrac{13}{5}$
W: The perpendicular is $\sqrt{169 - 144} = 5$.

Q: For any acute angle $\theta$, $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta$ is equal to
- 0
* 1
- 2
- $\tan^2\theta$
W: It follows from the Pythagoras theorem.

Q: In triangle ABC, $\angle C = 90°$, AC = 8 cm and BC = 15 cm. Then $\cos B$ is
- $\dfrac{8}{17}$
* $\dfrac{15}{17}$
- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $\dfrac{15}{8}$
W: AB = 17 cm, and the side adjacent to B is BC.

Q: If $\tan A = 1$ and A is acute, then $\sin A$ is
- 1
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
* $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $\sqrt{2}$
W: The legs are equal, say 1 and 1, so the hypotenuse is $\sqrt{2}$.

Q: If $3\tan\theta = 4$, then $\dfrac{3\sin\theta + 2\cos\theta}{3\sin\theta - 2\cos\theta}$ is
- 2
* 3
- 4
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
W: Divide top and bottom by $\cos\theta$: $\dfrac{3\tan\theta + 2}{3\tan\theta - 2} = \dfrac{6}{2}$.
