Q: The class mark of the class 10-20 is
- 10
* 15
- 20
- 30
W: $\dfrac{10 + 20}{2} = 15$.

Q: The classes 1-10, 11-20, 21-30 are made continuous. The first class becomes
- 0-10
* 0.5-10.5
- 1-10.5
- 1-11
W: Half of the gap (0.5) is subtracted from each lower limit and added to each upper limit.

Q: The frequencies of four classes are 3, 5, 8 and 4. The cumulative frequencies are
- 3, 5, 8, 4
* 3, 8, 16, 20
- 3, 8, 13, 12
- 20, 17, 12, 4
W: Each cumulative frequency is the running total.

Q: In a histogram, the class intervals are shown along
* the horizontal axis
- the vertical axis
- both axes
- neither axis
W: Frequencies go on the vertical axis.

Q: A frequency polygon is drawn by joining
- the upper corners of the bars
* the mid-points of the tops of the bars of the histogram
- the lower limits of the classes
- the cumulative frequencies
W: The points are plotted at the class marks.

Q: The range of the data 23, 45, 12, 67, 34 is
- 34
- 45
* 55
- 67
W: $67 - 12 = 55$.

Q: The class marks of a distribution are 12, 18, 24, 30. The class size is
- 3
* 6
- 12
- 18
W: The class size is the difference between consecutive class marks.

Q: Data collected by the investigator personally is called
- secondary data
* primary data
- grouped data
- arrayed data
W: Data taken from another source is secondary data.

Q: A class has class mark 25 and class size 10. Its lower limit is
- 15
* 20
- 25
- 30
W: The limits are $25 - 5$ and $25 + 5$.

Q: Which of these is a discrete variable?
- The height of a student
- The weight of a parcel
* The number of students in a class
- The time taken to run 100 m
W: It can take only whole-number values.
