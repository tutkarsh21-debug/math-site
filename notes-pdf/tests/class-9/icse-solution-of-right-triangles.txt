Q: In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is 10 cm and one angle is 30°. The side opposite this angle is
* 5 cm
- $5\sqrt{3}$ cm
- 10 cm
- 20 cm
W: Opposite side = hypotenuse × $\sin 30°$.

Q: In a right triangle, one angle is 45° and the side adjacent to it is 7 cm. The side opposite this angle is
- 3.5 cm
* 7 cm
- $7\sqrt{2}$ cm
- 14 cm
W: $\tan 45° = 1$, so the two legs are equal.

Q: In a right triangle, one angle is 60° and the side adjacent to it is 5 cm. The side opposite this angle is
- 5 cm
- 10 cm
* $5\sqrt{3}$ cm
- $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{3}}$ cm
W: Opposite side = adjacent side × $\tan 60°$.

Q: In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is 12 cm and one angle is 60°. The side adjacent to this angle is
* 6 cm
- $6\sqrt{3}$ cm
- 12 cm
- 24 cm
W: Adjacent side = hypotenuse × $\cos 60°$.

Q: A right triangle has both legs equal to 4 cm. Its acute angles are
- 30° and 60°
* 45° and 45°
- 40° and 50°
- 60° and 60°
W: Equal legs mean equal angles, which add up to 90°.

Q: A triangle has sides 1 cm, $\sqrt{3}$ cm and 2 cm. Its angles are
- 45°, 45°, 90°
* 30°, 60°, 90°
- 60°, 60°, 60°
- 30°, 30°, 120°
W: $1 + 3 = 4$, so it is right-angled, and the side 1 is half the hypotenuse.

Q: The altitude of an equilateral triangle of side 8 cm is
- 4 cm
* $4\sqrt{3}$ cm
- $8\sqrt{3}$ cm
- 8 cm
W: Altitude = side × $\sin 60°$.

Q: In a right triangle, the side opposite an angle of 30° is 6 cm. The hypotenuse is
- 3 cm
- $6\sqrt{3}$ cm
* 12 cm
- $4\sqrt{3}$ cm
W: Hypotenuse = opposite side ÷ $\sin 30°$.

Q: A rhombus has side 10 cm and one angle 60°. Its longer diagonal is
- 10 cm
* $10\sqrt{3}$ cm
- 20 cm
- $5\sqrt{3}$ cm
W: Half the longer diagonal is $10\cos 30° = 5\sqrt{3}$ cm.

Q: In triangle ABC, $\angle B = 90°$, AB = $5\sqrt{3}$ cm and BC = 5 cm. Then $\angle A$ is
* 30°
- 45°
- 60°
- 90°
W: $\tan A = \dfrac{BC}{AB} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.
