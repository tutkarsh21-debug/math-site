Q: $\log_2 8$ is equal to
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 16
W: $2^3 = 8$.

Q: The logarithm of 1 to any base is
* 0
- 1
- the base
- not defined
W: $a^0 = 1$ for every valid base $a$.

Q: $\log_5 5 + \log_5 1$ is equal to
- 0
* 1
- 5
- 6
W: $\log_5 5 = 1$ and $\log_5 1 = 0$.

Q: $\log_{10} 2 + \log_{10} 5$ is equal to
- $\log_{10} 7$
* 1
- 10
- $\log_{10} 2.5$
W: $\log 2 + \log 5 = \log 10 = 1$.

Q: $\log_3 81$ is equal to
- 3
* 4
- 9
- 27
W: $3^4 = 81$.

Q: If $\log_x 16 = 2$, then $x$ is
- 2
* 4
- 8
- 32
W: $x^2 = 16$, and a base must be positive.

Q: $\log \dfrac{m}{n}$ is equal to
- $\dfrac{\log m}{\log n}$
* $\log m - \log n$
- $\log m + \log n$
- $\log (m - n)$
W: This is the quotient law.

Q: $2 \log 3$ is equal to
- $\log 6$
- $\log 5$
* $\log 9$
- $\log 8$
W: $n \log a = \log a^n$, and $3^2 = 9$.

Q: If $\log_{10} 2 = 0.3010$, then $\log_{10} 8$ is
- 0.6020
* 0.9030

W: $\log 8 = 3 \log 2$.

Q: $\log_{10} 0.01$ is equal to
