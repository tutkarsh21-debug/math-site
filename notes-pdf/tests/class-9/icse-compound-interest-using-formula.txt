Q: The formula for the amount at compound interest, compounded yearly, is
- $A = P + \dfrac{PRT}{100}$
* $A = P\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^n$
- $A = P\left(1 + \dfrac{rn}{100}\right)$
- $A = P\left(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^n$
W: The principal is multiplied by $\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)$ once for each year.

Q: The amount on Rs 8000 at 10% per year compound interest for 3 years is
- Rs 10,400
* Rs 10,648
- Rs 10,800
- Rs 11,000
W: $8000 \times 1.1^3 = 8000 \times 1.331$.

Q: Rs 2000 is invested for 2 years at 5% for the first year and 10% for the second. The amount is
- Rs 2300
* Rs 2310
- Rs 2315
- Rs 2205
W: $2000 \times 1.05 \times 1.10 = 2310$.

Q: Interest is 12% per year compounded half-yearly for $1\tfrac{1}{2}$ years. In the formula we use
- $r = 12$, $n = 1.5$
* $r = 6$, $n = 3$
- $r = 24$, $n = 3$
- $r = 6$, $n = 1.5$
W: The rate is halved and the number of periods is doubled.

Q: A sum amounts to Rs 13,310 in 3 years at 10% per year compound interest. The sum is
- Rs 9000
* Rs 10,000
- Rs 11,000
- Rs 12,100
W: $P = 13310 \div 1.331 = 10000$.

Q: Rs 10,000 amounts to Rs 12,100 in 2 years at compound interest. The rate per year is
- 5%
* 10%
- 10.5%
- 21%
W: $\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^2 = 1.21$, so $1 + \dfrac{r}{100} = 1.1$.

Q: In how many years will Rs 8000 amount to Rs 9261 at 5% per year compound interest?
- 2 years
* 3 years
- 4 years
- 5 years
W: $\dfrac{9261}{8000} = \left(\dfrac{21}{20}\right)^3$.

Q: The value of a machine falls by 10% every year. After 2 years it is worth Rs 72,900. Its original value was
- Rs 81,000
* Rs 90,000
- Rs 87,480
- Rs 1,00,000
W: $P \times 0.9^2 = 72900$, so $P = 72900 \div 0.81$.

Q: A town of 50,000 people grows by 4% every year. Its population after 2 years is
- 54,000
* 54,080
- 54,800
- 58,000
W: $50000 \times 1.04^2 = 50000 \times 1.0816$.

Q: When interest at 8% per year is compounded quarterly, the rate for each period is
- 8%
- 4%
* 2%
- 32%
W: There are 4 quarters in a year, so the rate per quarter is $8 \div 4$.
