Q: The total surface area of a cuboid 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm is
- 47 sq cm
- 60 sq cm
* 94 sq cm
- 120 sq cm
W: $2(20 + 12 + 15) = 94$.

Q: The curved surface area of a cone of radius 7 cm and slant height 25 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 275 sq cm
* 550 sq cm
- 704 sq cm
- 1100 sq cm
W: $\pi r l = \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25$.

Q: The volume of a cone of radius 3 cm and height 4 cm is
- $36\pi$ cubic cm
* $12\pi$ cubic cm
- $16\pi$ cubic cm
- $48\pi$ cubic cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi \times 9 \times 4 = 12\pi$.

Q: A cone has radius 5 cm and height 12 cm. Its slant height is
- 7 cm
* 13 cm
- 17 cm
- 60 cm
W: $l = \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} = 13$.

Q: The surface area of a sphere of radius 7 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 154 sq cm
- 308 sq cm
* 616 sq cm
- 1232 sq cm
W: $4\pi r^2 = 4 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 49$.

Q: The volume of a hemisphere of radius 3 cm is
- $9\pi$ cubic cm
* $18\pi$ cubic cm
- $27\pi$ cubic cm
- $36\pi$ cubic cm
W: $\dfrac{2}{3}\pi \times 27 = 18\pi$.

Q: If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its volume becomes
- 2 times
- 4 times
- 6 times
* 8 times
W: Volume depends on $r^3$, and $2^3 = 8$.

Q: The volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 5 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 220 cubic cm
- 385 cubic cm
* 770 cubic cm
- 1540 cubic cm
W: $\dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 5 = 770$.

Q: A stone is dropped into a cylindrical jar of radius 7 cm and the water level rises by 2 cm. The volume of the stone is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 88 cubic cm
- 154 cubic cm
* 308 cubic cm
- 616 cubic cm
W: The stone pushes up a cylinder of water: $\dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 2$.

Q: A pyramid has a square base of side 6 cm and height 4 cm. Its volume is
- 144 cubic cm
- 72 cubic cm
* 48 cubic cm
- 24 cubic cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{3} \times 36 \times 4 = 48$.
