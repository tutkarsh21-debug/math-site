Q: Which of these is an irrational number?
- $0.\overline{3}$
- $\sqrt{16}$
* $0.101001000100001\ldots$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
W: Its decimal expansion neither ends nor repeats.

Q: The rationalising factor of $\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}$ is
- $\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}$
* $\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{10}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
W: $(\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2})(\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{2}) = 5 - 2 = 3$, which is rational.

Q: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}}$ is equal to
- $\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}$
* $\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2}}{5}$
- 1
W: Multiply top and bottom by $\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2}$. The denominator becomes $3 - 2 = 1$.

Q: The decimal form of $\dfrac{7}{8}$ is
- 0.78
* 0.875
- $0.\overline{87}$
- 0.8
W: $8 = 2^3$, so the decimal terminates: $7 \div 8 = 0.875$.

Q: $\sqrt{12} \times \sqrt{3}$ is equal to
- $\sqrt{15}$
* 6
- 36
- $3\sqrt{2}$
W: $\sqrt{36} = 6$.

Q: $(\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{3})(\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{3})$ is equal to
- 8
* 2
- $2\sqrt{15}$
- $\sqrt{2}$
W: $5 - 3 = 2$.

Q: $\sqrt{50}$ in simplest form is
- $2\sqrt{5}$
- $10\sqrt{5}$
* $5\sqrt{2}$
- $25\sqrt{2}$
W: $\sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5\sqrt{2}$.

Q: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ with a rational denominator is
- $\sqrt{2}$
* $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2\sqrt{2}$
W: Multiply top and bottom by $\sqrt{2}$.

Q: $\sqrt{18} + \sqrt{8}$ is equal to
- $\sqrt{26}$
* $5\sqrt{2}$
- $6\sqrt{2}$
- $13\sqrt{2}$
W: $3\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{2} = 5\sqrt{2}$.

Q: $(3 + \sqrt{2})^2$ is equal to
- 11
- $9 + 2\sqrt{2}$
* $11 + 6\sqrt{2}$
- $11 + 3\sqrt{2}$
W: $9 + 6\sqrt{2} + 2$.
