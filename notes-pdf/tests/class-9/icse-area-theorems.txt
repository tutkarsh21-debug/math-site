Q: Parallelograms on the same base and between the same parallels are
- congruent
* equal in area
- similar
- equal in perimeter
W: They have the same base and the same height.

Q: A triangle and a parallelogram are on the same base and between the same parallels. The area of the triangle is
- equal to that of the parallelogram
* half that of the parallelogram
- twice that of the parallelogram
- one-third that of the parallelogram
W: Triangle $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times$ base $\times$ height; parallelogram = base × height.

Q: A parallelogram has area 80 sq cm. A triangle on the same base and between the same parallels has area
- 160 sq cm
- 80 sq cm
* 40 sq cm
- 20 sq cm
W: The triangle is half the parallelogram.

Q: AD is a median of triangle ABC, whose area is 36 sq cm. The area of triangle ABD is
- 9 sq cm
- 12 sq cm
* 18 sq cm
- 36 sq cm
W: A median divides a triangle into two triangles of equal area.

Q: The diagonals of a parallelogram divide it into four triangles that are
- congruent
* equal in area
- right-angled
- equilateral
W: Each diagonal is bisected, so each of the four triangles is a quarter of the parallelogram.

Q: Two triangles on the same base, on the same side of it and with equal areas, lie
- on the same circle
* between the same parallels
- on perpendicular lines
- one inside the other
W: Equal areas on the same base mean equal heights.

Q: A parallelogram has base 12 cm and corresponding height 7.5 cm. Its area is
- 45 sq cm
* 90 sq cm
- 39 sq cm
- 180 sq cm
W: $12 \times 7.5 = 90$.

Q: In triangle ABC of area 48 sq cm, D is the mid-point of BC and E is the mid-point of AD. The area of triangle BED is
- 24 sq cm
* 12 sq cm
- 8 sq cm
- 6 sq cm
W: ABD is half of ABC (24), and BE is a median of ABD, so BED is half of that.

Q: A rectangle and a parallelogram stand on the same base of 10 cm and between the same parallels. If the rectangle is 6 cm high, the area of the parallelogram is
- 30 sq cm
* 60 sq cm
- 16 sq cm
- 120 sq cm
W: Both have base 10 cm and height 6 cm.

Q: The three medians of a triangle of area 60 sq cm divide it into six triangles. The area of each is
- 20 sq cm
- 15 sq cm
* 10 sq cm
- 6 sq cm
W: The six small triangles have equal areas.
