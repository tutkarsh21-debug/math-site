Q: $27^{2/3}$ is equal to
- 6
* 9
- 18
- 3
W: The cube root of 27 is 3, and $3^2 = 9$.

Q: $\left(\dfrac{16}{81}\right)^{-3/4}$ is equal to
- $\dfrac{8}{27}$
* $\dfrac{27}{8}$
- $\dfrac{-8}{27}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
W: Invert to get $\left(\dfrac{81}{16}\right)^{3/4} = \left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^3$.

Q: If $2^{x - 3} = 1$, then $x$ is
- 0
- 1
* 3
- 4
W: $1 = 2^0$, so $x - 3 = 0$.

Q: If $3^{2x + 1} = 27$, then $x$ is
* 1
- 2
- 3
- 13
W: $27 = 3^3$, so $2x + 1 = 3$.

Q: $x^{1/2} \times x^{1/3}$ is equal to
- $x^{1/6}$
- $x^{1/5}$
* $x^{5/6}$
- $x^{2/5}$
W: Add the exponents: $\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{5}{6}$.

Q: $64^{-1/3}$ is equal to
- $-4$
* $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- 4
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
W: The cube root of 64 is 4, and the negative sign gives the reciprocal.

Q: If $4^x = 8$, then $x$ is
- 2
* $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: $2^{2x} = 2^3$, so $2x = 3$.

Q: $2^0 + 3^0 + 4^0$ is equal to
- 0
- 1
* 3
- 9
W: Each term is 1.

Q: $5^{-1} \times 5^3$ is equal to
- $5^{-3}$
- 5
* 25
- 125
W: $5^{-1 + 3} = 5^2$.

Q: If $9^{x + 1} = 27^x$, then $x$ is
- 1
* 2
- 3
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: $3^{2x + 2} = 3^{3x}$, so $2x + 2 = 3x$.
