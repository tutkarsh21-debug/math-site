Q: The solution of $x + y = 7$ and $x - y = 1$ is
- $x = 3$, $y = 4$
* $x = 4$, $y = 3$
- $x = 5$, $y = 2$
- $x = 6$, $y = 1$
W: Adding gives $2x = 8$.

Q: If $2x + 3y = 12$ and $x = 3$, then $y$ is
- 1
* 2
- 3
- 6
W: $6 + 3y = 12$.

Q: The solution of $3x + 2y = 11$ and $x - 2y = 1$ is
- $x = 2$, $y = 2.5$
* $x = 3$, $y = 1$
- $x = 1$, $y = 4$
- $x = 5$, $y = 2$
W: Adding eliminates $y$: $4x = 12$.

Q: If $\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{y} = 5$ and $\dfrac{1}{x} - \dfrac{1}{y} = 1$, then
- $x = 3$, $y = 2$
* $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$, $y = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $y = \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $x = 2$, $y = 3$
W: Adding gives $\dfrac{2}{x} = 6$, so $\dfrac{1}{x} = 3$ and $\dfrac{1}{y} = 2$.

Q: The sum of two numbers is 50 and their difference is 12. The numbers are
- 30 and 20
* 31 and 19
- 32 and 18
- 38 and 12
W: $2x = 62$ gives $x = 31$.

Q: The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. The number is 27 more than the number obtained by reversing its digits. The number is
- 36
- 72
* 63
- 54
W: $9(a - b) = 27$ gives $a - b = 3$; with $a + b = 9$, $a = 6$ and $b = 3$.

Q: 5 pens and 3 pencils cost Rs 34, and 3 pens and 5 pencils cost Rs 30. The cost of one pen is
- Rs 3
- Rs 4
* Rs 5
- Rs 6
W: Adding gives $p + c = 8$ and subtracting gives $p - c = 2$.

Q: A father is three times as old as his son. After 12 years he will be twice as old. The father's present age is
- 24 years
- 30 years
* 36 years
- 48 years
W: $3x + 12 = 2(x + 12)$ gives $x = 12$.

Q: If $x = 2$, $y = 1$ is a solution of $2x + ky = 7$, then $k$ is
- 1
- 2
* 3
- 5
W: $4 + k = 7$.

Q: A fraction becomes $\dfrac{1}{2}$ when 1 is added to its numerator, and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ when 1 is added to its denominator. The fraction is
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
* $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
W: $2(n + 1) = d$ and $3n = d + 1$ give $n = 3$, $d = 8$.
