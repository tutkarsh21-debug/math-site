Q: The degree of the polynomial $5x - 7$ is
- 0
* 1
- 5
- 7
W: The highest power of $x$ is 1, so it is a linear polynomial.

Q: If $p(x) = 3x - 4$, then $p(2)$ is
- $-2$
* 2
- 6
- 10
W: $3 \times 2 - 4 = 2$.

Q: The zero of $p(x) = 2x + 6$ is
- 3
- 6
* $-3$
- $-6$
W: $2x + 6 = 0$ gives $x = -3$.

Q: Which of these is not a polynomial?
- $3x + 1$
- $x^2 - 5$
- 7
* $\sqrt{x} + 2$
W: In a polynomial, the exponents of the variable are whole numbers. $\sqrt{x} = x^{1/2}$.

Q: The $n$th term of the pattern 5, 8, 11, 14, ... is
- $n + 4$
- $5n$
* $3n + 2$
- $3n + 5$
W: The terms go up by 3, and the first term is $3 \times 1 + 2 = 5$.

Q: A plant is 10 cm tall and grows 2 cm every week. Its height after $w$ weeks is
- $12w$ cm
- $10w + 2$ cm
* $10 + 2w$ cm
- $2w$ cm
W: Start at 10 and add 2 for each week.

Q: A tank holds 500 litres and leaks 20 litres every hour. It will be empty after
- 20 hours
* 25 hours
- 48 hours
- 480 hours
W: $500 - 20t = 0$ gives $t = 25$.

Q: The graph of $y = 2x + 3$ cuts the $y$-axis at
- $(3, 0)$
- $(0, 2)$
* $(0, 3)$
- $(2, 3)$
W: On the $y$-axis $x = 0$, so $y = 3$.

Q: The coefficient of $x$ in $7 - 4x$ is
- 7
- 4
* $-4$
- 3
W: The sign belongs to the coefficient.

Q: A taxi charges a fixed Rs 50 and Rs 12 per km. The fare for 8 km is
- Rs 96
- Rs 136
* Rs 146
- Rs 496
W: $50 + 12 \times 8 = 146$.
