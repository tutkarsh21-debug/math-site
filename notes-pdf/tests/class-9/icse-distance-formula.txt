Q: The distance of the point $(8, 15)$ from the origin is
- 23 units
* 17 units
- 7 units
- 13 units
W: $\sqrt{64 + 225} = 17$.

Q: The distance between $(-1, 2)$ and $(2, 6)$ is
- 3 units
- 4 units
* 5 units
- 7 units
W: $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$.

Q: The point on the $x$-axis that is equidistant from $(2, 3)$ and $(6, 3)$ is
- $(3, 0)$
* $(4, 0)$
- $(8, 0)$
- $(0, 4)$
W: $(x - 2)^2 + 9 = (x - 6)^2 + 9$ gives $x = 4$.

Q: The distance between $(x, 0)$ and $(0, 3)$ is 5 units. The values of $x$ are
- $\pm 2$
* $\pm 4$
- $\pm 5$
- $\pm 8$
W: $x^2 + 9 = 25$.

Q: For A$(0, 0)$, B$(3, 4)$ and C$(6, 8)$, AB = 5, BC = 5 and AC = 10. The points
* are collinear
- form an isosceles triangle
- form a right triangle
- form an equilateral triangle
W: AB + BC = AC.

Q: The triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(4, 0)$ and $(0, 3)$ is
* right-angled
- equilateral
- isosceles
- obtuse-angled
W: Its sides are 4, 3 and 5, and $16 + 9 = 25$.

Q: A circle with centre at the origin passes through $(5, 12)$. Its radius is
- 7 units
* 13 units
- 17 units
- 60 units
W: $\sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$.

Q: The points $(1, 1)$, $(4, 1)$, $(4, 4)$ and $(1, 4)$, in order, are the vertices of a
- rectangle that is not a square
* square
- rhombus that is not a square
- trapezium
W: All four sides are 3 units and both diagonals are $3\sqrt{2}$ units.

Q: The distance of the point $(-3, -4)$ from the origin is
- 7 units
- 1 unit
* 5 units
- 25 units
W: $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.

Q: The triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(2, 0)$ and $(1, \sqrt{3})$ is
- right-angled
- isosceles but not equilateral
* equilateral
- scalene
W: Each side is 2 units.
