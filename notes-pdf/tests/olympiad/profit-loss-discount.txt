Q: An article bought for Rs 120 is sold for Rs 150. Find the profit percent.
- 20%
* 25%
- 30%
- 35%
W: Profit $= 30$, and $\dfrac{30}{120} \times 100 = 25$.

Q: An article bought for Rs 500 is sold for Rs 450. Find the loss percent.
- 5%
* 10%
- 12%
- 15%
W: Loss $= 50$, and $\dfrac{50}{500} \times 100 = 10$.

Q: A bag marked at Rs 600 is sold at a discount of 25%. Find the selling price.
- Rs 400
- Rs 425
* Rs 450
- Rs 475
W: $600 \times 0.75 = 450$.

Q: Find the selling price needed to gain 15% on a cost price of Rs 800.
- Rs 900
* Rs 920
- Rs 940
- Rs 960
W: $800 \times 1.15 = 920$.

Q: An article is sold at a 10% gain for Rs 1155. Find its cost price.
- Rs 1000
* Rs 1050
- Rs 1100
- Rs 1045
W: $1155 \div 1.1 = 1050$.

Q: An article is sold at a 20% loss for Rs 640. Find its cost price.
- Rs 750
* Rs 800
- Rs 850
- Rs 768
W: $640 \div 0.8 = 800$.

Q: Find the single discount equal to successive discounts of 10% and 10%.
- 20%
- 18%
* 19%
- 21%
W: $0.9 \times 0.9 = 0.81$, so the single discount is $19\%$.

Q: A shopkeeper marks an article 25% above its cost and then gives a 20% discount. What is the result?
- 5% profit
- 5% loss
* No profit and no loss
- 10% profit
W: $1.25 \times 0.8 = 1$, so the selling price equals the cost price.

Q: An article is marked at Rs 750 and sold at a 20% discount. Find the selling price.
- Rs 550
- Rs 580
* Rs 600
- Rs 650
W: $750 \times 0.8 = 600$.

Q: The cost price of 10 articles equals the selling price of 8 articles. Find the profit percent.
- 20%
* 25%
- 30%
- 40%
W: Let each article cost Rs 1, so the cost of 10 is Rs 10. Eight articles sell for Rs 10, so each sells for $1.25$. Profit is $25\%$.
