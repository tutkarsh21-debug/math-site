Q: What is the mirror image of the time 12:20?
- 12:40
* 11:40
- 10:40
- 11:20
W: Treat 12:20 as 0:20. Then $12{:}00 - 0{:}20 = 11{:}40$.

Q: Which letter looks the same in a mirror placed at its side?
* A
- B
- C
- D
W: Only A is symmetrical about a vertical line. B, C and D are symmetrical about a horizontal line.

Q: Which letter looks the same in a water image?
- A
- M
- T
* C
W: C is symmetrical about a horizontal line, so it looks the same upside down. A, M and T are not.

Q: Which word looks the same in a mirror placed at its side?
* TOT
- TIP
- PAT
- CAT
W: T and O are symmetrical about a vertical line, and TOT reads the same backwards.

Q: Which word looks the same in a water image?
* DICE
- MAN
- TAP
- WIN
W: D, I, C and E are all symmetrical about a horizontal line.

Q: A girl raises her right hand in front of a mirror. Which hand does her image seem to raise?
- Right hand
* Left hand
- Both hands
- Neither hand
W: A mirror turns left and right, so the image seems to raise its left hand.

Q: A mirror is placed on the right of the word STAR. Which letter appears at the extreme left of the image?
- S
- T
- A
* R
W: The last letter R is nearest to the mirror, so it appears first from the left.
