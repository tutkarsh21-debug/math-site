Q: Kiran is 7th from the top and 15th from the bottom. How many students are in the class?
- 20
* 21
- 22
- 23
W: $7 + 15 - 1 = 21$.

* 20
- 21
- 22

Q: A class has 40 students. Sonu's rank from the top is 12. What is his rank from the bottom?
- 27
- 28

- 30

Q: A is taller than B. B is taller than C. Who is the shortest?
- A
- B
* C
- Cannot be found
W: $A > B > C$, so C is the shortest.

Q: In a row of 30 boys, P is 9th from the left and Q is 7th from the right. If they exchange places, what is P's position from the left?
- 22nd
- 23rd
* 24th
- 25th
W: Q's position from the left is $30 + 1 - 7 = 24$, and P takes it.

Q: In a queue of 50 people, Gita is 18th from the front. What is her position from the back?
- 31st
- 32nd
* 33rd
- 34th
W: $50 + 1 - 18 = 33$.

Q: Arrange in dictionary order: Mango, Apple, Guava, Banana. Which word comes second?
- Apple
* Banana
- Guava
- Mango
W: The order is Apple, Banana, Guava, Mango.

Q: A is heavier than B. C is lighter than B. D is heavier than A. E is lighter than C. Who is the second heaviest?

- D
* A
- C
W: $D > A > B > C > E$, so A is the second heaviest.
