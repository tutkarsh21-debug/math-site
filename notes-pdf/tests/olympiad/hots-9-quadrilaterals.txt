Q: In a parallelogram ABCD, $\angle A = 65^\circ$. Then $\angle B =$
- $65^\circ$
- $105^\circ$
* $115^\circ$
- $125^\circ$
W: Adjacent angles add up to $180^\circ$, so $\angle B = 115^\circ$.

Q: D and E are the midpoints of AB and AC in a triangle ABC and $BC = 20$ cm. Then $DE =$
- 5 cm
* 10 cm
- 20 cm
- 40 cm
W: By the midpoint theorem $DE = \dfrac{1}{2}BC$.

Q: A rectangle has sides 6 cm and 8 cm. Its diagonal is
- 7 cm
- 9 cm
* 10 cm
- 14 cm
W: $\sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$.

Q: A rhombus has diagonals of 6 cm and 8 cm. Its side is
- 4 cm
* 5 cm
- 7 cm
- 10 cm
W: The half diagonals are 3 and 4, so the side is $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$ cm.

Q: The sum of the angles of any quadrilateral is
- $180^\circ$
- $270^\circ$
* $360^\circ$
- $540^\circ$
W: A diagonal splits a quadrilateral into two triangles, each with angle sum $180^\circ$.

Q: The diagonal of a square of side 5 cm is
- 5 cm
- 10 cm
* $5\sqrt{2}$ cm
- $25\sqrt{2}$ cm
W: $\sqrt{25 + 25} = 5\sqrt{2}$.

Q: D, E and F are the midpoints of the sides of triangle ABC. The area of triangle DEF is what part of the area of ABC?
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
* $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
W: Triangle DEF is similar to ABC with sides half as long, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{4}$.

Q: The diagonals of a parallelogram
- are always equal
- are always perpendicular
* bisect each other
- are always parallel
W: Bisecting each other is the defining property of the diagonals of a parallelogram.
