Q: Marks of 70 and 90 have equal weights. The weighted mean is
- 70
- 75
* 80
- 90
W: With equal weights it is the simple mean $\dfrac{70 + 90}{2} = 80$.

Q: 10 kg of an item at Rs 20 per kg is mixed with 10 kg at Rs 30 per kg. The cost per kg of the mixture is
- Rs 20
* Rs 25
- Rs 28
- Rs 30
W: $\dfrac{200 + 300}{20} = 25$.

Q: 30 students have an average mark of 40 and 20 students have an average mark of 60. The average of all 50 students is
- 46
* 48
- 50
- 52
W: $\dfrac{1200 + 1200}{50} = 48$.

Q: A student's average in 3 tests is 60. She scores 100 in the fourth test. The average of the four tests is
- 65
* 70
- 75
- 80
W: Total $= 180 + 100 = 280$, and $\dfrac{280}{4} = 70$.

Q: In a stacked bar of 120 people, 30 are in one part. That part is
- 20%
* 25%
- 30%
- 40%
W: $\dfrac{30}{120} \times 100 = 25$.

Q: Two kinds of tea cost Rs 30 and Rs 40 per kg. They are mixed to make a mixture costing Rs 36 per kg. The ratio of the quantity at Rs 30 to the quantity at Rs 40 is
- $3 : 2$
* $2 : 3$
- $1 : 1$
- $3 : 5$
W: Taking quantities $2x$ and $3x$: $\dfrac{60x + 120x}{5x} = 36$. The ratio is $2 : 3$.

Q: Five boys have an average weight of 40 kg and five girls have an average weight of 30 kg. The average weight of all ten is
- 32 kg
- 34 kg
* 35 kg
- 36 kg
W: The groups are equal in size, so the mean is $\dfrac{40 + 30}{2} = 35$.

Q: A weighted mean of the values 10, 20 and 30 can never be
- 12
- 20
- 29
* 35
W: A weighted mean lies between the smallest and the largest value, so it cannot be more than 30.
