Q: Find the average of 10, 20, 30 and 40.
- 20
* 25
- 30
- 35
W: $\dfrac{10 + 20 + 30 + 40}{4} = \dfrac{100}{4} = 25$.

Q: The average of 12 numbers is 15. What is their sum?
- 150
- 170
* 180
- 190
W: Sum $= 12 \times 15 = 180$.

Q: Find the average of the first 10 odd numbers.
- 5
- 9
* 10
- 11
W: The sum of the first 10 odd numbers is $10^2 = 100$, so the average is 10.

Q: The average of 6 numbers is 25. The average of 5 of them is 23. Find the sixth number.
- 30
* 35
- 40
- 45
W: Total $= 150$, and the five add up to $115$. The sixth number is $150 - 115 = 35$.

Q: 25 boys have an average mark of 40 and 15 girls have an average mark of 60. Find the average of all 40 children.
- 46
* 47.5
- 50
- 52.5
W: Total $= 1000 + 900 = 1900$, and $\dfrac{1900}{40} = 47.5$.

Q: A batsman has an average of 40 after 10 innings. He scores 106 in the next innings. What is his new average?
- 44
- 45
* 46
- 47
W: Total after 11 innings $= 400 + 106 = 506$, and $\dfrac{506}{11} = 46$.

Q: Find the average of 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14.
- 8
* 10
- 11
- 12
W: The numbers are equally spaced, so the average is the middle number, 10.

Q: The average of three numbers is 12. Two of them are 10 and 14. Find the third.
- 10
* 12
- 14
- 16
W: Sum $= 36$, and $36 - 24 = 12$.

Q: The average age of 20 students is 15 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average becomes 16 years. Find the teacher's age.
- 32 years
- 34 years
* 36 years
- 38 years
W: New total $= 21 \times 16 = 336$ and old total $= 300$. The teacher is $36$.

Q: The average of 60, 70, 80 and $x$ is 70. Find $x$.
- 60
* 70
- 80
- 90
W: Total $= 4 \times 70 = 280$, and $280 - 210 = 70$.
