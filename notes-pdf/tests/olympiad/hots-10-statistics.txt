Q: The mean of 5, 7, 9, 11 and 13 is
- 8
* 9
- 10
- 11
W: The sum is 45 and $\dfrac{45}{5} = 9$.

Q: The mean of 10 observations is 12. The sum of the observations is
- 22
- 102
* 120
- 130
W: Sum $= 10 \times 12 = 120$.

Q: For a data set the mode is 12 and the mean is 9. Using the empirical relation, the median is
- 9
* 10
- 10.5
- 11
W: $3\,\text{median} = \text{mode} + 2\,\text{mean} = 12 + 18 = 30$, so the median is 10.

Q: The class mark of the class 20 - 30 is
- 20
* 25
- 30
- 50
W: The class mark is $\dfrac{20 + 30}{2} = 25$.

Q: The median of 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15 is
- 7
* 9
- 11
- 13
W: The middle (4th) value of the seven ordered values is 9.

Q: The mean of 8 numbers is 25. One number, 40, is replaced by 16. The new mean is
- 20
- 21
* 22
- 23
W: The sum falls from 200 to $200 - 40 + 16 = 176$, and $\dfrac{176}{8} = 22$.

Q: 15 observations have mean 20 and 5 observations have mean 40. The mean of all 20 observations is
- 22
* 25
- 30
- 35
W: $\dfrac{300 + 200}{20} = 25$.

Q: In a frequency distribution the classes 0 - 10, 10 - 20 and 20 - 30 have frequencies 3, 9 and 6. The modal class is
- 0 - 10
* 10 - 20
- 20 - 30
- None of these
W: The modal class has the highest frequency, which is 9.
