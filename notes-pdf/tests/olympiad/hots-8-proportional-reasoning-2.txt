Q: On a map 1 cm stands for 5 km. Two towns are 3.2 cm apart on the map. Their real distance is
- 12 km
- 15 km
* 16 km
- 32 km
W: $3.2 \times 5 = 16$ km.

Q: Rs 600 is divided in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$. The largest share is
- Rs 100
- Rs 200
* Rs 300
- Rs 360
W: One part is Rs 100, so the largest share is $3 \times 100 = 300$.

Q: In a pie chart of 450 people a sector has a central angle of $120^\circ$. It represents
- 100 people
* 150 people
- 180 people
- 225 people
W: $\dfrac{120}{360} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, and $\dfrac{450}{3} = 150$.

Q: 5 men can finish a job in 24 days. 8 men will take
- 12 days
* 15 days
- 18 days
- 20 days
W: Work $= 120$ man-days, and $\dfrac{120}{8} = 15$.

Q: A can do a job in 20 days and B in 30 days. Together they take
- 10 days
* 12 days
- 15 days
- 25 days
W: One day: $\dfrac{1}{20} + \dfrac{1}{30} = \dfrac{1}{12}$.

Q: A sector of a pie chart has a central angle of $36^\circ$. It represents what percentage of the whole?
- 5%
* 10%
- 12%
- 15%
W: $\dfrac{36}{360} = \dfrac{1}{10}$.

Q: 12 men can finish a job in 10 days. 15 men will finish it in
- 6 days
- 7 days
* 8 days
- 9 days
W: $\dfrac{120}{15} = 8$.

Q: A tap fills a tank in 4 hours and a drain empties the full tank in 12 hours. With both open the tank fills in
- 3 hours
- 4 hours
* 6 hours
- 8 hours
W: Net rate $= \dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{12} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ per hour.
