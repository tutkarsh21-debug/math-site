Q: Change 36 km/h to m/s.
- 5 m/s
* 10 m/s
- 12 m/s
- 18 m/s
W: $36 \times \dfrac{5}{18} = 10$.

Q: Change 25 m/s to km/h.
- 70 km/h
- 80 km/h
* 90 km/h
- 100 km/h
W: $25 \times \dfrac{18}{5} = 90$.

Q: A car moves at 45 km/h for 4 hours. How far does it go?
- 160 km
* 180 km
- 200 km
- 225 km
W: Distance $= 45 \times 4 = 180$ km.

Q: A person walks 30 km at 10 km/h and then 30 km at 15 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole journey.
- 12.5 km/h
* 12 km/h
- 13 km/h
- 11.5 km/h
W: Total distance $= 60$ km, total time $= 3 + 2 = 5$ hours, and $\dfrac{60}{5} = 12$.

Q: A train 200 m long runs at 72 km/h. How long does it take to pass a pole?
- 5 s
* 10 s
- 12 s
- 14.4 s
W: Speed $= 20$ m/s and time $= \dfrac{200}{20} = 10$ s.

Q: A train 100 m long runs at 20 m/s. How long does it take to cross a bridge 400 m long?
- 20 s
* 25 s
- 30 s
- 5 s
W: Distance $= 100 + 400 = 500$ m, and $\dfrac{500}{20} = 25$ s.

Q: Two people start 120 km apart and walk towards each other at 30 km/h and 50 km/h. After how long do they meet?
- 1 hour
* 1.5 hours
- 2 hours
- 2.5 hours
W: Relative speed $= 80$ km/h, and $\dfrac{120}{80} = 1.5$ hours.

Q: Two cyclists are 20 km apart, going in the same direction at 12 km/h and 8 km/h, the faster one behind. After how long does the faster one catch up?
- 2 hours
- 4 hours
* 5 hours
- 10 hours
W: Relative speed $= 12 - 8 = 4$ km/h, and $\dfrac{20}{4} = 5$ hours.

Q: A boat moves at 15 km/h in still water and the stream flows at 3 km/h. What is its speed upstream?
- 18 km/h
* 12 km/h
- 15 km/h
- 10 km/h
W: Upstream speed $= 15 - 3 = 12$ km/h.

Q: An athlete runs 400 m in 80 seconds. What is the speed in m/s?
- 4 m/s
* 5 m/s
- 6 m/s
- 8 m/s
W: $\dfrac{400}{80} = 5$ m/s.
