Q: Find the simple interest on Rs 6000 at 5% per year for 4 years.
- Rs 1000
* Rs 1200
- Rs 1500
- Rs 1800
W: $\dfrac{6000 \times 5 \times 4}{100} = 1200$.

Q: Find the simple interest on Rs 2000 at 10% per year for $1\dfrac{1}{2}$ years.
- Rs 200
- Rs 250
* Rs 300
- Rs 350
W: $\dfrac{2000 \times 10 \times 1.5}{100} = 300$.

Q: Rs 2000 earns a simple interest of Rs 600 in 3 years. Find the rate per year.
- 5%
- 8%
* 10%
- 12%
W: $R = \dfrac{600 \times 100}{2000 \times 3} = 10$.

Q: Rs 1000 becomes Rs 1300 at 10% simple interest per year. Find the time.
- 2 years
* 3 years
- 4 years
- 5 years
W: Interest $= 300 = \dfrac{1000 \times 10 \times T}{100}$, so $T = 3$.

Q: A sum earns Rs 450 simple interest in 3 years at 5% per year. Find the sum.
- Rs 2500
* Rs 3000
- Rs 3500
- Rs 4000
W: $450 = \dfrac{P \times 5 \times 3}{100}$, so $P = 3000$.

Q: Find the compound interest on Rs 5000 at 20% per year for 2 years.
- Rs 2000
* Rs 2200
- Rs 2400
- Rs 2500
W: $A = 5000 \times 1.2 \times 1.2 = 7200$, and $\text{CI} = 7200 - 5000 = 2200$.

Q: Find the amount of Rs 4000 at 10% compound interest per year for 2 years.
- Rs 4800
* Rs 4840
- Rs 4400
- Rs 4880
W: $4000 \times 1.1 \times 1.1 = 4840$.

Q: Find the difference between CI and SI on Rs 10 000 at 5% per year for 2 years.
- Rs 20
* Rs 25
- Rs 30
- Rs 50
W: $10\,000 \times \left(\dfrac{5}{100}\right)^2 = 10\,000 \times 0.0025 = 25$.

Q: The population of a town is 20 000 and it grows by 5% every year. What is it after 2 years?
- 22 000
* 22 050
- 22 100
- 22 500
W: $20\,000 \times 1.05 \times 1.05 = 22\,050$.

Q: For the first year, compound interest on a sum compared with simple interest at the same rate is
- greater
- smaller
* equal
- double
W: In the first year interest is worked out on the same principal, so both are equal.
