Q: Write the ratio $18 : 24$ in its simplest form.
- $2 : 3$
* $3 : 4$
- $4 : 5$
- $6 : 8$
W: Dividing both terms by 6 gives $3 : 4$.

Q: Find $x$ if $4 : 7 = 12 : x$.
- 18
- 20
* 21
- 28
W: $4x = 7 \times 12 = 84$, so $x = 21$.

Q: Rs 1200 is divided in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$. What is the largest share?
- Rs 400
- Rs 500
* Rs 600
- Rs 700
W: One part is $\dfrac{1200}{6} = 200$. The largest share is $3 \times 200 = 600$.

Q: If $a : b = 3 : 4$ and $b : c = 2 : 5$, what is $a : c$?
- $3 : 5$
- $3 : 8$
* $3 : 10$
- $6 : 5$
W: $a : b = 6 : 8$ and $b : c = 8 : 20$, so $a : b : c = 6 : 8 : 20$, and $a : c = 3 : 10$.

Q: 8 men build a wall in 15 days. How many days will 10 men take?
- 10 days
* 12 days
- 14 days
- 18 days
W: Work $= 8 \times 15 = 120$ man-days, and $\dfrac{120}{10} = 12$.

Q: 12 notebooks cost Rs 96. What is the cost of 20 notebooks?
- Rs 144
- Rs 150
* Rs 160
- Rs 180
W: One notebook costs Rs 8, so 20 cost Rs 160.

Q: Find the fourth proportional to 2, 3 and 8.
- 10
* 12
- 16
- 24
W: $d = \dfrac{3 \times 8}{2} = 12$.

Q: The ages of a father and his son are in the ratio $7 : 2$ and their sum is 63 years. What is the son's age?
- 12 years
* 14 years
- 16 years
- 18 years
W: One part is $\dfrac{63}{9} = 7$, and the son is $2 \times 7 = 14$.

Q: In 20 litres of a mixture, milk and water are in the ratio $3 : 2$. How much water is there?
- 6 litres
* 8 litres
- 10 litres
- 12 litres
W: One part is $\dfrac{20}{5} = 4$ litres, so water is $2 \times 4 = 8$ litres.

Q: Which four numbers are in proportion?
* 2, 3, 4, 6
- 2, 3, 4, 5
- 3, 4, 5, 6
- 1, 2, 3, 5
W: $2 : 3 = 4 : 6$ because $2 \times 6 = 3 \times 4 = 12$.
