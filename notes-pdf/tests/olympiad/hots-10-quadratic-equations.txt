Q: The roots of $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$ are
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$
* $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $-2$
W: $6x^2 - x - 2 = (3x - 2)(2x + 1)$, so $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$ or $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: The equation $kx(x - 2) + 6 = 0$ has equal roots. Then $k =$
- 2
- 4
* 6
- 12
W: $kx^2 - 2kx + 6 = 0$ has $D = 4k^2 - 24k = 4k(k - 6)$, which is zero for $k = 6$ ($k \ne 0$).

Q: A train travels 300 km at a uniform speed. If the speed were 10 km/h more, the journey would take 1 hour less. The speed is
- 40 km/h
* 50 km/h
- 60 km/h
- 75 km/h
W: $\dfrac{300}{x} - \dfrac{300}{x + 10} = 1$ gives $x^2 + 10x - 3000 = 0$, so $(x + 60)(x - 50) = 0$ and $x = 50$.

Q: The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306. The larger integer is
- 16
- 17
* 18
- 19
W: $n(n + 1) = 306$ gives $n^2 + n - 306 = 0$, so $(n + 18)(n - 17) = 0$. The integers are 17 and 18.

Q: The equation $2x^2 - 6x + 3 = 0$ has
- no real roots
- two equal real roots
* two different real roots
- one real and one imaginary root
W: $D = 36 - 24 = 12 > 0$.

Q: A number added to its reciprocal gives $\dfrac{10}{3}$. The number which is greater than 1 is
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 5
W: $x + \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{10}{3}$ gives $3x^2 - 10x + 3 = 0$, so $x = 3$ or $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

Q: The length of a garden is 3 m more than its breadth and its area is 70 square metres. Its perimeter is
- 30 m
* 34 m
- 36 m
- 40 m
W: $b(b + 3) = 70$ gives $(b + 10)(b - 7) = 0$, so $b = 7$ and $l = 10$. Perimeter $= 2(17) = 34$.

Q: If $2$ is a root of $x^2 + kx - 10 = 0$, the other root is
- 5
- $-2$
* $-5$
- 10
W: $4 + 2k - 10 = 0$ gives $k = 3$, and $x^2 + 3x - 10 = (x + 5)(x - 2)$.
