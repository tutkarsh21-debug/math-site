Q: If $\dfrac{3}{x} + \dfrac{2}{y} = 7$ and $\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{y} = 3$, then $x + y =$
- 1
* $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- 2
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: With $u = \dfrac{1}{x}$ and $v = \dfrac{1}{y}$: $3u + 2v = 7$ and $u + v = 3$ give $u = 1$ and $v = 2$. So $x = 1$, $y = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x + y = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

Q: A two-digit number is 4 times the sum of its digits and the digits add up to 6. The number is
- 15
* 24
- 33
- 42
W: $10x + y = 4(x + y)$ gives $y = 2x$. With $x + y = 6$ we get $x = 2$ and $y = 4$.

Q: The sum of the ages of A and B is 40. Five years ago A was twice as old as B. The present age of A is
- 20 years
- 24 years
* 25 years
- 30 years
W: $a + b = 40$ and $a - 5 = 2(b - 5)$ give $a = 2b - 5$. So $3b = 45$, $b = 15$ and $a = 25$.

Q: For what value of $k$ does $2x + 3y = 7$ and $(k - 1)x + (k + 2)y = 3k$ have infinitely many solutions?
- 3
- 5
* 7
- 9
W: $\dfrac{2}{k - 1} = \dfrac{3}{k + 2}$ gives $2k + 4 = 3k - 3$, so $k = 7$. Then $\dfrac{7}{3k} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ also matches.

Q: For what value of $k$ does $x + 2y = 3$ and $2x + ky = 7$ have no solution?
- 2
- 3
* 4
- 6
W: No solution needs $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{2}{k} \ne \dfrac{3}{7}$. So $k = 4$.

Q: A boat goes 12 km/h in still water and the stream flows at 3 km/h. How long does it take to go 27 km upstream?
- 2 hours
* 3 hours
- 4 hours
- 4.5 hours
W: Upstream speed $= 12 - 3 = 9$ km/h, and $\dfrac{27}{9} = 3$ hours.

Q: The lines $2x + y = 7$ and $x - y = 2$ meet at the point
- $(2, 3)$
* $(3, 1)$
- $(4, -1)$
- $(1, 5)$
W: Adding the equations gives $3x = 9$, so $x = 3$ and $y = 1$.

Q: If $2x + 3y = 12$ and $3x - 2y = 5$, then $x + y =$
- 4
* 5
- 6
- 7
W: Multiplying gives $6x + 9y = 36$ and $6x - 4y = 10$, so $13y = 26$, $y = 2$ and $x = 3$.
