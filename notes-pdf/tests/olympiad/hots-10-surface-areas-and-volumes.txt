Q: The volume of a sphere of radius 3 cm is
- $12\pi$ cubic cm
- $27\pi$ cubic cm
* $36\pi$ cubic cm
- $108\pi$ cubic cm
W: $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi \times 27 = 36\pi$.

Q: The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 220 square cm
* 440 square cm
- 1540 square cm
- 154 square cm
W: $2\pi r h = 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 10 = 440$.

Q: The slant height of a cone of radius 6 cm and height 8 cm is
- 7 cm
- 9 cm
* 10 cm
- 14 cm
W: $\sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$ cm.

Q: The volume of a cone of radius 7 cm and height 6 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 154 cubic cm
* 308 cubic cm
- 462 cubic cm
- 924 cubic cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{3} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 6 = 308$.

Q: If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its volume becomes
- 2 times
- 4 times
* 8 times
- 16 times
W: Volume is proportional to $r^3$, and $2^3 = 8$.

Q: A cube of side 6 cm is melted and recast into small cubes of side 2 cm. The number of small cubes is
- 3
- 9
* 27
- 36
W: $\dfrac{6^3}{2^3} = \dfrac{216}{8} = 27$.

Q: The total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius 7 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 154 square cm
- 308 square cm
* 462 square cm
- 616 square cm
W: $3\pi r^2 = 3 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 = 462$.

Q: The volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 440 cubic cm
- 770 cubic cm
* 1540 cubic cm
- 3080 cubic cm
W: $\dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 10 = 1540$.
