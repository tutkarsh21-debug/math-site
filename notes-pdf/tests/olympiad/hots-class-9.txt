Q: If $x = 3 + 2\sqrt{2}$, then $x + \dfrac{1}{x} =$
- 3
- 4
- 5
* 6
W: $\dfrac{1}{x} = 3 - 2\sqrt{2}$, so the sum is 6.

Q: Simplify $\sqrt{7 + 4\sqrt{3}}$.
- $\sqrt{7} + 2$
* $2 + \sqrt{3}$
- $3 + \sqrt{3}$
- $4 + \sqrt{3}$
W: $(2 + \sqrt{3})^2 = 4 + 3 + 4\sqrt{3} = 7 + 4\sqrt{3}$.

Q: If $x + \dfrac{1}{x} = 4$, then $x^3 + \dfrac{1}{x^3} =$
- 48
- 50
* 52
- 64
W: $x^3 + \dfrac{1}{x^3} = 4^3 - 3 \times 4 = 52$.

Q: For what value of $k$ is $(x - 2)$ a factor of $x^3 - 4x^2 + kx + 4$?
- 0
- 1
* 2
- 4
W: $p(2) = 8 - 16 + 2k + 4 = 0$, so $2k = 4$ and $k = 2$.

Q: In triangle ABC, angle A is $70^\circ$. The bisectors of angles B and C meet at O. Angle BOC is
- $110^\circ$
* $125^\circ$
- $135^\circ$
- $145^\circ$
W: $\angle BOC = 90^\circ + \dfrac{1}{2} \times 70^\circ = 125^\circ$.

Q: In a parallelogram ABCD, angle A is $(3x + 10)^\circ$ and angle B is $(2x + 30)^\circ$. Find angle A.
- $90^\circ$
- $92^\circ$
* $94^\circ$
- $98^\circ$
W: $5x + 40 = 180$, so $x = 28$ and $\angle A = 94^\circ$.

Q: The midpoints of the sides of a triangle are joined to form a smaller triangle of perimeter 18 cm. What is the perimeter of the original triangle?
- 9 cm
- 27 cm
* 36 cm
- 54 cm
W: Each side of the smaller triangle is half a side of the original, so the original perimeter is $2 \times 18 = 36$ cm.

Q: A chord 16 cm long is drawn in a circle of radius 10 cm. How far is it from the centre?
- 4 cm
- 5 cm
* 6 cm
- 8 cm
W: $d = \sqrt{10^2 - 8^2} = 6$ cm.

Q: A cone has radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Its curved surface area is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 154 square cm
- 462 square cm
* 550 square cm
- 600 square cm
W: Slant height $= 25$ cm and the curved surface area is $\dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25 = 550$.

Q: The mean of 10 numbers is 20. One number, 25, is replaced by 45. What is the new mean?
- 21
* 22
- 23
- 25
W: The sum rises by 20, from 200 to 220, so the mean is 22.
