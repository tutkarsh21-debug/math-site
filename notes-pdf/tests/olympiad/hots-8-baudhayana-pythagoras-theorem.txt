Q: The legs of a right triangle are 6 cm and 8 cm. The hypotenuse is
- 7 cm
- 9 cm
* 10 cm
- 14 cm
W: $\sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$.

Q: Which of these is a Pythagorean triple?
- 4, 5, 6
- 5, 11, 13
* 5, 12, 13
- 6, 7, 9
W: $25 + 144 = 169 = 13^2$.

Q: A ladder 10 m long has its foot 6 m from a wall. It reaches a height of
- 4 m
- 6 m
* 8 m
- 12 m
W: $\sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$.

Q: The diagonal of a rectangle of sides 3 cm and 4 cm is
* 5 cm
- 6 cm
- 7 cm
- 12 cm
W: $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.

Q: The diagonal of a square of side 5 cm is
- 5 cm
- 10 cm
* $5\sqrt{2}$ cm
- $25\sqrt{2}$ cm
W: $\sqrt{25 + 25} = 5\sqrt{2}$.

Q: Two poles 4 m and 9 m tall stand 12 m apart. The distance between their tops is
- 12 m
* 13 m
- 14 m
- 15 m
W: The difference in height is 5 m, so the distance is $\sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = 13$ m.

Q: The hypotenuse of a right triangle is 5 cm and one leg is 3 cm. The other leg is
- 2 cm
* 4 cm
- 6 cm
- 8 cm
W: $\sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$.

Q: A triangle has sides 7 cm, 8 cm and 11 cm. It is
- right-angled
* not right-angled
- equilateral
- impossible to draw
W: $7^2 + 8^2 = 113$, which is not equal to $11^2 = 121$.
