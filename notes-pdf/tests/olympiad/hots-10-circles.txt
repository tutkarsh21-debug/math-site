Q: PA and PB are tangents from P to a circle with centre O and $\angle APB = 80^\circ$. Then $\angle AOB =$
- $80^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
* $100^\circ$
- $110^\circ$
W: In quadrilateral OAPB the angles at A and B are $90^\circ$, so $\angle AOB = 360^\circ - 180^\circ - 80^\circ = 100^\circ$.

Q: A point P is 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm. The length of the tangent from P is
- 8 cm
- 10 cm
* 12 cm
- 18 cm
W: $\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12$ cm.

Q: A quadrilateral ABCD is drawn around a circle. If $AB = 7$, $BC = 9$ and $CD = 5$, then $AD =$
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 21
W: $AB + CD = BC + AD$ gives $12 = 9 + AD$, so $AD = 3$.

Q: Two concentric circles have radii 5 cm and 3 cm. A chord of the larger circle touches the smaller. Its length is
- 4 cm
- 6 cm
* 8 cm
- 10 cm
W: Half the chord is $\sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$ cm, so the chord is 8 cm.

Q: The tangent from a point 25 cm from the centre is 24 cm long. The radius of the circle is
- 5 cm
* 7 cm
- 9 cm
- 12 cm
W: $r = \sqrt{25^2 - 24^2} = 7$ cm.

Q: The angle between a tangent to a circle and the radius at the point of contact is
- $0^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
* $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
W: A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.

Q: From an outside point, the number of tangents that can be drawn to a circle is
- 1
* 2
- 3
- Infinite
W: Exactly two tangents can be drawn from a point outside a circle.

Q: PA and PB are tangents from P and $PA = 7$ cm. Then $PB =$
- 3.5 cm
* 7 cm
- 14 cm
- 49 cm
W: Tangents from an outside point are equal in length.
