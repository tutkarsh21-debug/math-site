Q: Three angles of a quadrilateral are $90^\circ$, $80^\circ$ and $100^\circ$. The fourth angle is
- $60^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
* $90^\circ$
- $100^\circ$
W: $360^\circ - (90^\circ + 80^\circ + 100^\circ) = 90^\circ$.

Q: One angle of a parallelogram is $110^\circ$. An adjacent angle is
- $110^\circ$
* $70^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $250^\circ$
W: Adjacent angles add up to $180^\circ$.

Q: A rhombus has diagonals of 6 cm and 8 cm. Its area is
- 14 square cm
- 20 square cm
* 24 square cm
- 48 square cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 = 24$.

Q: A rectangle has a diagonal of 10 cm and one side of 6 cm. The other side is
- 4 cm
- 6 cm
* 8 cm
- 12 cm
W: $\sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$.

Q: The diagonals of a kite are 8 cm and 10 cm. Its area is
- 18 square cm
* 40 square cm
- 80 square cm
- 36 square cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 10 = 40$.

Q: The sum of the interior angles of a hexagon is
- $540^\circ$
* $720^\circ$
- $900^\circ$
- $360^\circ$
W: $(6 - 2) \times 180^\circ = 720^\circ$.

Q: The diagonals of a rhombus
- are equal
* bisect each other at right angles
- are parallel
- do not meet
W: This is the characteristic property of a rhombus.

Q: The diagonal of a square of side 4 cm is
- 4 cm
- 8 cm
* $4\sqrt{2}$ cm
- $16\sqrt{2}$ cm
W: $\sqrt{16 + 16} = 4\sqrt{2}$.
