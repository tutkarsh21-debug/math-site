Q: Find the area of a rectangle 12 m long and 7 m wide.
- 38 square metres
* 84 square metres
- 76 square metres
- 96 square metres
W: Area $= 12 \times 7 = 84$.

Q: Find the perimeter of a square of side 9 m.
- 18 m
- 27 m
* 36 m
- 81 m
W: Perimeter $= 4 \times 9 = 36$ m.

Q: Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.
- 16 square cm
* 30 square cm
- 60 square cm
- 36 square cm
W: Area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 6 = 30$.

Q: Find the circumference of a circle of radius 7 cm (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$).
- 22 cm
* 44 cm
- 154 cm
- 88 cm
W: $2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 = 44$.

Q: A field is 30 m by 20 m. Find the cost of fencing it at Rs 15 per metre.
- Rs 750
- Rs 1200
* Rs 1500
- Rs 9000
W: Perimeter $= 2(30 + 20) = 100$ m, and $100 \times 15 = 1500$.

Q: A floor 3.6 m by 2.4 m is covered with square tiles of side 30 cm. How many tiles are needed?
- 64
- 80
* 96
- 120
W: Floor $= 3.6 \times 2.4 = 8.64$ square metres and a tile is $0.09$ square metres. $\dfrac{8.64}{0.09} = 96$.

Q: Find the volume of a cuboid 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm.
- 12 cubic cm
- 47 cubic cm
* 60 cubic cm
- 94 cubic cm
W: Volume $= 5 \times 4 \times 3 = 60$.

Q: A tank holds 4000 litres. What is its volume in cubic metres?
- 0.4
- 40
* 4
- 400
W: $1\ \text{m}^3 = 1000$ litres, so 4000 litres is 4 cubic metres.

Q: A path 1 m wide runs around the outside of a garden 12 m by 8 m. Find the area of the path.
- 40 square metres
* 44 square metres
- 48 square metres
- 52 square metres
W: Outer rectangle $= 14 \times 10 = 140$ and the garden is $96$, so the path is $44$.

Q: Find the total surface area of a cube of side 5 cm.
- 100 square cm
- 125 square cm
* 150 square cm
- 200 square cm
W: Surface area $= 6 \times 5^2 = 150$.
