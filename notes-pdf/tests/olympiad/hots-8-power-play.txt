Q: The value of $\dfrac{2^5}{2^2}$ is
- 2
- 6
* 8
- 16
W: $2^{5 - 2} = 2^3 = 8$.

Q: The value of $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3}$ is
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- 6
* 8
- $-8$
W: Turning the fraction over gives $2^3 = 8$.

Q: The value of $5^0 + 5^{-1}$ is
- 1
* $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- 5
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
W: $1 + \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{6}{5}$.

Q: The number $4.5 \times 10^3$ equals
- 450
* 4500
- 45 000
- 0.0045
W: Moving the decimal point 3 places to the right gives 4500.

Q: If $3^x = 243$, then $x =$
- 4
* 5
- 6
- 7
W: $243 = 3^5$.

Q: The number $8 \times 10^{-3}$ equals
- 8000
- 0.08
* 0.008
- 0.0008
W: $10^{-3} = 0.001$, so the value is 0.008.

Q: The value of $(2^3)^2$ is
- 32
* 64
- 16
- 128
W: $(2^3)^2 = 2^6 = 64$.

Q: The value of $2^{-2}$ is
- $-4$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
* $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- 4
W: $2^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{2^2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
