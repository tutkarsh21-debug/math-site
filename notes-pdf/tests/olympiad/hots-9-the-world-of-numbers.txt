@minutes 12
Q: The number $0.555\ldots$ (5 repeating) equals
- $\dfrac{5}{10}$
- $\dfrac{5}{99}$
* $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{55}{100}$
W: Let $x = 0.555\ldots$, then $10x - x = 5$ and $x = \dfrac{5}{9}$.

Q: The value of $\sqrt{18} \times \sqrt{2}$ is
- 3
* 6
- 9
- 36
W: $\sqrt{36} = 6$.

Q: Rationalising $\dfrac{1}{2 + \sqrt{3}}$ gives
- $2 + \sqrt{3}$
* $2 - \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{2 - \sqrt{3}}{7}$
- $\sqrt{3} - 2$
W: Multiplying the top and bottom by $2 - \sqrt{3}$ gives denominator $4 - 3 = 1$.

Q: Which of these numbers is irrational?
- 0.25
- $\sqrt{25}$
* $\sqrt{7}$
- $\dfrac{22}{7}$
W: 7 is not a perfect square, so $\sqrt{7}$ is irrational. The others are rational.

Q: The decimal expansion of $\dfrac{3}{8}$ is
- 0.38
* 0.375
- 0.3777...
- 0.3125
W: $3 \div 8 = 0.375$, which terminates.

Q: The decimal expansion of $\dfrac{1}{7}$ is
- terminating
* non-terminating and repeating
- non-terminating and non-repeating
- 0.17
W: $\dfrac{1}{7} = 0.142857142857\ldots$, which repeats.

Q: The value of $(\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{2})(\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2})$ is
- 1
- 7
* 3
- $\sqrt{3}$
W: $(\sqrt{5})^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 5 - 2 = 3$.

Q: The value of $\sqrt{0.0004}$ is
- 0.2
* 0.02
- 0.002
- 0.04
W: $0.02^2 = 0.0004$.
