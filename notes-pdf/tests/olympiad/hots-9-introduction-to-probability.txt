Q: A coin is tossed twice. The probability of getting two heads is
* $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- 1
W: The outcomes are HH, HT, TH and TT, and only HH has two heads.

Q: A die is thrown once. The probability of getting a multiple of 3 is
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
* $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
W: The multiples are 3 and 6, so $\dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

Q: Two dice are thrown together. The probability that the sum is 12 is
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
* $\dfrac{1}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
W: Only (6, 6) gives a sum of 12.

Q: A letter is chosen at random from the word MATHEMATICS. The probability that it is the letter T is
- $\dfrac{1}{11}$
* $\dfrac{2}{11}$
- $\dfrac{3}{11}$
- $\dfrac{4}{11}$
W: The word has 11 letters and T appears twice.

Q: A bag has 2 red and 3 blue balls. The probability of drawing a blue ball is
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
* $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
W: $\dfrac{3}{2 + 3} = \dfrac{3}{5}$.

Q: If $P(E) = 0.7$, then $P(\text{not } E) =$
- 0.7
- 0.07
* 0.3
- 1.7
W: $P(\text{not } E) = 1 - 0.7 = 0.3$.

Q: A die is thrown once. The probability of getting a prime number is
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
* $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
W: The primes are 2, 3 and 5, so $\dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: Two coins are tossed together. The probability that the two faces are different is
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
* $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- 1
W: The outcomes HT and TH are the different ones, so $\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
