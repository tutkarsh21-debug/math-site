Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 3x - 4$, then $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 =$
- 1
- 9
* 17
- 25
W: $\alpha + \beta = 3$ and $\alpha\beta = -4$, so $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = 9 + 8 = 17$.

Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $2x^2 - 8x + 6$, then $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta} =$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
* $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- 4
- 3
W: The sum is 4 and the product is 3, so the value is $\dfrac{4}{3}$.

Q: If one zero of $4x^2 - 17x + k$ is the reciprocal of the other, then $k =$
- 1
- 2
* 4
- 17
W: The product of the zeroes is $\dfrac{k}{4} = 1$, so $k = 4$.

Q: The sum of the zeroes of $x^2 - (k + 1)x + 5$ equals their product. Then $k =$
- 3
* 4
- 5
- 6
W: Sum $= k + 1$ and product $= 5$, so $k + 1 = 5$ and $k = 4$.

Q: A quadratic polynomial with zeroes 3 and $-2$ is
- $x^2 + x - 6$
* $x^2 - x - 6$
- $x^2 - x + 6$
- $x^2 + 5x - 6$
W: Sum $= 1$ and product $= -6$, so the polynomial is $x^2 - x - 6$.

Q: The graph of a cubic polynomial cuts the x-axis at three different points. How many zeroes does the polynomial have?
- 1
- 2
* 3
- 4
W: The number of zeroes is the number of points where the graph meets the x-axis.

Q: If $-1$ is a zero of $x^2 + 3x + k$, then $k =$
- $-2$
- 1
* 2
- 4
W: $1 - 3 + k = 0$ gives $k = 2$.

Q: The remainder when $x^3 + x^2 + x + 1$ is divided by $x - 1$ is
- 0
- 2
* 4
- 8
W: The remainder is the value at $x = 1$: $1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4$.
