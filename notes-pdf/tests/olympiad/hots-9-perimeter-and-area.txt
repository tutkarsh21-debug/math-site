Q: The area of a triangle with sides 5 cm, 12 cm and 13 cm is
- 15 square cm
* 30 square cm
- 60 square cm
- 65 square cm
W: $s = 15$ and the area is $\sqrt{15 \times 10 \times 3 \times 2} = 30$. (The triangle is right-angled, so also $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 12 = 30$.)

Q: The area of an equilateral triangle of side 6 cm is
- $6\sqrt{3}$ square cm
* $9\sqrt{3}$ square cm
- $12\sqrt{3}$ square cm
- $36\sqrt{3}$ square cm
W: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 36 = 9\sqrt{3}$.

Q: The circumference of a circle is 44 cm. Its area is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 77 square cm
* 154 square cm
- 308 square cm
- 616 square cm
W: $2\pi r = 44$ gives $r = 7$, and the area is $\dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 = 154$.

Q: A rhombus has diagonals of 10 cm and 24 cm. Its side is
- 12 cm
* 13 cm
- 14 cm
- 17 cm
W: The half diagonals are 5 and 12, so the side is $\sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$ cm.

Q: A square has a diagonal of 8 cm. Its area is
- 16 square cm
* 32 square cm
- 64 square cm
- 8 square cm
W: Area $= \dfrac{d^2}{2} = \dfrac{64}{2} = 32$.

Q: A parallelogram has base 15 cm and height 8 cm. Its area is
- 23 square cm
- 60 square cm
* 120 square cm
- 240 square cm
W: $15 \times 8 = 120$.

Q: A wire of length 88 cm is bent into a circle. Its radius is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 7 cm
* 14 cm
- 21 cm
- 28 cm
W: $2\pi r = 88$ gives $r = 14$.

Q: The area of a right triangle with legs 9 cm and 12 cm is
- 21 square cm
* 54 square cm
- 108 square cm
- 45 square cm
W: Area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 12 = 54$.
