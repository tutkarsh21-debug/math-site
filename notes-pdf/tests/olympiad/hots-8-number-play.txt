Q: The sum of three consecutive numbers $n - 1$, $n$ and $n + 1$ is
- $n$
- $2n$
* $3n$
- $3n + 1$
W: $(n - 1) + n + (n + 1) = 3n$.

Q: The digit $*$ for which $42{*}6$ is divisible by 9 is
- 3
* 6
- 7
- 9
W: $4 + 2 + * + 6 = 12 + *$ must be 18, so $* = 6$.

Q: The remainder when 1234 is divided by 9 is
- 0
* 1
- 2
- 3
W: The digit sum is $1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10$, and $1 + 0 = 1$.

Q: The digital root of 9876 is
* 3
- 6
- 9
- 30
W: $9 + 8 + 7 + 6 = 30$ and $3 + 0 = 3$.

Q: Which of these numbers is divisible by 11?
- 2341
* 1342
- 1352
- 5231
W: For 1342: $(1 + 4) - (3 + 2) = 0$. The other numbers give a difference that is not a multiple of 11.

Q: The difference between a two-digit number and the number formed by reversing its digits is always a multiple of
- 7
* 9
- 10
- 11
W: $(10a + b) - (10b + a) = 9(a - b)$.

Q: The sum of an odd number and an even number is
- always even
* always odd
- sometimes odd, sometimes even
- always a multiple of 3
W: For example $3 + 4 = 7$. In general $(2m + 1) + 2n = 2(m + n) + 1$.

Q: The smallest three-digit number divisible by 9 is
- 99
- 100
* 108
- 111
W: $9 \times 12 = 108$, and $99$ has only two digits.
