Q: Find 15% of 200.
- 20
* 30
- 35
- 40
W: $0.15 \times 200 = 30$.

Q: The fraction $\dfrac{3}{5}$ written as a percentage is
- 30%
- 35%
* 60%
- 65%
W: $\dfrac{3}{5} \times 100 = 60$.

Q: 20 is what percent of 80?
- 20%
* 25%
- 30%
- 40%
W: $\dfrac{20}{80} \times 100 = 25$.

Q: An article bought for Rs 200 is sold for Rs 250. The profit percent is
- 20%
* 25%
- 30%
- 50%
W: Profit $= 50$, and $\dfrac{50}{200} \times 100 = 25$.

Q: An article marked at Rs 500 is sold at a discount of 20%. The selling price is
- Rs 300
* Rs 400
- Rs 450
- Rs 480
W: $500 \times 0.8 = 400$.

Q: A price of Rs 1000 increases by 10%. The new price is
- Rs 1010
* Rs 1100
- Rs 1110
- Rs 1200
W: $1000 \times 1.1 = 1100$.

Q: Rs 5000 grows at 10% per year, compounded yearly. The amount after 2 years is
- Rs 5500
- Rs 6000
* Rs 6050
- Rs 6500
W: $5000 \times 1.1 \times 1.1 = 6050$.

Q: After a discount of 20% an article costs Rs 960. Its marked price was
- Rs 1000
- Rs 1100
* Rs 1200
- Rs 1250
W: $\text{marked price} \times 0.8 = 960$, so the marked price is $\dfrac{960}{0.8} = 1200$.
