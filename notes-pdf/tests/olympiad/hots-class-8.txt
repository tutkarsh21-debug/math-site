Q: Find the smallest number by which 245 must be multiplied to get a perfect square.
- 3
- 7
* 5
- 35
W: $245 = 5 \times 7^2$. Only the prime 5 has an odd power, so multiply by 5 to get $1225 = 35^2$.

Q: Find the smallest number by which 1372 must be divided to give a perfect cube.
- 2
* 4
- 7
- 14
W: $1372 = 2^2 \times 7^3$. Remove $2^2 = 4$ and the quotient is $343 = 7^3$.

Q: The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 7. If 9 is added to the number, its digits are reversed. Find the number.
- 25
- 16
* 34
- 43
W: Let the digits be $a$ and $b$. $a + b = 7$ and $10a + b + 9 = 10b + a$ gives $b - a = 1$. So $a = 3$ and $b = 4$.

Q: The denominator of a fraction is 3 more than its numerator. If 1 is added to both, the fraction becomes $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Find the fraction.
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
* $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{6}{9}$
- $\dfrac{3}{6}$
W: $\dfrac{n + 1}{n + 4} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ gives $3n + 3 = 2n + 8$, so $n = 5$ and the fraction is $\dfrac{5}{8}$.

Q: If $a + \dfrac{1}{a} = 4$, then $a^2 + \dfrac{1}{a^2} =$
- 8
- 12
* 14
- 16
W: Squaring gives $a^2 + 2 + \dfrac{1}{a^2} = 16$, so $a^2 + \dfrac{1}{a^2} = 14$.

Q: Which is the complete factorisation of $x^4 - 16$?
- $(x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4)$
* $(x - 2)(x + 2)(x^2 + 4)$
- $(x - 4)(x + 4)(x^2 + 1)$
- $(x - 2)^2 (x + 2)^2$
W: $x^4 - 16 = (x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4)$ and $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$. The factor $x^2 + 4$ cannot be factorised further.

Q: A shopkeeper sells two articles for Rs 1200 each, one at a 20% gain and the other at a 20% loss. What is his overall result?
- No gain, no loss
- A gain of 4%
* A loss of 4%
- A loss of 2%
W: The costs are Rs 1000 and Rs 1500, so the total cost is Rs 2500 against Rs 2400 received. Loss $= \dfrac{100}{2500} \times 100 = 4\%$.

Q: Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is $36^\circ$. How many sides does it have?
- 8
- 9
* 10
- 12
W: The exterior angles add up to $360^\circ$, so the number of sides is $\dfrac{360}{36} = 10$.

Q: The mean of 4 numbers is 15. Each number is multiplied by 2 and then 1 is added. What is the new mean?
- 30
* 31
- 32
- 16
W: Doubling gives a mean of 30, and adding 1 gives 31.

Q: A bag has 3 red, 5 blue and 2 green balls. A ball is picked at random. What is the probability that it is not blue?
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
* $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
W: There are $3 + 2 = 5$ balls that are not blue out of 10, so the probability is $\dfrac{5}{10} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
