Q: Which diagram best shows Mango, Fruit and Food?
- Three separate circles
- Two overlapping circles and a third apart
* Three circles, each inside the next
- Three circles that all overlap in one region
W: Every mango is a fruit and every fruit is food, so each circle lies inside the next.

Q: In a class of 40, 25 like tea, 20 like coffee and 5 like neither. How many like both?
- 5
- 8
* 10
- 15
W: At least one: $40 - 5 = 35$. Both $= 25 + 20 - 35 = 10$.

Q: Of 30 students, 18 play cricket, 15 play football and 5 play neither. How many play both?
- 6
* 8
- 10
- 12
W: At least one: $30 - 5 = 25$. Both $= 18 + 15 - 25 = 8$.

Q: $n(A) = 35$, $n(B) = 25$ and $n(A \cap B) = 10$. Find $n(A \cup B)$.
- 60
- 45
* 50
- 70
W: $35 + 25 - 10 = 50$.

Q: $n(A) = 42$ and $n(A \cap B) = 17$. How many belong to $A$ only?
- 17
- 59
- 35
* 25
W: Only $A$: $42 - 17 = 25$.

Q: Which diagram shows Dog, Cat and Animal?
- Three circles, each inside the next
- Three circles that overlap
* Two separate circles inside a bigger circle
- Three separate circles
W: A dog and a cat are different animals, so there are two separate circles inside the circle of animals.

Q: All roses are flowers. Some flowers are red. Which conclusion follows?
- Some roses are red
- All roses are red
- No rose is red
* None of these follows
W: The red flowers may be flowers other than roses, so we cannot say anything about red roses.

Q: In two overlapping circles, the part of A only has 18 members, the part of B only has 22, the overlap has 7, and 3 are outside both. How many belong to exactly one of A and B?
- 29
- 47
* 40
- 43
W: $18 + 22 = 40$.

Q: Of 120 people, 70 like tea, 60 like coffee and 20 like neither. How many like both?
- 10
- 20
* 30
- 40
W: At least one: $120 - 20 = 100$. Both $= 70 + 60 - 100 = 30$.

Q: The circle of Doctors lies completely inside the circle of Graduates. Which statement is true?
- All graduates are doctors
* All doctors are graduates
- No doctor is a graduate
- Some graduates are not doctors, but no doctor is a graduate
W: A circle inside another circle means all of the first group belong to the second group.
