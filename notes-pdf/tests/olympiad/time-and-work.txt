Q: A can finish a job in 12 days. What part of the job does A do in one day?
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
* $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}$
- 12
W: In one day A does $\dfrac{1}{12}$ of the job.

Q: A can do a job in 20 days and B in 30 days. How many days will they take together?
- 10
* 12
- 15
- 25
W: One day: $\dfrac{1}{20} + \dfrac{1}{30} = \dfrac{5}{60} = \dfrac{1}{12}$.

Q: A and B together do a job in 8 days. A alone does it in 12 days. How many days will B take alone?
- 16
- 20
* 24
- 30
W: B's one day $= \dfrac{1}{8} - \dfrac{1}{12} = \dfrac{1}{24}$.

Q: 10 men can finish a job in 12 days. How many days will 15 men take?
- 6
* 8
- 9
- 10
W: Work $= 120$ man-days, and $\dfrac{120}{15} = 8$.

Q: A can do a job in 15 days and B in 10 days. They work together for 3 days and then A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the rest?
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 6
W: Together they do $\dfrac{1}{15} + \dfrac{1}{10} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ per day, so in 3 days they do $\dfrac{1}{2}$. B does the other half in $5$ days.

Q: A pipe fills a tank in 4 hours and another pipe empties the full tank in 8 hours. If both are open, how long does it take to fill the tank?
- 4 hours
- 6 hours
* 8 hours
- 12 hours
W: Net in one hour $= \dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

Q: A can do a job in 4 days and B can do it in 12 days. They are paid Rs 800 for working together. What is A's share?
- Rs 400
* Rs 600
- Rs 650
- Rs 700
W: The ratio of their one-day work is $\dfrac{1}{4} : \dfrac{1}{12} = 3 : 1$, so A gets $\dfrac{3}{4} \times 800 = 600$.

Q: A can do a job in 24 days. B is twice as fast as A. How long will they take together?
- 6 days
* 8 days
- 12 days
- 16 days
W: B takes 12 days. One day: $\dfrac{1}{24} + \dfrac{1}{12} = \dfrac{3}{24} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

Q: One pipe fills a tank in 6 hours and another fills it in 3 hours. How long will they take together?
- 1 hour
* 2 hours
- 3 hours
- 4.5 hours
W: One hour: $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: A does $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a job in 5 days. How long will A take for the whole job?
- 10 days
* 15 days
- 18 days
- 20 days
W: The whole job takes $3 \times 5 = 15$ days.
