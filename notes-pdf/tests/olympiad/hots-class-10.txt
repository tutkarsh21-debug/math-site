Q: The HCF of two numbers is 9 and their LCM is 360. If one number is 72, the other number is
- 40
* 45
- 54
- 90
W: The product of the two numbers $= 9 \times 360 = 3240$, and $\dfrac{3240}{72} = 45$.

Q: The largest number that divides 245 and 1029 leaving remainder 5 in each case is
- 8
- 12
* 16
- 20
W: The number is the HCF of $240$ and $1024$, which is 16.

Q: The equation $x^2 + kx + 16 = 0$ has equal roots. The values of $k$ are
- 4 only
- $-8$ only
* $8$ or $-8$
- 16 or $-16$
W: $D = k^2 - 64 = 0$, so $k = \pm 8$.

Q: The sum of the squares of two consecutive positive odd numbers is 290. The larger number is
- 9
- 11
* 13
- 15
W: The numbers are 11 and 13, because $121 + 169 = 290$.

Q: The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $3n^2 + 5n$. Its 20th term is
- 118
- 120
* 122
- 125
W: The $n$th term is $S_n - S_{n-1} = 6n + 2$, so $a_{20} = 122$.

Q: In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC, AD = 3 cm and DB = 5 cm. If the area of triangle ADE is 18 square cm, the area of triangle ABC is
- 48 square cm
- 96 square cm
* 128 square cm
- 144 square cm
W: Area ratio $= \left(\dfrac{3}{8}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{64}$, so the area is $18 \times \dfrac{64}{9} = 128$.

Q: The x-axis divides the line segment joining $(2, -3)$ and $(5, 6)$ in the ratio
- $2 : 1$
* $1 : 2$
- $1 : 3$
- $3 : 1$
W: With ratio $k : 1$, the y-coordinate $\dfrac{6k - 3}{k + 1} = 0$ gives $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: If $\tan\theta = \dfrac{3}{4}$, then $\sin\theta \cos\theta =$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{7}{25}$
* $\dfrac{12}{25}$
- $\dfrac{24}{25}$
W: $\sin\theta = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\cos\theta = \dfrac{4}{5}$, so the product is $\dfrac{12}{25}$.

Q: From the top of a building 60 m high the angles of depression of the top and the bottom of a tower are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. The height of the tower is
- 20 m
- 30 m
* 40 m
- 50 m
W: The tower is $20\sqrt{3}$ m away, and the top is $20$ m below the observer. The height is $60 - 20 = 40$ m.

Q: Two dice are thrown together. The probability that the sum is a prime number is
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{18}$
* $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: There are $1 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 2 = 15$ favourable outcomes out of 36, so the probability is $\dfrac{15}{36} = \dfrac{5}{12}$.
