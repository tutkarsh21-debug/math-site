Q: Find 30% of 250.
- 65
* 75
- 80
- 85
W: $\dfrac{30}{100} \times 250 = 75$.

Q: Express $\dfrac{3}{8}$ as a percentage.
- 33.3%
- 35%
* 37.5%
- 38.5%
W: $\dfrac{3}{8} \times 100 = 37.5$.

Q: 40 is what percentage of 160?
- 20%
* 25%
- 30%
- 40%
W: $\dfrac{40}{160} \times 100 = 25$.

Q: A quantity increases from 50 to 65. Find the percentage increase.
- 15%
- 25%
* 30%
- 35%
W: Change $= 15$, and $\dfrac{15}{50} \times 100 = 30$.

Q: After a 20% decrease the price of a bag is Rs 400. What was the original price?
- Rs 420
- Rs 450
- Rs 480
* Rs 500
W: The price is $0.8$ of the original, so the original is $400 \div 0.8 = 500$.

Q: A price is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net change?
- 5% increase
- 5% decrease
* No change
- 2% increase
W: $1.25 \times 0.8 = 1$, so the price is the same.

Q: Find 12.5% of 640.
- 64
* 80
- 90
- 100
W: $12.5\% = \dfrac{1}{8}$ and $640 \div 8 = 80$.

Q: A class has 40 students and 55% of them are girls. How many boys are there?
- 16
* 18
- 20
- 22
W: Boys are $45\%$ of 40, which is $18$.

Q: 15% of a number is 45. Find the number.
- 250
* 300
- 350
- 400
W: Number $= 45 \div 0.15 = 300$.

Q: A price of Rs 200 rises by 10% and then rises by 10% again. What is the final price?
- Rs 240
* Rs 242
- Rs 244
- Rs 220
W: $200 \times 1.1 \times 1.1 = 242$.
