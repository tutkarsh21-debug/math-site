Q: A boy faces south and turns left. Which direction does he face?
- West
* East
- North
- South-east
W: Facing south, the left hand points east, so a left turn gives east.

Q: A girl faces north and turns left. Which direction does she face?
* West
- East
- South
- North-west
W: A left turn is anticlockwise, and from north that gives west.

Q: A man walks 12 km north and then 5 km east. How far is he from the start?
- 7 km
- 15 km
* 13 km
- 17 km
W: $\sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{169} = 13$ km.

Q: Ria walks 6 km west and then 8 km north. How far is she from the start?
- 8 km
- 12 km
- 14 km
* 10 km
W: $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10$ km.

Q: A boy faces east. He turns left and then left again. Which direction does he face?
- North
* West
- South
- East
W: East to north to west with two left turns.

Q: At sunset the shadow of a tall pillar falls towards which direction?
- West
- North
* East
- South
W: At sunset the sun is in the west, so the shadow falls towards the east.

Q: Sam walks 7 km east, then 3 km north, then 7 km west. Where is he from the start?
- 3 km south
* 3 km north
- 7 km north
- 17 km north-east
W: The 7 km east and 7 km west cancel. He is 3 km north of the start.

Q: A girl faces north-west and turns 90° anticlockwise. Which direction does she face?
- North-east
- South-east
* South-west
- West
W: Anticlockwise from north-west goes to west and then to south-west.

Q: A boy faces north. He turns right, then left, then left. Which direction does he face?
- North
- East
- South
* West
W: North to east (right), east to north (left), north to west (left).

Q: A man walks 9 m south and then 12 m east. How far is he from the start?
- 3 m
- 21 m
- 14 m
* 15 m
W: $\sqrt{9^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{225} = 15$ m.
