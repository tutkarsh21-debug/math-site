Q: The distance between $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 6)$ is
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 7
W: $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.

Q: The point on the x-axis equidistant from $(1, 2)$ and $(5, 2)$ is
- $(2, 0)$
* $(3, 0)$
- $(4, 0)$
- $(0, 3)$
W: $(x - 1)^2 = (x - 5)^2$ gives $-2x + 1 = -10x + 25$, so $x = 3$.

Q: The triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(3, 4)$ and $(-3, 4)$ is
- equilateral
* isosceles
- right-angled
- scalene
W: The sides are 5, 5 and 6, so two sides are equal.

Q: The point $(a, a + 1)$ is 5 units from the origin and $a > 0$. Then $a =$
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 5
W: $a^2 + (a + 1)^2 = 25$ gives $a^2 + a - 12 = 0$, so $a = 3$ (or $-4$).

Q: The perimeter of the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(4, 0)$ and $(4, 3)$ is
- 7
- 10
* 12
- 14
W: The sides are 4, 3 and 5.

Q: The distance between $(-2, -3)$ and $(2, 0)$ is
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 7
W: $\sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$.

Q: Which point is at distance 5 from the origin?
- $(2, 3)$
* $(3, -4)$
- $(4, 4)$
- $(1, 5)$
W: $3^2 + 4^2 = 25$.

Q: The points $(0, 0)$, $(2, 2)$ and $(4, 4)$ are
- the vertices of an equilateral triangle
- the vertices of a right triangle
* collinear
- the vertices of a square
W: They all lie on the line $y = x$, and $\sqrt{8} + \sqrt{8} = \sqrt{32}$.
