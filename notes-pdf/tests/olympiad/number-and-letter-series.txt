Q: What comes next? 6, 11, 16, 21, ?
- 24
* 26
- 27
- 31
W: The difference is 5 each time, so the next term is $21 + 5 = 26$.

Q: What comes next? 3, 12, 48, 192, ?
- 384
- 576
* 768
- 960
W: Each term is 4 times the one before. $192 \times 4 = 768$.

Q: What comes next? 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, ?
- 48
* 50
- 52
- 63
W: The terms are $n^2 + 1$. The next is $7^2 + 1 = 50$.

Q: Find the missing term: 5, 6, 8, 11, 15, ?, 26
- 18
- 19
* 20
- 21
W: The differences are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and then 6. So the missing term is $15 + 5 = 20$, and $20 + 6 = 26$ confirms it.

Q: Find the next term: 4, 6, 9, 13, 18, ?
- 22
* 24
- 25
- 27
W: The differences are 2, 3, 4, 5, so the next difference is 6 and the term is $18 + 6 = 24$.

Q: Find the next term: 1, 8, 27, 64, ?
- 100
- 121
- 128
* 125
W: These are the cubes of 1, 2, 3, 4. The next is $5^3 = 125$.

Q: Find the next term: 7, 14, 12, 24, 22, 44, ?
- 46
- 88
* 42
- 66
W: The rule alternates: multiply by 2, then subtract 2. So $44 - 2 = 42$.

Q: Find the next term: C, F, I, L, ?
- M
- N
* O
- P
W: The positions are 3, 6, 9, 12, so the next is 15, which is O.

Q: Find the next term: AB, DE, GH, JK, ?
- LM
* MN
- NO
- MO
W: Each pair moves forward by 3 letters: JK becomes MN.

Q: One number in this series is wrong: 2, 4, 8, 16, 30, 64. Which one should be replaced?
- 16
* 30
- 8
- 64
W: Each term is double the one before. The term after 16 should be 32, so 30 is wrong.
