Q: The square root of 1764 is
- 32
- 38
* 42
- 44
W: $1764 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 7^2$, so the root is $2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$.

Q: The cube root of 2744 is
- 12
* 14
- 16
- 18
W: $14^3 = 2744$.

Q: The smallest number by which 50 must be multiplied to get a perfect square is
* 2
- 5
- 10
- 25
W: $50 = 2 \times 5^2$, so multiplying by 2 gives $100 = 10^2$.

Q: Which of these cannot be a perfect square?
- 1521
- 2025
* 2042
- 1296
W: A perfect square never ends in 2.

Q: The sum of the first 12 odd numbers is
- 24
- 78
* 144
- 156
W: $12^2 = 144$.

Q: The number of integers between $9^2$ and $10^2$ is
- 9
- 10
* 18
- 19
W: There are $2 \times 9 = 18$ numbers between $n^2$ and $(n + 1)^2$.

Q: The cube root of 0.027 is
- 0.03
* 0.3
- 0.9
- 3
W: $0.3^3 = 0.027$.

Q: The value of $15^2$ is
- 215
- 220
* 225
- 235
W: $15^2 = 225$.
