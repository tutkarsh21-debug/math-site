Q: Two similar triangles have areas in the ratio $4 : 9$. A side of the smaller one is 6 cm. The corresponding side of the larger one is
- 8 cm
* 9 cm
- 13.5 cm
- 24 cm
W: The ratio of the sides is $2 : 3$, so the side is $6 \times \dfrac{3}{2} = 9$ cm.

Q: In a right triangle the legs are 6 cm and 8 cm. The altitude to the hypotenuse is
- 4 cm
* 4.8 cm
- 5 cm
- 5.6 cm
W: The hypotenuse is 10 cm, and the altitude is $\dfrac{6 \times 8}{10} = 4.8$ cm.

Q: A 17 m ladder reaches a height of 15 m on a wall. Its top slips down 7 m. How far does the foot move away from the wall?
- 5 m
- 6 m
* 7 m
- 8 m
W: The foot was $\sqrt{289 - 225} = 8$ m away. The new height is 8 m, so the foot is $\sqrt{289 - 64} = 15$ m away. It moves $15 - 8 = 7$ m.

Q: In a trapezium ABCD with AB parallel to DC, the diagonals meet at O. If $AB = 5$ cm and $CD = 15$ cm, the ratio of the areas of triangles AOB and COD is
- $1 : 3$
* $1 : 9$
- $1 : 6$
- $5 : 15$
W: The areas are in the ratio of the squares of $5 : 15$, which is $1 : 9$.

Q: In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC and $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. If $AE = 4$ cm, then $EC =$
- 4 cm
- 5 cm
* 6 cm
- 8 cm
W: $\dfrac{AE}{EC} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so $EC = 4 \times \dfrac{3}{2} = 6$ cm.

Q: The areas of two similar triangles are in the ratio $16 : 25$. The ratio of their corresponding sides is
- $16 : 25$
* $4 : 5$
- $8 : 10$
- $2 : 5$
W: The ratio of the sides is the square root of the ratio of the areas.

Q: Two similar triangles have areas 9 square cm and 16 square cm. The perimeter of the smaller is 18 cm. The perimeter of the larger is
- 20 cm
- 22 cm
* 24 cm
- 32 cm
W: The ratio of the sides (and perimeters) is $3 : 4$, so the perimeter is $18 \times \dfrac{4}{3} = 24$ cm.

Q: A boy 1.5 m tall casts a shadow 2 m long. At the same time a tree casts a shadow 12 m long. The height of the tree is
- 6 m
- 8 m
* 9 m
- 10 m
W: The triangles are similar, so $\dfrac{h}{12} = \dfrac{1.5}{2}$ and $h = 9$ m.
