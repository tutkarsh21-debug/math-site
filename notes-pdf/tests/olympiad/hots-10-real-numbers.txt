Q: Three bells ring at intervals of 10, 15 and 25 minutes. After how many minutes do they ring together again?
- 50
- 75
* 150
- 300
W: The LCM of 10, 15 and 25 is $2 \times 3 \times 5^2 = 150$.

Q: The HCF of 135 and 225 is
- 15
- 25
* 45
- 75
W: $135 = 3^3 \times 5$ and $225 = 3^2 \times 5^2$, so $\text{HCF} = 3^2 \times 5 = 45$.

Q: The smallest number which leaves remainder 7 when divided by 9, 15 and 21 is
- 315
* 322
- 630
- 637
W: $\text{LCM}(9, 15, 21) = 315$, so the number is $315 + 7 = 322$.

Q: Which of these fractions has a terminating decimal expansion?
- $\dfrac{13}{30}$
- $\dfrac{17}{12}$
* $\dfrac{13}{3125}$
- $\dfrac{11}{21}$
W: $3125 = 5^5$, so the denominator has only the prime factor 5. The others have 3 or 7 in the denominator.

Q: The HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 84. If one number is 42, the other is
- 6
* 12
- 14
- 24
W: Product $= 6 \times 84 = 504$, and $\dfrac{504}{42} = 12$.

Q: Which of these is irrational?
- $7 + \sqrt{16}$
- $\dfrac{22}{7}$
* $7 + \sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{49}$
W: $7 + \sqrt{3}$ is a rational number plus an irrational number, so it is irrational. The others are rational.

Q: The largest number that divides 1251, 9377 and 15628 leaving remainders 1, 2 and 3 respectively is
- 125
- 250
* 625
- 1250
W: It is the HCF of $1250$, $9375$ and $15625$, which is 625.

Q: For which natural numbers $n$ does $6^n$ end with the digit 0?
- All $n$
- Even $n$ only
- Only $n = 5$
* No natural number $n$
W: $6^n = 2^n \times 3^n$ has no factor 5, so it can never end in 0.
