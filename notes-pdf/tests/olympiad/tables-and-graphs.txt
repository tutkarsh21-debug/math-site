Q: The number of pencils sold in 5 weeks was 12, 18, 15, 21 and 9. Find the total number sold.
- 65
- 70
* 75
- 80
W: $12 + 18 + 15 + 21 + 9 = 75$.

Q: For the data 12, 18, 15, 21 and 9, find the average number sold per week.
- 12
- 14
* 15
- 16
W: $\dfrac{75}{5} = 15$.

Q: For the data 12, 18, 15, 21 and 9 (weeks 1 to 5), in which week were the most pencils sold?
- Week 2
- Week 3
* Week 4
- Week 5
W: The largest value, 21, is in week 4.

Q: For the same data, find the percentage increase from week 1 to week 2.
- 33%
* 50%
- 60%
- 75%
W: Change $= 6$, and $\dfrac{6}{12} \times 100 = 50$.

Q: What angle of a pie chart stands for 20% of the data?
- $36^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
* $72^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
W: $20\% \times 360^\circ = 72^\circ$.

Q: What angle of a pie chart stands for 15% of the data?
- $45^\circ$
* $54^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $72^\circ$
W: $1\% = 3.6^\circ$, so $15\% = 54^\circ$.

Q: In a pie chart of total expenses of Rs 50 000, one sector has an angle of $144^\circ$. How much is this sector worth?
- Rs 15 000
* Rs 20 000
- Rs 25 000
- Rs 30 000
W: $\dfrac{144}{360} \times 50\,000 = 20\,000$.

Q: In a bar graph, the bar for A is 20 units high and the bar for B is 30 units high. What is the ratio $A : B$?
- $3 : 2$
* $2 : 3$
- $1 : 2$
- $4 : 5$
W: $20 : 30 = 2 : 3$.

Q: For the data 12, 18, 15, 21 and 9, what is the difference between the sales in week 2 and in week 4?
- 2
* 3
- 6
- 9
W: $21 - 18 = 3$.

Q: Which kind of graph is best for showing how a plant's height changes over several weeks?
- Pie chart
- Bar graph with one bar
* Line graph
- Table with one row
W: A line graph joins the points and shows how a value changes over time.
