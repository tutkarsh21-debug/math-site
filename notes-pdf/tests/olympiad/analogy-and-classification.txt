Q: Eye : See :: Ear : ?
- Sound
- Listen
* Hear
- Nose
W: The eye is used to see. The ear is used to hear.

Q: Calf : Cow :: Kitten : ?
- Dog
* Cat
- Lion
- Puppy
W: A calf is the young one of a cow. A kitten is the young one of a cat.

Q: 5 : 25 :: 12 : ?
- 24
- 60
* 144
- 121
W: The second number is the square of the first. $12^2 = 144$.

Q: 2 : 9 :: 3 : ?
- 12
- 27
* 28
- 36
W: The rule is cube plus 1: $2^3 + 1 = 9$, so $3^3 + 1 = 28$.

Q: Which number is the odd one out? 9, 16, 25, 30, 36
- 9
- 16
* 30
- 36
W: 9, 16, 25 and 36 are perfect squares. 30 is not.

Q: Which is the odd one out? Delhi, Mumbai, Paris, Chennai
- Delhi
- Mumbai
* Paris
- Chennai
W: Delhi, Mumbai and Chennai are cities in India. Paris is in France.

Q: Which letter pair is the odd one out? BD, CE, DF, EH
- BD
- CE
- DF
* EH
W: In BD, CE and DF the second letter is two places after the first. In EH it is three places after.

Q: LAMP : MBNQ :: DESK : ?
- EFSL
* EFTL
- FGTM
- DFTL
W: Every letter moves forward by one: D to E, E to F, S to T, K to L.

Q: Page : Book :: Wheel : ?
- Road
* Car
- Petrol
- Driver
W: A page is a part of a book. A wheel is a part of a car.

Q: 3 : 27 :: 4 : ?
- 16
- 48
* 64
- 81
W: The second number is the cube of the first. $4^3 = 64$.
