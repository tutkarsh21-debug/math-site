@minutes 12
Q: The number of vertices of a cube is
- 6
* 8
- 10
- 12
W: A cube has 8 corners.

Q: A square pyramid has 5 vertices and 5 faces. The number of edges is
- 6
- 7
* 8
- 10
W: $5 - E + 5 = 2$ gives $E = 8$.

Q: The number of triangles left in a Sierpinski triangle after 2 steps is
- 4
- 6
* 9
- 16
W: Each step multiplies the number by 3: $3 \times 3 = 9$.

Q: The top view of a cone standing on its circular base is
- a triangle
- a rectangle
* a circle
- a square
W: Looking from above you see the circular base with the tip at the centre.

Q: The number of faces of a triangular prism is
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 6
W: Two triangles and three rectangles make 5 faces.

Q: A segment of length 1 goes through one Koch step. Its new length is
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- 1
* $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{9}$
W: It is replaced by 4 pieces of length $\dfrac{1}{3}$ each.

Q: The number of squares in the net of a cube is
- 4
- 5
* 6
- 8
W: A cube has 6 square faces.

Q: For any convex solid, $V - E + F$ equals
- 0
- 1
* 2
- 3
W: This is Euler's relation.
