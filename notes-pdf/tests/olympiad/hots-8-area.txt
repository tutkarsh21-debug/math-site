Q: The area of a rectangle 9 m by 7 m is
- 16 square m
- 32 square m
* 63 square m
- 72 square m
W: $9 \times 7 = 63$.

Q: The area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 8 cm is
- 18 square cm
* 40 square cm
- 80 square cm
- 36 square cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 8 = 40$.

Q: The area of a parallelogram with base 15 cm and height 6 cm is
- 45 square cm
* 90 square cm
- 21 square cm
- 180 square cm
W: $15 \times 6 = 90$.

Q: The area of a trapezium with parallel sides 6 cm and 10 cm and height 5 cm is
- 30 square cm
* 40 square cm
- 50 square cm
- 80 square cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2}(6 + 10) \times 5 = 40$.

Q: The area of a rhombus with diagonals 8 cm and 6 cm is
- 14 square cm
* 24 square cm
- 48 square cm
- 28 square cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 6 = 24$.

Q: A path 1 m wide runs around the outside of a garden 10 m by 8 m. The area of the path is
- 36 square m
* 40 square m
- 44 square m
- 80 square m
W: The outer rectangle is $12 \times 10 = 120$ and the garden is 80, so the path is 40.

Q: One hectare is equal to
- 100 square m
- 1000 square m
* 10 000 square m
- 100 000 square m
W: A hectare is the area of a square of side 100 m.

Q: The perimeter of a square is 36 cm. Its area is
- 36 square cm
- 72 square cm
* 81 square cm
- 144 square cm
W: The side is 9 cm, so the area is 81.
