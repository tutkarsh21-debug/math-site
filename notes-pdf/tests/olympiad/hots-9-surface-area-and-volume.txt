- 770 cubic cm

Q: A cube of side 6 cm is cut into small cubes of side 2 cm. The number of small cubes is
- 9
- 18
* 27
- 36
W: $\dfrac{6^3}{2^3} = 27$.

Q: The total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius 7 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 154 square cm
- 308 square cm
* 462 square cm
- 616 square cm
W: $3\pi r^2 = 3 \times 154 = 462$.

Q: A sphere of radius 3 cm is melted into a cone of base radius 3 cm. The height of the cone is
- 6 cm
- 9 cm
* 12 cm
- 18 cm
W: $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi \times 27 = \dfrac{1}{3}\pi \times 9 \times h$ gives $36 = 3h$, so $h = 12$.
