Q: Rs 600 is divided between A and B in the ratio $2 : 3$. B's share is
- Rs 200
- Rs 240
* Rs 360
- Rs 400
W: One part is $\dfrac{600}{5} = 120$, so B gets $3 \times 120 = 360$.

Q: If 6 pens cost Rs 48, then 15 pens cost
- Rs 90
- Rs 100
* Rs 120
- Rs 144
W: One pen costs Rs 8, so 15 pens cost Rs 120.

Q: If $3 : 7 = 15 : x$, then $x =$
- 21
* 35
- 45
- 105
W: $3x = 7 \times 15 = 105$, so $x = 35$.

Q: The ratio of 30 minutes to 2 hours in simplest form is
- $1 : 2$
- $1 : 3$
* $1 : 4$
- $3 : 4$
W: 2 hours is 120 minutes, and $30 : 120 = 1 : 4$.

Q: The ratio of the ages of two brothers is $5 : 3$ and their sum is 48 years. The elder is
- 18 years
- 24 years
* 30 years
- 32 years
W: One part is $\dfrac{48}{8} = 6$, so the elder is $5 \times 6 = 30$.

Q: A car uses 8 litres of petrol to go 120 km. How far does it go on 20 litres?
- 240 km
- 280 km
* 300 km
- 360 km
W: It goes 15 km per litre, so on 20 litres it goes 300 km.

Q: The sides of a rectangle are in the ratio $3 : 2$ and its perimeter is 50 cm. The longer side is
- 10 cm
- 12 cm
* 15 cm
- 25 cm
W: Perimeter $= 2 \times 5$ parts $= 10$ parts $= 50$, so one part is 5 cm and the longer side is 15 cm.

Q: A recipe for 4 people uses 300 g of flour. For 6 people it needs
- 400 g
- 425 g
* 450 g
- 600 g
W: 75 g per person, so $6 \times 75 = 450$ g.
