Q: The 5th term of the AP $3, 7, 11, \ldots$ is
- 15
- 17
* 19
- 23
W: $a_5 = 3 + 4 \times 4 = 19$.

Q: The $n$th term of a sequence is $2n + 5$. Its 10th term is
- 20
* 25
- 27
- 30
W: $2 \times 10 + 5 = 25$.

Q: The 6th term of the GP $5, 10, 20, \ldots$ is
- 80
- 120
* 160
- 320
W: $5 \times 2^5 = 160$.

Q: The sum $1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + 50$ is
- 1225
* 1275
- 1325
- 2550
W: $\dfrac{50 \times 51}{2} = 1275$.

Q: Which of these is a GP?
- $2, 4, 6, 8$
- $1, 4, 9, 16$
* $3, 9, 27, 81$
- $5, 10, 15, 20$
W: In $3, 9, 27, 81$ every term is 3 times the term before.

Q: The common difference of the AP $10, 7, 4, \ldots$ is
- 3
* $-3$
- 7
- $-7$
W: $7 - 10 = -3$.

Q: The sum of the first 6 terms of the GP $1, 2, 4, \ldots$ is
- 31
- 32
* 63
- 64
W: $\dfrac{2^6 - 1}{2 - 1} = 63$.

Q: The next term of the sequence $1, 3, 6, 10, \ldots$ is
- 13
- 14
* 15
- 16
W: The differences are 2, 3, 4, so the next difference is 5 and the term is $10 + 5 = 15$.
