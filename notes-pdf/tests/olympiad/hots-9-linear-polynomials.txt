Q: The zero of the polynomial $4x - 12$ is
- $-3$
- 0
* 3
- 12
W: $4x - 12 = 0$ gives $x = 3$.

Q: The polynomial $2x + k$ has the zero $x = 5$. Then $k =$
* $-10$
- $-5$
- 5
- 10
W: $10 + k = 0$ gives $k = -10$.

Q: The line $y = 3x - 6$ cuts the x-axis at
- $(0, -6)$
- $(-2, 0)$
* $(2, 0)$
- $(6, 0)$
W: $3x - 6 = 0$ gives $x = 2$.

Q: The cost of $n$ notebooks is given by $20 + 5n$ rupees. The cost for 10 notebooks is
- Rs 50
- Rs 60
* Rs 70
- Rs 250
W: $20 + 50 = 70$.

Q: A row of $n$ triangles needs $2n + 1$ matchsticks. The number of sticks needed for 30 triangles is
- 60
* 61
- 62
- 90
W: $2 \times 30 + 1 = 61$.

Q: The zero of the polynomial $5x + 15$ is
* $-3$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- 3
- 15
W: $x = -\dfrac{15}{5} = -3$.

Q: Which of these lines passes through the origin?
- $y = 4x + 1$
- $y = x - 4$
* $y = 4x$
- $y = 4$
W: A line $y = ax + b$ passes through the origin when $b = 0$.

Q: Plan A costs $40 + 2m$ rupees and plan B costs $4m$ rupees for $m$ minutes. The costs are equal when $m =$
- 10
* 20
- 30
- 40
W: $40 + 2m = 4m$ gives $m = 20$.
