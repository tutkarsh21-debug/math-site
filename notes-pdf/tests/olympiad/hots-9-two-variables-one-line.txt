@minutes 12
Q: If $x + y = 12$ and $x - y = 4$, then $x =$
- 4
- 6
* 8
- 10
W: Adding gives $2x = 16$, so $x = 8$.

Q: The slope of the line through $(0, 1)$ and $(2, 5)$ is
- 1
* 2
- 3
- 4
W: $\dfrac{5 - 1}{2 - 0} = 2$.

Q: The line $y = 2x + 3$ passes through the point
- $(1, 4)$
* $(1, 5)$
- $(2, 5)$
- $(0, 2)$
W: At $x = 1$, $y = 2 + 3 = 5$.

Q: If $2x + y = 7$ and $x + y = 4$, then $xy =$
- 2
* 3
- 6
- 12
W: Subtracting gives $x = 3$ and then $y = 1$, so $xy = 3$.

Q: The sum of two numbers is 25 and their difference is 5. The larger number is
- 10
- 12
* 15
- 20
W: $x = \dfrac{25 + 5}{2} = 15$.

Q: The line $x + 2y = 6$ meets the y-axis at
- $(6, 0)$
- $(0, 6)$
* $(0, 3)$
- $(3, 0)$
W: Putting $x = 0$ gives $2y = 6$, so $y = 3$.

Q: The line $3x + 4y = 12$ meets the x-axis at
- $(0, 3)$
* $(4, 0)$
- $(3, 0)$
- $(0, 4)$
W: Putting $y = 0$ gives $3x = 12$, so $x = 4$.

Q: The number of solutions of the single equation $x + y = 5$ is
- 1
- 2
- 5
* infinitely many
W: Every point on the line $x + y = 5$ is a solution.
