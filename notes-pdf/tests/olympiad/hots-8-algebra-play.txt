Q: Think of a number, add 7, double it, subtract 10, halve it and subtract the number you started with. The answer is always
- 1
* 2
- 4
- 7
W: $\dfrac{2(n + 7) - 10}{2} - n = n + 2 - n = 2$.

Q: The sum of three consecutive numbers is 51. The smallest number is
- 15
* 16
- 17
- 18
W: $3n + 3 = 51$ gives $n = 16$.

Q: In a calendar, the middle date of a $3 \times 3$ square is 10. The sum of the nine dates is
- 80
- 81
* 90
- 100
W: The sum is $9 \times 10 = 90$.

Q: Two numbers add up to 16. The largest possible product is
- 48
- 60
* 64
- 72
W: The product is largest when both numbers are 8.

Q: A father is three times as old as his son and the sum of their ages is 48 years. The son is
- 10 years
* 12 years
- 14 years
- 16 years
W: $s + 3s = 48$ gives $s = 12$.

Q: Solve $4x + 3 = 19$.
- $x = 3$
* $x = 4$
- $x = 5$
- $x = 6$
W: $4x = 16$, so $x = 4$.

Q: In a number pyramid the bottom row is 1, 2, 3 and each number above is the sum of the two below it. The top number is
- 6
- 7
* 8
- 9
W: The middle row is 3 and 5, so the top is 8.

Q: The sum of three consecutive even numbers is 54. The largest is
- 16
- 18
* 20
- 22
W: The numbers are 16, 18 and 20.
