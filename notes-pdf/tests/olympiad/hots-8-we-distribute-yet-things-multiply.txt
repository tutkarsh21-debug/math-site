@minutes 12
Q: $(x + 4)(x - 4) =$
- $x^2 + 16$
- $x^2 - 8$
* $x^2 - 16$
- $x^2 + 8x - 16$
W: $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$.

Q: $(x + 5)^2 =$
- $x^2 + 25$
- $x^2 + 5x + 25$
* $x^2 + 10x + 25$
- $x^2 + 10x + 10$
W: $(x + 5)^2 = x^2 + 2 \times 5 \times x + 25$.

Q: The value of $101^2$ is
- 10 001
- 10 101
* 10 201
- 10 211
W: $(100 + 1)^2 = 10\,000 + 200 + 1$.

Q: The value of $52 \times 48$ is
- 2486
* 2496
- 2506
- 2516
W: $(50 + 2)(50 - 2) = 2500 - 4 = 2496$.

Q: If $a + b = 8$ and $ab = 12$, then $(a - b)^2 =$
- 4
- 8
* 16
- 20
W: $(a - b)^2 = (a + b)^2 - 4ab = 64 - 48 = 16$.

Q: Factorising $x^2 - 25$ gives
- $(x - 5)^2$
* $(x - 5)(x + 5)$
- $(x + 5)^2$
- $x(x - 25)$
W: $x^2 - 5^2 = (x - 5)(x + 5)$.

Q: In the expansion of $(2x + 1)^2$ the coefficient of $x$ is
- 2
* 4
- 1
- 5
W: $(2x + 1)^2 = 4x^2 + 4x + 1$.

Q: The value of $99 \times 101$ is
- 9899
- 9901
* 9999
- 10 001
W: $(100 - 1)(100 + 1) = 10\,000 - 1$.
