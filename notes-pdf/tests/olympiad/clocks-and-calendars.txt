Q: What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:00?
- 60 degrees
* 90 degrees
- 120 degrees
- 180 degrees
W: The hour hand is at 3 and the minute hand is at 12, which are 3 hour marks apart: $3 \times 30 = 90$ degrees.

Q: What is the angle between the hands at 4:30?
- 30 degrees
* 45 degrees
- 60 degrees
- 75 degrees
W: $\lvert 30 \times 4 - 5.5 \times 30 \rvert = \lvert 120 - 165 \rvert = 45$.

Q: What is the angle between the hands at 7:20?
- 80 degrees
* 100 degrees
- 110 degrees
- 130 degrees
W: $\lvert 210 - 110 \rvert = 100$.

Q: What is the angle between the hands at 12:30?
- 150 degrees
* 165 degrees
- 175 degrees
- 180 degrees
W: $\lvert 0 - 165 \rvert = 165$.

Q: How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in 12 hours?
- 10
* 11
- 12
- 13
W: In 12 hours the hands coincide 11 times, because the meeting at 12 o'clock is counted once.

Q: At what time between 4 and 5 do the hands of a clock coincide?
- 4:20
- 4:22
* 21 and 9/11 minutes past 4
- 4:25
W: The hands coincide $\dfrac{60 \times 4}{11} = 21\dfrac{9}{11}$ minutes past 4.

Q: How many odd days are there in a leap year?
- 0
- 1
* 2
- 3
W: $366 = 52 \times 7 + 2$, so there are 2 odd days.

Q: 5 March of a year is a Wednesday. What day is 26 March of the same year?
- Tuesday
* Wednesday
- Thursday
- Sunday
W: The gap is 21 days, which is exactly 3 weeks, so the day is the same.

Q: Which of these is a leap year?
- 1900
- 2100
* 2024
- 2023
W: 2024 is divisible by 4. 1900 and 2100 are century years not divisible by 400, and 2023 is not divisible by 4.

Q: Today is Friday. What day will it be after 45 days?
- Sunday
* Monday
- Tuesday
- Wednesday
W: $45 = 6 \times 7 + 3$. Three days after Friday is Monday.
