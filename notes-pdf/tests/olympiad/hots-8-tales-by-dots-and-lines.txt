Q: The mean of 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 is
- 5
* 6
- 7
- 30
W: The sum is 30 and $\dfrac{30}{5} = 6$.

Q: The median of 3, 9, 1, 7 and 5 is
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 7
W: Arranged in order: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. The middle value is 5.

Q: The mean of 4 numbers is 15. A fifth number, 20, is added. The new mean is
- 15
* 16
- 17
- 18
W: The sum becomes $60 + 20 = 80$, and $\dfrac{80}{5} = 16$.

Q: The median of 10, 20, 30 and 40 is
- 20
- 22.5
* 25
- 30
W: The median of an even number of values is the mean of the two middle ones: $\dfrac{20 + 30}{2} = 25$.

Q: The mean of 5 numbers is 10. One number, 20, is replaced by 10. The new mean is
- 7
* 8
- 9
- 10
W: The sum falls from 50 to $50 - 20 + 10 = 40$, and $\dfrac{40}{5} = 8$.

Q: The value 2 occurs 3 times and the value 4 occurs once. The mean is
- 2
* 2.5
- 3
- 3.5
W: $\dfrac{2 \times 3 + 4 \times 1}{4} = \dfrac{10}{4} = 2.5$.

Q: On a line graph a line that slopes upwards from left to right shows
* an increase
- a decrease
- no change
- a mistake in the data
W: The values are getting larger as time goes on.

Q: The mean of 7, 8 and 9 is
- 7
* 8
- 9
- 24
W: The sum is 24 and $\dfrac{24}{3} = 8$.
