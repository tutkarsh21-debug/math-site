@minutes 12
Q: An arc subtends an angle of $80^\circ$ at the centre. The angle it subtends at a point on the major arc is
- $20^\circ$
* $40^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $160^\circ$
W: The angle at the circumference is half of the angle at the centre.

Q: A chord of length 30 cm is drawn in a circle of radius 17 cm. Its distance from the centre is
- 6 cm
* 8 cm
- 10 cm
- 15 cm
W: Half the chord is 15 cm, so the distance is $\sqrt{17^2 - 15^2} = 8$ cm.

Q: In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, $\angle A = 70^\circ$. Then $\angle C =$
- $70^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
* $110^\circ$
- $140^\circ$
W: Opposite angles add up to $180^\circ$.

Q: The angle in a semicircle is
- $45^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
* $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
W: A diameter subtends a right angle at any point on the circle.

Q: Two equal chords of a circle are
* the same distance from the centre
- different distances from the centre
- always parallel
- always diameters
W: Equal chords are equally distant from the centre.

Q: AB is a diameter and C lies on the circle. If $\angle CAB = 25^\circ$, then $\angle ABC =$
- $25^\circ$
- $55^\circ$
* $65^\circ$
- $75^\circ$
W: $\angle ACB = 90^\circ$, so $\angle ABC = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 25^\circ = 65^\circ$.

Q: The exterior angle of a cyclic quadrilateral is equal to
- the adjacent interior angle
* the interior opposite angle
- twice the interior angle
- $90^\circ$
W: A side is extended, so the exterior angle and its adjacent interior angle add to $180^\circ$; the interior opposite angle also adds with it to $180^\circ$.

Q: The number of circles that can be drawn through three non-collinear points is
- 0
* 1
- 2
- Infinite
W: Exactly one circle passes through three points that are not on a line.
