Q: The reflection of $(3, 4)$ in the $x$-axis is
- $(-3, 4)$
* $(3, -4)$
- $(-3, -4)$
- $(4, 3)$
W: The sign of the $y$-co-ordinate changes.

Q: The reflection of $(-2, 5)$ in the $y$-axis is
* $(2, 5)$
- $(-2, -5)$
- $(2, -5)$
- $(5, -2)$
W: The sign of the $x$-co-ordinate changes.

Q: The reflection of $(4, -7)$ in the origin is
- $(4, 7)$
- $(-4, -7)$
* $(-4, 7)$
- $(-7, 4)$
W: Both signs change.

Q: The reflection of $(2, 3)$ in the line $x = 5$ is
- $(5, 3)$
* $(8, 3)$
- $(2, 7)$
- $(-2, 3)$
W: The point is 3 units to the left of the line, so its image is 3 units to the right.

Q: The reflection of $(1, 4)$ in the line $y = 1$ is
- $(1, -4)$
* $(1, -2)$
- $(1, 2)$
- $(-1, 4)$
W: The point is 3 units above the line, so its image is 3 units below.

Q: The points that are invariant under reflection in the $x$-axis are
- all points of the plane
* the points on the $x$-axis
- the points on the $y$-axis
- the origin only
W: A point on the mirror line is its own image.

Q: A point P is reflected in the $x$-axis to give $(3, -2)$. The point P is
- $(-3, -2)$
* $(3, 2)$
- $(-3, 2)$
- $(2, 3)$
W: Reflecting the image again in the $x$-axis gives back P.

Q: The point $(5, 2)$ is reflected in the $y$-axis and the image is then reflected in the $x$-axis. The final image is
- $(5, -2)$
- $(-5, 2)$
* $(-5, -2)$
- $(2, 5)$
W: First $(-5, 2)$, then $(-5, -2)$. This is the same as reflection in the origin.

Q: The reflection of $(0, 6)$ in the $y$-axis is
- $(6, 0)$
- $(0, -6)$
* $(0, 6)$
- $(-6, 0)$
W: The point lies on the $y$-axis, so it is invariant.

Q: A$(2, 3)$ is reflected in the $y$-axis to A'. The length AA' is
- 2 units
* 4 units
- 6 units
- 3 units
W: A' is $(-2, 3)$, and the distance is $2 - (-2)$.
