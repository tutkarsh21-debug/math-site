Q: The HCF of 96 and 404 is
- 2
* 4
- 8
- 12
W: $96 = 2^5 \times 3$ and $404 = 2^2 \times 101$, so the HCF is $2^2 = 4$.

Q: If the HCF of two numbers is 12 and their product is 4320, their LCM is
- 240
- 432
* 360
- 180
W: HCF $\times$ LCM $=$ product of the numbers, so LCM $= 4320 \div 12 = 360$.

Q: The prime factorisation of 1260 is
- $2 \times 3^2 \times 5 \times 7$
- $2^2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7$
- $2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^2 \times 7$
* $2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 \times 7$
W: $1260 = 4 \times 9 \times 5 \times 7$.

Q: Which of these is an irrational number?
- $\sqrt{49}$
* $2 + \sqrt{3}$
- $0.\overline{27}$
- $\dfrac{22}{7}$
W: A rational number plus an irrational number is irrational. The other three are rational.

Q: The number $7 \times 11 \times 13 + 13$ is
- a prime number
* a composite number
- neither prime nor composite
- an irrational number
W: It equals $13(77 + 1) = 13 \times 78$, so it has factors other than 1 and itself.

Q: For which natural number $n$ can $6^n$ end with the digit 0?
- $n = 2$
- every even $n$
- every $n$
* no value of $n$
W: To end in 0 a number needs the prime factor 5, but $6^n = 2^n \times 3^n$ has only 2 and 3.

Q: The LCM of 12, 15 and 21 is
* 420
- 210
- 630
- 3
W: $12 = 2^2 \times 3$, $15 = 3 \times 5$, $21 = 3 \times 7$; LCM $= 2^2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7 = 420$.

Q: Two bells ring every 18 minutes and 24 minutes. If they ring together at 9:00 am, they next ring together at
- 9:42 am
- 10:00 am
* 10:12 am
- 10:48 am
W: The LCM of 18 and 24 is 72 minutes, that is 1 hour 12 minutes.

Q: If $p$ is a prime number and $p$ divides $a^2$, where $a$ is a positive integer, then
* $p$ divides $a$
- $p^2$ divides $a$
- $a$ divides $p$
- $p$ does not divide $a$
W: This theorem is the key step in proving that $\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{3}$ and $\sqrt{5}$ are irrational.

Q: The largest number that divides 70 and 125, leaving remainders 5 and 8, is
- 9
- 5
- 65
* 13
W: It must divide $70 - 5 = 65$ and $125 - 8 = 117$. The HCF of 65 and 117 is 13.
