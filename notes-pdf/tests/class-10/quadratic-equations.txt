Q: The roots of $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$ are
- 2 and 6
* 3 and 4
- $-3$ and $-4$
- 1 and 12
W: $(x - 3)(x - 4) = 0$.

Q: The discriminant of $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ is
- 40
- 8
* $-8$
- $-40$

Q: The roots of $x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0$ are
- real and unequal
* real and equal
- not real
- irrational
W: The discriminant is $36 - 36 = 0$.

Q: For which positive value of $k$ does $x^2 + kx + 16 = 0$ have equal roots?
- 4
* 8
- 16
- 32
W: $k^2 - 64 = 0$.

Q: The roots of $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$ are
- $-1$ and $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
* 1 and $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- 2 and 3
- 1 and 3
W: $(2x - 3)(x - 1) = 0$.

Q: The roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ are given by
- $\dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 + 4ac}}{2a}$
* $\dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
- $\dfrac{b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
- $\dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{a}$
W: This is the quadratic formula.

Q: Which of these is a quadratic equation?
- $x^2 + 3x = x^2 - 4$
- $(x + 2)(x - 2) = x^2 + x$
* $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
- $x^3 - x = 0$
W: It simplifies to $x^2 + 7 = 0$. In the first two the $x^2$ terms cancel, and the last has degree 3.

Q: The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306. The smaller integer is
- 16
* 17
- 18
- 153
W: $x(x + 1) = 306$ gives $x^2 + x - 306 = 0$, so $x = 17$.

Q: The length of a rectangle is 3 cm more than its breadth and its area is 40 sq cm. The breadth is
- 4 cm
* 5 cm
- 8 cm
- 10 cm
W: $x(x + 3) = 40$ gives $(x + 8)(x - 5) = 0$.
