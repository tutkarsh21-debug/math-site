Q: The angle of elevation is the angle between the horizontal and the line of sight when the object is
- at the same level as the eye
* above the level of the eye
- below the level of the eye
- behind the observer
W: When the object is below eye level, the angle is called the angle of depression.

Q: From a point 30 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of its top is 30°. The height of the tower is
- 30 m
- $30\sqrt{3}$ m
* $10\sqrt{3}$ m
- 15 m
W: $h = 30 \tan 30° = \dfrac{30}{\sqrt{3}}$.

Q: A pole 10 m high casts a shadow 10 m long. The angle of elevation of the Sun is
- 30°
* 45°
- 60°
- 90°
W: $\tan\theta = \dfrac{10}{10} = 1$.

Q: A kite is flying with a string 60 m long that makes an angle of 60° with the ground. The height of the kite is
- 30 m
* $30\sqrt{3}$ m
- $60\sqrt{3}$ m
- $20\sqrt{3}$ m
W: $h = 60 \sin 60°$.

Q: A ladder 15 m long makes an angle of 60° with the wall. The height it reaches on the wall is
* 7.5 m
- $7.5\sqrt{3}$ m
- 15 m
- 30 m
W: The height is adjacent to the 60° angle: $15 \cos 60°$.

Q: From the top of a tower 50 m high, the angle of depression of a car on the ground is 45°. The distance of the car from the foot of the tower is
- 25 m
* 50 m
- $50\sqrt{3}$ m
- 100 m
W: $\tan 45° = 1$, so the distance equals the height.

Q: The shadow of a tower is $\sqrt{3}$ times its height. The angle of elevation of the Sun is
* 30°
- 45°
- 60°
- 90°
W: $\tan\theta = \dfrac{h}{\sqrt{3}h} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.

Q: The angle of elevation of the top of a tower changes from 30° to 60° as an observer walks 40 m towards it. The height of the tower is
- 20 m
* $20\sqrt{3}$ m
- 40 m
- $40\sqrt{3}$ m
W: $h\sqrt{3} - \dfrac{h}{\sqrt{3}} = 40$ gives $\dfrac{2h}{\sqrt{3}} = 40$.

Q: The angle of depression of an object from the top of a building is equal to
- the angle of elevation of the object from the top
* the angle of elevation of the top from the object
- 90° minus the angle of elevation
- twice the angle of elevation
W: They are alternate angles between parallel horizontal lines.

Q: A tree breaks in a storm and its top touches the ground 8 m from its foot, making an angle of 30° with the ground. The original height of the tree was
- 8 m
- 16 m
* $8\sqrt{3}$ m
- $16\sqrt{3}$ m
W: Standing part $= 8\tan 30° = \dfrac{8}{\sqrt{3}}$ and broken part $= \dfrac{8}{\cos 30°} = \dfrac{16}{\sqrt{3}}$. The total is $\dfrac{24}{\sqrt{3}} = 8\sqrt{3}$.
