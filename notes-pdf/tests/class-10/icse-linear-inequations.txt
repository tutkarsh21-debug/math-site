Q: If $3x - 5 < 4$ and $x$ is a natural number, the solution set is
- $\{1, 2, 3\}$
* $\{1, 2\}$
- $\{0, 1, 2\}$
- $\{3, 4, 5, \ldots\}$
W: $x < 3$, and natural numbers start from 1.

Q: If $-3 \le 2x - 1 < 5$ and $x$ is an integer, the solution set is
- $\{-1, 0, 1, 2, 3\}$
* $\{-1, 0, 1, 2\}$
- $\{0, 1, 2\}$
- $\{-2, -1, 0, 1, 2\}$
W: Adding 1 and dividing by 2 gives $-1 \le x < 3$.

Q: The solution of $-\dfrac{x}{3} \le 2$ is
- $x \le -6$
* $x \ge -6$
- $x \le 6$
- $x \ge 6$
W: Multiplying by $-3$ reverses the sign.

Q: The values of $x$ that satisfy both $2x + 1 \ge 7$ and $3x - 5 \le 10$ are
- $x \ge 3$
- $x \le 5$
* $3 \le x \le 5$
- $x \le 3$ or $x \ge 5$
W: The first gives $x \ge 3$ and the second $x \le 5$.

Q: The smallest whole number that satisfies $5 - 2x < 1$ is
- 1
- 2
* 3
- 4
W: $-2x < -4$ gives $x > 2$.

Q: On a number line, $-2 < x \le 3$ (for real $x$) is shown with
- solid dots at both $-2$ and 3
* a hollow circle at $-2$ and a solid dot at 3
- a solid dot at $-2$ and a hollow circle at 3
- hollow circles at both $-2$ and 3
W: A hollow circle marks an end that is not included.

Q: If $a > b$, then
- $-a > -b$
* $-a < -b$
- $-a = -b$
- $a - b < 0$
W: Multiplying by $-1$ reverses an inequality.

Q: If $12 - 5x > 1$ and $x$ is a whole number, the solution set is
- $\{1, 2\}$
* $\{0, 1, 2\}$
- $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$
- $\{3, 4, 5, \ldots\}$
W: $x < 2.2$, and whole numbers start from 0.

Q: The solution of $x + 2 \le 2x - 1$ is
- $x \le 3$
* $x \ge 3$
- $x \ge 1$
- $x \le -3$
W: $2 + 1 \le 2x - x$.

Q: The largest integer that satisfies $4x - 3 < 2x + 8$ is
- 4
* 5
- 6
- 11
W: $2x < 11$ gives $x < 5.5$.
