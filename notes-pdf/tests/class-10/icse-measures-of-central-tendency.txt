Q: The mean of 3, 7, 11, 15 and 19 is
- 10
* 11
- 12
- 55
W: The sum is 55, and $55 \div 5 = 11$.

Q: For 9 observations arranged in order, the median is the
- 4th observation
* 5th observation
- 6th observation
- mean of the 4th and 5th
W: $\dfrac{n + 1}{2} = 5$.

Q: The median of 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12, 13 is
- 7
* 7.5
- 8
- 7.6
W: The mean of the 4th and 5th values: $\dfrac{7 + 8}{2}$.

Q: The lower quartile of 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 15, 18, 20, 22 is
- 5
* 7
- 8
- 12
W: With $n = 11$, the lower quartile is the $\dfrac{n + 1}{4}$ = 3rd term.

Q: The upper quartile of 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 15, 18, 20, 22 is
- 15
* 18
- 20
- 14
W: The upper quartile is the $\dfrac{3(n + 1)}{4}$ = 9th term.

Q: If the lower quartile is 7 and the upper quartile is 18, the inter-quartile range is
- 25
* 11
- 12.5
- 5.5
W: $Q_3 - Q_1 = 18 - 7$.

Q: The mode of 2, 3, 3, 5, 5, 5, 7 is
- 3
* 5
- 7
- 4
W: 5 occurs most often.

Q: In the step-deviation method, $A = 25$, $\Sigma ft = 10$, $\Sigma f = 50$ and the class width is 10. The mean is
- 25.2
* 27
- 35
- 30
W: $25 + \dfrac{10}{50} \times 10$.

Q: The classes 0-10 and 10-20 have frequencies 4 and 6. The mean is
- 10
* 11
- 12
- 15
W: Using class marks 5 and 15: $\dfrac{20 + 90}{10}$.

Q: The mean of 5, 7, $x$ and 11 is 8. The value of $x$ is
- 8
* 9
- 10
- 7
W: $23 + x = 32$.
