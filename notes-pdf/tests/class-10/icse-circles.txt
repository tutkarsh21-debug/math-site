Q: An arc subtends an angle of 140° at the centre. The angle it subtends at a point on the remaining part of the circle is
- 140°
* 70°
- 40°
- 280°
W: The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference.

Q: AB is a diameter of a circle and C is a point on the circle. If $\angle CAB = 35°$, then $\angle ABC$ is
- 35°
* 55°
- 90°
- 145°
W: $\angle ACB = 90°$ (angle in a semicircle), so $\angle ABC = 90° - 35°$.

Q: ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral with $\angle A = 110°$. Then $\angle C$ is
- 110°
* 70°
- 20°
- 250°
W: Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary.

Q: An exterior angle of a cyclic quadrilateral is equal to
- the adjacent interior angle
* the interior opposite angle
- 90°
- half the interior opposite angle
W: Both are supplements of the same angle.

Q: C and D lie on the same side of chord AB of a circle. If $\angle ACB = 40°$, then $\angle ADB$ is
- 80°
- 20°
* 40°
- 140°
W: Angles in the same segment are equal.

Q: A chord of a circle is equal to its radius. The angle it subtends at a point on the major arc is
- 60°
* 30°
- 120°
- 15°
W: The chord and two radii form an equilateral triangle, so the angle at the centre is 60°.

Q: A parallelogram inscribed in a circle is always a
- rhombus
* rectangle
- trapezium
- kite
W: Its opposite angles are equal and also supplementary, so each is 90°.

Q: The reflex angle subtended by a major arc at the centre is 250°. The angle this arc subtends at a point on the minor arc is
- 55°
- 110°
* 125°
- 250°
W: Half of 250°.

Q: In cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, $\angle A = (2x + 10)°$ and $\angle C = (3x + 20)°$. The value of $x$ is
- 10
- 25
* 30
- 34
W: $5x + 30 = 180$.

Q: Equal arcs of a circle subtend at the centre
- right angles
* equal angles
- supplementary angles
- unequal angles
W: Equal arcs have equal chords, and equal chords subtend equal angles at the centre.
