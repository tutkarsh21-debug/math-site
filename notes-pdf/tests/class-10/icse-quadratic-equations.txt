Q: The roots of $x^2 - 9x + 20 = 0$ are
- 2 and 10
* 4 and 5
- $-4$ and $-5$
- 1 and 20
W: $(x - 4)(x - 5) = 0$.

Q: The roots of $3x^2 - 5x - 2 = 0$ are
- $-2$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$
* 2 and $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- 2 and $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- 1 and $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
W: $(3x + 1)(x - 2) = 0$.

Q: The equation $x^2 + 4x + 5 = 0$ has
- two equal real roots
- two unequal real roots
* no real roots
- one real root
W: The discriminant is $16 - 20 = -4$, which is negative.

Q: The equation $kx^2 - 12x + 9 = 0$ has equal roots when $k$ is
- 3
* 4
- 6
- 16
W: $144 - 36k = 0$.

Q: The roots of $x^2 - 4x - 1 = 0$ are
- $2 \pm \sqrt{3}$
* $2 \pm \sqrt{5}$
- $4 \pm \sqrt{5}$
- $-2 \pm \sqrt{5}$
W: $x = \dfrac{4 \pm \sqrt{16 + 4}}{2}$.

Q: If $x = 2$ is a root of $x^2 + px - 6 = 0$, then $p$ is
- $-1$
* 1
- 2
- 5
W: $4 + 2p - 6 = 0$.

Q: The roots of $x^2 = 5x$ are
- 5 only
* 0 and 5
- 0 only
- 5 and $-5$
W: $x(x - 5) = 0$. Dividing by $x$ would lose the root 0.

Q: The roots of $2x^2 + 5x + 2 = 0$ are
- not real
- real and equal
* real, unequal and rational
- real, unequal and irrational
W: The discriminant is $25 - 16 = 9$, a perfect square.

Q: The roots of $x + \dfrac{1}{x} = 2.5$ are
- 1 and 2.5
* 2 and $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- 5 and $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- 2 and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: $2x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$ gives $(2x - 1)(x - 2) = 0$.

Q: The sum of the roots of $2x^2 - 7x + 3 = 0$ is
- $-\dfrac{7}{2}$
* $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- 7
W: Sum $= -\dfrac{b}{a}$.
