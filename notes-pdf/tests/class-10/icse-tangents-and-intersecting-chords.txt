Q: A point is 25 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 7 cm. The length of the tangent from the point is
- 18 cm
* 24 cm
- 26 cm
- 32 cm
W: $\sqrt{25^2 - 7^2} = \sqrt{576}$.

Q: Two circles of radii 5 cm and 3 cm touch each other externally. The distance between their centres is
- 2 cm
* 8 cm
- 15 cm
- 4 cm
W: For external contact, the distance is the sum of the radii.

Q: Two circles of radii 8 cm and 3 cm touch each other internally. The distance between their centres is
- 11 cm
* 5 cm
- 24 cm
- 2.5 cm
W: For internal contact, the distance is the difference of the radii.

Q: Chords AB and CD of a circle meet at a point P inside it. If PA = 4 cm, PB = 6 cm and PC = 3 cm, then PD is
- 7 cm
* 8 cm
- 4.5 cm
- 2 cm
W: $PA \times PB = PC \times PD$ gives $24 = 3 \times PD$.

Q: From an external point P, a tangent PT and a secant PAB are drawn to a circle. If PA = 4 cm and PB = 9 cm, then PT is
- 5 cm
* 6 cm
- 6.5 cm
- 36 cm
W: $PT^2 = PA \times PB = 36$.

Q: The angle between a tangent and a chord through the point of contact is 55°. The angle in the alternate segment is
- 35°
* 55°
- 110°
- 125°
W: This is the alternate segment theorem.

Q: A circle touches the sides BC, CA and AB of triangle ABC at D, E and F. If AF = 3 cm, BD = 4 cm and CE = 5 cm, the perimeter of the triangle is
- 12 cm
* 24 cm
- 18 cm
- 36 cm
W: Tangents from each vertex are equal, so the perimeter is $2(3 + 4 + 5)$.

Q: The number of common tangents to two circles that touch each other externally is
- 1
- 2
* 3
- 4
W: Two direct common tangents and one at the point of contact.

Q: Two secants PAB and PCD are drawn to a circle from an external point P. If PA = 3 cm, PB = 12 cm and PC = 4 cm, then PD is
- 8 cm
* 9 cm
- 11 cm
- 16 cm
W: $PA \times PB = PC \times PD$ gives $36 = 4 \times PD$.

Q: The angle between a tangent and the radius drawn to the point of contact is
- 0°
- 45°
* 90°
- 180°
W: A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
