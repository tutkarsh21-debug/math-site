Q: A matrix with 2 rows and 3 columns has order
- $3 \times 2$
* $2 \times 3$
- $2 \times 2$
- 6
W: The order is written as rows × columns.

Q: If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$, then $A + B$ is
- $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 3 & 12 \end{bmatrix}$
* $\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 4 & 7 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 3 & 7 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 6 \\ 10 & 12 \end{bmatrix}$
W: Add the corresponding elements.

Q: If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$, then $2A$ is
- $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$
* $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & -4 \\ 0 & 6 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 2 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & -4 \\ 0 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
W: Every element is multiplied by 2.

Q: The product $AB$ of two matrices is defined when
- A and B have the same order
* the number of columns of A equals the number of rows of B
- the number of rows of A equals the number of rows of B
- both are square matrices only
W: Each row of A is multiplied by each column of B, so their lengths must match.

Q: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$ is equal to
- $\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 7 \end{bmatrix}$
* $\begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 11 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 3 & 8 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 7 \\ 10 \end{bmatrix}$
W: $1 \times 1 + 2 \times 2 = 5$ and $3 \times 1 + 4 \times 2 = 11$.

Q: The product of a $2 \times 3$ matrix and a $3 \times 1$ matrix has order
- $3 \times 3$
* $2 \times 1$
- $1 \times 2$
- $3 \times 2$
W: The outer numbers give the order of the product.

Q: The identity matrix of order 2 is
- $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
* $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$
W: It has 1 on the leading diagonal and 0 elsewhere, and $AI = IA = A$.

Q: If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$, then $A^2$ is
- $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 0 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
* $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ 0 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ 0 & 6 \end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 0 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
W: $A^2 = A \times A$. The top-right element is $2 \times 1 + 1 \times 3 = 5$.

Q: For matrices A and B for which both products are defined, in general
- $AB = BA$
* $AB \ne BA$
- $AB = 0$
- $AB = A + B$
W: Matrix multiplication is not commutative.

Q: If $\begin{bmatrix} x & 2 \\ 3 & y \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ 3 & 7 \end{bmatrix}$, then
- $x = 4$, $y = 7$
* $x = 3$, $y = 5$
- $x = 5$, $y = 9$
- $x = 3$, $y = 9$
W: $x + 1 = 4$ and $y + 2 = 7$.
