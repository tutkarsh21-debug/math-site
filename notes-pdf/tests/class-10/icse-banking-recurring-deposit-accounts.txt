Q: In a recurring deposit account, with monthly deposit P for $n$ months at $r$% per year, the interest is
- $\dfrac{P \times n \times r}{100}$
* $P \times \dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \dfrac{r}{100}$
- $P \times \dfrac{n(n - 1)}{2} \times \dfrac{r}{100}$
- $P \times \dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2} \times \dfrac{r}{100}$
W: The deposits together are equal to $P \times \dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2}$ kept for one month, that is $\dfrac{1}{12}$ of a year.

Q: Rs 500 is deposited every month for 12 months at 10% per year. The interest is
- Rs 600
* Rs 325
- Rs 300
- Rs 650
W: $500 \times \dfrac{12 \times 13}{24} \times \dfrac{10}{100} = 500 \times 6.5 \times 0.1$.

Q: Rs 500 is deposited every month for 12 months and the interest earned is Rs 325. The maturity value is
- Rs 6000
* Rs 6325
- Rs 6500
- Rs 825
W: Total deposits Rs 6000 plus interest Rs 325.

Q: Rs 200 is deposited every month for 36 months at 11% per year. The interest is
- Rs 792
* Rs 1221
- Rs 2442
- Rs 1110
W: $200 \times \dfrac{36 \times 37}{24} \times \dfrac{11}{100} = 200 \times 55.5 \times 0.11$.

Q: The maturity value of a recurring deposit account is
- the interest only
- the total of the deposits only
* the total of the deposits plus the interest
- the last deposit plus the interest
W: The bank returns all the money deposited, together with the interest.

Q: Rs 1000 is deposited every month for 24 months at 6% per year. The maturity value is
- Rs 24,000
- Rs 25,440
* Rs 25,500
- Rs 26,880
W: Interest $= 1000 \times \dfrac{24 \times 25}{24} \times 0.06 = 1500$, added to Rs 24,000.

Q: A recurring deposit for 12 months at 8% per year earns interest of Rs 416. The monthly deposit is
- Rs 400
- Rs 520
* Rs 800
- Rs 1000
W: $P \times 6.5 \times 0.08 = 416$ gives $P = 800$.

Q: Rs 600 is deposited every month for 20 months and the interest earned is Rs 630. The rate of interest per year is
- 5%
* 6%
- 8%
- 10%
W: $600 \times \dfrac{20 \times 21}{24} \times \dfrac{r}{100} = 105r = 630$.

Q: Rs 100 deposited every month for 12 months is equivalent to how much kept for one month?
- Rs 1200
- Rs 6600
* Rs 7800
- Rs 15,600
W: $100 \times \dfrac{12 \times 13}{2} = 7800$.

Q: In a recurring deposit account, the depositor pays
- one lump sum at the start
* a fixed amount every month
- a different amount each month
- only the interest every month
W: The same instalment is paid each month for a fixed period.
