Q: The slope of the line through $(2, 3)$ and $(4, 7)$ is
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
* 2
- 4
- 5
W: $\dfrac{7 - 3}{4 - 2}$.

Q: The slope of the line $3x + 4y = 12$ is
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
* $-\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- 3
W: $y = -\dfrac{3}{4}x + 3$.

Q: The equation of the line with slope 2 that passes through $(1, 3)$ is
- $y = 2x + 3$
* $y = 2x + 1$
- $y = 2x - 1$
- $y = 3x + 2$
W: $y - 3 = 2(x - 1)$.

Q: The slope of a line parallel to $y = 5x - 2$ is
- $-2$
- $-\dfrac{1}{5}$
* 5
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
W: Parallel lines have equal slopes.

Q: The slope of a line perpendicular to a line of slope $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
* $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
W: The product of the slopes of perpendicular lines is $-1$.

Q: Which point lies on the line $3x - y = 1$?
- $(1, 3)$
* $(2, 5)$
- $(3, 2)$
- $(0, 1)$
W: $3 \times 2 - 5 = 1$.

Q: The $y$-intercept of the line $2x - 3y = 6$ is
- 3
- 2
* $-2$
- $-3$
W: Put $x = 0$: $-3y = 6$.

Q: The $x$-intercept of the line $4x + 5y = 20$ is
- 4
* 5
- 20
- $-5$
W: Put $y = 0$: $4x = 20$.

Q: The equation of the line through $(0, 4)$ and $(2, 0)$ is
- $x + 2y = 4$
* $2x + y = 4$
- $2x - y = 4$
- $x + y = 4$
W: The slope is $-2$ and the $y$-intercept is 4: $y = -2x + 4$.

Q: The lines $2x + 3y = 7$ and $3x - 2y = 4$ are
- parallel
* perpendicular
- the same line
- neither parallel nor perpendicular
W: Their slopes are $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}$, with product $-1$.
