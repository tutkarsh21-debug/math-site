Q: The sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 2 : 3. Their areas are in the ratio
- 2 : 3
* 4 : 9
- 8 : 27
- 3 : 2
W: The ratio of areas is the square of the ratio of sides.

Q: In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC, with D on AB and E on AC. If AD = 3 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 4 cm, then EC is
- 2 cm
* 8 cm
- 4.5 cm
- 12 cm
W: $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$.

Q: A map is drawn to a scale of 1 : 20,000. A road 5 cm long on the map has an actual length of
- 100 m
* 1 km
- 10 km
- 4 km
W: $5 \times 20000 = 100000$ cm = 1 km.

Q: $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$, and their areas are 64 sq cm and 121 sq cm. If EF = 15.4 cm, then BC is
- 8.1 cm
* 11.2 cm
- 21.2 cm
- 8 cm
W: The sides are in the ratio $\sqrt{64} : \sqrt{121} = 8 : 11$, and $\dfrac{8}{11} \times 15.4 = 11.2$.

Q: A model of a building is made to a scale of 1 : 50. The model is 20 cm high. The actual height is
- 2.5 m
* 10 m
- 100 m
- 1000 m
W: $20 \times 50 = 1000$ cm.

Q: One triangle has angles 50° and 60°, and another has angles 60° and 70°. The triangles are
- congruent
* similar
- neither similar nor congruent
- equal in area
W: The third angles are 70° and 50°, so all three angles match.

Q: A model is made to a scale of 1 : 100. A floor in the model has area 6 sq cm. The actual area of the floor is
- 600 sq cm
* 6 sq m
- 60 sq m
- 600 sq m
W: Areas are multiplied by $100^2$: 60,000 sq cm = 6 sq m.

Q: A model is made to a scale of 1 : 10. A tank in the model has volume 3 cubic cm. The actual volume of the tank is
- 30 cubic cm
- 300 cubic cm
* 3000 cubic cm
- 30,000 cubic cm
W: Volumes are multiplied by $10^3$.

Q: In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC and AD : DB = 2 : 3. Then DE : BC is
- 2 : 3
* 2 : 5
- 3 : 5
- 4 : 9
W: Triangles ADE and ABC are similar, and AD : AB = 2 : 5.

Q: In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC and AD : DB = 2 : 3. The ratio of the area of triangle ADE to the area of triangle ABC is
- 2 : 5
- 4 : 9
* 4 : 25
- 2 : 3
W: The sides are in the ratio 2 : 5, so the areas are in the ratio $2^2 : 5^2$.
