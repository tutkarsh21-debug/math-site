Q: An ogive is a
- bar graph
* cumulative frequency curve
- frequency polygon
- pie chart
W: It is drawn by plotting cumulative frequencies and joining the points with a smooth curve.

Q: A histogram is used to estimate the
- mean
- median
* mode
- range
W: The mode is read from the tallest rectangle and its neighbours.

Q: An ogive is used to estimate the
- mean
* median
- mode
- class size
W: The median and the quartiles are read from the cumulative frequency curve.

Q: To draw a "less than" ogive, cumulative frequencies are plotted against
- the lower class limits
* the upper class limits
- the class marks
- the frequencies
W: A cumulative frequency counts all the values below the upper limit of its class.

Q: The classes 10-19, 20-29, 30-39 are made continuous. The first class becomes
- 10-20
* 9.5-19.5
- 10-19.5
- 9-20
W: The gap is 1, so 0.5 is subtracted from each lower limit and added to each upper limit.

Q: An ogive is drawn for 80 students. To estimate the median, the curve is read at a cumulative frequency of
- 20
* 40
- 60
- 80
W: The median corresponds to $\dfrac{n}{2}$.

Q: An ogive is drawn for 80 students. To estimate the lower quartile, the curve is read at a cumulative frequency of
* 20
- 40
- 60
- 80
W: The lower quartile corresponds to $\dfrac{n}{4}$.

Q: An ogive for the marks of 80 students shows a cumulative frequency of 65 at 60 marks. The number of students who scored more than 60 is
- 65
* 15
- 20
- 60
W: $80 - 65$.

Q: In a histogram with equal class widths, the height of each rectangle is proportional to
- the class mark
* the frequency of the class
- the cumulative frequency
- the class width
W: With equal widths, areas and heights both represent frequencies.

Q: To find the mode from a histogram, we join the upper corners of the tallest rectangle to the nearer upper corners of the two adjacent rectangles, and read
- the height of the tallest rectangle
* the value on the horizontal axis below the point where the two lines cross
- the mid-point of the tallest rectangle
- the upper limit of the modal class
W: A perpendicular is dropped from the point of intersection to the horizontal axis.
