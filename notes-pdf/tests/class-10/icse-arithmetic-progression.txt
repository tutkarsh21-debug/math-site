Q: The 20th term of the A.P. 5, 9, 13, ... is
- 80
* 81
- 85
- 100
W: $5 + 19 \times 4$.

Q: In the A.P. 4, 7, 10, ..., the number 100 is
- not a term
* the 33rd term
- the 32nd term
- the 34th term
W: $4 + (n - 1) \times 3 = 100$ gives $n - 1 = 32$.

Q: The sum of the first 30 terms of the A.P. 1, 3, 5, ... is
- 870
* 900
- 930
- 465
W: $\dfrac{30}{2}[2 + 29 \times 2] = 15 \times 60$.

Q: The arithmetic mean between 7 and 19 is
- 12
* 13
- 14
- 26
W: $\dfrac{7 + 19}{2}$.

Q: Three numbers in A.P. have sum 24 and product 440. The numbers are
- 6, 8, 10
* 5, 8, 11
- 4, 8, 12
- 2, 8, 14
W: Take $a - d$, $a$, $a + d$: $a = 8$ and $8(64 - d^2) = 440$ gives $d = 3$.

Q: The $n$th term of an A.P. is $4n - 1$. Its common difference is
- $-1$
- 3
* 4
- 5
W: The terms are 3, 7, 11, ...

Q: The 4th term of an A.P. is 11 and the 9th term is 26. The common difference is
- 2
* 3
- 5
- 15
W: $5d = 26 - 11$.

Q: The sum of the first $n$ terms of an A.P. with first term $a$ and common difference $d$ is
- $n[2a + (n - 1)d]$
* $\dfrac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$
- $\dfrac{n}{2}[a + (n - 1)d]$
- $a + (n - 1)d$
W: It is $\dfrac{n}{2}$ times the sum of the first and last terms.

Q: How many terms of the A.P. 3, 5, 7, ... must be added to get 120?
- 8
* 10
- 12
- 15
W: $\dfrac{n}{2}[6 + 2(n - 1)] = n(n + 2) = 120$.

Q: The 10th term from the end of the A.P. 3, 6, 9, ..., 99 is
- 30
- 69
* 72
- 75
W: From the end the A.P. is 99, 96, ...: $99 - 9 \times 3$.
