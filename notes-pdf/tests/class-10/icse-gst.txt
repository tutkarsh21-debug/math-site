Q: For a sale within a state, GST at 18% is charged as
- IGST at 18%
* CGST at 9% and SGST at 9%
- CGST at 18% and SGST at 18%
- CGST at 6% and SGST at 12%
W: Within a state the rate is split equally between the centre and the state.

Q: For a sale from one state to another, the tax charged is
- CGST only
- SGST only
- CGST and SGST
* IGST
W: Integrated GST applies to inter-state sales, at the full rate.

Q: An article is priced at Rs 5000 and GST is 12%. The bill amount is
- Rs 5012
- Rs 5060
* Rs 5600
- Rs 6200
W: The tax is Rs 600.

Q: An article priced at Rs 2000 is sold within a state with GST at 18%. The CGST is
- Rs 360
* Rs 180
- Rs 90
- Rs 18
W: CGST is half of the GST: 9% of Rs 2000.

Q: The bill for an article, including GST at 18%, is Rs 5900. The price before tax is
- Rs 4838
* Rs 5000
- Rs 5200
- Rs 4900
W: $5900 \div 1.18 = 5000$.

Q: An article marked at Rs 1000 is sold at a discount of 10%, and GST at 12% is charged. The amount paid is
- Rs 1020
* Rs 1008
- Rs 1120
- Rs 990
W: The discounted price is Rs 900, and $900 \times 1.12 = 1008$.

Q: A dealer buys goods for Rs 10,000 and sells them for Rs 12,000, both within the same state, with GST at 18%. After input tax credit, the GST he pays to the government is
- Rs 2160
- Rs 1800
* Rs 360
- Rs 180
W: Output tax Rs 2160 minus input tax Rs 1800, which is 18% of the value added, Rs 2000.

Q: A bill has one item of Rs 400 taxed at 5% and another of Rs 600 taxed at 18%. The total GST is
- Rs 230
- Rs 115
* Rs 128
- Rs 108
W: $20 + 108 = 128$.

Q: An article of Rs 5000 is sold within a state with GST at 28%. The SGST is
- Rs 1400
* Rs 700
- Rs 350
- Rs 280
W: SGST is 14% of Rs 5000.

Q: When a discount is given, GST is calculated on
- the marked price
* the price after the discount
- the discount
- the cost price of the dealer
W: Tax is charged on the amount at which the article is actually sold.
