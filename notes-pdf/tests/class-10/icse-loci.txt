Q: The locus of a point equidistant from two fixed points is
- the line joining the points
* the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining them
- a circle through the points
- a line parallel to the segment joining them
W: Every point on the perpendicular bisector is equally far from the two ends.

Q: The locus of a point equidistant from two intersecting lines is
- a circle
* the pair of bisectors of the angles between the lines
- a line parallel to one of them
- the point of intersection only
W: A point on an angle bisector is equally far from the two arms.

Q: The locus of a point that stays at a fixed distance from a fixed point is
- a straight line
* a circle
- a square
- a pair of parallel lines
W: The fixed point is the centre and the fixed distance is the radius.

Q: The locus of a point equidistant from two parallel lines is
- a circle
* a line parallel to them and midway between them
- a line perpendicular to them
- a pair of lines
W: Every point of the midway line is at the same distance from both.

Q: The point equidistant from the three vertices of a triangle is its
- incentre
* circumcentre
- centroid
- orthocentre
W: It is where the perpendicular bisectors of the sides meet.

Q: The point equidistant from the three sides of a triangle is its
* incentre
- circumcentre
- centroid
- orthocentre
W: It is where the bisectors of the angles meet.

Q: The locus of a point that is 3 cm from a given straight line is
- one line parallel to it
* a pair of lines parallel to it, one on each side
- a circle of radius 3 cm
- a line perpendicular to it
W: Points 3 cm away exist on both sides of the line.

Q: The locus of the centres of all circles that pass through two fixed points A and B is
- the line AB
* the perpendicular bisector of AB
- a circle on AB as diameter
- the mid-point of AB only
W: Each centre is equidistant from A and B.

Q: The locus of the mid-points of all equal chords of a circle is
- a diameter
* a circle concentric with the given circle
- a chord
- a tangent
W: Equal chords are at the same distance from the centre, so their mid-points are too.

Q: A and B are fixed points. The locus of a point P that moves so that $\angle APB = 90°$ is
- the perpendicular bisector of AB
* the circle on AB as diameter (apart from A and B)
- a line parallel to AB
- the segment AB
W: The angle in a semicircle is a right angle.
