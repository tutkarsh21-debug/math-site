Q: The distance between $(-2, 3)$ and $(4, -5)$ is
- 6 units
- 8 units
* 10 units
- 14 units
W: $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10$.

Q: The mid-point of the segment joining $(2, -3)$ and $(6, 7)$ is
- $(8, 4)$
* $(4, 2)$
- $(2, 5)$
- $(4, 5)$
W: $\left(\dfrac{2 + 6}{2}, \dfrac{-3 + 7}{2}\right)$.

Q: The point that divides the segment joining $(1, 2)$ and $(7, 8)$ internally in the ratio 1 : 2 is
- $(5, 6)$
* $(3, 4)$
- $(4, 5)$
- $(2, 3)$
W: $\left(\dfrac{1 \times 7 + 2 \times 1}{3}, \dfrac{1 \times 8 + 2 \times 2}{3}\right)$.

Q: The $x$-axis divides the segment joining $(2, -3)$ and $(5, 6)$ in the ratio
- 2 : 1
* 1 : 2
- 1 : 1
- 2 : 5
W: The $y$-coordinate is 0: $\dfrac{6k - 3}{k + 1} = 0$ gives $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: The distance of the point $(3, -4)$ from the $y$-axis is
* 3 units
- 4 units
- 5 units
- 7 units
W: The distance from the $y$-axis is the size of the $x$-coordinate.

Q: The points $(1, 7)$, $(4, 2)$, $(-1, -1)$ and $(-4, 4)$, in order, are the vertices of a
- rectangle that is not a square
* square
- rhombus that is not a square
- trapezium
W: Each side is $\sqrt{34}$ and each diagonal is $\sqrt{68}$.

Q: $(x, 4)$ is the mid-point of the segment joining $(2, 3)$ and $(8, 5)$. The value of $x$ is
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 10
W: $x = \dfrac{2 + 8}{2}$.

Q: The ends of a diameter of a circle are $(-2, 4)$ and $(6, -2)$. The centre is
- $(4, 2)$
* $(2, 1)$
- $(8, -6)$
- $(4, 3)$
W: The centre is the mid-point of the diameter.

Q: The point on the $y$-axis that is equidistant from $(5, -2)$ and $(-3, 2)$ is
- $(0, 2)$
* $(0, -2)$
- $(0, 1)$
- $(-2, 0)$
W: $25 + (y + 2)^2 = 9 + (y - 2)^2$ gives $8y = -16$.

Q: A$(1, 2)$, B$(4, 3)$ and C$(6, 6)$ are three vertices of parallelogram ABCD. The vertex D is
- $(9, 7)$
* $(3, 5)$
- $(5, 3)$
- $(2, 4)$
W: The diagonals bisect each other, so D = A + C − B $= (1 + 6 - 4, 2 + 6 - 3)$.
