Q: The mean proportional between 4 and 9 is
- 6.5
* 6
- 13
- 36
W: $\sqrt{4 \times 9} = 6$.

Q: The third proportional to 4 and 6 is
- 8
* 9
- 12
- 24
W: $4 : 6 = 6 : x$ gives $x = \dfrac{36}{4}$.

Q: The fourth proportional to 2, 3 and 8 is
- 9
- 10
* 12
- 16
W: $2 : 3 = 8 : x$ gives $x = 12$.

Q: If $a : b = 2 : 3$, then $(3a + 2b) : (4a - b)$ is
- 5 : 12
* 12 : 5
- 6 : 5
- 13 : 5
W: Put $a = 2$, $b = 3$: $(6 + 6) : (8 - 3)$.

Q: The compounded ratio of 2 : 3 and 4 : 5 is
- 6 : 8
* 8 : 15
- 10 : 12
- 5 : 6
W: Multiply the first terms and the second terms.

Q: The duplicate ratio of 3 : 4 is
- 6 : 8
* 9 : 16
- 27 : 64
- $\sqrt{3} : 2$
W: The duplicate ratio of $a : b$ is $a^2 : b^2$.

Q: If $\dfrac{a}{b} = \dfrac{c}{d}$, then by componendo and dividendo
- $\dfrac{a + b}{b} = \dfrac{c + d}{d}$
* $\dfrac{a + b}{a - b} = \dfrac{c + d}{c - d}$
- $\dfrac{a}{c} = \dfrac{b}{d}$
- $\dfrac{b}{a} = \dfrac{d}{c}$
W: The others are componendo, alternendo and invertendo.

Q: If $\dfrac{x + 3}{x - 3} = \dfrac{5}{2}$, then $x$ is
- 5
* 7
- 9
- 11
W: By componendo and dividendo, $\dfrac{2x}{6} = \dfrac{7}{3}$.

Q: What number must be added to each of 3, 5, 7 and 10 so that the results are in proportion?
- 3
* 5
- 7
- 10
W: $(3 + x)(10 + x) = (5 + x)(7 + x)$ gives $30 + 13x = 35 + 12x$.

Q: If $a$, $b$, $c$ are in continued proportion, then
- $a^2 = bc$
* $b^2 = ac$
- $c^2 = ab$
- $a + c = 2b$
W: $a : b = b : c$.
