Q: The remainder when $x^3 - 2x^2 + 3x - 4$ is divided by $x - 2$ is
- 0
* 2
- 4
- $-4$
W: $f(2) = 8 - 8 + 6 - 4$.

Q: The remainder when $f(x)$ is divided by $x + 1$ is
- $f(1)$
* $f(-1)$
- $f(0)$
- $-f(1)$
W: Put $x + 1 = 0$, that is $x = -1$.

Q: Which of these is a factor of $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$?
- $x + 1$
* $x - 1$
- $x + 2$
- $x - 4$
W: $f(1) = 1 - 6 + 11 - 6 = 0$.

Q: $x - 2$ is a factor of $x^2 + kx - 6$. The value of $k$ is
- $-1$
* 1
- 2
- 5
W: $4 + 2k - 6 = 0$.

Q: The remainder when $2x^2 - 5x + 1$ is divided by $2x - 1$ is
- 1
- 0
* $-1$
- $-2$
W: $f\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{5}{2} + 1$.

Q: The complete factorisation of $x^3 - 7x + 6$ is
- $(x - 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)$
* $(x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 3)$
- $(x + 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 6)(x + 1)$
W: $f(1) = 0$, and dividing by $x - 1$ leaves $x^2 + x - 6 = (x - 2)(x + 3)$.

Q: When $x^3 + ax^2 - 2x + 1$ is divided by $x - 1$, the remainder is 5. The value of $a$ is
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 7
W: $1 + a - 2 + 1 = 5$.

Q: By the factor theorem, $x - a$ is a factor of $f(x)$ exactly when
- $f(0) = a$
* $f(a) = 0$
- $f(-a) = 0$
- $f(a) = a$
W: A zero remainder means exact division.

Q: The remainder when $x^4 + 1$ is divided by $x + 1$ is
- 0
- 1
* 2
- $-2$
W: $f(-1) = 1 + 1$.

Q: $x^2 + px + q$ has factors $x - 2$ and $x + 3$. Then
- $p = -1$, $q = -6$
* $p = 1$, $q = -6$
- $p = 1$, $q = 6$
- $p = 5$, $q = 6$
W: $(x - 2)(x + 3) = x^2 + x - 6$.
