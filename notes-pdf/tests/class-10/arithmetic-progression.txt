Q: The 15th term of the AP 4, 9, 14, ... is
- 70
* 74
- 75
- 79
W: $a + 14d = 4 + 70$.

Q: The common difference of the AP 7, 3, $-1$, $-5$, ... is
- 4
* $-4$
- 10
- $-10$
W: $3 - 7 = -4$.

Q: Which term of the AP 3, 8, 13, ... is 78?
- 15th
* 16th
- 17th
- 25th
W: $3 + (n - 1) \times 5 = 78$ gives $n - 1 = 15$.

Q: The sum of the first 20 terms of the AP 2, 5, 8, ... is
- 590
* 610
- 620
- 1220
W: $\dfrac{20}{2}[2 \times 2 + 19 \times 3] = 10 \times 61$.

Q: The sum of the first 15 odd natural numbers is
- 120
- 210
* 225
- 240
W: The sum of the first $n$ odd numbers is $n^2$.

Q: An AP has first term 5, last term 45 and sum 400. The number of terms is
- 8
- 10
* 16
- 20
W: $\dfrac{n}{2}(5 + 45) = 400$.

Q: The 3rd term of an AP is 12 and the 7th term is 28. The first term is
- 2
* 4
- 6
- 8
W: $4d = 16$ gives $d = 4$, and $a = 12 - 2 \times 4$.

Q: How many two-digit numbers are divisible by 3?
- 29
* 30
- 31
- 33
W: From 12 to 99: $\dfrac{99 - 12}{3} + 1 = 30$.

Q: If $k$, $2k - 1$ and $2k + 1$ are consecutive terms of an AP, then $k$ is
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 5
W: $2(2k - 1) = k + (2k + 1)$.

Q: The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $3n^2 + n$. Its second term is
- 4
* 10
- 14
- 16
W: $a_2 = S_2 - S_1 = 14 - 4$.
