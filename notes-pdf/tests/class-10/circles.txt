Q: The angle between a tangent to a circle and the radius at the point of contact is
- 45°
- 60°
* 90°
- 180°
W: The tangent at any point is perpendicular to the radius through that point.

Q: The number of tangents that can be drawn to a circle from a point outside it is
- 1
* 2
- 3
- infinitely many
W: Two tangents can be drawn, and they are equal in length.

Q: A point is 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm. The length of the tangent from the point is
- 8 cm
* 12 cm
- 18 cm
- 14 cm
W: $\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12$.

Q: The lengths of the two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are
- in the ratio 1 : 2
* equal
- perpendicular
- unequal
W: The two right triangles formed with the centre are congruent.

Q: PA and PB are tangents to a circle with centre O, and $\angle APB = 60°$. Then $\angle AOB$ is
- 60°
- 90°
* 120°
- 150°
W: In quadrilateral OAPB the angles at A and B are 90°, so $\angle AOB = 180° - 60°$.

Q: The number of tangents that can be drawn to a circle from a point inside it is
* 0
- 1
- 2
- infinitely many
W: Every line through an interior point cuts the circle at two points.

Q: The tangent from a point P to a circle of radius 7 cm is 24 cm long. The distance of P from the centre is
- 17 cm
* 25 cm
- 31 cm
- 23 cm
W: $\sqrt{24^2 + 7^2} = 25$.

Q: Two concentric circles have radii 5 cm and 3 cm. The length of a chord of the larger circle that touches the smaller circle is
- 4 cm
* 8 cm
- 6 cm
- 10 cm
W: Half the chord is $\sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = 4$ cm.

Q: A quadrilateral ABCD is drawn to circumscribe a circle. Then
- AB + BC = CD + DA
* AB + CD = AD + BC
- AB = CD
- AC = BD
W: Tangents from each vertex are equal, and adding them gives this result.

Q: PA and PB are tangents to a circle with centre O, and $\angle APB = 80°$. Then $\angle OAB$ is
- 50°
* 40°
- 80°
- 100°
W: $\angle AOB = 100°$, and triangle OAB is isosceles, so $\angle OAB = \dfrac{180° - 100°}{2}$.
