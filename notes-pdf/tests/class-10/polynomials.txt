Q: The zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$ are
- 1 and 6
* 2 and 3
- $-2$ and $-3$
- $-1$ and 6
W: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)$.

Q: The sum of the zeroes of $2x^2 - 8x + 6$ is
- $-4$
- 3
* 4
- 8
W: Sum $= -\dfrac{b}{a} = \dfrac{8}{2}$.

Q: The product of the zeroes of $3x^2 + 5x - 6$ is
- 2
* $-2$
- $-\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $-6$
W: Product $= \dfrac{c}{a} = \dfrac{-6}{3}$.

Q: The graph of $y = p(x)$ cuts the $x$-axis at three points. The number of zeroes of $p(x)$ is
- 1
- 2
* 3
- 4
W: Each point where the graph meets the $x$-axis gives a zero.

Q: A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes have sum 4 and product $-5$ is
- $x^2 + 4x - 5$
* $x^2 - 4x - 5$
- $x^2 - 4x + 5$
- $x^2 - 5x + 4$
W: Use $x^2 - (\text{sum})x + (\text{product})$.

Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 6x + 8$, then $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$ is
- 36
- 28
* 20
- 52
W: $(\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = 36 - 16$.

Q: If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$, then $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta}$ is
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
* $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- 5
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
W: $\dfrac{\alpha + \beta}{\alpha\beta} = \dfrac{5}{6}$.

Q: One zero of $x^2 + kx - 12$ is 3. The value of $k$ is
- $-1$
* 1
- 4
- $-4$
W: $9 + 3k - 12 = 0$.

Q: The sum of the zeroes of the cubic polynomial $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$ is
- $-6$
* 6
- 11
- $-11$
W: For $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$, the sum of the zeroes is $-\dfrac{b}{a}$.

Q: The zeroes of $x^2 - 7$ are
- 7 and $-7$
* $\sqrt{7}$ and $-\sqrt{7}$
- 0 and 7
- 49 and $-49$
W: $x^2 = 7$ gives $x = \pm\sqrt{7}$.
