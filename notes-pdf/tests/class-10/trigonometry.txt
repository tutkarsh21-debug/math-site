Q: If $\sin A = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and A is acute, then $\tan A$ is
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
* $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
W: The adjacent side is $\sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$.

Q: $\sin 60° \cos 30° + \sin 30° \cos 60°$ is equal to
- 0
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
* 1
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
W: $\dfrac{3}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} = 1$.

Q: $1 + \tan^2\theta$ is equal to
- $\sin^2\theta$
* $\sec^2\theta$
- $\operatorname{cosec}^2\theta$
- $\cot^2\theta$
W: Divide $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$ by $\cos^2\theta$.

Q: $(1 - \sin^2\theta)\sec^2\theta$ is equal to
- 0
* 1
- $\tan^2\theta$
- $\cos^2\theta$
W: $\cos^2\theta \times \dfrac{1}{\cos^2\theta} = 1$.

Q: $\tan 45° + 2\cos 60°$ is equal to
- 1
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
* 2
- 3
W: $1 + 2 \times \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Q: If $\tan\theta = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ and $\theta$ is acute, then $\theta$ is
* 30°
- 45°
- 60°
- 90°
W: $\tan 30° = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.

Q: $9\sec^2 A - 9\tan^2 A$ is equal to
- 1
* 9
- 0
- 81
W: $9(\sec^2 A - \tan^2 A) = 9 \times 1$.

Q: $(\sec A + \tan A)(1 - \sin A)$ is equal to
- $\sec A$
- $\sin A$
* $\cos A$
- $\operatorname{cosec} A$
W: $\dfrac{(1 + \sin A)(1 - \sin A)}{\cos A} = \dfrac{\cos^2 A}{\cos A}$.

Q: If $\cos A = \dfrac{4}{5}$ and A is acute, then $\cot A$ is
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
* $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
W: The opposite side is 3, and $\cot A = \dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{opposite}}$.

Q: $\dfrac{2\tan 30°}{1 + \tan^2 30°}$ is equal to
* $\sin 60°$
- $\cos 60°$
- $\tan 60°$
- $\sin 30°$
W: $\dfrac{2/\sqrt{3}}{4/3} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
