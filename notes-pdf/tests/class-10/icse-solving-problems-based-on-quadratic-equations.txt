Q: The product of two consecutive natural numbers is 132. The numbers are
- 10 and 11
* 11 and 12
- 12 and 13
- 6 and 22
W: $x(x + 1) = 132$ gives $(x + 12)(x - 11) = 0$.

Q: The sum of a natural number and its square is 72. The number is
- 6
* 8
- 9
- 12
W: $x^2 + x - 72 = 0$ gives $(x + 9)(x - 8) = 0$.

Q: The sum of a positive integer and its reciprocal is $\dfrac{10}{3}$. The integer is
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 10
W: $3x^2 - 10x + 3 = 0$ gives $x = 3$ or $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

Q: A rectangle has perimeter 34 cm and area 60 sq cm. Its length is
- 10 cm
* 12 cm
- 15 cm
- 20 cm
W: $x(17 - x) = 60$ gives $x = 12$ or 5.

Q: The hypotenuse of a right triangle is 13 cm and the other two sides differ by 7 cm. The shortest side is
- 4 cm
* 5 cm
- 6 cm
- 7 cm
W: $x^2 + (x + 7)^2 = 169$ gives $x^2 + 7x - 60 = 0$, so $x = 5$.

Q: The product of Ritu's age 2 years ago and her age 4 years from now is 40. Her present age is
- 4 years
* 6 years
- 8 years
- 10 years
W: $(x - 2)(x + 4) = 40$ gives $x^2 + 2x - 48 = 0$.

Q: A train covers 360 km at a uniform speed. If the speed were 5 km/h more, the journey would take 1 hour less. The speed of the train is
- 36 km/h
* 40 km/h
- 45 km/h
- 60 km/h
W: $\dfrac{360}{x} - \dfrac{360}{x + 5} = 1$ gives $x^2 + 5x - 1800 = 0$.

Q: Some books are bought for Rs 600. If each book cost Rs 5 less, 4 more books could be bought for the same amount. The number of books bought is
- 15
* 20
- 24
- 30
W: $\dfrac{600}{x} - \dfrac{600}{x + 4} = 5$ gives $x^2 + 4x - 480 = 0$.

Q: The sides of two squares differ by 4 cm and the sum of their areas is 400 sq cm. The side of the smaller square is
- 8 cm
* 12 cm
- 14 cm
- 16 cm
W: $x^2 + (x + 4)^2 = 400$ gives $x^2 + 4x - 192 = 0$.

Q: A takes 5 days less than B to do a job, and together they finish it in 6 days. A alone takes
- 6 days
* 10 days
- 12 days
- 15 days
W: $\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{x + 5} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ gives $x^2 - 7x - 30 = 0$.
