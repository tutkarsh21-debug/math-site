Q: Which of these are always similar?
- Two isosceles triangles
- Two right triangles
* Two equilateral triangles
- Two scalene triangles
W: All their angles are 60° and their sides are in the same ratio.

Q: In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC, with D on AB and E on AC. If AD = 2 cm, DB = 3 cm and AE = 4 cm, then EC is
- 5 cm
* 6 cm
- 8 cm
- 1.5 cm
W: $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$ gives $\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{4}{EC}$.

Q: Two triangles are similar by the AA criterion if
- two sides of one are equal to two sides of the other
* two angles of one are equal to two angles of the other
- their areas are equal
- their perimeters are equal
W: The third angles are then equal as well.

Q: $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$, with AB = 4 cm, DE = 6 cm and BC = 6 cm. Then EF is
- 4 cm
- 8 cm
* 9 cm
- 12 cm
W: $\dfrac{AB}{DE} = \dfrac{BC}{EF}$ gives $EF = \dfrac{6 \times 6}{4}$.

Q: A vertical pole 6 m high casts a shadow 4 m long. At the same time a tower casts a shadow 28 m long. The height of the tower is
- 18.7 m
* 42 m
- 36 m
- 168 m
W: The triangles are similar: $\dfrac{h}{28} = \dfrac{6}{4}$.

Q: Triangles with sides 3, 4, 5 and 6, 8, 10 are similar by
- AA
* SSS
- SAS
- RHS
W: The three pairs of sides are in the same ratio, 1 : 2.

Q: In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC, $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and AC = 16 cm. Then AE is
- 3 cm
* 6 cm
- 9.6 cm
- 10 cm
W: $\dfrac{AE}{AC} = \dfrac{3}{8}$.

Q: $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR$, with $\angle A = 50°$ and $\angle B = 60°$. Then $\angle R$ is
- 50°
- 60°
* 70°
- 110°
W: $\angle R = \angle C = 180° - 110°$.

Q: If a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is
- perpendicular to the third side
* parallel to the third side
- equal to the third side
- a median
W: This is the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

Q: The sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio 2 : 3. Their perimeters are in the ratio
* 2 : 3
- 4 : 9
- 3 : 2
- 8 : 27
W: Every length, and so the perimeter, is in the same ratio as the sides.
