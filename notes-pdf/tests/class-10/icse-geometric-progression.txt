Q: The 6th term of the G.P. 2, 6, 18, ... is
- 162
* 486
- 729
- 1458
W: $2 \times 3^5$.

Q: The common ratio of the G.P. 16, 8, 4, ... is
- 2
* $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-8$
- 8
W: $8 \div 16$.

Q: The sum of the first 5 terms of the G.P. 1, 2, 4, ... is
- 16
* 31
- 32
- 63
W: $\dfrac{2^5 - 1}{2 - 1}$.

Q: The geometric mean between 4 and 25 is
- 14.5
* 10
- 29
- 100
W: $\sqrt{4 \times 25}$.

Q: The product of three numbers in G.P. is 216. The middle number is
- 3
* 6
- 8
- 72
W: Take $\dfrac{a}{r}$, $a$, $ar$: the product is $a^3 = 216$.

Q: The $n$th term of a G.P. with first term $a$ and common ratio $r$ is
- $a + (n - 1)r$
* $ar^{n - 1}$
- $ar^n$
- $a^n r$
W: Each term is $r$ times the one before.

Q: Which term of the G.P. 3, 6, 12, ... is 384?
- 7th
* 8th
- 9th
- 128th
W: $3 \times 2^{n - 1} = 384$ gives $2^{n - 1} = 128 = 2^7$.

Q: The sum of the first 4 terms of the G.P. 81, 27, 9, ... is
- 117
* 120
- 121
- 108
W: $81 + 27 + 9 + 3$.

Q: The 4th term of a G.P. is 24 and the 7th term is 192. The first term is
- 2
* 3
- 6
- 8
W: $r^3 = 8$ gives $r = 2$, and $a \times 8 = 24$.

Q: If $k$, 6 and 18 are in G.P., then $k$ is
- 1
* 2
- 3
- 12
W: $6^2 = 18k$.
