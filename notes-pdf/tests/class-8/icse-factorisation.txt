Q: The factors of $6x^2y - 9xy^2$ are
- $3x(2xy - 3y^2)$
* $3xy(2x - 3y)$
- $xy(6x - 9)$
- $3xy(2x + 3y)$
W: The HCF of the two terms is $3xy$.

Q: $ax + ay + bx + by$ is equal to
- $(a + x)(b + y)$
* $(a + b)(x + y)$
- $ab(x + y)$
- $(a + y)(b + x)$
W: Group: $a(x + y) + b(x + y)$.

Q: $4x^2 - 25$ is equal to
- $(2x - 5)^2$
* $(2x - 5)(2x + 5)$
- $(4x - 5)(x + 5)$
- $(4x - 25)(x + 1)$
W: It is a difference of two squares, $(2x)^2 - 5^2$.

Q: $x^2 + 6x + 9$ is equal to
- $(x + 9)(x + 1)$
- $(x - 3)^2$
* $(x + 3)^2$
- $(x + 3)(x - 3)$
W: $x^2 + 2 \times 3 \times x + 3^2$.

Q: $x^2 + 7x + 12$ is equal to
- $(x + 2)(x + 6)$
* $(x + 3)(x + 4)$
- $(x + 1)(x + 12)$
- $(x - 3)(x - 4)$
W: Two numbers with sum 7 and product 12 are 3 and 4.

Q: $x^2 - x - 12$ is equal to
- $(x + 4)(x - 3)$
* $(x - 4)(x + 3)$
- $(x - 6)(x + 2)$
- $(x - 12)(x + 1)$
W: Two numbers with sum $-1$ and product $-12$ are $-4$ and 3.

Q: $2x^2 + 7x + 3$ is equal to
- $(2x + 3)(x + 1)$
* $(2x + 1)(x + 3)$
- $(x + 1)(x + 3)$
- $(2x - 1)(x - 3)$
W: Split $7x$ as $6x + x$: $2x(x + 3) + 1(x + 3)$.

Q: The complete factorisation of $x^4 - 16$ is
- $(x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4)$
- $(x - 2)^4$
* $(x - 2)(x + 2)(x^2 + 4)$
- $(x - 4)(x + 4)$
W: $x^4 - 16 = (x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4)$, and $x^2 - 4$ factorises again.

Q: $(x^2 - 9) \div (x + 3)$ is equal to
- $x + 3$
* $x - 3$
- $x - 6$
- $x^2 - 3$
W: $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$, and the factor $x + 3$ cancels.

Q: The complete factorisation of $3x^2 - 12$ is
- $3(x^2 - 4)$
- $(3x - 6)(x + 2)$
* $3(x - 2)(x + 2)$
- $3(x - 2)^2$
W: Take out the common factor 3 first, then use the difference of squares.
