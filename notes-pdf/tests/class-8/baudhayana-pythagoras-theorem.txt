Q: The legs of a right triangle are 6 cm and 8 cm. The hypotenuse is
- 9 cm
* 10 cm
- 12 cm
- 14 cm
W: $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10$.

Q: The hypotenuse of a right triangle is 13 cm and one leg is 5 cm. The other leg is
- 8 cm
- 10 cm
* 12 cm
- 18 cm
W: $\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$.

Q: The diagonal of a square of side 1 unit is
- 1 unit
* $\sqrt{2}$ units
- 2 units
- $\sqrt{3}$ units
W: The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 1 and 1.

Q: Which of these is a Baudhayana (Pythagorean) triple?
- 5, 6, 7
- 8, 10, 12
* 7, 24, 25
- 9, 12, 16
W: $7^2 + 24^2 = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25^2$.

Q: Each leg of an isosceles right triangle is 5 cm. The hypotenuse is
- 5 cm
- 10 cm
- 25 cm
* $5\sqrt{2}$ cm
W: $\sqrt{25 + 25} = \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$.

Q: A 17 m ladder leans against a wall with its foot 8 m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?
- 9 m
- 12 m
* 15 m
- 25 m
W: $\sqrt{17^2 - 8^2} = \sqrt{225} = 15$.

Q: A square has side $a$. The side of a square with double its area is
- $2a$
* $a\sqrt{2}$
- $4a$
- $\dfrac{a}{2}$
W: The new area is $2a^2$, so the side is $\sqrt{2a^2} = a\sqrt{2}$. This is the diagonal of the first square.

Q: The diagonal of a rectangle 9 cm by 12 cm is
- 13 cm
* 15 cm
- 18 cm
- 21 cm
W: $\sqrt{81 + 144} = \sqrt{225} = 15$.

Q: The number $\sqrt{2}$ lies between
- 1.2 and 1.3
* 1.4 and 1.5
- 1.5 and 1.6
- 1.7 and 1.8
W: $1.4^2 = 1.96$ and $1.5^2 = 2.25$, and 2 lies between them.

Q: A triangle has sides 9 cm, 40 cm and 41 cm. It is
* right-angled
- acute-angled
- obtuse-angled
- equilateral
W: $9^2 + 40^2 = 81 + 1600 = 1681 = 41^2$, so the converse of the theorem applies.
