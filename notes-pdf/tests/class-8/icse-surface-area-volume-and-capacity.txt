Q: The volume of a cuboid 8 cm by 5 cm by 3 cm is
- 16 cubic cm
- 79 cubic cm
* 120 cubic cm
- 158 cubic cm
W: $8 \times 5 \times 3 = 120$.

Q: The total surface area of a cube of edge 5 cm is
- 25 sq cm
- 100 sq cm
- 125 sq cm
* 150 sq cm
W: $6 \times 5^2 = 150$.

Q: The volume of a cube is 343 cubic cm. Its edge is
- 6 cm
* 7 cm
- 8 cm
- 49 cm
W: $7^3 = 343$.

Q: One cubic metre is equal to
- 10 litres
- 100 litres
* 1000 litres
- 10,000 litres
W: 1 cubic metre = 10,00,000 cubic cm, and 1 litre = 1000 cubic cm.

Q: How many bricks, each 20 cm by 10 cm by 5 cm, are needed for a wall 4 m long, 2 m high and 25 cm thick?
- 200
- 1000
* 2000
- 4000
W: The wall is $400 \times 200 \times 25 = 20,00,000$ cubic cm and each brick is 1000 cubic cm.

Q: The volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 440 cubic cm
- 490 cubic cm
* 1540 cubic cm
- 3080 cubic cm
W: $\pi r^2 h = \dfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 10 = 1540$.

Q: The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is (take $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$)
- 220 sq cm
* 440 sq cm
- 748 sq cm
- 1540 sq cm
W: $2\pi rh = 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 10 = 440$.

Q: A room is 6 m long, 4 m wide and 3 m high. The area of its four walls is
- 72 sq m
* 60 sq m
- 48 sq m
- 108 sq m
W: $2(l + b)h = 2 \times 10 \times 3 = 60$.

Q: A metal cube of edge 6 cm is melted and recast into a cuboid 9 cm long and 8 cm wide. The height of the cuboid is
- 2 cm
* 3 cm
- 4 cm
- 6 cm
W: The volume stays 216 cubic cm, and $216 \div (9 \times 8) = 3$.

Q: A tank is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1 m deep. Its capacity is
- 30 litres
- 300 litres
* 3000 litres
- 30,000 litres
W: The volume is 3 cubic metres, and each cubic metre holds 1000 litres.
