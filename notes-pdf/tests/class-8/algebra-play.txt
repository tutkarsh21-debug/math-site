Q: Think of a number, double it, add 6, halve the result and subtract the number you first thought of. The answer is always
- 0
* 3
- 6
- the starting number
W: $\dfrac{2x + 6}{2} - x = x + 3 - x = 3$.

Q: The sum of three consecutive numbers is 72. The smallest of them is
- 22
* 23
- 24
- 25
W: The middle number is $72 \div 3 = 24$, so the numbers are 23, 24 and 25.

Q: In a calendar, a 2 by 2 block of dates has $n$ in its top-left corner. The sum of the four dates is
- $4n + 8$
- $4n + 14$
* $4n + 16$
- $4n + 18$
W: The dates are $n$, $n + 1$, $n + 7$ and $n + 8$.

Q: In a number pyramid, each block is the sum of the two blocks below it. The bottom row is 3, 5, 4. The top block is
- 12
- 15
* 17
- 20
W: The middle row is 8 and 9, and $8 + 9 = 17$.

Q: A two-digit number has tens digit $a$ and units digit $b$. The number is
- $a + b$
- $ab$
* $10a + b$
- $a + 10b$
W: The tens digit is worth 10 times its face value.

Q: A two-digit number minus the number formed by reversing its digits is always divisible by
- 2
- 5
* 9
- 11
W: $(10a + b) - (10b + a) = 9(a - b)$.

Q: If $2x + 7 = 19$, then $x$ is
- 5
* 6
- 12
- 13
W: $2x = 12$, so $x = 6$.

Q: A father is 3 times as old as his son, and the sum of their ages is 48 years. The son's age is
- 9 years
* 12 years
- 16 years
- 36 years
W: $x + 3x = 48$, so $x = 12$.

Q: Two whole numbers add up to 20. The largest possible value of their product is
- 64
- 96
- 99
* 100
W: For a fixed sum, the product is largest when the numbers are equal: $10 \times 10 = 100$.

Q: For any digits $a$, $b$, $c$, the sum of the three-digit numbers $abc$, $bca$ and $cab$ is always divisible by
- 7
- 11
- 13
* 37
W: The sum is $111(a + b + c)$, and $111 = 3 \times 37$.
