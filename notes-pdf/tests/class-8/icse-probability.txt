Q: The probability of a sure event is
- 0
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
* 1
- 100
W: A probability lies between 0 (impossible) and 1 (sure).

Q: Two coins are tossed together. The probability of getting two heads is
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
* $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
W: The outcomes are HH, HT, TH, TT, and one of the four is HH.

Q: A die is thrown once. The probability of getting a prime number is
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
* $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
W: The primes are 2, 3 and 5: three outcomes out of six.

Q: A die is thrown once. The probability of getting a number greater than 4 is
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
* $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
W: The favourable outcomes are 5 and 6: two out of six.

Q: A card is drawn from a well-shuffled pack of 52 cards. The probability that it is a king is
- $\dfrac{1}{52}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
* $\dfrac{1}{13}$
- $\dfrac{4}{13}$
W: There are 4 kings, and $\dfrac{4}{52} = \dfrac{1}{13}$.

Q: A card is drawn from a pack of 52 cards. The probability that it is a red face card is
- $\dfrac{1}{26}$
* $\dfrac{3}{26}$
- $\dfrac{3}{13}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: There are 6 red face cards (jack, queen and king of hearts and diamonds).

Q: A bag has 3 red, 5 blue and 2 green balls. One ball is drawn at random. The probability that it is not red is
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
* $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
W: 7 of the 10 balls are not red.

Q: A letter is chosen at random from the word PROBABILITY. The probability that it is a vowel is
- $\dfrac{3}{11}$
* $\dfrac{4}{11}$
- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: The 11 letters include the vowels O, A, I and I.

Q: A number is chosen at random from 1 to 20. The probability that it is a multiple of 3 is
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
* $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
W: The multiples are 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 and 18: six out of twenty.

Q: A coin is tossed 200 times and heads comes up 110 times. The experimental probability of getting a tail is
- $\dfrac{11}{20}$
* $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{11}$
W: Tails came up 90 times, and $\dfrac{90}{200} = \dfrac{9}{20}$.
