Q: $35^2$ is equal to
- 925
- 1025
* 1225
- 1525
W: For a number ending in 5: $3 \times 4 = 12$, followed by 25.

Q: Which of these cannot be a perfect square?
- 4900
* 36000
- 1600
- 250000
W: A perfect square ends in an even number of zeros. 36000 ends in three.

Q: $\sqrt{7056}$ is equal to
- 76
* 84
- 86
- 94
W: $7056 = 2^4 \times 3^2 \times 7^2$, so the root is $4 \times 3 \times 7 = 84$.

Q: $\sqrt{0.0144}$ is equal to
- 0.012
* 0.12
- 1.2
- 0.0012
W: $\sqrt{144} = 12$, and four decimal places become two.

Q: $\sqrt{1\tfrac{9}{16}}$ is equal to
- $1\tfrac{3}{4}$
* $1\tfrac{1}{4}$
- $\tfrac{3}{4}$
- $1\tfrac{3}{16}$
W: $1\tfrac{9}{16} = \dfrac{25}{16}$, whose square root is $\dfrac{5}{4}$.

Q: The least number that must be subtracted from 2030 to get a perfect square is
- 3
* 5
- 6
- 30
W: $45^2 = 2025$, and $2030 - 2025 = 5$.

Q: The least number that must be added to 1500 to get a perfect square is
- 11
- 19
* 21
- 56
W: $38^2 = 1444$ and $39^2 = 1521$, so add 21.

Q: The area of a square field is 5184 sq m. Its side is
- 68 m
* 72 m
- 78 m
- 82 m
W: $72^2 = 5184$.

Q: The square root of a 5-digit perfect square has
- 2 digits
* 3 digits
- 4 digits
- 5 digits
W: Pair the digits from the right: a 5-digit number gives 3 pairs (the last pair has one digit).

Q: $\sqrt{12.25}$ is equal to
- 3.05
* 3.5
- 3.75
- 6.125
W: $35^2 = 1225$, so $3.5^2 = 12.25$.
