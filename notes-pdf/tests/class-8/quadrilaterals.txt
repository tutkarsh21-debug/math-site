Q: The sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral is
- 180°
- 270°
* 360°
- 540°
W: A diagonal splits a quadrilateral into two triangles, each with angle sum 180°.

Q: Three angles of a quadrilateral are 80°, 95° and 110°. The fourth angle is
- 65°
* 75°
- 85°
- 95°
W: $360° - (80° + 95° + 110°) = 75°$.

Q: One angle of a parallelogram is 70°. The angle adjacent to it is
- 70°
- 20°
- 90°
* 110°
W: Adjacent angles of a parallelogram add up to 180°.

Q: In which quadrilateral do the diagonals always bisect each other at right angles?
- Rectangle
* Rhombus
- Trapezium
- Every parallelogram
W: The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular bisectors of each other.

Q: The diagonals of a rectangle are always
- perpendicular
- unequal
* equal and bisect each other
- bisectors of the angles
W: A rectangle is a parallelogram (diagonals bisect each other) with equal diagonals.

Q: The diagonals of a rhombus are 6 cm and 8 cm. Its side is
* 5 cm
- 7 cm
- 10 cm
- 14 cm
W: The half-diagonals 3 cm and 4 cm are the legs of a right triangle whose hypotenuse is the side: $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.

Q: A quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides is a
- kite
- rhombus
- parallelogram
* trapezium
W: That is the definition of a trapezium.

Q: A kite has
- both pairs of opposite sides equal
* two pairs of equal adjacent sides
- all angles equal
- both pairs of opposite sides parallel
W: In a kite, the equal sides are next to each other, not opposite.

Q: Every square is
- a rectangle but not a rhombus
- a rhombus but not a rectangle
* both a rectangle and a rhombus
- a trapezium only
W: A square has four right angles (rectangle) and four equal sides (rhombus).

Q: In parallelogram ABCD, $\angle A = 3x$ and $\angle B = 2x$. The value of $x$ is
- 30°
* 36°
- 45°
- 60°
W: Adjacent angles add up to 180°, so $5x = 180°$ and $x = 36°$.
