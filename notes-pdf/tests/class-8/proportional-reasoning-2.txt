Q: A map has a scale of 1 : 50,000. Two towns are 4 cm apart on the map. The actual distance is
- 200 m
* 2 km
- 20 km
- 200 km
W: $4 \times 50000 = 200000$ cm, which is 2000 m or 2 km.

Q: Rs 600 is divided in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. The largest share is
- Rs 120
- Rs 180
- Rs 250
* Rs 300
W: There are 10 parts of Rs 60 each. The largest share is $5 \times 60 = 300$.

Q: 6 workers can finish a job in 12 days. At the same rate, 8 workers finish it in
- 16 days
- 10 days
* 9 days
- 8 days
W: The job needs $6 \times 12 = 72$ worker-days, and $72 \div 8 = 9$.

Q: In a pie chart, a sector that stands for 25% of the whole has a central angle of
- 25°
- 45°
* 90°
- 100°
W: 25% of 360° is 90°.

Q: $x$ and $y$ are inversely proportional, and $x = 4$ when $y = 15$. When $x = 10$, $y$ is
- 37.5
- 9
* 6
- 1.5
W: The product stays the same: $xy = 60$, so $y = 60 \div 10 = 6$.

Q: A can do a job in 6 days and B can do it in 3 days. Working together they finish it in
- 9 days
- 4.5 days
* 2 days
- 1 day
W: In one day they do $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ of the job.

Q: The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. The largest angle is
- 60°
* 90°
- 100°
- 120°
W: There are 6 parts of 30° each, so the largest angle is 90°.

Q: A journey takes 4 hours at 60 km/h. At 80 km/h it takes
- 2 hours
* 3 hours
- 3.5 hours
- 5 hours 20 minutes
W: The distance is 240 km, and $240 \div 80 = 3$.

Q: A pie chart shows the favourite sports of 500 students. The sector for cricket has an angle of 72°. How many students chose cricket?
- 72
* 100
- 125
- 144
W: $\dfrac{72}{360} \times 500 = 100$.

Q: Which pair of quantities is inversely proportional?
- The number of pens bought and their total cost
- The side of a square and its perimeter
* The speed and the time taken for a fixed distance
- The distance covered and the time taken at a steady speed
W: When the distance is fixed, doubling the speed halves the time.
