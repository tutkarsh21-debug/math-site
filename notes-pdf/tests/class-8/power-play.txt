Q: The value of $(-2)^5$ is
- 32
- $-10$
* $-32$
- $-16$
W: An odd power of a negative number is negative, and $2^5 = 32$.

Q: $3^4 \times 3^3$ is equal to
* $3^7$
- $3^{12}$
- $9^7$
- $9^{12}$
W: With the same base, add the exponents: $4 + 3 = 7$.

Q: $(5^2)^3$ is equal to
- $5^5$
* $5^6$
- $5^8$
- $5^9$
W: A power of a power: multiply the exponents, $2 \times 3 = 6$.

Q: The value of $7^0 + 7^1$ is
- 0
- 1
- 7
* 8
W: $7^0 = 1$ and $7^1 = 7$, so the sum is 8.

Q: $2^{-3}$ is equal to
- $-8$
- $-6$
* $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
W: $2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{2^3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

Q: The value of $6^5 \div 6^2$ is
- 36
* 216
- 1296
- 7776
W: Subtract the exponents: $6^{5-2} = 6^3 = 216$.

Q: $2^4 \times 5^4$ is equal to
- 1000
- $7^4$
* 10000
- $10^8$
W: With the same exponent, multiply the bases: $(2 \times 5)^4 = 10^4 = 10000$.

Q: In scientific notation, 4,50,000 is written as
- $45 \times 10^4$
- $4.5 \times 10^4$
- $0.45 \times 10^6$
* $4.5 \times 10^5$
W: The first factor must be at least 1 and less than 10. The decimal point moves 5 places.

Q: A pile has 1 coin. The number of coins is doubled 10 times. How many coins are there now?
- 20
- 100
- 512
* 1024
W: Ten doublings multiply by $2^{10} = 1024$.

Q: Ravi has 3 shirts, 4 trousers and 2 caps. In how many ways can he choose one of each?
- 9
- 12
- 14
* 24
W: Multiply the choices: $3 \times 4 \times 2 = 24$.
