Q: An article bought for Rs 250 is sold for Rs 300. The profit per cent is
- 16.67%
* 20%
- 25%
- 50%
W: The profit of Rs 50 is compared with the C.P.: $\dfrac{50}{250} \times 100 = 20$.

Q: The C.P. of an article is Rs 600 and it is sold at a loss of 15%. The S.P. is
- Rs 585
- Rs 540
* Rs 510
- Rs 690
W: The loss is Rs 90, so the S.P. is Rs 510.

Q: An article is sold for Rs 690 at a profit of 15%. Its C.P. is
- Rs 586.50
* Rs 600
- Rs 675
- Rs 793.50
W: S.P. is 115% of C.P., so C.P. $= 690 \times \dfrac{100}{115} = 600$.
