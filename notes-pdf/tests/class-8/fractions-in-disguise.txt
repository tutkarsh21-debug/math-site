Q: $\dfrac{3}{8}$ written as a percentage is
- 26.6%
- 35%
* 37.5%
- 38%
W: $\dfrac{3}{8} \times 100 = 37.5$.

Q: 15% of 240 is
- 24
* 36
- 48
- 16
W: 10% is 24 and 5% is 12, so 15% is 36.

Q: 150% of 80 is
- 40
- 95
* 120
- 150
W: 150% means one and a half times: $1.5 \times 80 = 120$.

Q: The price of a bag rises from Rs 400 to Rs 460. The percentage increase is
- 6%
- 13%
* 15%
- 60%
W: The increase is Rs 60 on Rs 400: $\dfrac{60}{400} \times 100 = 15$.

Q: A shopkeeper buys a toy for Rs 800 and sells it for Rs 720. The loss percentage is
- 8%
* 10%
- 11.1%
- 80%
W: The loss is Rs 80 on a cost price of Rs 800, which is 10%. Loss per cent is always found on the cost price.

Q: A shirt marked at Rs 1200 is sold at a discount of 25%. Its selling price is
- Rs 300
- Rs 950
- Rs 1175
* Rs 900
W: The discount is Rs 300, so the selling price is Rs 900.

Q: The simple interest on Rs 5000 at 8% per year for 3 years is
- Rs 400
- Rs 800
* Rs 1200
- Rs 1500
W: $\dfrac{5000 \times 8 \times 3}{100} = 1200$.

Q: Rs 2000 is invested at 10% per year, compounded yearly. The amount after 2 years is
- Rs 2200
- Rs 2400
* Rs 2420
- Rs 2440
W: After one year it is Rs 2200, and 10% of that is Rs 220, giving Rs 2420.

Q: A price is increased by 20% and the new price is then decreased by 20%. Compared with the original, the final price is
- the same
- 4% more
* 4% less
- 2% less
W: $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$, which is 4% less than the original.

Q: A machine worth Rs 50,000 loses 10% of its value every year. Its value after 2 years is
- Rs 40,000
* Rs 40,500
- Rs 41,000
- Rs 45,000
W: After one year it is Rs 45,000, and after two years it is $0.9 \times 45000 = 40500$.
