Q: $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-2}$ is equal to
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{-4}{9}$
* $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{-9}{4}$
W: A negative exponent inverts the fraction: $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}$.

Q: $5^0 \times 5^{-1}$ is equal to
- 0
- $-5$
- 5
* $\dfrac{1}{5}$
W: $5^0 = 1$ and $5^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{5}$.

Q: $8^{2/3}$ is equal to
- 2
* 4
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- 16
W: The cube root of 8 is 2, and $2^2 = 4$.

Q: $(x^3)^4 \div x^5$ is equal to
- $x^2$
* $x^7$
- $x^{12}$
- $x^{17}$
W: $x^{12} \div x^5 = x^{12 - 5} = x^7$.

Q: If $3^x = 81$, then $x$ is
- 3
* 4
- 9
- 27
W: $81 = 3^4$.

Q: If $2^{x+1} = 32$, then $x$ is
- 3
* 4
- 5
- 6
W: $32 = 2^5$, so $x + 1 = 5$.

Q: 0.00045 in standard form is
- $45 \times 10^{-5}$
- $4.5 \times 10^{-3}$
* $4.5 \times 10^{-4}$
- $4.5 \times 10^{4}$
W: The decimal point moves 4 places to the right, so the exponent is $-4$.

Q: The value of $(-1)^{101}$ is
- 1
* $-1$
- 101
- $-101$
W: An odd power of $-1$ is $-1$.

Q: $16^{3/4}$ is equal to
- 4
- 6
* 8
- 12
W: The fourth root of 16 is 2, and $2^3 = 8$.

Q: $\left(3^{-1} + 2^{-1}\right)^{-1}$ is equal to
- 5
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
* $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
W: $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{6}$, and its reciprocal is $\dfrac{6}{5}$.
