Q: The standard form of $\dfrac{-18}{24}$ is
- $\dfrac{-9}{12}$
* $\dfrac{-3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{-4}$
- $\dfrac{-6}{8}$
W: Divide both by their HCF, 6, and keep the denominator positive.

Q: The additive inverse of $\dfrac{-5}{7}$ is
- $\dfrac{-7}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{5}$
* $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- 0
W: A number and its additive inverse add up to 0.

Q: The multiplicative inverse of $\dfrac{-3}{8}$ is
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
* $\dfrac{-8}{3}$
- 1
W: The product of a number and its reciprocal is 1, and $\dfrac{-3}{8} \times \dfrac{-8}{3} = 1$.

Q: $\dfrac{2}{3} + \left(\dfrac{-5}{6}\right)$ is equal to
- $\dfrac{-3}{9}$
* $\dfrac{-1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{-1}{3}$
W: $\dfrac{4}{6} - \dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{-1}{6}$.

Q: $\dfrac{-3}{4} \times \dfrac{8}{9}$ is equal to
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{-27}{32}$
* $\dfrac{-2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{-5}{13}$
W: $\dfrac{-24}{36} = \dfrac{-2}{3}$.

Q: $\dfrac{5}{6} \div \left(\dfrac{-10}{3}\right)$ is equal to
- $\dfrac{-25}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $-4$
* $\dfrac{-1}{4}$
W: Multiply by the reciprocal: $\dfrac{5}{6} \times \dfrac{-3}{10} = \dfrac{-15}{60} = \dfrac{-1}{4}$.

Q: Which of these rational numbers is the greatest?
- $\dfrac{-2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{-3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{-5}{6}$
* $\dfrac{-7}{12}$
W: With denominator 12 they are $-8$, $-9$, $-10$ and $-7$ twelfths. The greatest is $\dfrac{-7}{12}$.

Q: Which rational number lies between $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$?
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
* $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
W: $\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{2}{8}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{4}{8}$, and $\dfrac{3}{8}$ lies between them.

Q: $a \times (b + c) = a \times b + a \times c$ is called the
- commutative property
- associative property
* distributive property
- closure property
W: Multiplication is distributed over addition.

Q: Which rational number has no reciprocal?
- 1
- $-1$
* 0
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
W: Division by zero is not defined, so 0 has no reciprocal.
