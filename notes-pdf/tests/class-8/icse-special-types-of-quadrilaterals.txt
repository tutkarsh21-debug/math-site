Q: In parallelogram ABCD, $\angle A = 65°$. Then $\angle C$ is
* 65°
- 115°
- 25°
- 130°
W: Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal.

Q: The perimeter of a rhombus is 40 cm and one diagonal is 12 cm. The other diagonal is
- 8 cm
- 10 cm
* 16 cm
- 20 cm
W: The side is 10 cm and half of the given diagonal is 6 cm, so half of the other is $\sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$ cm.

Q: The diagonals of rectangle ABCD meet at O. If AO = 6.5 cm, then BD is
- 6.5 cm
* 13 cm
- 3.25 cm
- 26 cm
W: The diagonals are equal and bisect each other, so BD = AC = 2 × AO.

Q: The angle between a diagonal of a square and a side is
- 30°
* 45°
- 60°
- 90°
W: The diagonal bisects the right angle at the vertex.

Q: In an isosceles trapezium, the diagonals are
- perpendicular
* equal
- bisectors of each other
- parallel
W: The non-parallel sides are equal, which makes the diagonals equal. They do not bisect each other.

Q: The diagonals of a kite
- are equal
- bisect each other
* meet at right angles
- are parallel
W: One diagonal is the perpendicular bisector of the other.

Q: The adjacent sides of a parallelogram are in the ratio 2 : 3 and its perimeter is 50 cm. The longer side is
- 10 cm
* 15 cm
- 20 cm
- 30 cm
W: $2(2x + 3x) = 50$ gives $x = 5$, so the sides are 10 cm and 15 cm.

Q: A quadrilateral whose diagonals are equal and bisect each other at right angles is a
- rectangle
- rhombus
* square
- kite
W: Equal diagonals come from the rectangle and perpendicular diagonals from the rhombus. Only a square has both.

Q: One angle of a rhombus is 60° and its side is 8 cm. The shorter diagonal is
- 4 cm
* 8 cm
- $8\sqrt{3}$ cm
- 16 cm
W: The shorter diagonal forms an equilateral triangle with two sides of the rhombus.

Q: In trapezium ABCD, AB is parallel to DC and $\angle A = 110°$. Then $\angle D$ is
- 110°
* 70°
- 90°
- 250°
W: Angles A and D are co-interior angles between the parallel sides, so they add up to 180°.
