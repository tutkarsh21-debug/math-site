Q: The degree of the polynomial $3x^2y^3 - 5xy + 7$ is
- 2
- 3
* 5
- 6
W: The degree of a term is the sum of the exponents of its variables. The first term has degree $2 + 3 = 5$.

Q: The number of terms in $4x^2 - 3xy + 2y^2 - 7$ is
- 2
- 3
* 4
- 7
W: Terms are separated by + and − signs.

Q: The sum of $3a - 2b + c$ and $-a + 5b - 4c$ is
- $4a + 3b - 3c$
* $2a + 3b - 3c$
- $2a - 7b + 5c$
- $2a + 3b + 5c$
W: Add like terms: $3a - a$, $-2b + 5b$, $c - 4c$.

Q: When $2x^2 - 3x + 1$ is subtracted from $5x^2 + x - 4$, the result is
- $3x^2 - 2x - 3$
- $-3x^2 - 4x + 5$
* $3x^2 + 4x - 5$
- $7x^2 - 2x - 3$
W: $5x^2 + x - 4 - 2x^2 + 3x - 1 = 3x^2 + 4x - 5$.

Q: $(3x) \times (-4x^2y)$ is equal to
- $-12x^2y$
* $-12x^3y$
- $-x^3y$
- $12x^3y$
W: Multiply the coefficients and add the exponents of $x$.

Q: $(x + 2)(x - 7)$ is equal to
- $x^2 - 14$
- $x^2 + 5x - 14$
* $x^2 - 5x - 14$
- $x^2 - 9x - 14$
W: $x^2 - 7x + 2x - 14$.

Q: $(12x^3 - 8x^2) \div 4x$ is equal to
- $3x^2 - 2$
* $3x^2 - 2x$
- $3x^3 - 2x^2$
- $8x^2 - 4x$
W: Divide each term by $4x$.

Q: $a - [b - (a - b)]$ simplifies to
- 0
- $2a$
* $2a - 2b$
- $-2b$
W: $a - [b - a + b] = a - 2b + a$.

Q: The value of $2x^2 - 3x + 5$ when $x = -2$ is
- 7
- 3
* 19
- $-9$
W: $2(4) + 6 + 5 = 19$.

Q: A polynomial with exactly two terms is called a
- monomial
* binomial
- trinomial
- constant
W: "Bi" means two.
