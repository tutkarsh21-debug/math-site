Q: The area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 7 cm is
- 84 sq cm
* 42 sq cm
- 38 sq cm
- 19 sq cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 7 = 42$.

Q: The area of a parallelogram with base 15 cm and height 8 cm is
- 46 sq cm
- 60 sq cm
* 120 sq cm
- 240 sq cm
W: Area = base × height = $15 \times 8$.

Q: The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. Its area is
- 48 sq cm
* 96 sq cm
- 192 sq cm
- 28 sq cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 16 \times 12 = 96$.

Q: The parallel sides of a trapezium are 10 cm and 14 cm, and the distance between them is 5 cm. Its area is
- 120 sq cm
- 70 sq cm
* 60 sq cm
- 29 sq cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times (10 + 14) \times 5 = 60$.

Q: A rectangular lawn 20 m by 10 m has a path 2 m wide all around it on the outside. The area of the path is
- 64 sq m
- 124 sq m
* 136 sq m
- 336 sq m
W: The outer rectangle is 24 m by 14 m, with area 336 sq m. Subtract the lawn: $336 - 200 = 136$.

Q: One hectare is equal to
- 100 sq m
- 1000 sq m
* 10,000 sq m
- 1,00,000 sq m
W: A hectare is a square of side 100 m.

Q: A triangle has area 54 sq cm and base 12 cm. Its height is
- 4.5 cm
* 9 cm
- 18 cm
- 21 cm
W: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times 12 \times h = 54$, so $h = 9$.

Q: Two triangles stand on the same base and lie between the same pair of parallel lines. Their areas are
* equal
- in the ratio 1 : 2
- in the ratio of their perimeters
- impossible to compare
W: They have the same base and the same height.

Q: The perimeter of a square is 36 cm. Its area is
- 36 sq cm
- 72 sq cm
* 81 sq cm
- 324 sq cm
W: The side is $36 \div 4 = 9$ cm, and $9^2 = 81$.

Q: A median of a triangle divides it into two triangles that
- are always congruent
* have equal areas
- have equal perimeters
- are always right-angled
W: The two triangles have equal bases (the median bisects the side) and the same height.
