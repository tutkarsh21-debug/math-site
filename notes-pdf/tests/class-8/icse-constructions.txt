Q: How many independent measurements are needed to construct a unique quadrilateral?
- 3
- 4
* 5
- 6
W: For example four sides and one diagonal, or three sides and two included angles.

Q: An angle of 60° is constructed by
- bisecting a straight angle
* cutting an arc with the same radius from a point on the first arc
- drawing a perpendicular
- bisecting an angle of 90°
W: The three points form an equilateral triangle, each angle of which is 60°.

Q: Which angle cannot be constructed using a ruler and compasses only?
- 15°
- 22.5°
- 75°
* 40°
W: 15°, 22.5° and 75° come from bisecting and adding 60° and 90°. An angle of 40° cannot be obtained in this way.

Q: The least number of measurements needed to construct a parallelogram is
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 5
W: For example two adjacent sides and the included angle. The other sides follow because opposite sides are equal and parallel.

Q: The least number of measurements needed to construct a square is
* 1
- 2
- 3
- 4
W: One side (or one diagonal) is enough, as all sides are equal and all angles are 90°.

Q: To construct a rhombus when both diagonals are given, we first draw one diagonal and then
- an angle of 60° at one end
* its perpendicular bisector
- a parallel line through its midpoint
- a circle on it as diameter
W: The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles.

Q: Every point on the perpendicular bisector of a line segment AB is
- nearer to A than to B
- at a distance AB from A
* equidistant from A and B
- on the segment AB
W: This property is what makes the construction with equal arcs work.

Q: A rectangle has sides 6 cm and 8 cm. Its diagonal, which can be used to check the construction, is
- 7 cm
* 10 cm
- 12 cm
- 14 cm
W: $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10$.

Q: To construct quadrilateral ABCD given its four sides and diagonal AC, the first step is to construct
- angle ABC
* triangle ABC from AB, BC and AC
- side CD
- the perpendicular bisector of AC
W: The diagonal splits the quadrilateral into two triangles, each of which can be built from three sides.

Q: A quadrilateral ABCD with AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm and diagonal AC = 8 cm cannot be constructed because
- the sides are too short
* AB + BC is less than AC
- no angle is given
- AC must be equal to BC
W: In triangle ABC, the sum of two sides must be greater than the third, but $3 + 4 < 8$.
