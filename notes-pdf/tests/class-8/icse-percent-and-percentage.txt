Q: $\dfrac{7}{20}$ as a percentage is
- 7%
- 14%
* 35%
- 70%
W: $\dfrac{7}{20} \times 100 = 35$.

Q: 12.5% as a fraction is
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
* $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
W: $\dfrac{12.5}{100} = \dfrac{125}{1000} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

Q: 18% of 250 is
- 36
* 45
- 54
- 72
W: $\dfrac{18}{100} \times 250 = 45$.

Q: What per cent of 80 is 20?
- 4%
- 20%
* 25%
- 40%
W: $\dfrac{20}{80} \times 100 = 25$.

Q: 30% of a number is 45. The number is
- 13.5
- 135
* 150
- 175
W: The number is $45 \times \dfrac{100}{30} = 150$.

Q: 250 increased by 12% becomes
- 262
- 270
* 280
- 300
W: 12% of 250 is 30, and $250 + 30 = 280$.

Q: A salary is reduced from Rs 20,000 to Rs 17,000. The percentage decrease is
- 3%
* 15%
- 17.6%
- 30%
W: The decrease of Rs 3000 is compared with the original Rs 20,000: 15%.

Q: In a class of 40 students, 26 are girls. The percentage of boys is
- 14%
- 26%
* 35%
- 65%
W: There are 14 boys, and $\dfrac{14}{40} \times 100 = 35$.

Q: A is 25% more than B. Then B is less than A by
- 25%
* 20%
- 80%
- 12.5%
W: If B is 100, A is 125. B is 25 less than 125, which is 20% of A.

Q: A student scores 432 marks out of 600. The percentage of marks is
- 68%
* 72%
- 75%
- 78%
W: $\dfrac{432}{600} \times 100 = 72$.
