Q: A cube has how many edges?
- 6
- 8
* 12
- 16
W: A cube has 6 faces, 8 vertices and 12 edges.

Q: A solid with flat faces has 7 faces and 10 vertices. How many edges does it have?
- 12
* 15
- 17
- 19
W: Euler's formula gives $F + V - E = 2$, so $E = 7 + 10 - 2 = 15$.

Q: In the Sierpinski triangle, step 0 has 1 shaded triangle and each step replaces every shaded triangle by 3 smaller ones. How many shaded triangles are there at step 3?
- 9
- 12
* 27
- 81
W: The count is multiplied by 3 at each step: 1, 3, 9, 27.

Q: In the Sierpinski carpet, each square is cut into 9 equal squares and the middle one is removed. How many squares remain from each square?
- 3
- 6
* 8
- 9
W: 9 small squares minus the one in the middle leaves 8.

Q: A Koch snowflake starts as an equilateral triangle. In one step, each side is replaced by 4 shorter segments. How many sides does the shape have after the first step?
- 6
- 9
* 12
- 16
W: Each of the 3 sides becomes 4 sides: $3 \times 4 = 12$.

Q: A net of a cube is made of
- 4 squares
* 6 squares
- 8 squares
- 12 squares
W: A cube has 6 square faces, so its net has 6 squares.

Q: How many faces does a square pyramid have?
- 4
* 5
- 6
- 8
W: One square base and four triangular faces.

Q: To find the shortest path between two points on the surface of a cuboid, we
- measure along the edges only
* unfold the faces into a flat net and join the points with a straight line
- join the points through the inside of the cuboid
- go around the largest face
W: On a flat net the shortest path is a straight line. Different unfoldings are compared and the shortest is chosen.

Q: A cylinder stands upright on its base. Its top view is a
- rectangle
- triangle
- square
* circle
W: Looking from above, only the circular top is seen.

Q: A triangular prism has how many vertices?
- 5
* 6
- 8
- 9
W: Each of the two triangular ends has 3 vertices.
