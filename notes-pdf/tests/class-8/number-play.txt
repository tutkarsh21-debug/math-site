Q: The sum of two odd numbers is always
- odd
* even
- a multiple of 4
- prime
W: Odd + odd = even, for example $3 + 5 = 8$.

Q: Which number is divisible by 4?
- 2314
- 7122
* 3516
- 9030
W: Check the last two digits: 16 is divisible by 4, while 14, 22 and 30 are not.

Q: Which number is divisible by 9?
- 4721
* 5814
- 6130
- 8254
W: The digits of 5814 add up to 18, which is divisible by 9.

Q: Which number is divisible by 11?
- 5216
- 7302
- 4825
* 9174
W: For 9174, $9 - 1 + 7 - 4 = 11$, which is divisible by 11.

Q: The sum of any three consecutive whole numbers is always divisible by
- 2
* 3
- 4
- 6
W: $n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) = 3(n + 1)$.

Q: The remainder when 2347 is divided by 9 is
- 5
- 6
* 7
- 8
W: A number and the sum of its digits leave the same remainder on division by 9. The digit sum is 16, and 16 leaves remainder 7.

Q: The digital root of 9875 is
* 2
- 9
- 11
- 29
W: $9 + 8 + 7 + 5 = 29$, then $2 + 9 = 11$, then $1 + 1 = 2$.

Q: A number divisible by both 3 and 4 is always divisible by
- 7
- 8
* 12
- 24
W: 3 and 4 have no common factor, so the number is divisible by $3 \times 4 = 12$.

Q: The three-digit number 3d5 is divisible by 9. The digit d is
- 0
* 1
- 4
- 7
W: The digit sum $8 + d$ must be a multiple of 9, so $d = 1$.

Q: A is a digit and BA is a two-digit number such that A + A + A = BA. The value of A is
- 2
- 4
* 5
- 9
W: $3 \times A$ must end in A. Only $3 \times 5 = 15$ works, so A = 5 and B = 1.
