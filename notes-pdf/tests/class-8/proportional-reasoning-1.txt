Q: The ratio of 45 cm to 1.5 m in simplest form is
- 30 : 1
- 3 : 1
* 3 : 10
- 9 : 3
W: Use the same unit: 45 cm to 150 cm, which is 3 : 10.

Q: Which ratio is proportional to 4 : 6?
- 6 : 8
- 8 : 10
* 10 : 15
- 12 : 16
W: Both 4 : 6 and 10 : 15 simplify to 2 : 3.

Q: 5 pens cost Rs 60. At the same rate, 8 pens cost
- Rs 84
- Rs 90
* Rs 96
- Rs 100
W: One pen costs Rs 12, so 8 pens cost Rs 96.

Q: If $x : 12 = 5 : 4$, then $x$ is
- 9.6
- 12
* 15
- 20
W: $4x = 60$, so $x = 15$.

Q: Rs 720 is divided between two people in the ratio 4 : 5. The smaller share is
- Rs 288
* Rs 320
- Rs 360
- Rs 400
W: There are 9 parts of Rs 80 each. The smaller share is $4 \times 80 = 320$.

Q: Car A covers 150 km in 3 hours and car B covers 220 km in 4 hours. Which is faster?
- Car A, at 50 km/h
* Car B, at 55 km/h
- Car A, at 55 km/h
- Both have the same speed
W: Compare unit rates: A does 50 km in an hour and B does 55 km in an hour.

Q: A 20-litre mixture has milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. The quantity of milk is
- 5 litres
- 12 litres
* 15 litres
- 18 litres
W: Milk is 3 parts out of 4: $\dfrac{3}{4} \times 20 = 15$.

Q: In which situation is it wrong to use the Rule of Three?
- The cost of 7 notebooks, given the cost of 3
- The distance covered in 5 hours at a steady speed, given the distance in 2 hours
* The weight of a child at age 12, given the weight at age 6
- The wages for 9 days, given the wages for 4 days
W: Weight does not grow in proportion to age, so the two quantities are not proportional.

Q: 2.5 km is equal to
- 25 m
- 250 m
* 2500 m
- 25000 m
W: 1 km = 1000 m, so 2.5 km = 2500 m.

Q: A 12-litre drink has juice and water in the ratio 1 : 2. If 4 litres of juice are added, the new ratio of juice to water is
* 1 : 1
- 2 : 1
- 1 : 3
- 3 : 2
W: At first there are 4 litres of juice and 8 of water. After adding, there are 8 litres of each.
