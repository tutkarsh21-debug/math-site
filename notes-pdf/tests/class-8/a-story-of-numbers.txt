Q: The Roman numeral XLIV stands for
* 44
- 46
- 64
- 66
W: XL is 40 (10 before 50) and IV is 4 (1 before 5).

Q: In Roman numerals, 99 is written as
- IC
- LXXXXIX
* XCIX
- XCVIIII
W: 90 is XC and 9 is IX. A symbol is not repeated four times, and I is not placed before C.

Q: Which Roman symbol stands for 500?
- C
* D
- L
- M
W: L is 50, C is 100, D is 500 and M is 1000.

Q: How many different digit symbols does the Hindu (decimal) number system use?
- 2
- 9
* 10
- 12
W: The ten digits 0 to 9 are enough to write every number, because of place value.

Q: The place value of 7 in 4,72,315 is
- 7
- 7,000
* 70,000
- 7,00,000
W: The 7 is in the ten-thousands place.

Q: The symbol that lets us tell 52 apart from 502 is
* 0
- 1
- 2
- 5
W: Zero holds an empty place, which is what makes a place-value system work.

Q: The base-2 number 1011 is equal to which number in base 10?
- 7
- 9
* 11
- 13
W: $1 \times 8 + 0 \times 4 + 1 \times 2 + 1 \times 1 = 11$.

Q: The number 13 written in base 2 is
- 1011
* 1101
- 1110
- 1001
W: $13 = 8 + 4 + 1$, so the digits are 1, 1, 0, 1.

Q: In base 5, the numeral 43 stands for which number in base 10?
- 19
* 23
- 35
- 43
W: $4 \times 5 + 3 = 23$.

Q: In a base-8 system, the place values are powers of
- 2
* 8
- 10
- 16
W: In any base, each place is worth the base times the place to its right: 1, 8, 64, 512 and so on.
