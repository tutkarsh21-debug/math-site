Q: The cube of $-7$ is
- 343
- $-49$
- $-21$
* $-343$
W: The cube of a negative number is negative, and $7^3 = 343$.

Q: Which of these is a perfect cube?
- 121
- 900
* 1331
- 1600
W: $11^3 = 1331$.

Q: $\sqrt[3]{-512}$ is equal to
- 8
* $-8$
- $-6$
- not defined
W: $(-8)^3 = -512$. Negative numbers do have cube roots.

Q: The cube of an odd number is always
- even
* odd
- a multiple of 3
- a perfect square
W: Odd × odd × odd is odd.

Q: The smallest number by which 392 must be multiplied to get a perfect cube is
- 2
- 3
* 7
- 14
W: $392 = 2^3 \times 7^2$. One more 7 completes the triple.

Q: The smallest number by which 1323 must be divided to get a perfect cube is
- 3
- 7
- 21
* 49
W: $1323 = 3^3 \times 7^2$. Removing $7^2 = 49$ leaves $27 = 3^3$.

Q: $\sqrt[3]{0.027}$ is equal to
- 0.03
* 0.3
- 0.9
- 3
W: $0.3^3 = 0.027$.

Q: $\sqrt[3]{8 \times 125}$ is equal to
- 7
* 10
- 20
- 40
W: $\sqrt[3]{8} \times \sqrt[3]{125} = 2 \times 5 = 10$.

Q: The volume of a cube is 2197 cubic cm. Its edge is
- 11 cm
- 12 cm
* 13 cm
- 17 cm
W: $13^3 = 2197$.

Q: By inspection, the cube root of 17576 is
- 16
* 26
- 36
- 24
W: The units digit 6 gives a units digit of 6. Ignoring the last three digits leaves 17, and $2^3 = 8 < 17 < 27 = 3^3$, so the tens digit is 2.
