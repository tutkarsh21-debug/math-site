Q: The sum of the interior angles of a hexagon is
- 540°
* 720°
- 900°
- 1080°
W: $(n - 2) \times 180° = 4 \times 180°$.

Q: Each interior angle of a regular octagon is
- 108°
- 120°
* 135°
- 144°
W: Each exterior angle is $360° \div 8 = 45°$, and $180° - 45° = 135°$.

Q: Each exterior angle of a regular polygon with 12 sides is
- 15°
* 30°
- 36°
- 150°
W: $360° \div 12 = 30°$.

Q: The sum of the exterior angles of any convex polygon, one at each vertex, is
- 180°
* 360°
- 540°
- different for different polygons
W: It is always 360°, whatever the number of sides.

Q: Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 40°. The number of its sides is
- 6
- 8
* 9
- 10
W: $360° \div 40° = 9$.

Q: The number of diagonals of a pentagon is
- 2
* 5
- 7
- 10
W: $\dfrac{n(n - 3)}{2} = \dfrac{5 \times 2}{2} = 5$.

Q: The number of diagonals of an octagon is
- 8
- 16
* 20
- 28
W: $\dfrac{8 \times 5}{2} = 20$.

Q: Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 144°. The number of its sides is
- 8
* 10
- 12
- 15
W: Each exterior angle is 36°, and $360° \div 36° = 10$.

Q: The sum of the interior angles of a polygon is 900°. The number of its sides is
- 5
- 6
* 7
- 9
W: $(n - 2) \times 180° = 900°$ gives $n - 2 = 5$.

Q: A polygon is concave if
- all its sides are equal
* at least one interior angle is greater than 180°
- all its interior angles are less than 180°
- it has more than six sides
W: In a concave polygon, at least one vertex points inwards.
