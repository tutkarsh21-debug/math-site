Q: If $3x - 7 = 11$, then $x$ is
- 4
* 6
- 18
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
W: $3x = 18$.

Q: If $5(x - 2) = 3(x + 4)$, then $x$ is
- 1
- 3
* 11
- 22
W: $5x - 10 = 3x + 12$, so $2x = 22$.

Q: If $\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{x}{3} = 10$, then $x$ is
- 6
- 10
* 12
- 60
W: $\dfrac{5x}{6} = 10$, so $x = 12$.

Q: If $\dfrac{2x + 1}{3} = \dfrac{x + 3}{2}$, then $x$ is
- 5
* 7
- 9
- 11
W: Cross-multiply: $4x + 2 = 3x + 9$.

Q: Three times a number decreased by 4 is 17. The number is
- 5
- 6
* 7
- 13
W: $3x - 4 = 17$, so $3x = 21$.

Q: The sum of two consecutive odd numbers is 56. The larger number is
- 27
* 29
- 31
- 28
W: $x + (x + 2) = 56$ gives $x = 27$, and the larger number is 29.

Q: A mother is 4 times as old as her daughter. After 5 years she will be 3 times as old. The daughter's present age is
- 5 years
- 8 years
* 10 years
- 15 years
W: $4x + 5 = 3(x + 5)$ gives $x = 10$.

Q: The perimeter of a rectangle is 48 cm and its length is twice its breadth. The breadth is
- 6 cm
* 8 cm
- 12 cm
- 16 cm
W: $2(2x + x) = 48$, so $x = 8$.

Q: A purse has 20 coins, some of Rs 5 and the rest of Rs 2, worth Rs 70 in all. The number of Rs 5 coins is
- 6
- 8
* 10
- 12
W: $5x + 2(20 - x) = 70$ gives $3x = 30$.

Q: Two cars start from the same place in opposite directions at 40 km/h and 50 km/h. They are 270 km apart after
- 2 hours
* 3 hours
- 5 hours
- 27 hours
W: They move apart at 90 km/h, and $270 \div 90 = 3$.
