Q: The simple interest on Rs 4000 at 5% per year for 3 years is
- Rs 200
- Rs 500
* Rs 600
- Rs 630
W: $\dfrac{4000 \times 5 \times 3}{100} = 600$.

Q: Rs 2500 is lent at 8% per year simple interest for 2 years. The amount is
- Rs 2700
* Rs 2900
- Rs 2916
- Rs 2540
W: The interest is Rs 400, and amount = principal + interest.

Q: The simple interest on Rs 3000 for 4 years is Rs 720. The rate per year is
- 4%
- 5%
* 6%
- 8%
W: $R = \dfrac{720 \times 100}{3000 \times 4} = 6$.

Q: In how many years will Rs 5000 earn a simple interest of Rs 1500 at 10% per year?
- 2 years
* 3 years
- 5 years
- 30 years
W: The interest is Rs 500 a year, and $1500 \div 500 = 3$.

Q: The compound interest on Rs 10,000 at 10% per year for 2 years, compounded yearly, is
- Rs 2000
* Rs 2100
- Rs 2200
- Rs 12,100
W: The amount is $10000 \times 1.1^2 = 12100$, so the interest is Rs 2100.

Q: Rs 8000 is invested at 5% per year, compounded yearly. The amount after 2 years is
- Rs 8400
- Rs 8800
* Rs 8820
- Rs 8840
W: $8000 \times 1.05^2 = 8000 \times 1.1025 = 8820$.

Q: The difference between the compound interest and the simple interest on Rs 5000 at 10% per year for 2 years is
- Rs 0
- Rs 25
* Rs 50
- Rs 100
W: S.I. is Rs 1000 and C.I. is Rs 1050. The difference is the interest on the first year's interest: 10% of Rs 500.

Q: A town has 20,000 people and its population grows by 5% every year. The population after 2 years is
- 21,000
- 22,000
* 22,050
- 22,500
W: $20000 \times 1.05^2 = 22050$.

Q: A sum of money doubles itself in 10 years at simple interest. The rate per year is
- 5%
* 10%
- 20%
- 100%
W: The interest equals the principal in 10 years, so the rate is $100 \div 10 = 10$ per cent.

Q: A car worth Rs 4,00,000 depreciates by 20% every year. Its value after 2 years is
- Rs 2,40,000
* Rs 2,56,000
- Rs 3,20,000
- Rs 3,60,000
W: $400000 \times 0.8^2 = 256000$.
