Q: 8 books cost Rs 240. At the same rate, 15 books cost
- Rs 360
- Rs 420
* Rs 450
- Rs 480
W: One book costs Rs 30.

Q: $x$ and $y$ vary directly, and $y = 15$ when $x = 6$. When $x = 10$, $y$ is
- 9
- 19
* 25
- 4
W: $\dfrac{y}{x} = \dfrac{15}{6} = 2.5$, so $y = 25$.

Q: 12 men can do a piece of work in 15 days. 20 men can do it in
- 25 days
- 12 days
* 9 days
- 7 days
W: The work is $12 \times 15 = 180$ man-days, and $180 \div 20 = 9$.

Q: Two quantities $x$ and $y$ vary inversely when
- $x + y$ is constant
- $x - y$ is constant
- $\dfrac{x}{y}$ is constant
* $xy$ is constant
W: In inverse variation, when one quantity is doubled the other is halved, so the product stays the same.

Q: A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. Together they can do it in
- 25 days
- 12.5 days
* 6 days
- 5 days
W: In one day they do $\dfrac{1}{10} + \dfrac{1}{15} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ of the job.

Q: A and B together finish a job in 4 days. A alone can finish it in 12 days. B alone can finish it in
- 8 days
* 6 days
- 16 days
- 3 days
W: B's one-day work is $\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{12} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.

Q: One pipe fills a tank in 6 hours and another in 12 hours. Together they fill it in
- 18 hours
- 9 hours
* 4 hours
- 3 hours
W: In one hour they fill $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{12} = \dfrac{1}{4}$ of the tank.

Q: A car travelling at 60 km/h takes 5 hours for a journey. To finish it in 4 hours, its speed must be
- 48 km/h
- 65 km/h
- 70 km/h
* 75 km/h
W: The distance is 300 km, and $300 \div 4 = 75$.

Q: 6 pumps can empty a tank in 8 hours. 4 pumps of the same kind empty it in
- 5 hours 20 minutes
- 10 hours
* 12 hours
- 16 hours
W: $6 \times 8 = 48$ pump-hours, and $48 \div 4 = 12$.

Q: 5 workers earn Rs 3000 in 4 days. At the same rate, 8 workers earn in 6 days
- Rs 4800
- Rs 5760
* Rs 7200
- Rs 9000
W: One worker earns Rs 150 a day. $8 \times 6 \times 150 = 7200$.
